Type A - 3 Bedroom Home In Lalela, Sheffield Beach

Welcome to an established North Coast community where family-inspired amenities, open skies and serene, green hills paint the backdrop to home life.

Lalela is situated just off the new N2 interchange in Sheffield, within steps of the iconic Manor House, a beloved North Coast venue. Here you will find residents playing padel and kids riding their bikes freely through the estate.

This Type A home offers 3 spacious bedrooms 2 bathrooms with a spacious open plan kitchen, dining room and lounge leading out to a garden.

Option to upgrade to a lock up garage and covered patio.

The estate offers family friendly amenities such as a community centre, tennis courts, cricket nets, pools slides and so much more!

Lalela residents will enjoy links to the new interchange planned from the N2 Highway, with a North bound off-ramp and South bound on-ramp. 20 minutes to King Shaka International Airport
