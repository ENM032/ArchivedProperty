Palm Lakes Family Estate situated on the Kwa-Zulu Natal North Coast - less than 4km inland from the Tinley Manor beachfront - has become a firm family favourite. It is one of the fastest-growing Estates and a perfect investment for young families and for those looking to retire in a beautiful secure estate with its very own bespoke retirement village. Palm Lakes provides community living and a life where children get to enjoy a sense of freedom with a number of jungle gyms and clubhouses, pools to swim in and dams to fish in. You can enjoy the beautiful wildlife throughout the estate, often right on your doorstep. The estate also features its own Mama G''s restaurant which includes a take-away service for welcome convenience. Palm Lakes Family Estate is Equipped with an array of upmarket and modern facilities which are aimed at enhancing family living and promoting an active lifestyle. From vibrant community gathering areas - such as the sunny decks and sparkling pools surrounding the Dragonfly Clubhouse - to the quiet dams teeming with fish, this estate has something for everyone.
