Sole & Exclusive Mandate | Modern 3 Bedroom Family Home in Sought-After Sunset Place, Palm Lakes

Step into effortless living with this beautifully presented 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom home, perfectly positioned in the secure and highly desirable Sunset Place within Palm Lakes Estate. Designed with comfort and style in mind, this home offers a light filled open-plan kitchen, dining, and living area that flows seamlessly ideal for both relaxed family living and entertaining. The modern kitchen is thoughtfully laid out with quality finishes and ample storage, making it the heart of the home.

All three bedrooms are generously sized, with the main bedroom featuring an en-suite bathroom. The additional bathroom is well-appointed to serve family and guests alike. Outside, enjoy a spacious private garden perfect for children, pets, or simply unwinding in your own tranquil outdoor space.

Residents of Palm Lakes enjoy access to an exceptional range of lifestyle amenities, including:

- Multiple swimming pools, including a lap pool and leisure pools

- Clubhouse with restaurant and bar

- Scenic walking and cycling trails

- Fishing dams and picnic areas

- Kids play areas and jungle gyms

- Skate park and sports facilities

- 24-hour security with controlled access

This is a rare opportunity to secure a stylish, move-in-ready home in one of the North Coasts most family-friendly estates.
