3 Bedroom House in Palm Lakes Estate

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 4 196 m²

Contemporary Estate Living with Scenic Views

Located in the sort after Palm Family Lakes Estate, this beautifully designed 3 bedroom double storey townhouse offers the perfect blend of modern comfort, security, and lifestyle living.

The 196m2 home welcomes you on the upper level where three well-appointed bedrooms are located. The master suite enjoys panoramic views across the surrounding farmlands, complete with air conditioning and a stylish en-suite bathroom.

The lower level has been thoughtfully designed for entertaining and relaxed family living, featuring a fully fitted kitchen with centre island, separate scullery, guest bathroom, and open-plan living space.

Sliding doors open onto a covered patio and level pet-friendly garden, where you can enjoy uninterrupted countryside views and peaceful estate living.

Additional features include:

Double automated garage

Additional parking

Pet-friendly garden

Secure estate lifestyle

Residents of Palm Lakes Estate benefit from exceptional lifestyle amenities, including swimming pools, walking trails, fishing dams, playgrounds, and 24-hour security.

This home offers the perfect opportunity to experience North Coast estate living at its finest.
