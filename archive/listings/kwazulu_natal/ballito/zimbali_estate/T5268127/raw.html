Opportunity of a lifetime!! Elegant Family Home with Forest Views in Zimbali

Set across 3 levels amongst the indigenous forest of the prestigious Zimbali Coastal Estate. This unique residence offers a unique blend of tranquility, privacy and refined living. From the moment you step inside this gem, you''re welcome by light filled interiors.

The heart of the home being an expansive open-plan living area that leads to a swimming pool in a private courtyard with a spacious under cover patio perfect for relaxed family living or entertaining guests in style. The ground floor has 3 car garages, an office, a staff suite or flatlet.

First floor has the living areas, a guest suite, and on the 2nd floor you will find 3 bedrooms all ensuite. The spacious main with a walk-in closet and balcony. This home has an airconditioning, underfloor heating , gas fireplace, 40 KVA diesel generator & 2 water tanks with a filter for drinking.

A rare opportunity to own a forest sanctuary in one of South Africa''s most exclusive estates.
