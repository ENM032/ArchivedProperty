PRACTICAL MODERN LIVING MEETS ZIMBALI'S UNDERSTATED REFINED ELEGANCE

Sole & Exclusive Mandate to Seeff Zimbali Welcome to 2 Ilanga Villas, an elegant single-level sectional title home located within the prestigious Zimbali Estate. Perfectly positioned near the North Gate, this residence offers exceptional convenience with quick access for both entry and exit, while still enjoying the privacy and security of estate living. Designed for modern lifestyles, the home features light, bright interiors and a beautifully balanced open-plan layout.

The contemporary kitchen, complete with a discreet scullery, keeps meal preparation tucked away, ensuring the main kitchen remains immaculate. Seamless flow into the dining area creates the ideal setting for effortless entertaining and refined dining experiences. The spacious lounge integrates smoothly with an enclosed patio equipped with a built-in gas braai, extending the living space for year-round enjoyment. Large sliding doors connect or separate these areas as needed, offering flexibility for both intimate family moments and larger gatherings. Accommodation includes three generously sized bedrooms plus an additional room ideal for a dedicated study or optional fourth bedroom. The primary suite stands out with its luxurious full bathroom and abundant cupboard space, offering a serene retreat at the end of the day. Additional features include a double garage with direct access into the home, enhancing convenience and security, as well as visitor parking situated directly opposite the property.

Elegant, functional, and ideally located within Zimbalis lush surroundings, 2 Ilanga Villas presents a great opportunity to secure a sophisticated home in one of the estates most sought-after villa developments.

Zimbali has developed into South Africa''s premier and most exclusive lifestyle estate within one of the country''s most naturally beautiful locations. The impressive resident clubhouses with the children''s playground have become the benchmark for state-of-the-art communal amenities. Full access to all the amazing amenities is provided, which include

-18-hole par 72 Championship Zimbali Golf Course,

-Six tennis courts,

-Two squash courts,

-Three cricket nets,

-Extensive communal pools and direct access to the 3,5km of pristine beach.

-Five-star gymnasium and fitness centre: usage is free and exclusive to owners and long-term tenants

-World-class soccer pitch with astroturf

-Nature and walking trails with over 200 species of birds to see.
