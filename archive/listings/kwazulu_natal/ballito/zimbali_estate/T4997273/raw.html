Opulent 3-Bedroom Fully Furnished Villa in Zimbali Estate

This move-in-ready villa offers the ultimate perfect blend of luxury, convenience, and exclusivity in one of South Africas most prestigious, sought-after coastal estates. Zimbali Estate is internationally recognized for its exclusive coastal lifestyle, offering resort-style living with stunning, vibrant, natural surroundings. It has set a global standard for prestigious coastal living, offering residents an exclusive lifestyle with the perfect blend of comfort and sophistication,with private beaches, a choice of pools, championship golf courses, high-end restaurants, and top-tier 24/7 security.

Designed for those high end serious buyers,who appreciate style, luxury and comfort, this villa features three opulent, spacious bedrooms that are stylishly furnished with modern and classy fittings,for ultimate style and comfort and splendidly designed en-suites, luxuriously appointed with elegant finishes. The anterior bedroom includes a large, sun facing patio, which allows natural light to bathe the space .A fully fitted spacious kitchen with high-end appliances, sleek cabinetry, and premium countertops is a culinary delight. The opulent open plan living and dining areas encompasses modern décor, top notch finishes, elegant flooring, which seamlessly blends indoor and outdoor living with an open patio and built-in braai area and a beautifully landscaped garden. perfect for relaxation or entertaining guests. A single extended garage includes a laundry area and provides easy access to an outdoor courtyard.

.

Residents have exclusive access to the world-class 18 hole Zimbali Golf Course,The Zimbali fitness and wellness centre and The Zimbali gym. The Zimbali Coastal Resort Sports Club features well maintained tennis and basketball courts, providing the perfect space for friendly matches or serious training. The world class restaurants, cater of every culinary desire, along with The Capital Zimbali, a luxurious hotel that enhances the estates world-class lifestyle.The estates lush surroundings ,that are teeming with indigenous fauna and flora, make outdoor activities even more pleasurable for nature lovers and hiking enthusiasts .The estate also offers impressive clubhouse facilities, including a childrens playground, cricket nets, and a soccer field.

The Zimbali Coastal Resort is in excess of 456 hectares, incorporating approximately 120 hectares of indigenous KwaZulu-Natal (KZN) coastal forest which is preserved and retained as part of the conservation-amenity zone(green belt). It also has a 3 kilometre stretch of unspoilt Indian Ocean shoreline on the KZN North Coast.

Whether you''re looking for a serene escape from the concrete jungle or a stylish coastal residence, this villa is an exceptional choice.
