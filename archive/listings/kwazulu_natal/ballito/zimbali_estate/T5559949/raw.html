5 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

Contact agent for street address
5 4.5 2 976 m² 500 m²

Perfectly created by this happy family . Exclusive Sole Mandate to Pam Golding Properties - Simply magnificent! Refurbished to excellence, tastefully appointed and oh-so ideal for the larger family. This home seriously ticks boxes and so much more.

Privately positioned with east facing, level elevation and sea views, this double storey family home offers great open plan reception and family areas as well as five superbly sized bedrooms and additional full staff accommodation.

Lets talk about that kitchen! Generally accepted for many years as the heartbeat of any familys social interaction, kitchens have become the focal point to open plan living areas the world over and this principle is exemplified within the ambience of this home like no other. And yet, despite its impressive 8-metre island counter, the over-all flow of this open plan area remains beautifully proportioned with lounges and dining areas commanding their own generous spaces.

A superb application of whites throughout the property, to emphasize on propertys vast spaces and to celebrate its abundant natural light, is designed to interact with contrasting darker feature textures and fitted elements, including a stylish glassed-in wine room, giving this property such a crisp and streamlined appeal.

The home delivers too on its exterior with a large patios and level lawns playing to the needs of children and entertainers alike, whilst double lock-up garaging is well complemented by additional on-site parking.
