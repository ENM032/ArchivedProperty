3 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

51 zimbali wedge, 51 r627 road
3 2 2 7 081 m² 161 m²

Modern Family Home in a Secure Lifestyle Estate

Experience the perfect combination of modern living, security, and convenience in this beautifully maintained three-bedroom home, ideally positioned within one of Ballito''s most sought-after secure complexes.

Enjoy the luxury of being just a short stroll from gourmet restaurants, boutique shopping, and popular cafs, while still benefiting from quick access to major transport routes and all of Ballito''s premier amenities.

Designed for effortless living, this light-filled home offers a spacious open-plan layout where the contemporary kitchen, complete with a separate scullery, flows seamlessly into the dining and lounge areas. A stylish built-in TV unit enhances the living space, while sliding doors lead to a private wraparound garden, creating the ideal setting for entertaining, relaxing, or enjoying time with your pets. The fully enclosed garden comfortably accommodates two dogs, making it perfect for pet-loving families.

A convenient guest toilet on the ground floor adds to the home''s practical design, making entertaining family and friends effortless.

Upstairs, you''ll find three generously proportioned bedrooms. The spacious main bedroom boasts a private en-suite bathroom, while the remaining two bedrooms are served by a modern family bathroom.

Additional features include:

- Two dedicated parking bays conveniently located directly outside the front door.

- Abundant natural light throughout the home.

- Modern finishes and a well-maintained interior.

- Pet-friendly, fully enclosed wraparound garden.

- Access to a communal swimming pool within the secure estate.

- Inverter ready.

- Air con.

Excellent security for complete peace of mind.

Whether you''re looking for a stylish family home, a lock-up-and-go lifestyle, or an excellent investment in the heart of Ballito, this exceptional property offers outstanding value in an unbeatable location.
