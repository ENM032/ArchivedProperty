A sophisticated modern home in Zimbali Estate

This exquisite home is tucked away in a serene cul-de-sac within the prestigious Zimbali Estate. Upon entry, a grand staircase welcomes you, leading into the expansive open-plan living area. The layout is designed to maximize space and natural light, creating a warm and inviting ambiance. The space seamlessly integrates the dining room, kitchen, and TV lounge, making it perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. For a more relaxed vibe, an informal lounge with a built-in braai opens directly onto the pool and patio, ideal for outdoor gatherings.

A charming courtyard extends from the dining room, offering a peaceful retreat within this tranquil environment. The modern kitchen is a true standout, featuring a gas stove, eye-level oven, and built-in coffee maker for ultimate convenience. A separate scullery keeps the area tidy and organized, ensuring the kitchen remains clutter-free. Centrally located, the kitchen encourages interaction with family and guests, further enhancing the sense of connection in the home. A guest toilet is also conveniently located on this level.

The upper floor is dedicated to rest and relaxation, with generously sized bedrooms, each with its own ensuite bathroom, ensuring privacy and comfort. For added convenience, theres secure parking with a double garage, a golf cart garage, and at least three additional parking spaces.

Zimbali Estate has evolved into South Africas premier and most exclusive lifestyle destination, nestled in one of the countrys most naturally beautiful locations. It offers discerning homeowners the ultimate in privacy, with a harmonious blend of nature and luxury. Zimbali provides barefoot luxury for those who choose to make it their home. With its prime beachfront location, spanning 3.5 km of pristine beach and direct oceanfront access, Zimbali sets itself apart, offering an unparalleled lifestyle. The renowned Valley of the Pools resident amenity is among the finest in the world, featuring direct beach access, a daytime restaurant and cocktail bar, and several swimming pools, including one heated for year-round use. The impressive Bush Buck Club, with its childrens playground, has set the standard for state-of-the-art communal amenities.

Zimbali offers an array of world-class amenities, including:

18-hole par 72 Championship Zimbali Golf Course

Four tennis courts

Two squash courts

Three cricket nets

Extensive communal pools and direct access to the 3.5 km of pristine beach

A five-star gymnasium and fitness center exclusive to owners and long-term tenants

World-class Astroturf soccer pitch

Nature and walking trails with over 200 species of birds

Strategically placed viewing decks for enjoying the waterfall, birdlife, or witnessing migrating humpback whales on their journey north

Zimbali has become an international benchmark for excellence in large-scale residential and resort developments, offering a lifestyle that is second to none.
