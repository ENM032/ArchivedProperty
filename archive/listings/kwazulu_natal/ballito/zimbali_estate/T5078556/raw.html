4 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

Contact agent for street address
4 4 2 1 303 m² 500 m²

Charming Family Home in Zimbali with Expansive Garden and Endless Potential. Tucked away in a peaceful cul-de-sac and overlooking a serene greenbelt, this inviting family home in Zimbali is bursting with potential and ready for a new chapter. Set on a generously sized plot with a large, lush garden, this home offers the perfect canvas to create something truly special.

Featuring four spacious bedrooms, a dedicated study, and two comfortable lounges, there is ample room for family living and entertaining. The main lounge opens out to a sunny swimming pool area and a rolling grassy garden ideal for children to play and for hosting memorable outdoor gatherings.

The home is well-positioned in a quiet and secure part of the estate, offering privacy, tranquility, and a strong sense of community. With a little vision and care, this property can be beautifully transformed into a warm and elegant family haven in one of Zimbali''s most desirable locations.

A rare opportunity to own a property with heart, space, and endlesspotential.
