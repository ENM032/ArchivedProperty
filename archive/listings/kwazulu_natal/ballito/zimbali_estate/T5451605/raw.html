3 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

Contact agent for street address
3 3 4 9 ha 367 m²

Prestigious blend of elegance and class on the Beach Estate . Dual Mandate

Set within the prestigious Zimbali Coastal Resort, this gorgeous beach estate unit is a seamless blend of elegance, light, and laid-back coastal luxury. As you arrive, you are welcomed by ample parking and a spacious double garage, setting the tone for both comfort and convenience. The home immediately draws you in with its expansive glass finishes, where natural light simply pours through every corner, creating a warm, inviting atmosphere throughout. The ground floor is thoughtfully designed with all bedrooms privately positioned, offering a sense of calm and retreat. Each room feels connected to the lush surroundings, while a dedicated study provides the perfect work from home space. A well appointed laundry adds to the homes effortless practicality.

Upstairs, the home truly comes to life. The open plan layout is nothing short of spectacular crafted for both relaxation and entertaining. Multiple living areas flow seamlessly into one another, from a sophisticated lounge to a cosy pyjama lounge, and a stylish bar area that invites easy evenings with friends and family. Its a space where every moment feels elevated.

Positioned just moments from the beach access, this home captures the essence of coastal living, morning walks on the sand, ocean air, and the soothing rhythm of the sea just steps away. Beyond the home, Zimbali Coastal Resort offers a lifestyle few can match. This world-class estate is renowned for its pristine beaches, indigenous coastal forest, and exceptional security. Residents enjoy access to championship golf, scenic walking trails, and a selection of top tier dining venues. Just a short drive away lies the vibrant energy of Ballito, where trendy restaurants, cafs, and boutique shopping create a lively coastal hub. Whether youre seeking tranquillity or a social buzz, everything is right at your doorstep. This is more than a home, its a lifestyle defined by light, space, and the effortless beauty of coastal living.
