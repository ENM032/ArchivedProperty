4 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

11 ebuhleni, 143 yellowwood drive
4 4.5 8 888 m² 398 m²

A Private Zimbali Retreat Designed for Effortless Living

Tucked away on a private stand within Zimbali Estate, this beautifully curated townhouse offers more than just a place to live it offers a way of life. Calm, refined and quietly luxurious, the home is designed for those who appreciate space, flow and moments that feel unhurried.

The living spaces open effortlessly to the outdoors, with full-length stack-back doors inviting natural light and garden views into the heart of the home. Whether its slow mornings, long lunches or evenings spent entertaining, the covered patio, private pool and tropical garden create a setting that feels both indulgent and deeply relaxed.

Set over two levels, the home lends itself equally well to permanent living or an exclusive lock-up-and-go retreat. The undercover veranda is generous yet intimate, designed for connection shared meals, laughter and time well spent. Subtle garden lighting transforms the outdoor spaces at night, creating a soft, resort-like ambience that invites you to linger.

The kitchen sits at the centre of the home modern, elegant and practical flowing seamlessly into the dining and living areas. Its a space designed for easy entertaining and everyday comfort, supported by a separate scullery and laundry. A guest cloakroom completes the lower level.

Upstairs, four en-suite bedrooms offer privacy and sanctuary. Two open onto private balconies, overlooking the estates tree canopy and birdlife, while the main suite feels indulgent yet serene, with generous proportions, a dressing area and a beautifully finished full en-suite bathroom.

With a double garage, additional guest parking and the security and lifestyle Zimbali is known for, this home speaks to those seeking refined living without compromise.

This is a residence for those who value lifestyle over excess, privacy over noise, and quality over trends a home that feels as good as it looks.

World-Class Zimbali Estate Amenities:

- 3.5 km of pristine beachfront access

- Championship 18-hole Golf Course

- Tennis & squash courts, cricket nets, soccer pitch

- Resort-style pools & resident clubhouses

- Five-star gym, spa & wellness facilities

- Walking trails, birdwatching & indigenous forest paths

- Fine dining & luxury hospitality at The Capital Zimbali Hotel
