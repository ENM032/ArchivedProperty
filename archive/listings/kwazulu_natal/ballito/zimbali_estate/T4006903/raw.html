4 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

. zimbali beach estate, 18 weavers pond
4 3.5 2 9 ha 372 m²
HD Media

IMMACULATE SPACIOUS TOWNHOUSE WITHIN PRIVATE SETTING

Sole&Exclusive Mandate to Seeff Zimbali

Immaculately presented and instantly desirable, this uber spacious free-standing home permeates quality. The conveniently attached double garage leads directly into the property, and the ground floor offers four bedrooms and a separate laundry room. The recently fitted new wooden flooring in the four bedrooms is stunning. The surrounding natural coastal forest ensures a very high level of privacy.

The magnificent stairwell allows natural light to flood through the home and leads to the first floor, which reveals all the living and entertainment spaces.

The gourmet kitchen has a separate scullery. The double lounge, dining, and bar areas open onto an under-cover patio at the tree-top level with ocean vistas beyond. This home is situated in the exclusive Zimbali Beach Estate section of Zimbali. It allows easy access for residents here to Compensation Beach Road and the Ballito village, which has become very popular with coffee shops, bars and restaurants. Those residents who own properties within the Zimbali Beach Estate are rarely sellers and tend to hold their properties for extensive periods. For this property, the option exists to purchase the registered owner Pty Ltd as a going concern - full financials are available.

Zimbali has developed into South Africa''s premier and exclusive lifestyle estate in one of the country''s most naturally beautiful locations. Its full beachfront position with 3,5km of pristine beach and ocean-front access sets it apart and provides the AAA-grade rating. The impressive residents'' clubhouses with the children''s playground have become the benchmark for the state-of-the-art communal amenities. Full access to all the amazing amenities is provided, which include

-18-hole par 72 Championship Zimbali Golf Course,

-Six tennis courts,

-Two squash courts,

-Three cricket nets,

-Extensive communal pools and direct access to the 3,5km of pristine beach.

-Five-star gymnasium and fitness centre: usage is free and exclusive to owners and long-term tenants

-World-class soccer pitch with AstroTurf

-Nature and walking trails with over 200 species of birds to see.

Zimbali has become an international benchmark for excellence in large-scale residential and resort developments.
