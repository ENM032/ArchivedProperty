Four Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

This modern four-bedroom home in Zimbali Hills offers four spacious en-suite bedrooms, including one on the entrance level next to the double garage and three upstairs of which two has their own balconies ,while the open-plan living area flows to the swimming pool.

Designed for easy family living, the home combines stylish finishes, generous living spaces, and excellent indoor-outdoor flow.

Zimbali Hills Estate offers 24-hour security, walking trails, children''s play areas, and is conveniently located close to Ballito''s beaches, shopping centres, schools, and King Shaka International Airport.
