6 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

18 brittlewood drive
6 6 6 1 165 m² 515 m²

STRIKING ZIMBALI RESIDENCE WITH PANORAMIC OCEAN, GOLF AND HOTEL VIEWS

Offering a rare combination of space, flexibility, and character, this Balinese-inspired home is elevated to capture sweeping views of the Indian Ocean, the 5th fairway, golf course dam, and the nearby Capital Hotel. It presents a compelling opportunity for both lifestyle enjoyment and long-term investment within the prestigious Zimbali Estate. Thoughtfully laid out across two levels, the home features six en-suite bedrooms, multiple entertainment areas, and dual open-plan living zones, each with lounges, dining spaces, and fully equipped kitchens and sculleries. One of the suites functions as a separate flatlet off the pool deck, ideal for guests, extended family, or short-term rental income, while another loft-style bedroom enjoys elevated views over the pool. The spacious ground-floor suite allows for single-level living, perfect for elderly occupants or convenience. Architecturally inspired by tropical resort living, the design incorporates expansive under-cover balconies and breezy outdoor spaces ideal for entertaining or unwinding in privacy. Located in a prime position within the estate, residents enjoy easy access to Zimbali''s main gates, as well as proximity to the Capital Hotel, the popular Bushbuck Club with incredible amenities and the beautiful estate pools.

Zimbali has developed into South Africa''s premier and exclusive lifestyle estate in one of the country''s most naturally beautiful locations. This is a place for discerning homeowners who value privacy and interacting with a pristine natural environment a place for authentic living where barefoot luxury is available to those who choose to call it home. Its full beachfront position with 3,5km of pristine beach and ocean-front access sets it apart and provides the AAA-grade rating. The always popular Valley of the Pools residents amenity is one of the finest in the world, offering direct beach access, a daytime restaurant and cocktail bar, and a series of swimming pools, with one even heated to permit year-round use. The impressive resident Bush Buck Club with the children''s playground has become the benchmark for state-of-the-art communal amenities. The amazing Zimbali amenities include:

- 18-hole par 72 Championship Zimbali Golf Course,

- Four tennis courts,

- Two squash courts,

- Three cricket nets,

- Two Padel courts,

- Extensive communal pools and direct access to the 3,5km of pristine beach.

- Five-star gymnasium and fitness centre: usage is free and exclusive to owners and long-term tenants

- World-class soccer pitch with Astroturf

- Nature and walking trails with over 200 species of birds to see.

- Strategically positioned viewing decks to enjoy the waterfall, and birdlife around the lake or witness migrating humpback whales on their journey northwards.

Zimbali has become an international benchmark for excellence in large-scale residential and resort developments.
