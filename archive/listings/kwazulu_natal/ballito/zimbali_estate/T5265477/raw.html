3 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

16 zimbali estate, 16 phezulu villas
3 3 2 1.6 ha 289 m²

Contemporary Penthouse Elegance in Zimbali Coastal Resort

Experience the pinnacle of coastal luxury with this immaculate penthouse apartment, perfectly positioned within the prestigious Zimbali Coastal Resort. Designed for those who appreciate refined living and effortless style, this residence combines modern sophistication with the ease of a secure, lock-up-and-go lifestyle.

This fully furnished home offers three beautifully appointed en-suite bedrooms, each designed to provide comfort and privacy. The expansive open-plan layout integrates a sleek designer kitchen, dining area, and elegant living space, all flowing seamlessly onto an enclosed entertainment patioideal for relaxed gatherings throughout the year. Every detail, from the high-end finishes to the ambient lighting and curated dcor, has been thoughtfully considered to create a space that is both stylish and inviting.

Perfect as a primary residence, holiday home, or investment opportunity, this penthouse enjoys a prime location near Zimbalis South Gate, offering easy access to King Shaka International Airport, Ballitos premier shopping and dining destinations, and major transport routes.

Key Features:

3 spacious en-suite bedrooms

Guest cloakroom

Gourmet kitchen with separate scullery and laundry

Enclosed patio for year-round entertaining

Open-plan living and dining areas with premium finishes

Double automated garage plus private storage space

Inverter system for uninterrupted power supply

Exclusive top-floor position ensuring privacy and security

Option to purchase fully furnished

Residents enjoy full access to Zimbalis world-class amenities, including the championship golf course, resort-style pools, tennis courts, scenic walking trails, and direct private beach accessoffering a lifestyle of leisure and exclusivity.

This is a rare opportunity to own a sophisticated coastal sanctuary in one of South Africas most sought-after estates.
