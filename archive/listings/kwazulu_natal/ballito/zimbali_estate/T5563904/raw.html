3 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

Contact agent for street address
3 4 1 1 132 m²

FOR SALE- Camwood Drive, Zimbali Estate

This contemporary coastal residence offers a seamless blend of modern elegance and relaxed estate living. Designed with both family comfort and effortless entertaining in mind, the home is turnkey and freshly updated, making it ready for immediate occupation.

Key Features

3 spacious bedrooms, 4 bathrooms plus guest cloakroom

Open-plan living areas with stackable doors leading to two covered patios

Swimming pool and level lawn ideal for family gatherings and outdoor relaxation

Ground-floor master suite with direct garden access and luxurious en-suite

Two upstairs bedrooms each with private bathrooms and balconies showcasing ocean views

Modern kitchen with scullery, perfect for everyday living and entertaining

Recently repainted inside and out, ensuring a fresh, move-in-ready feel

Lifestyle & Location

Placed in one of Zimbalis most peaceful and prestigious roads, the property offers privacy, exclusivity, and tranquil sea views. The manicured garden and pool area create a serene retreat, making this home ideal as a permanent residence or a lock-up-and-go coastal escape.

Zimbali Estate Amenities

Residents enjoy access to world-class facilities, including:

Championship 18-hole golf course

Direct access to 3.5 km of pristine beachfront

Exclusive fitness centre and gym

Communal pools, tennis and squash courts, cricket nets, and soccer pitch

Scenic walking trails with abundant birdlife

Clubhouses and childrens playgrounds
