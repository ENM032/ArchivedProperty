4 Bedroom House in Zimbali Estate

19 flatcrown drive
4 3.5 2 1 607 m² 500 m²

Tranquil 4 Bedroom Family Home Near Zimbalis Valley of the Pools

Nestled amongst expansive indigenous greenery and mature trees, this warm and character-filled 4 bedroom home in Zimbali Estate offers a rare sense of privacy, space and connection to nature. With one of the four bedrooms configured as a private flatlet, this residence is ideal for extended family, guests, work-from-home flexibility, or independent accommodation.

Designed in a timeless Mediterranean-inspired style, the home welcomes you with a generous driveway, ample parking, and an inviting sense of arrival. Large windows frame the surrounding foliage beautifully, allowing natural light to filter through the interiors while drawing the outdoors in.

The living spaces are comfortable and spacious, flowing seamlessly to the outdoor entertainment area with a private pool and timber deck, creating an easy lifestyle for both relaxed family living and entertaining. The kitchen is well proportioned and functional, with solid wood cabinetry, a central breakfast counter, and easy access to the dining and lounge areas.

The bedroom accommodation is generous, with peaceful garden outlooks and a calm, restful atmosphere throughout. The bathrooms are spacious and well-appointed, while the separate flatlet adds valuable versatility to the home.

Positioned close to the Valley of the Pools and the beach club, yet sheltered from the prevailing winds, this property offers the best of Zimbali living convenience, privacy, and a setting that feels deeply immersed in nature.

This is a home for buyers who value authentic estate living, timeless design, and the beauty of a lush, established setting within one of the North Coasts most sought-after estates.

Contact me to arrange a private viewing of this charming Zimbali home.
