Fully Renovated Family Home

SOLE MANDATE | This immaculate home in Caledon Estate has been fully renovated to a high standard and offers modern, move-in-ready living with quality finishes throughout. The open-plan living and dining area extends onto a covered patio and private garden, while the contemporary kitchen features a large centre island, gas hob, and direct access to the courtyard.
