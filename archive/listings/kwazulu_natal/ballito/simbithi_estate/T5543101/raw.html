Tranquil Ground Floor Garden Apartment in Simbithi Eco Estate

Discover effortless estate living in this beautifully presented three bedroom ground floor corner apartment, perfectly positioned within the prestigious Simbithi Eco Estate. Offering exceptional privacy, generous proportions and seamless indoor-outdoor living, this home is ideal for families, professionals, retirees or investors seeking an exceptional lifestyle in Simbithi Eco Estate.

One of the apartment''s most impressive features is its expansive covered veranda that stretches the full length of the home, creating an inviting outdoor living and entertainment space. Surrounded by indigenous gardens, it offers a peaceful sanctuary where you can entertain with ease, enjoy morning coffee amongst the birdlife or unwind after a busy day.

Natural light fills the open plan living and dining areas, which flow effortlessly onto the veranda, creating a spacious and airy atmosphere. A bespoke built-in serving and display bar adds both style and practicality, making the home perfectly suited to relaxed entertaining. The kitchen is positioned to maximise functionality while allowing the living spaces to remain the focal point of the home.

All three generously sized bedrooms enjoy direct access to the outdoors, creating a wonderful connection with the surrounding natural environment. The luxurious main suite features a walk-through dressing area leading to the en-suite bathroom, while the remaining bedrooms are serviced by a family bathroom. External sliding shutters on each bedroom provide additional privacy, security and climate control.

Whether you''re searching for a permanent residence, a secure lock up and go coastal home or a high-demand investment property, this apartment offers outstanding value within one of Ballito''s most desirable estates.

Life in Simbithi Eco Estate is about so much more than owning a home it''s about embracing an exceptional lifestyle.

Residents enjoy an award winning 18 hole golf course, multiple community centres, swimming pools, restaurants, a sports bar, clubhouse facilities, tennis and padel courts, squash courts, a gymnasium, spa, bowling green, conference facilities, children''s play areas, horse stables and beautifully maintained catch-and-release dams. Scenic walking, running and cycling trails wind through indigenous forests and around the estate''s 27 dams, creating a lifestyle centred around wellness, nature and outdoor living.

Renowned for its exceptional security, Simbithi Eco Estate offers 24-hour access control, giving residents complete peace of mind while enjoying one of the North Coast''s most sought-after addresses. Ideally located just minutes from Ballito''s pristine beaches, leading schools, shopping centres, private hospitals and King Shaka International Airport, every convenience is within easy reach.

Ground floor apartments with spacious private gardens, premium privacy and access to world class estate facilities are increasingly difficult to find. Combining modern comfort, tranquil surroundings, outstanding amenities and an unbeatable location, this is a property that represents exceptional value and a lifestyle that is second to none.

Arrange your private viewing today and experience everything this remarkable home and the Simbithi lifestyle have to offer.
