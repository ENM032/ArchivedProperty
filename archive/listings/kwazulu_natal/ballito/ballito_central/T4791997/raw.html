3 Bedroom House in Ballito Central

5266 palm view estate
3 2.5 326 m² 89 m²

Family Estate Modern Home

SOLE MANDATE | Off plan home in Palm View Estate, to be completed in July 2026. Located 15 minutes from Ballito Central, Palm View Estate offers freestanding homes within a secure estate in the sugarcane valleys of the North Coast. This three-bedroom unit offers open plan living on entrance. The kitchen is fitted with an electric hob and features modern finishes throughout. The lounge leads out to the covered patio overlooking the large garden.

The bedroom wing comprises of three well-sized bedrooms and two bathrooms. Additional features include a guest toilet, solar power geyser and rainwater harvesting. Palm View Estate will offer multiple communal facilities include a clubhouse and pools, walking trails, fitness centre, kids play areas, and more. Contact the HUNT team to view.

Transfer and bond costs are included in the purchase price.
