House For Sale in Ballito Central Ballito KwaZulu Natal

Spacious Single-Level Living with Exceptional IndoorOutdoor Appeal in Central Ballito

Situated in a peaceful cul-de-sac in secure boutique estate in the heart of Ballito, this neat single level stand-alone cluster home is set in a level fully fenced private garden, offers very generous proportions throughout the home, and, has a design that wraps around a central outdoor covered verandah enhancing seamless indooroutdoor living from all the living rooms as well as the master suite.

An easy walk in the entrance area framed by large palms and an attractive walkway leads into the home, opening into a very spacious light-filled lounge that opens directly onto the covered verandah and enjoys serene garden views. Adjoining the lounge is the dining room, set alongside the kitchen and with a bay window feature to bring the outdoors in. The dining room also has direct access to the outdoor verandah, allowing for al fresco dining or easy entertaining if required.

The chic kitchen has an ergonomic layout that enables easy circulation, a practical breakfast counter and a separate scullery area.

All three bedrooms have large feature bay windows that augment their spaciousness as well as providing outdoor vistas and ensuring the rooms enjoy ample natural light. The main bedroom has both a ceiling fan and air-conditioning, enjoys direct outdoor access and has a full en suite bathroom. Bedrooms two and three also have use of a full family bathroom and are fitted with ceiling fans.

There is also easy access into the home from the drivewayguest parking area in front of the home making this residence wheelchair friendly if needed. The home also has a double automated garage adding to the homes practicality.

This property is also pet friendly, two dogs are allowed in accordance with Homeowners Association regulations, a huge bonus in Ballito.

With a 24-hour manned entrance gate in central Ballito, this home is a 2-minute drive to Ballito Junction and is close to several schools and medical facilities, making it an ideal choice for those seeking secure everyday living with easy access to daily essentials.
