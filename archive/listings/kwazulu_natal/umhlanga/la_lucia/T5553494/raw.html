4 Bedroom House in La Lucia

33 shelbourne avenue
4 3 2 1 140 m²

Elegant Family Living with Exceptional Outdoor Appeal in Sought-After La Lucia

Positioned on a generous 1 140m2 freehold stand in the heart of La Lucia, this spacious and beautifully maintained home offers the perfect blend of comfort, functionality, and effortless family living.

Designed with both entertaining and everyday living in mind, the home boasts four generously proportioned bedrooms and three bathrooms, including two en-suite bathrooms for added privacy. Multiple living spaces comprise two inviting lounges, a dining room, and a well-appointed kitchen featuring granite countertops, ample cabinetry, a scullerylaundry, making it as practical as it is stylish. Step outside to a private entertainment haven where a sparkling swimming pool, pool lounge and patio overlook the lush garden. Children will love the jungle gym, while the expansive outdoor space provides plenty of room for the whole family to enjoy.

Thoughtfully equipped for modern living, the home includes CCTV, air conditioning, built-in wardrobes, an alarm system, staff accommodation, 9 x solar panel plus a backup battery and inverter, as well as a 2500l water tank to provide backup water supply, ensuring greater convenience and peace of mind. Parking is well catered for with a double automated garage and four additional parking spaces. Ideally located close to top schools, shopping centres, and all the amenities La Lucia is renowned for, this exceptional residence presents an outstanding opportunity to secure a spacious family home in one of Umhlanga''s most desirable suburbs.

This is a home that effortlessly combines generous proportions, practical features, and wonderful outdoor livingperfect for families looking to enjoy the sought-after La Lucia lifestyle.
