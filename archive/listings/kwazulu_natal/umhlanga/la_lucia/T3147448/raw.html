3 Bedroom House in La Lucia

Contact agent for street address
3 2 4 1 037 m²

Bright And Trendy Home

This bright and trendy home is set on level land in prime La Lucia, in a quiet road with a 24-hour manned security post.

There are three bedrooms, with a modern main en-suite and a family bathroom. The living area flows onto the entertainment and pool area and the newly renovated kitchen has a spacious scullery. There is a flatlet with bathroom and kitchen, opening onto a courtyard. In addition, there is solar heating for the geyser, a double garage and lovely flat garden.

G GAVIN PROPERTIES PTY LTD trading as REMAX Panache North Durban operates in terms of a franchise agreement with REMAX of Southern Africa.
