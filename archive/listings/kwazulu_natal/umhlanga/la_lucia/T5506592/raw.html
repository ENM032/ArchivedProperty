5 Bedroom House in La Lucia

Contact agent for street address
5 4 6 1 331 m²

Please contact me for an appointment to view - Sunday 2 August 2:30 - 4pm. PHENOMENAL SEA VIEWS | PRIME LA LUCIA ADDRESS | SELF CONTAINED, VERSATILE FLATLET.

Beautifully renovated throughout, this exceptional family home combines generous proportions, effortless entertaining and breathtaking sea views in one of La Lucia''s most desirable addresses. Designed to maximise its elevated position, the home enjoys spectacular ocean views from the main living areas, entertainment patio and three of the bedrooms, while offering the versatility modern family living demands.

A welcoming entrance leads into expansive open-plan living spaces where the formal lounge, family lounge and dining room flow effortlessly together. Large sliding doors draw in natural light and open onto an impressive, covered patio, creating seamless indoor-outdoor living while framing uninterrupted views across the swimming pool, landscaped garden and the Indian Ocean beyond.

The beautifully renovated kitchen forms the heart of the home, finished with quality cabinetry, stone worktops, ample preparation space and a generous breakfast counter. A separate scullery keeps the kitchen practical and uncluttered while offering additional storage and workspace.

The covered entertainment patio is designed for year-round enjoyment, enclosed with storm blinds and perfectly positioned to overlook the sparkling pool and level garden. Whether entertaining family and friends or simply enjoying the view, this is a space you''ll never want to leave.

The accommodation offers four spacious bedrooms and four beautifully renovated bathrooms within the main residence. The main bedroom is a luxurious retreat with spectacular sea views and a stylish en-suite bathroom. A second bedroom also enjoys an en-suite bathroom, while the third sea-facing bedroom and fourth bedroom are served by a beautifully appointed family bathroom. Warm timber flooring, quality built-in cupboards and contemporary finishes create comfortable, elegant spaces throughout.

On the lower level, a fully self-contained flatlet provides exceptional flexibility, comprising a spacious lounge, kitchen, bedroom and bathroom with direct access to the pool and garden. Ideal for extended family, guests, teenagers, a work-from-home office or as an income-generating opportunity.

Completing this exceptional property are a double automated garage, separate laundry, staff ablutions conveniently positioned off the garage, excellent storage throughout and ample additional off-street parking.

Security includes an alarm system, perimeter beams, electric fencing and security cameras, offering complete peace of mind.

A home that combines exceptional sea views, beautifully renovated interiors, versatile accommodation and one of La Lucia''s most sought-after addresses, delivering an outstanding lifestyle for families who love to entertain.
