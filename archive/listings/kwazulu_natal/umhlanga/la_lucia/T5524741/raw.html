3 Bedroom House in La Lucia

Contact agent for street address
3 2 3 1 417 m²

Prime location, refurbished home, huge land in La Lucia

This completely renovated home from floor to ceiling is a situated less than 2 km from La Lucia mall. This part of the suburb exudes peace and tranquility, in a safe neighbor-hood.

The house features three bedrooms and two bathrooms-one being an en-suite to the well sized master bedroom, an open plan lounge and dining-room, as well as a kitchen-that is still to be fully fitted in the next week or two.

The house is filly tiled, with modern bathrooms and fittings. The bedrooms have just been fitted with high-end cupboards, featuring glass doors and lighting. The cladding on the entertainment wall in the living-room has also just been installed, modern, high-end and aesthetically pleasing. The seller has also purchased a dining-room suite-still to be delivered-hence not on the current pics.

The yard has been landscaped by an expert gardener-work in progress. The remaining land at the rear of the house is expansive and one can build another property, should one elect to. The pool also has a huge space for a pergola, thatch roof, braai-pit, pizza oven, patio furniture etc. the potential is limitless.

There is also a 1 bed granny cottage, adjacent to one lock-up garage and additional parking for 3 vehicles. A longer drive-way can be erected-should that be a requirement for the prospective buyer.

This home is in a sought-after area-close to Umhlanga, Dbn North, drive up to Umdloti and King Shaka.

Very well priced to sell soon. Contact me to view.
