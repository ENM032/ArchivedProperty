3 Bedroom House in La Lucia

Expansive La Lucia Home with Sweeping Sea Views & Self-Contained 3-Bedroom Flatlet

Set on a substantial 1,305m² erf in a sought-after La Lucia address, this spacious residence offers generous proportions, flexible accommodation and sweeping sea views across the coastline and Indian Ocean. With the option of three or four bedrooms in the main home, extensive living and entertainment areas and a fully self-contained three-bedroom flatlet, the property offers exceptional versatility for family living.

The entrance hall leads into the expansive living areas, comprising two spacious lounges, a separate dining room and a dedicated study area. The layout flows comfortably from one space to the next, providing generous areas for both everyday family living and entertaining. The second lounge offers additional flexibility and can easily be utilised as a fourth bedroom, allowing the home to be configured as either a three-bedroom residence with two lounges or a four-bedroom home with one main lounge.

The kitchen is well positioned alongside the living and dining areas and offers ample cabinetry, workspace and storage, complemented by a separate scullery and laundry.

From the living areas, the home flows out onto a large covered patio, creating an inviting extension of the entertainment space and taking full advantage of the property''s elevated position. The patio overlooks the established tropical garden and sparkling swimming pool, while beyond, breathtaking sea views extend across the coastline and out over the Indian Ocean. The combination of the covered entertainment area, pool, garden and expansive ocean outlook creates a superb setting for relaxed outdoor living and entertaining.

The main residence offers three generously proportioned bedrooms, and 3 bathrooms, with the option of a fourth bedroom by utilising the second lounge. The spacious main bedroom is en-suite, while the additional bedrooms are serviced by a family bathroom.

Adding significant versatility is a fully self-contained three-bedroom, three-bathroom flatlet, complete with its own kitchen and lounge. Offering substantial independent accommodation, the flatlet is ideally suited to extended family, older children or guests, while also providing excellent potential as an income-generating unit.

With its flexible three or four-bedroom main residence, multiple living areas, self-contained three-bedroom flatlet, substantial landholding and magnificent sea views, 19 Davallen Avenue offers an exceptional combination of space and versatility in a highly desirable La Lucia location.
