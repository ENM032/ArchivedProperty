4 Bedroom House in La Lucia

Contact agent for street address
4 3 8 387 m²

Exceptional, Modern, Elevated Living with Panoramic Ocean Views | Prime La Lucia Address

This impressive double-storey home captures sweeping panoramic views across the surrounding landscape and out towards the ocean beyond. Offering the privacy of freestanding living, expansive entertaining spaces and generous proportions throughout, this is a home designed to embrace its remarkable position plus exquisite views. A beautifully landscaped entrance, and striking glass feature create an unforgettable first impression. Step inside and you are welcomed by light-filled, open plan interiors where expansive glazing, clean architectural lines and oversized living spaces draw the views into every room. Several beautifully proportioned reception areas unfold from the entrance hall, each designed to maximise natural light and the home''s elevated outlook. The formal lounge offers an elegant setting for entertaining, while a spacious family lounge provides a more relaxed everyday living space. Large sliding doors and picture windows create a seamless connection between the interiors plus exterior areas with the exceptional seaviews beyond. The generously proportioned, open plan kitchen combines timeless finishes with practicality, featuring rich timber cabinetry, Caesarstone countertops, a gas hob, undercounter oven, feature splashbacks and extensive cupboard space. A separate scullery and laundry area ensure the kitchen remains as functional as it is attractive. The dining area occupies a central position between the living spaces and kitchen, creating an effortless flow for both family living and entertaining. It is a space that feels both welcoming and sophisticated. The true centrepiece of the home lies beyond the living areas. Seamless, stack back doors open onto an expansive entertainment terrace that stretches across the front of the property. Covered outdoor lounging and dining spaces overlook the sparkling swimming pool, while the extensive deck provides an extraordinary setting for entertaining on any scale. Elevated above the surrounding properties, the panoramic outlook creates a spectacular backdrop that can be enjoyed from sunrise through to evening whilst admiring the ocean beyond. Also located on the lower level is a versatile fourth bedroom with its own en-suite bathroom. Equally suited as a guest suite, work-from-home office, teenage retreat or additional living area, it offers flexibility to suit a variety of lifestyles. Upstairs, the accommodation continues to take full advantage of the home''s elevated position. The main suite is exceptionally spacious and beautifully appointed. Opening directly onto a private, covered,balcony, it enjoys breathtaking panoramic views and offers ample space for a sitting area. A walk-through dressing room leads into the elegant en-suite bathroom, creating a luxurious and private retreat. Two additional bedrooms are generously proportioned, and both enjoy direct access to balconies, allowing each room to benefit from the surrounding views. These bedrooms are serviced by a stylish family bathroom. This beautifully appointed home is a dual site, in a sought after cul-de-sac. Additional features: Double garage Double carport Additional off-road parking Staff accommodation 8.8kW inverter with roof-mounted solar panels 2 x 50,000-litre water tanks with pump system Filtered drinking water system in the kitchen Combining breathtaking panoramic views, exceptional entertaining spaces, expansive living areas and the exclusivity of sharing the address with just one neighbouring residence, this is a rare opportunity to secure an elevated La Lucia home that offers both privacy and an outstanding lifestyle.
