Fully Renovated Family Home with Self Contained Flatlet

Step into this beautifully renovated single-storey residence where contemporary design, natural light, and effortless living come together to create the perfect family home. Clean architectural lines, bright open spaces, and elegant aluminium shutters throughout give the home a timeless, modern appeal.

Designed with flexibility in mind, the home offers three spacious bedrooms plus a family room, or the option of a fourth bedroom to suit your family''s needs. Please note that the current bedrooms are being utilised as home offices, and built-in cupboards can easily be installed to transform them back into traditional bedrooms. At the heart of the home is a stunning brand-new designer kitchen, complemented by a separate outside laundry. The expansive open-plan living areas flow seamlessly overlooking a sparkling oversized swimming pool and a large level gardenan absolute paradise for children and the perfect setting for entertaining family and friends.

Adding exceptional value is a fully self-contained flatlet, ideal for extended family, guests, a home office, or as an income-generating rental opportunity. The double automated garage offers extensive built-in storage cupboards, while ample off-street parking easily accommodates additional vehicles. Additional features include a fitted inverter system and JoJo water tanks, ensuring convenience and peace of mind.

Perfectly positioned in a sought-after location, this immaculate home is within close proximity to leading schools, shopping centres, restaurants, and major transport routes. This is a home that offers modern comfort, exceptional versatility, and outstanding value. Move in, unpack, and start enjoying the lifestyle you''ve been searching for.
