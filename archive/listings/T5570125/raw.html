3 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 533 m²

Sole Mandate!

Come live in lovely Lonehill and be just a stone throw away from the park!

Alluring entrance hall flows onto an open plan family lounge with a vaulted ceiling and exposed trusses plus a wood burning fireplace. The dining area is situated adjacent to the kitchen, making entertaining an absolute dream. Both these reception areas spill out onto a patio, overlooking the sparkling splash pool . An ideal home for outdoor indoor living. Spacious kitchen with granite counter tops, gas hob with electric oven, single door fridge space, walk-in pantry, under counter space for 2 appliances, plus ample built-in cupboards.

3 Sun-filled bedrooms plus 2 full bathrooms. The stunning master suite with walk-through dressing area onto a full en-suite bathroom boasts his and hers vanities.

This family residence offers the following: Double automated garages, ample parking for your visitors, deluxe staff accommodation, plus the home is fibre enabled.

Lonehill Shopping Centre is very easily accessible for all residents to benefit from showcasing numerous well-known anchor tenants such as Woolworths, Pick n Pay, Clicks as well as an elite Virgin Active gym. The Lonehill Residents Association takes great initiative in making sure to improve all aspects of their community. Lonehill environment is manned 247 with dedicated and well trained security encouraging residents to run, walk dogs and push prams. Come get involved in the strong family driven community that Lonehill has to offer!

Make this dream home your reality!
