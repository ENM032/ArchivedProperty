3 Bedroom House in Paulshof

Contact agent for street address
3 3 2 417 m² 234 m²

Private sanctuary in secure enclave of four homes, offering space and seamless indoor-outdoor living

Tucked away in an exclusive gated enclave of just four homes, this gorgeous private sanctuary offers a rare blend of security, space, and seamless indoor-outdoor living.

The home welcomes you with a spacious, air-conditioned lounge that flows effortlessly through doors onto a lush garden, creating a perfect setting for relaxation or entertaining. A generously sized dining room and a versatile study or family roomboth opening onto the covered patio and gardenenhance the homes easy, open feel and connection to nature.

Accommodation includes three well-proportioned bedrooms, with two en-suite bathroomsan uncommon and highly desirable feature. The serene master suite is a true retreat, complete with air conditioning, direct garden access, and a luxurious en-suite bathroom featuring a jetted bath.

Designed for lifestyle living, the entire side of the home opens up to the garden and a sparkling pool, allowing for effortless entertaining and tranquil everyday living. The open-plan kitchen is both stylish and functional, boasting a built-in convection microwave, ample storage, and a convenient bar area.

Additional features include an outside laundry with space for extra appliances, a double garage, and the invaluable benefit of being a freestanding home with no levies.

A rare opportunity to own a secure, private haven that combines comfort, elegance, and exceptional indoor-outdoor flow.
