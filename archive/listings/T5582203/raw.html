4 Bedroom House in Sunninghill

Contact agent for street address
4 3.5 2 203 m²

Renovated to Perfection, Made for Living. Tasteful finishes throughout this home oozes class and sophistication from the moment you step inside.

This beautifully renovated 4-bedroom home has been reimagined from top to bottom, offering the perfect blend of elegance, comfort, and effortless family living.

Upstairs, two of the four spacious bedrooms boast their own en-suite bathrooms, with a third full bathroom serving the remaining bedrooms. The main bedroom is fitted with air conditioning for year-round comfort.

Downstairs, you''re welcomed by a guest cloakroom and a seamless open-plan lounge and dining area finished with elegant American shutters that add both style and privacy. Stacking doors fold away completely, blurring the line between indoors and out, opening onto a landscaped garden that is nothing short of a piece of paradise.

Outside living has been taken to its best: a sparkling solar-heated pool invites year-round enjoyment and tiled-patio, all set within a private, tranquil garden designed for true relaxation and entertaining and to top it all off there is composite decking around the pool.

Practical luxury features include a double garage, an irrigation system, child protective fencing around the pool, existing high-speed fibre line which can be transferred to the new owner and back-up power, ensuring peace of mind and everyday convenience.

Enjoy the energy this home generates come and experience the beauty for yourself, and make this home yours.
