Spacious Family Home with Income-Generating Flat in the Heart of Port Elizabeth

Step into this beautifully expansive property where love, care, and attention can be felt throughout every space. Perfectly positioned in the heart of Port Elizabeth, this exceptional home is ready to welcome its new owners.

Designed for comfortable family living and effortless entertaining, the home offers a huge living area together with a semi open-plan kitchen and dining space that flows into a fantastic indoor braai room the perfect setting for gatherings with family and friends.

The wonderfully sized main bedroom creates a peaceful retreat, while two additional guest bedrooms provide comfortable accommodation. One of the guest bedrooms features its own en-suite toilet and basin for added convenience. The property further offers 2.5 bathrooms, as well as an additional outside toilet.

One of the standout features of this property is the fully functional income-generating flat an incredible bonus that can help contribute toward your bond repayment. This versatile space is complete with its own living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, and private courtyard. Whether used for rental income, extended family, a teenagers retreat, guest accommodation, or even the ultimate man cave, the possibilities are endless.

This is more than just a property it is a warm and welcoming home filled with opportunity.
