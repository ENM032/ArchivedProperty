Brand new home awaits new family

Dual Mandate

Brand new, beautifully crafted and undeniably easy to live in -this single-level home makes starting out or scaling down feel like youve hit the upgrade button. The central living zones wrap around a chic kitchen, while the undercover patio is your every day retreat: private, sheltered and ready for braais, banter and weekend rugby.

Now for the accommodation: Two bedrooms share a stylish full bathroom, and the sunny master suite enjoys a luxurious en suite that might just make you late for work

Outside, the pet-friendly garden offers space for four-legged family members, with newly planted trees that will soon grow into a lush screen of natural privacy. The location of this property is a winner too - conveniently close to medical facilities, Leisure Isle and Knysnas popular golf courses.

And then theres the garage!! 55 square metres of glorious potential. Whether youre into boats, bikes, DIY projects or simply need a seriously impressive storage zone- this garage is more like a bonus room. Ample space to tinker, restore, build, stash or even plot your next big project - its a playground for the practically minded.
