3 Bedroom House in Knysna Central

Prime Knysna Central Residence Coastal Living at Its Finest

Discover the perfect blend of comfort, style, and convenience in this beautifully appointed 3-bedroom home, ideally positioned in the heart of Knysna Central. Whether youre seeking a permanent coastal retreat, a family holiday home, or a savvy investment along the world-famous Garden Route, this property delivers an enviable lifestyle surrounded by Knysnas iconic lagoon, indigenous forests, and vibrant local community.

Spanning a generous 396.98 m² under roof on a low-maintenance 429 m² erf, this residence has been thoughtfully designed for effortless indoor-outdoor living.

Interior Highlights

Step through the welcoming entrance hall into a spacious, light-filled open-plan kitchen, dining, and loungeTV area featuring elegant pressed ceilings and sliding doors that seamlessly connect to the outdoors. This flowing, integrated living space is perfect for entertaining, family meals, and relaxed evenings in front of the TV.

The home offers two spacious bedrooms and four bathrooms: two en-suites one for each bedroom for optimal privacy and convenience, a third full bathroom in the separate poolside cottage, and a convenient separate staff toilet.

The main bedroom features a generous walk-in cupboard for ample storage. The second bedroom, ideally situated poolside with direct access to its own private patio, is bright and playfully decorated.

Outdoor Oasis

Entertainment and relaxation take center stage outdoors. A large paved patio with a traditional lapa provides the ideal setting for al fresco dining and gatherings, overlooking a sparkling private swimming pool. The separate poolside cottage adds excellent versatility for guests or extended family. The established garden, framed by mature trees and colorful bougainvillea, enhances the propertys excellent kerb appeal and creates a tranquil, pet-friendly environment.

Practical Features &amp Security

- Double automated garages with attached separate laundry room and dedicated workshop space

- Two additional secure parking bays

- Convenient outside toilet and separate staff toilet

- Comprehensive security: fully walled property, alarm system, 24-hour armed response, and CCTV coverage

- High-speed fiber internet connectivity
