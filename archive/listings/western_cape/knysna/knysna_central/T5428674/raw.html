House For Sale in Upper Knysna Central

Renovated Family Home with Lagoon Views | Upper Knysna Central.

Exclusively Marketed by Del-Monte from eXp Realty.

Positioned in one of Knysnas most convenient suburbs, this beautifully restored 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home offers the perfect balance of character, comfort, and everyday practicality. Located right next to Knysna High School and within easy walking distance to shops, churches, and medical facilities, its ideally suited to modern family living.

Set predominantly on one level, the home blends timeless charm with considered upgrades. Original wooden floors and a striking stained-glass front door speak to its heritage, while refined finishessuch as Shademaster wooden blinds throughoutadd a layer of quiet sophistication.

The open-plan living spaces are generous and filled with natural light, centred around a closed-combustion fireplace that brings warmth and atmosphere during Knysnas cooler months. The kitchen is both functional and distinctive, featuring granite countertops, a unique colour palette, and a built-in water filtration system.

The main bedroom enjoys a sunny north-eastern aspect, complete with ample built-in cupboards and a full en suite bathroom. Additional features such as ceiling fans in all bedrooms ensure comfort throughout the seasons.

A standout feature is the full-length wooden deck, perfectly positioned to capture elevated lagoon views. It flows effortlessly down to the pool terracecreating an ideal setting for entertaining or relaxed outdoor living.

The garden is spacious, private, and rich with established trees and birdlife, offering a secure and inviting environment for children. A treehouse adds a playful element.

Further features include a separate laundry, 2,500 litres of rainwater storage, shaded parking, and excellent privacy thanks to mature boundary hedging.

There is currently a longterm lease in place for a 12 month period to end May 2027, at 20k per month.

A rare offeringcombining character, lifestyle, and location in a move-in-ready family home with captivating lagoon views.
