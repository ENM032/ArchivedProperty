3 Bedroom House in Brenton On Lake

Contact agent for street address
3 2 1 158 m² 113 m²

Secure living in nature - How wonderful is this!

Now reduced, offering an opportunity too good to miss is this lovely home, ideal for holidays, permanent living or as an investment.

Set in the tranquil suburb of Lake Brenton, on the North facing side of the Western Head ridge, in easy walking distance to the Knysna Lagoon and only a few minutes drive to the Brenton beach, you are in the perfect position to enjoy the beauty of forest, water & beach living.

This low maintenance, 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom face brick home has been tastefully renovated to ensure modern convenient living with a country feeling.

The covered front veranda with built in braai, welcomes you into the comfortable open plan living area and on into the kitchen, well fitted with ample cupboards, a 4 plate gas hob No ovenand place for a microwave.

The spacious main bedroom has an en-suite shower bathroom and the family bathroom is shared by the other 2 bedrooms. All bedrooms have built in cupboards.

Special features include:-

An extra length garage able to house 2 smaller vehicles

Plus additional carport.

An extra room, perfect for storage, hobbies, officestudy or laundry plumbed already.

2nd braai at the back of the house for year round enjoyment

Fibre connection installed

Special Estate features include:

Gated security.

Communal pool fenced for safety.

Tennis courts, just bring your own racquets.

Childrens play area with jungle gyms, swings and ride-on activities.

Croquet lawn with strong community interaction.

A self-service laundry with coin operated machines.

Well maintained gardens & communal open areas for all to enjoy.

Free roaming bush buck, cape mongoose, wild Egyptian geese and an abundance of bird life.

Borehole water for gardening.

Secure boat parking.

Regretfully, No pets allowed to protect indigenous wildlife.

This lovely property, at such an affordable price, will be snapped up quickly. Be the one who congratulates themselves with every sunrise that you were quick to respond.

Arrange a viewing with us without delay.
