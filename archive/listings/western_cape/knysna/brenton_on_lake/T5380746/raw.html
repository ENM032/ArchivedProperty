3 Bedroom House in Brenton On Lake

33 ss brenton park, 1 kaptein wa duthie street
3 2 4 8.5 ha 199 m²

North-Facing Solar Home with Dual Lounges, Borehole Water and Tandem Garage - Secure Lifestyle Living in Brenton Park

| 3 Bedroom | 2 Bathroom | Office | Borehole | Tennis Courts | Swimming Pool | Indoor Braai | Large Tandem Garage |

Set within secure, access-controlled Brenton Park, this EXCLUSIVE MANDATE offers easy estate living in a low-crime environment known for its strong community feel. While the estate is not pet-friendly, residents enjoy something far more unique - bushbuck and small antelope roaming freely through the grounds, bringing a quiet connection to nature. The location is superb, with the Knysna Lagoon an easy five-minute walk away and the beaches of Brenton On Sea just a short five-minute drive.

The north-facing home is bright and welcoming, enhanced by four well-placed skylights that draw natural light into the living spaces throughout the day. Accommodation comprises three comfortable, carpeted bedrooms, along with a small office - ideal for working from home or as a quiet retreat. There are two bathrooms, including a main en-suite, both fitted with extractor fans for added comfort.

Designed with relaxed coastal living in mind, the home offers two generous lounges. One centres around a fireplace for cooler evenings, while the second features an indoor braai and stacking doors that open fully to the garden, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Ceiling fans in the living room, braai room, study, and main bedroom help maintain a comfortable climate year-round.

The kitchen is neat and practical, fitted with a newly installed oven, and complemented by a large scullery and laundry area with a glass door that brings in additional light. Fibre connectivity is already in place, and the televisions in the main bedroom and front lounge are included in the sale.

Practical features further enhance everyday living. Borehole points supply both the front and rear gardens, offering free water for irrigation and outdoor use. A 5kW inverter system with six solar panels provides meaningful energy support and long-term efficiency.

A standout feature is the full-length tandem garage, comfortably accommodating two vehicles and a boat. With minimal adjustment, a portion of this space could be incorporated into the home to expand the living area if required.

As a sectional title property, levies include building insurance as well as exterior maintenance to walls, gutters, roof, and window frames, making ownership low maintenance and well-suited to a lock-up-and-go lifestyle. Residents also enjoy access to a communal swimming pool, two tennis courts, and a croquet green, reinforcing the estate''s relaxed, lifestyle-driven appeal.

Positioned in sought-after Brenton On Lake, this property benefits from an area known for its lifestyle quality and consistent demand, making it a solid choice for both permanent living and long-term investment.

Highlights:

- Walk to the Knysna Lagoon in approximately 5 minutes

- Just a 5-minute drive to the beaches of Brenton On Sea

- Borehole water for the gardens - a major advantage in a water-conscious town

- 5kW inverter with solar system for energy security and reduced grid reliance

- Rare large tandem garage with space for two vehicles plus a boat

- North-facing, light-filled home enhanced by four skylights

- Secure, low-crime estate living with controlled access

- Free-roaming bushbuck and small antelope create a unique nature-rich lifestyle

- On-site coin-operated laundry
