Charming Cottage in the Heart of Brenton Park

Exclusively marketed by Team Webster associated with Keller Williams.

Nestled within the peaceful surroundings of Brenton Park, this charming 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home offers the perfect blend of comfort, privacy, and nature-inspired living.

The spacious open-plan lounge and kitchen create a warm, welcoming atmosphere, while large windows fill the home with natural light. A covered patio with a built-in braai overlooks the gardens, making it an ideal space to relax or entertain family and friends.

The home offers three comfortable bedrooms, including a generous main bedroom with an en-suite bathroom, while a second full bathroom serves the remaining two bedrooms. A separate laundrystorage room adds to the home''s practicality, and the single garage provides secure parking and additional storage.

Perfectly positioned close to the tennis courts, swimming pool, and children''s jungle gym, making it an excellent choice for families, retirees, or anyone looking for a relaxed lifestyle in a beautiful natural setting.

Brenton Park is well known for its relaxed atmosphere, abundant birdlife, roaming wildlife, and sense of community, offering a lifestyle that''s hard to find elsewhere.

Whether you''re searching for a permanent residence, holiday home, or investment property, this delightful home is ready for its next owners.
