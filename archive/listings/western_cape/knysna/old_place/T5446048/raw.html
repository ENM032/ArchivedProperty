Solid family home in Upper Old Place

Exclusively marketed by Hamilton''s. Discover the perfect blend of durability and comfort in this well-positioned family home. Located in the established and quiet suburb of Old Place, this property offers the ultimate low-maintenance lifestyle with a classic face brick exterior and modern aluminium windows.

Four bedrooms, three bathrooms and a guest loo provide ample space for a large family or guests.

Open plan flow, the heart of the home features an expansive lounge and dining area, perfect for entertaining.

Functional kitchen includes a separate scullery. Music room or study. Spacious level plot ideal for children and pets. Double garage and enclosed garden.
