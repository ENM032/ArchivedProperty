4 Bedroom House in Old Place

79 milkwood drive
4 3 2 901 m²

Discover this beautiful home in Upper Old Place!. This tastefully renovated four-bedroom home is situated in the established suburb of Upper Old Place. The ground floor features an open-plan living area that leads onto a spacious covered patio, ideal for indoor-outdoor living and entertaining. The patio includes a built-in braai, while the newly fitted kitchen offers contemporary finishes, ample cupboard space, and a breakfast counter positioned to enjoy lagoon views.

A study or library is conveniently tucked beneath the staircase, providing a practical workspace or reading nook, along with a guest toilet.

The home offers four bedrooms in total, with one bedroom located on the ground floor. This space can be used as the main bedroom or closed off to function as a flatlet, ideal for visiting family or Airbnb use. Upstairs, the three bedrooms are complemented by two bathrooms and a small balcony from which to enjoy the view.

A double garage is currently under construction and almost complete, adding the final touch to this versatile and move-in-ready family home.
