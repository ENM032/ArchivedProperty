3 Bedroom House in Eastford Country Estate

12c eastford street
3 2

Enjoy 42 days per year in share block

Be one of 8 holiday shareholders of this 3-bed, 2-bath home in Eastford Country Estate, with spectacular, wide views over Knysna and the lagoon. Each shareholder is allowed 42 days per year calculated on a rotational system.

If you do not use your designated days, you may rent it out via the in-house rental agency and enjoy a return on your investment. No monthly payments. The annual fee is R24 800, offset by letting out the weeks not used and includes rates and taxes, levies and insurance.

The home is modern, well managed, private, comes fully equipped and furnished, and is fully serviced.

The whole length of the house opens onto a wide deck with unobstructed views from the mountains to the north to the Knysna Heads on the south. The double-volume, open-plan living area, with a fireplace, as well as both bedrooms, flow out onto this deck.

The modern kitchen has a separate scullery with modern appliances. Off the kitchen and lounge on the northern side, surrounded by natural fynbos, is a protected braai area. Undercover parking is provided for 1 car.

Enjoy 42 days every year in the country and nearby golf courses and still be close to the picturesque town of Knysna!
