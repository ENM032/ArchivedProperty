3 Bedroom House in Green Pastures

4 green pastures village
3 2.5 4 456 m² 232.6 m²

The perfect family fit

This spacious three-bedroom, two-bathroom freehold home is situated on a corner stand within the secure Green Pastures Village Estate on Simola Road, Knysna.

The property offers two garages, as well as two additional parking bays beneath the front deck, with a staircase leading to the main entrance.

Inside, the home opens into a bright, open-plan lounge and kitchen.

The main bedroom, complete with an en-suite bathroom, is positioned to the left of the living area, while a guest cloakroom and staircase leading to the upper level are located to the right.

Upstairs, a versatile landing provides flexible space for family or guests. Two additional bedrooms share a full family bathroom, while a private upper-level deck offers the perfect place to relax and enjoy Knysna''s pleasant climate.

Designed for effortless indoor-outdoor living, the ground-floor lounge opens onto a north-facing patio and braai area, creating an ideal setting for entertaining and family gatherings throughout the year.

Green Pastures Village is a secure, gated estate offering communal gardens, a swimming pool, electric perimeter fencing and controlled access. The monthly levy includes garden maintenance, pool upkeep and estate security, while the common areas are managed by the Home Owners'' Association.

Conveniently located just five minutes from the Knysna CBD, this freehold home is ideal as a permanent residence, holiday home or secure lock-up-and-go investment.

Please note: This is a freehold property within the Green Pastures Village Estate and is not a sectional title unit.
