4 Bedroom House in Green Pastures

Contact agent for street address
4 3 2 859 m² 131 m²

Modern Knysna Home with Pool, Views, and Dual Kitchens

EXCLUSIVE MANDATE

Discover this modern and inviting home situated in the picturesque Green Pastures, Knysna. The property immediately impresses with its clean white exterior, dark gabled roof, and spacious paved forecourt. Inside, the residence has an open-plan layout, seamlessly connecting the living and kitchen areas. The main kitchen is well-appointed with modern white cabinetry, wooden countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a functional island, creating a bright and contemporary space.

The main house offers 3 comfortable bedrooms, complemented by 2 well-appointed bathrooms. These include modern designs with black fixtures, a glass-enclosed shower featuring speckled or terrazzo-style tiles, and a bathroom with a clean bathtub. Bedrooms benefit from ample natural light and modern white storage solutions, ensuring comfort and practicality.

Outdoor living is a highlight, featuring a private plunge pool with a wooden deck, perfect for relaxation. A green garden space and a charming covered outdoor patio with built in braai provide ideal settings for entertaining or enjoying the scenic views of the surrounding hills. The property is pet-friendly, and it is fully fenced.

Located within a secure estate with 24-hour security, residents can enjoy peace of mind. The home is also fibre-ready, catering to modern connectivity needs. Green Pastures offers a tranquil environment while being conveniently close to Knysna''s coastal attractions and aquatic activities, making it an ideal location for a family or holiday home.
