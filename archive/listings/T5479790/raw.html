4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 3 2 537 m² 276 m²

Sole Mandate!

Time to escape the hustle and bustle invest in this highly sought after location within Lonehill. This is the lifestyle you have dreamed of, and its at your fingertips! Just a stone throw away to all the Lonehill amenities this residence comes with all the bells and whistles.

A welcoming entrance flows onto a sizeable open concept family lounge and dining area boasting big windows and doors spilling onto private sections of the garden - these areas enjoy a covered patio, overlooking the peaceful wrap-around garden and sparkling heated swimming pool. The dining area is conveniently located adjacent to the open plan kitchen. This home is brilliantly flexible for both indoor and outdoor living and entertaining. Open concept kitchen with gorgeous countertops, a breakfast nook, gas hob with electric oven, prep bowl, separate scullery with double sink and under counter space for 2 appliances, a double door fridge space plus ample built-in cupboards.

3 Delightful sun-kissed bedrooms and 2 bathrooms MES. The sun-dappled master bedroom is luxuriously private and a divine space, spills onto the garden, giving you an open airy feeling. The garden is a special place to sit and enjoy your morning coffee whilst listening to the birds offering you ultimate peace and tranquillity. The master has ample built-in cupboard space plus a full en-suite bathroom with double vanities, double shower and bathtub.

A separate flatlet will be your 4th bedroom option or a great little extra income cottage - it boasts a kitchenette and lounge, 1 open plan bedroom and en-suite bathroom.

The meticulous wrap-around garden is an ideal space for your children and furry friends to run and play in a peaceful, tranquil and more importantly safe environment.

This corner stand residence offers the following: Double automated garage with direct access into the property, double covered carport, ample parking space for all your visitors, partial air-conditioning, Kodak invertor with 2 lithium batteries, pre-paid electricity, deluxe staff accommodation plus the home is fibre enabled.

Lonehill Shopping Centre is very easily accessible for all residents to benefit from showcasing numerous well-known anchor tenants such as Woolworths, Pick n Pay, Clicks as well as an elite Virgin Active gym. The Lonehill Residents Association takes great initiative in making sure to improve all aspects of their community. Lonehill environment is manned 247 with dedicated and well-trained security encouraging residents to run, walk dogs and push prams. Come and get involved in the strong family driven community that Lonehill has to offer!
