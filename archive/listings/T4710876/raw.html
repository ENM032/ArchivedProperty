4 Bedroom House in Rivonia

13 winston avenue
4 3.5 3 1 983 m²

Timeless 4 Bedroom Michael Sutton Design In Secure Enclave. From the elegant entrance courtyard to the skylit entrance hall and classiccontemporary design, enter a world of beautifully proportioned living rooms, sliding solid wood doors into cavity walls and an atmosphere of tranquility and complete harmony, with an integrated flow between the home, entertainment areas, pool and lush established garden.

A separate dining room and a TVhome theatre room with feature lighting.

Large eat-in kitchen with Smeg gaselectric appliances, built in microwave and separate scullery and laundry.

An informal lounge area leads onto a large covered patio, lovely pool, outside bar and guest toiletpool shower. The manicured garden also has a fire pit seating 10, pizza oven and childrens play area.

The bedroom wing consists of a large main bedroom with access to the garden, stunning bathroom with double shower and bath plus separate dressing area. 2 Double bedrooms with a shared full bathroom. Luxurious ensuite guest room with access to a private courtyard. A pyjama lounge plus study completes this area.

Staff accommodation. Triple garage plus ample guest parking. Excellent security and full inverter, batteries and solar system.

A HOME THAT HAS IT ALL.
