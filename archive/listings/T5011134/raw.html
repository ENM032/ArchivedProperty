3 Bedroom House in Paulshof

A Family Home Where Memories Are Made

Offers from R 2 499 000, asking R 2 600 000.

Step into this beautifully maintained family home, where comfort, style, and nature blend together to create an inviting space. This home is ready to move in, with no need for any renovations. The three spacious bedrooms, featuring warm wooden floors, open onto a lush, well-kept garden that offers serene views for every family member. Modern aluminium windows ensure plenty of natural light fills the rooms, enhancing the home''s bright and airy feel.

At the heart of the home is an open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area that is perfect for entertaining. This area flows seamlessly into a generous patio, ideal for creating a second outdoor lounge complete with a dining space. Imagine enjoying a meal outdoors, with the sparkling pool nearby and the gentle sounds of birds in the garden all around. The beautifully landscaped garden is effortlessly maintained with an automated irrigation system, ensuring it remains vibrant year-round.

For added flexibility, the property includes two Wendy houses. One is currently used as an office, while the other could serve as staff accommodation, a play area, or an extra room. The home also benefits from air conditioning in two bedrooms and the living room, ensuring comfort throughout the year. There is ample storage space in the garage, and a 3000kW inverter provides reliable backup power please note, there are no solar panels.

Enhanced Security

The property has a recently upgraded security system, which includes CCTV integrated with your phone. The alarm system can be controlled via your phone, sending warnings directly to both your device and the security company. Additionally, beams outside add an extra layer of protection, making you feel safe and secure.

Neighbourhood Amenities

This lovely home is ideally located near Rivonia Sports Club, perfect for those who enjoy a leisurely walk. The area also boasts stables and a nearby park where dogs can run freely, as well as a beautiful stream that adds to the charm of the surroundings. Conveniently positioned with easy access to highways, top schools, and several shopping centres including a Virgin Active gym, this home is a haven where you can create lasting memories with your family.
