5 Bedroom House in Sunninghill

Contact agent for street address
5 3 3 1 502 m²

Beautiful Family Home Nestled in Sunninghill Gardens. This flawless home is an excellent choice for the family who is looking for the safe and secure lifestyle this sought-after suburb offers.

North facing with natural light, makes the home warm and crisp. Wow factor double entertainers delight covered patio with built-in-braai set over tranquil garden and beautiful swimming pool.

Featuring a great space for the avid entertainer. Large kitchen and scullery with double eye level oven and ample cupboards. 3 Open plan reception rooms. 5 Spacious bedrooms, 3 bathrooms 2 en-suite. Or work from home office. Main bedroom boasts space and walking cupboards, sliding doors leading onto private garden.

Features of this home include, centralized air conditioning, wood burning fire place, aluminum windows, neutral tones and modern finishes throughout. This offers the new owners a blank canvas to put their unique individual stamp into this home. Storeroom, workshop and outside toilet.

Double auto-garage, plus additional covered parking for 3rd car, trailers, bikes etc. Walking distance to the dog park. Excellent security.

Make this your move-in-ready forever home.
