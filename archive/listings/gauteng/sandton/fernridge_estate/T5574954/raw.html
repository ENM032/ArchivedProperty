Modern Family Living in a Prestigious Secure Estate

EXCLUSIVE MANDATE

Discover the perfect balance of space, comfort, and modern convenience in this exceptional five-bedroom family home, nestled within one of the area''s most sought-after and secure estates.

The home boasts expansive open-plan living and dining areas and flow seamlessly into an enclosed patio, creating the ideal indoor-outdoor lifestyle. The patio overlooks a beautifully manicured garden and sparkling swimming pool, offering a private oasis for entertaining guests or simply relaxing with family.

The contemporary kitchen features a gas hob, ample cabinetry, generous work surfaces, and space for three appliances. A conveniently located guest cloakroom completes the downstairs living areas.

Upstairs, five well-proportioned bedrooms provide ample accommodation for the entire family. Two bathrooms service the home, while the luxurious main suite offers a spacious walk-in dressing room and an elegant en-suite bathroom, creating the perfect private retreat.

Additional features:

Inverter with solar panels and 2 batteries

Insect mesh on all windows and door openings

Generous-sized staff accommodation and double garage.
