3 Bedroom House in Fernridge Estate

Contact agent for street address
3 3 2

VIEW BY APPOINTMENT

Step into a home where timeless charm meets refined contemporary living.

From the moment you arrive, the striking curb appeal infused with a warm Spanish-inspired aesthetic sets the tone for what lies within. A grand double-volume entrance welcomes you inside, where a beautiful hallway effortlessly branches into the various living spaces, creating both flow and distinction. Laminate flooring gives you a warm and inviting feel.

To the right, a generously sized family room offers the perfect space for relaxed living, while to the left, a feature display of elegant wine racks sets a sophisticated tone for entertaining. Across the hallway, a formal lounge provides an inviting setting to host guests in style.

At the heart of the home lies a stunning contemporary kitchen, thoughtfully designed to overlook the dining area - perfect for both everyday living and entertaining. A separate scullery adds convenience while maintaining the kitchens sleek appeal.

The dining area and formal lounge seamlessly open onto a covered patio, where tiled finishes echo the homes Spanish character. This inviting outdoor space overlooks a sparkling pool, creating an ideal setting for entertaining or unwinding. Hidden, a generously sized garden offers ample space for family enjoyment, complete with a charming vegetable patch.

Adding versatility to the property is a fully self-contained flatlet with its own private entranceideal for staff accommodation, guest stays, or a home office.

Upstairs, three well-appointed bedrooms all feature en-suite bathrooms, each finished with tasteful, modern touches. The main suite is particularly spacious, boasting ample cupboard space and a serene, private feel.

Refuse: R550

Sewer: R803

This home perfectly blends character, comfort, and functionalityoffering a lifestyle of effortless elegance.

The Dainfern area offers grade A schools and the American International School is down the road. Nearby amenities include, golf courses, hospitals, Dainfern square is at the doorstep of the estate. Fourways Mall and Montecasino is a short drive away. Easy access to all major routes towards Sandton, Pretoria and Lanseria.
