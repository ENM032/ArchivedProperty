Fully Renovated Free-Standing Full Title in Secure Complex in Craigavon

Best Price. Best Position. Best Finishes. Low Levies.

Seize the opportunity to own this newly renovated, full-title, free-standing home situated on an expansive stand in the heart of Craigavon. Meticulously updated to a brand-new standard, this property perfectly blends modern luxury with everyday functionality. It is an absolute must-see for entertainers, families, and pet lovers alike.

Key Property Features:

Generous Space: 489 sqm stand with 176 sqm under-roof living area.

Entertainers Dream: Large covered patio with a built-in braai, overlooking a sparkling, brand-new swimming pool equipped with a heavy-duty safety cover.

Gourmet Kitchen: Brand-new open-plan layout with sleek finishes and dedicated plumbing space for two appliances. Gas Hob.

Luxury Bedrooms: Three spacious, tiled bedrooms with ample built-in cupboard space.

Modern Bathrooms: Main bedroom features a pristine ensuite with an extra-large shower. The second family bathroom is fully equipped with both a bath and a shower.

Parking & Expansion: Single carport and one open parking conveniently located at the entrance gate of the Home.

Additional Storage: Back room with Built In Cupboards.

Financial Advantage: Full-title ownership means exceptionally low levies of just R1,700 per month.

Pet-Friendly: Welcoming environment suitable for dogs of all sizes.

Secure & Prime Location:Peace of mind is guaranteed in this highly secure complex, featuring monitored, after-hours access control.

The property is ideally located within easy walking distance to Cedar Square Shopping Centre and the upcoming Fourways Precinct development. Get in early and secure your investment in one of the most sought-after growth nodes in Johannesburg.
