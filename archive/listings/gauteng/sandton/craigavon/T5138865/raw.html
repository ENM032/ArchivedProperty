4 Bedroom House in Craigavon

Contact agent for street address
4 2.5 2 261 m²

Don't miss out! Relaunched at a great new price, Sophisticated 4-Bedroom Townhouse with Pool in Craigavon's Premier Estate

Imagine life in this exceptional 4-bedroom townhouse, nestled within a highly sought-after and serene estate, offering both 24-hour security and a pet-friendly environment. This home is designed for seamless modern living and effortless entertaining.

Step inside to a welcoming entrance, complete with a practical mudroom and guest cloakroom. The ground floor unfolds into three spacious, semi-open plan living areas, ensuring versatile spaces for every occasion. Enjoy comfortable evenings in the dining room with air-conditioning and a cozy fireplace, or move into the lounge and family room, which flow effortlessly to an enclosed entertainer''s patio featuring a built-in braai perfect for year-round gatherings.

The gourmet kitchen is a true highlight, boasting elegant Caesarstone countertops, a breakfast bar, ample pantry space, and a gas stove. There''s dedicated room for a double-door fridge, and the separate scullery comfortably accommodates three appliances.

Upstairs, discover four generous bedrooms and two full bathrooms. The impressive main bedroom is a private retreat, offering an en-suite bathroom, air-conditioning, and access to a large private balcony.

Additional features include a refreshing pool, convenient linen storage, fibre readiness, and a double automated garage with direct home access. The paved driveway ensures plenty of extra parking for visitors.

Inviting offers from R2 500 000, with an asking price of R2 625 000.

Strategically positioned in Craigavon, this home offers superb access to key arterial roads like Cedar, Witkoppen, Main, and the N1. Enjoy unparalleled convenience with Fourways Mall, Cedar Square, Montecasino, and Broadacres Shopping Centre all within close proximity, catering to all your shopping, dining, and entertainment desires. The area also boasts an impressive selection of top-tier schools, including Montessori, Junior College, Crawford International, Heron Bridge, Steyn City, Broadacres Academy, Reddam House, Fourways High, St Peters, and Dainfern College. With the extensive Fourways Mall retail upgrade, new business developments, and the upcoming Gautrain expansion, Craigavon represents a strong and appreciating investment in a highly sought-after residential area.
