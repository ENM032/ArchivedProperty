LUXURY, CHARM & COMFORT. 5 Bedroom House in Fernbrook Estate, Fourways

Step into this stunning residence that greets you with beautiful, sunny entrance hall. The living areas on each side create a warm and inviting atmosphere, all flowing effortlessly onto a covered patio with built-in braai, overlooking a landscaped pet-friendly garden - perfect for entertaining and relaxing. Enjoy 2 spacious lounges, one featuring a built-in bar and a wine cellar tucked under the stairs, along with a dining area that flows into a cozy lounge complete with a double-sided wood fireplace adding charm and warmth to your gatherings.

A guest bedroom is conveniently located downstairs, providing privacy for visitors or family members. The 3rd garage has been transformed into a self-contained flatlet or staff accommodation, offering versatility and privacy, but it can easily be converted back if needed.

Upstairs, discover 3 massive bedrooms, all adorned with screed flooring and each opening onto a sunny balcony that overlooks the beautiful garden. The bathrooms are all stunning, and there''s additional space for a study or pyjama lounge on the landing, perfect for unwinding or catching up on work.

The Estate boasts fantastic amenities including cricket nets, a soccer field, a park and kiddies play area, a pool and clubhouse ensuring a vibrant community lifestyle. The estate boasts exceptional security.

This remarkable home is truly a lifestyle haven waiting for you!
