3 Bedroom House in Craigavon

3 2.5 4 360 m² 210 m²

3 Bedroom Cluster In Secure Estate

Charming cluster in a tranquil and secure estate.

Enter into an sun-filled and open plan lounge and dining room, that opens out to a side patio, overlooking the wrap around garden. The kitchen is well-equipped, offering a pantry, space for a fridge, and two under counter appliances. Guest cloakroom.

3 good-sized upstairs bedrooms, and 2 bathrooms. The main bedroom is en-suite.

The double automated garage has direct access into the home, and there is ample driveway parking for family and friends when visiting. The estate offers 24-hour guarded security, is pet friendly, has a children''s park, and fibre is installed.

Negotiating from R2 000 000 - Asking R2 100 000.

In Craigavon you are close to the major arteries of Cedar Road, Witkoppen Road, Main Road, Winnie Mandela Drive, and the N1. Also close by are Fourways Mall, Cedar Square, Monte Casino and Broadacres Shopping Center for all your shopping requirements, entertainment, and restaurants.

Top schools in the area include Montessori, Junior College, Crawford International, Heron Bridge, Steyn City, Broadacres Academy, Reddam House, Fourways High, St Peters, and Dainfern College, to name a few.

With the extensive Fourways Mall retail and commercial upgrade, the new business centre and the Gautrain coming past the area, Craigavon is most definitely a very attractive area to purchase where your investment will grow. Craigavon continues to be an extremely sought after area to live.
