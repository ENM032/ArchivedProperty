3 Bedroom House in Craigavon

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 4 308 m²

Immaculate Renovated 3 Bedroom Family Home with Domestic Quarters And Backup Power And Water

This absolutely stunning 3 bedroom family home has been beautifully renovated throughout and is presented in immaculate condition. Offering stylish finishes, generous living spaces, excellent entertainment areas, and the added benefit of domestic accommodation and backup power and water, this is a home that is ready to move straight into.

The light-filled open-plan living and dining areas feature air-conditioning and flow seamlessly outdoors to a covered patio with wooden decking, overlooking the beautiful wrap-around garden. The garden continues around to a second covered patio with a built-in braai, creating an additional outdoor entertainment space ideal for family gatherings and entertaining.

The beautifully renovated kitchen combines modern style with warmth, featuring wooden countertops, matte white cabinetry, ample cupboard space, room for one appliance and a fridge, with an additional two appliances accommodated in the garage.

Upstairs are three generously sized bedrooms, all fitted with laminate flooring and air-conditioning. The master bedroom is spacious and inviting, featuring a full en-suite bathroom and a private, sunny balcony. The second bedroom is particularly generous in size, while the third bedroom comfortably accommodates a queen-size bed. The two bedrooms share a beautifully renovated bathroom with a shower.

The property is completed by domestic accommodation, while the backup power and water systems provide added convenience and peace of mind.

Situated within a well-maintained complex with excellent 24-hour access-controlled security, the home is conveniently located close to local schools, shopping centres, places of worship, and other everyday amenities.

A beautifully renovated, move-in-ready family home offering exceptional comfort, style, and practicality.
