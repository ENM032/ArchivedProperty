Charming 5-Bedroom Property Including Income-Generating Cottage in a Secure Enclave!

Classic Family Lifestyle or Investor Dream with Spacious Interiors and Endless Potential!!!

Discover effortless family living in this beautifully renovated 5-bedroom home, perfectly positioned within a highly sought-after, boomed-and-guarded enclave. Impeccably maintained and move-in ready, this exceptional property combines generous living spaces, contemporary finishes, and outstanding lifestyle features, making it ideal for modern family living and effortless entertaining.

At the heart of the home is a stylish, modern kitchen, durable vinyl flooring, abundant cabinetry, a five-burner gas stove, space for under-counter appliances, and room for a double-door refrigerator. The kitchen flows seamlessly into the dining area, complete with a wall heater, and an expansive lounge featuring a charming wood-burning fireplace that creates a warm and inviting atmosphere. Large doors open onto the covered patio, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor living experience.

The spacious covered patio with views overlooks the sparkling swimming pool and beautifully maintained garden, offering the perfect setting for year-round entertaining.

The home offers five generously sized bedrooms with built-in cupboards and plenty of natural light. The luxurious main suite features a walk-in dressing room and a full en-suite bathroom, while the remaining bedroom is served by well-appointed family bathrooms.

Adding exceptional versatility are two separate cottages:

Cottage One is beautifully finished with a modern kitchen offering space for two appliances, an open-plan lounge and dining area overlooking the garden and pool, spacious bedroom en-suite, a stylish bathroom with a large walk-in shower. With its own private entrance and covered parking, it is currently operating successfully as an Airbnb, providing an excellent additional income opportunity.

Cottage Two comprises two bedrooms and a bathroom and is currently utilised as service room.
