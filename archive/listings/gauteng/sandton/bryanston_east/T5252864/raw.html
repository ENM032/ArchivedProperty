Ultra Modern 3 Bedroom Home in Secure Bryanston East Estate

Step into this newly renovated masterpiece, perfectly positioned in a sought after secure estate in Bryanston East. Designed for modern living, this home combines style, space, and comfort in every detail.

The impressive double volume entrance flows into an open-plan living area with a spacious lounge, dining room and feature wood-burning fireplace framed by soaring windows that flood the home with natural light. A sleek built-in modern wall unit and high wooden panel ceilings enhance the contemporary feel, while Cemcrete flooring and air-conditioning run throughout.

The striking black and white designer kitchen boasts a gas hob, centre island, and ample serving space, ideal for family living and entertaining.

A separate entertainment, family room with American shutters opens seamlessly to the low maintenance garden with a sparkling pool, firepit with built-in seating and outdoor relaxation spaces.

Accommodation includes

2 spacious downstairs bedrooms, both en-suite with full bathrooms, double vanities, built-in cupboards, wooden flooring and direct garden access.

Upstairs glass balustrading leads to a versatile open-plan study or TV room, plus a third en-suite spacious bedroom and built in cupboards.

Additional features

Full staff accommodation

Double automated garage

Courtyard and washing area

Security with cameras and electric fencing

This home is the perfect blend of contemporary design and lock-up-and-go convenience in one of Bryanstons most desirable estates.
