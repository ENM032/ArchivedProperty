3 Bedroom House in Bryanston East

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 6 188 m²

3 Bedroom spacious Townhouse with the Best Views in Bryanston 24-Hour Secure Complex

Set in one of the most sought-after areas of Bryanston, this exceptional 3-bedroom townhouse offers breathtaking views, luxurious finishes, and top-tier security, all within a beautifully maintained 24-hour guarded complex.

From the moment you enter, you are welcomed into a spacious open-plan home designed for effortless living and entertaining. The expansive lounge features a wood-burning fireplace, creating warmth and character, while the generous dining area flows seamlessly into a modern open-plan kitchen complete with a Centre island, Gas hob, eparate scullery with space for three undercounter appliances. Guest cloakroom downstairs, Stacking doors open onto a covered braai area and decked entertainment space, overlooking a spectacular tiered garden. The landscaped garden flows down to a second level featuring a portable private pool, additional decked entertainment area for entertaining guests. Manicured garden with private gate access directly into the complex park

Upstairs offers three spacious bedrooms, including a luxurious main suite with sliding doors opening onto a private balcony showcasing magnificent views. The main bedroom also features a walk-through dressing room and a full en-suite bathroom with double vanity, bath, and shower.

The two additional bedrooms are generously sized and share a full bathroom with both bath and shower. A built-in study area upstairs provides the perfect work-from-home space.

Additional Features Include:

American shutters throughout

Air-conditioning in all rooms

Heated bathrooms

Double automated garage

Under-stair storage

Security cameras and beams

Prepaid electricity

2500L water backup system

Solar system with backup batteries

The complex itself offers beautifully maintained communal gardens, a park, swimming pool, and entertainment area ideal for families and outdoor living.

This home truly combines luxury, security, sustainability, and the best views in Bryanston.
