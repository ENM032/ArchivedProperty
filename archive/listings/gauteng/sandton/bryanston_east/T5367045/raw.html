Exceptional Modern Contemporary 5-Bedroom Home in Prestigious Bryanston East

Set within one of Bryanston Easts most prestigious boomed-off and security-guarded avenues, this modern contemporary luxury home is located in an exclusive cluster estate of only three residences, offering rare privacy and multiple layers of security. With both controlled estate access and neighbourhood guarding, the property provides exceptional peace of mind while maintaining a refined, upmarket lifestyle.

Architecturally striking, the home is defined by clean contemporary lines, expansive glass elements, and impressive double-volume living spaces that flood the interiors with natural light and frame tranquil garden and treetop views. The open-plan lounge and dining areas, each flowing seamlessly to the garden, create a fluid indoor-outdoor connection ideal for both sophisticated entertaining and relaxed family living. A stylish entertainment lounge with a bespoke cocktail and preparation area opens onto a covered patio with drop-down blinds, overlooking a fire-pit, solar-heated swimming pool, and a beautifully established garden.

At the centre of the home is a designer chefs kitchen, thoughtfully configured with a central island, gas and electric cooking, and a generous breakfast area. A separate scullery and laundry provide additional functionality, complete with pantry storage and an extra gas hobperfectly balancing practicality with contemporary elegance.

Accommodation is generous and versatile. The ground floor offers a guest bedroom suite with en-suite bathroom, as well as an additional room ideal as a home office or bedroom with direct garden access. Upstairs, the home continues to impress with a luxurious main bedroom suite, featuring an open-plan full bathroom and dressing area, complemented by two further en-suite bedrooms, each incorporating loft spaces. Additional upstairs living areas include a childrens study or homework zone, a private air-conditioned study with scenic views, and a family lounge with fireplace and balconyideal as a playroom or relaxed retreat.

The property further benefits from staff accommodation comprising a bedroom, kitchenette, and shower room, as well as two automated garages. Practical features include air-conditioning, a comprehensive backup power solution consisting of a 5kW inverter supported by 16 solar panels, and three geysers, one of which is a solar geyserensuring efficiency, sustainability, and uninterrupted comfort. Advanced security includes a burglar alarm system, sensor beams, full perimeter walls, and layered access control within both the cluster and the boomed-off enclosure.

The home offers substantial proportions with an erf size of 857m² and an impressive under-roof area of approximately 621m², with monthly rates of approximately R4,550. Ideally positioned, the property enjoys convenient access to some of Johannesburgs most sought-after schools, including St Stithians College, Brescia House, Bryanston Primary and High, Bryandale Primary, St Peters, Redhill, and Crawford, as well as nearby shopping centres, dining destinations, and major transport routes.

This is a rare opportunity to secure a beautifully executed, energy-efficient modern contemporary luxury residence in one of Bryanston Easts most coveted and secure locations.

Contact me today to arrange a private viewing and experience this exceptional home firsthand.
