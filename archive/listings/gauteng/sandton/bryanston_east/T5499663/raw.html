Modern Farmhouse Elegance 4 Bedroom All En-Suite Entertainers Family Home in Secure Estate

This modern farmhouse style family home offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and effortless entertaining within a secure estate setting. Designed for stylish family living, this immaculate 4-bedroom all en-suite residence boasts exceptional finishes, spacious interiors, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow.

Step into a light-filled open-plan living area featuring a spacious lounge with a closed wood burning fireplace, elegant dining room, and a built-in bar area ideal for entertaining. The living spaces flow effortlessly onto a large undercover patio overlooking the heated pool and beautifully manicured garden.

The stylish kitchen is both functional and sophisticated, complete with a centre island and prep basin, gas hob, Bosch oven, space for a double-door fridge, and a separate scullery accommodating 3 under-counter appliances. The kitchen also opens onto a private courtyard area.

Upstairs offers a cosy pyjama lounge and four generously sized en-suite bedrooms. The luxurious main suite features air conditioning, a separate walk-in dressing room, and a full spa-style bathroom with a freestanding bath, spacious shower, and double vanity. Bedrooms 2, 3, and 4 all offer full en-suite bathrooms, while two bedrooms are fitted with air conditioning.
