5 Bedroom House in Bryanston East

Contact agent for street address
5 5.5 2 900 m² 875 m²

Modern, Contemporary, Stylish, Upmarket!!!!

Welcome to this beautifully designed 3 storey standalone home in Bryanston East, in a small secure Estate.

Boasting spacious, flowing open plan indoor outdoor living areas which are perfect for relaxing at home or entertaining with friends and family.

Downstairs- A statement entrance foyer leading left to the ultra modern kitchen with state of the art design finishes- Perfect for the aspiring home Chef.

Overlooking a T.V. Lounge with balcony and sliding doors.

Off the kitchen is a scullery laundry room with second gas stove.

Also, a convenient guest en-suite bedroom which is perfectly private.

There is also a convenient guest loo off the entrance.

Also on this level is a work from home office.

To the right of the entrance hall is the double volume lounge and dining room with large windows and stacking doors opening onto a covered entertainers patio with built in braai and lap pool

The garden beyond has a lovely fire pit and pool area, perfect for entertaining.

Bottom Level: Also downstairs on the garage level, on the opposite side of the house, is a separate en suite staff room garden cottage, with its own entrance and private garden access.

Upstairs- The large staircase takes you up into a pyjama lounge area with patio.

Off it to the right are 3 bedrooms en-suite with walk in closets and private patios.

To the left is the main bedroom en suite with an open plan bathroom, with bath and double shower, a his and hers walk in closets, separate toilet and a balcony, overlooking the pool and garden below.

There are 2 automated double garages and open visitor parking for 2 cars, so 6 parkings in total.

in the lovely Devonshire Road Bryanston, Close to Hobart Grove CentreRiverside Shopping Centre.
