Secure family home in Waterford Estate with back up power

A welcoming driveway and double garage lead to the entrance hall, where the home opens into three interconnected reception areas. The open-plan layout includes a dining room, a formal lounge, and a comfortable family lounge, creating a seamless flow for both family life and entertaining. The family lounge opens onto a covered patio overlooking the garden and swimming pool, providing an inviting indoor-outdoor lifestyle.

The kitchen is practical and well-appointed, complete with a separate scullery to keep daily living organised.

The home offers four well-sized bedrooms. The shared family bathroom is a full bathroom, while the main bedroom features a walk-in closet and a spacious full en-suite bathroom. Sliding doors from the main bedroom open directly onto the garden and pool area, creating a peaceful connection to the outdoor space.

Additional features include backup power, ensuring added convenience during power interruptions.

Lovingly cared for over the years, this home is neat, comfortable, and ready for its next family to enjoy. Offering secure estate living, functional living spaces, and a welcoming atmosphere, it is an ideal place to create lasting memories.
