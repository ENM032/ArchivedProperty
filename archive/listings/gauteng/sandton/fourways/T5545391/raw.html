3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 446 m² 221 m²

Freehold Property in Sought After Estate

Nestled within the highly sought-after Waterford Estate, this beautifully presented three-bedroom home offers the perfect balance of modern comfort, privacy, and effortless indoor and outdoor living. Set in a secure and intimate estate, this residence is ideal for families, professionals, or those looking to downsize without compromising on lifestyle.

Step inside to discover a light-filled open-plan layout where the modern kitchen seamlessly connects to the spacious lounge, creating a warm and inviting space for everyday living and entertaining. Contemporary finishes throughout enhance the homes stylish appeal, while the thoughtful design ensures both comfort and functionality.

The home offers three generously proportioned bedrooms and two well-appointed bathrooms, including a private main en-suite. The main bedroom and lounge both open directly onto a tranquil central courtyard, where a sparkling swimming pool and entertainment area create the perfect setting for relaxing throughout the year.

Outside, the beautifully landscaped garden provides a peaceful sanctuary with minimal maintenance, allowing you to enjoy your surroundings without the upkeep. Additional features include a double automated garage and a JoJo water storage tank, for added convenience and peace of mind.

Waterford Estate is renowned for its secure, family-friendly environment, beautifully maintained surroundings, and excellent location. Residents enjoy easy access to top schools, Fourways Mall, Cedar Square, Douglasdale Shopping Centre, Olivedale and Life Fourways Hospital, Lanseria International Airport, and major transport routes, making this an exceptional place to call home.

Whether youre searching for your forever family home or a secure lock-up-and-go lifestyle in one of Fourways most desirable estates, this exceptional property is not to be missed.
