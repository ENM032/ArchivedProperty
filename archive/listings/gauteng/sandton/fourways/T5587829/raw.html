3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 2 4 399 m² 180 m²

Well-presented 3 bedroom cluster

Beautifully positioned in a tranquil and secure Estate, we proudly present this move-in ready home.

Enter into a sun-filled split-level living area. Just off the dining room is a study nook, so ideal as a work from home space. The lounge, with air-conditioning and a feature gas fireplace, leads out to a covered entertainers patio, with a built-in gas braai. The entertainers patio overlooks the manicured garden, and heated splash pool.

The kitchen is well-equipped and boasts granite counter tops, space for a double door fridge, and a separate scullery with space for 3 appliances. The scullery opens out to a private back courtyard, with a fitted washing line and 2400L Jojo Tank.

Three good-sized bedrooms, all with ceiling fans and air-conditioning, and two full bathrooms. The main bedroom is en-suite, has a walk-in closet, and opens out to the garden.

The double automated garage has direct access into the home, and there are two covered carports, offering secure parking for family and friends when visiting. The garage also has a back door, offering secure parking for a trailer or motorbike.

The home is currently fitted with a RENTED solar backup power system, that can be taken over by its new family. The Estate offers residents peace of mind living with professional 24-hour security. and is pet-friendly.

Negotiating from R2 995 000 - Asking R3 150 000.

In Fourways you are close to the major arteries of Cedar Road, Witkoppen Road, Winnie Mandela Drive, and the N1. Also close by are Fourways Mall, Cedar Square, Monte Casino and Broadacres Shopping Center for all your shopping requirements, entertainment and restaurants.

Top schools in the area include Montessori, Crawford International, Heron Bridge, Steyn City, Broadacres Academy, Fourways High, and Dainfern College, to name a few.

With the extensive Fourways Mall retail and commercial upgrade, the new business centre and the Gautrain coming past the area, Fourways is most definitely a very attractive area to purchase where your investment will grow. Fourways continues to be an extremely sought after area to live.
