Four bedroom home for sale

A striking expression of contemporary design, this exceptional four-bedroom residence has been meticulously crafted to blend sophisticated modern living with the tranquillity of its natural surroundings.

Designed for effortless indoor-outdoor living, the expansive open-plan reception areas flow seamlessly onto a generous covered patio through floor-to-ceiling stacking glass doors. Whether entertaining guests, enjoying alfresco dining, or relaxing with family, every space has been thoughtfully created to embrace the beautifully landscaped gardens and bring the outdoors in.

All four generously proportioned bedrooms feature luxurious en-suite bathrooms and enjoy direct access to the surrounding gardens, creating a peaceful connection with nature throughout the home.

At the heart of the residence is a spectacular glass-enclosed three-car garagean architectural showpiece that serves as both a functional space and a striking design feature. Visible from multiple areas within the home, this versatile space can be adapted to suit your lifestyle, whether as a collector''s showcase, creative studio, or entertainment feature. Elegant water features and a tranquil fountain further enhance the home''s distinctive character.

Sustainability and convenience have been seamlessly integrated, with a comprehensive solar energy system comprising an inverter, two lithium batteries and solar panels, together with an automated backup water system and a four-stage irrigation system. Every detail has been carefully considered to deliver a home that combines luxury, innovation and everyday practicality.

This is far more than a beautiful residenceit is an exceptional lifestyle offering where timeless architecture, refined finishes and sustainable living come together in perfect harmony.
