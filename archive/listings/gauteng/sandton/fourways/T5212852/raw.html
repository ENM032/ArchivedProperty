Luxurious 5-Bedroom Haven in an Exclusive Secure Estate

Exclusive Sole Mandate | Elegant 5-Bedroom Family Retreat in Prestigious Secure Estate

Discover the perfect blend of comfort, style, and tranquillity in this beautifully designed 5-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom residence, ideally positioned within one of the most sought-after secure gated communities. With its north-facing orientation, the home is bathed in natural light and warmth, creating a welcoming atmosphere throughout the year.

From the moment you enter through the grand double-volume foyer, you are greeted by a sense of sophistication and space. The open-plan living and dining areas flow effortlessly onto a covered patio, providing a seamless indoor-outdoor lifestyle that is ideal for relaxed family living or refined entertaining.

The gourmet kitchen, designed with the discerning chef in mind, boasts top-of-the-line appliances, generous counter space, and captivating views. Practical features include a double automated garage with direct access to the home, complemented by additional parking for visitors.
