Luxury 3 Bedroom House For Sale!

Situated in the heart of leafy Broadacres, welcome to this beautiful 3 bedroom, 2,5 bathroom townhouse for sale. This home offers luxurious amenities and is perfect for those looking for a comfortable and convenient lifestyle.

As you enter the house, you will be greeted by a spacious living area with ample natural light and a sleek design. The open plan kitchen is fully equipped with SMEG appliances dishwasher, washing machine, dryer, microwave and an integrated fridge, making it perfect for cooking and entertaining guests. The outdoor terrace provides a relaxing space to enjoy a braai and pool area.

The property boasts a double garage, three bedrooms, master bedroom en- suite with all bedrooms with access to a private balcony where you can relax and enjoy the views.
