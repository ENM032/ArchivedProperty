3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 2 347 m²

Luxurious, modern and spacious 3 bedroom, 2 bathroom home.

Set within the highly sought-after Tezula Lifestyle Estate in the heart of Fourways, this beautiful modern double-storey home offers elegant living in a secure and well-positioned estate.

Upstairs, the home features three generously sized bedrooms, all air-conditioned for year-round comfort. The luxurious main suite boasts a full en-suite bathroom and opens onto a private balcony overlooking the beautifully manicured garden and sparkling swimming poola tranquil retreat to start or end your day. The remaining two bedrooms share a stylish second bathroom fitted with a shower, toilet, and vanity.

The downstairs living spaces are flooded with natural light and designed for seamless, open-plan living. The modern kitchen flows effortlessly into the dining area and spacious loungeTV and entertainment area, all of which open out onto the garden and pool.

Outdoors, a thoughtfully designed seating and entertainment area provides the perfect setting for hosting guests, enjoying family time, or simply relaxing while taking in the serene surroundings. A guest bathroom downstairs adds to the homes convenience and functionality. The property is complete with a double garage, offering secure parking and additional storage.

Residents of Tezula Lifestyle Estate enjoy access to an exceptional range of amenities, including a clubhouse, tennis court, swimming pool, childrens jungle gyms, astroturf putting green, and a soccer pitch. The estate offers 24-hour security, ensuring peace of mind and a safe lifestyle.

Ideally located, the estate provides easy access to major highways and routes, top shopping malls, restaurants, golf courses, outdoor facilities, and all that the Fourways area has to offer.

Dont miss this opportunity to own a home that perfectly blends luxury, comfort, and lifestyle.
