Architectural Four Bedroom Masterpiece with Dual Main Suites in Helderfontein Estate

Set within the sought after Helderfontein Estate, this exceptional four bedroom residence combines striking architecture with practical family living, creating a home that feels both impressive and effortlessly comfortable.

From the moment you enter, the scale is evident. Double volume living spaces and expansive floor to ceiling glazing flood the home with natural light, while clean architectural lines and carefully considered finishes create a timeless contemporary aesthetic.

At the heart of the home is a designer kitchen featuring bold quartz countertops, generous preparation space, and a seamless connection to the living and entertaining areas. The open plan design allows each space to flow naturally while maintaining its own identity.

A striking floating staircase serves as a centerpiece, connecting the homes two levels with elegance and architectural flair.The accommodation has been thoughtfully designed around modern family living. Four spacious en suite bedrooms offer privacy and flexibility, including a guest suite on the ground floor. Upstairs, two luxurious main suites occupy opposite wings of the home, creating a unique layout seldom found within the estate. A fourth en suite bedroom and comfortable pajama lounge complete the upper level.

The homes balance of design, functionality and space makes it equally suited to entertaining, family life and multigenerational living.

Located within Helderfontein Estate, residents enjoy secure estate living, walking trails, parks, greenbelts, excellent access to leading schools, and convenient connections to Fourways, Midrand and Sandton.

A home that stands apart not because it is the largest, but because every element has been thoughtfully considered.
