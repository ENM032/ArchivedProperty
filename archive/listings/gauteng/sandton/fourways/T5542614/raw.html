4 Bedroom House in Fourways

30 sagewood
4 2.5 6 800 m² 470 m²

Refined Elegance in Exclusive Estate. Situated in the exclusive Fernbrook Estate. From the moment you arrive, a grand courtyard sets a tone of sophisticated charm. Step through the threshold into a stately entrance hall that flows effortlessly into sun-drenched, open-plan living spaces each with a cozy gas fireplace. Folding doors blur the line between indoor luxury and outdoor leisure, opening to a magnificent entertainer''s patio. Equipped with air-conditioner and a built-in braai, this expansive patio looks out over a manicured garden and a sparkling swimming pool.

At the heart of the home, the gourmet kitchen is a masterpiece of modern design. It showcases sleek, soft-touch cabinetry, premium integrated appliances, a versatile hybrid gas-and-electric stove, and a discreet, separate scullery.

For the ultimate indulgence, a hidden underground wine cellar awaits below, offering an intimate retreat for tastings or a private sanctuary.

The upper level transitions into a private family haven, starting with a cozy pyjama lounge that opens onto a breezy balcony. 3 remarkably spacious bedrooms each boast their own private balconies, capturing scenic views and fresh air. A versatile 4th bedroom effortlessly adapts into a quiet executive study or a quiet home office. The main suite is an opulent retreat, complete with a dedicated dressing area and a lavish, full en-suite bathroom, while a 2nd beautifully appointed full bathroom services the remaining bedrooms.

Downstairs, a separate en-suite bedroom with an independent entrance provides the perfect layout for upscale Staff Accommodation or a private guest suite. Complete with a double automated garage offering secure, direct access.

Extras include:

-Wine Cellar

-Central Air-Conditioning

-Central Vacuum System

-Solar Panels

-7.5 Kva Inverter

-Salt Pool System

-Automated Sprinkler System

This home is nestled within the prestigious Fernbrook estate. Here, families enjoy elite, state-of-the-art security alongside world-class amenities, including a riverfront clubhouse, vibrant kids'' parks, and manicured sports fields.
