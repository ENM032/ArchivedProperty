Spacious single storey family home in boomed enclosure

Beautifully positioned in a secure boomed enclosure, we are proud to present this gorgeous family home.

This warm and inviting single storey family home offers spacious living areas designed for comfort, connection, and easy entertaining.

The entrance opens into a generous TV lounge and dining room, with air-conditioning, flowing into a the family room and an open-plan kitchen, equipped with a gas hob, granite counter tops, and a separate sculleryso ideal for busy family life.

The home features three good-sized bedrooms, with laminated flooring, all overlooking the garden and providing a peaceful retreat. Two bathrooms, the main is en-suite, comfortably accommodate family and guests.

The living areas extend onto a covered patio that overlooks an expansive and secure garden, featuring a large and refreshing swimming pool, creating the perfect space for children and pets to play for family gatherings.

Additional features include a detached double garage, full solar back-up, staff accommodation, and plenty of secure parking for family and friends.
