Sophisticated Modern Living: 4-Bedroom Luxury Home in Stone River Estate, Beverley Ext, Fourways

Discover the perfect blend of comfort, style, and security in this immaculate 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom residence. Located in a sought-after secure estate in Beverley, this home offers an exceptional lifestyle for the discerning family.

Property Features

Ground Floor: Entertainment & Functionality

Welcoming Entrance: A grand entrance hall leads into an expansive, open-plan living area.

Gourmet Kitchen: The heart of the home features a 5-plate gas stove, electric oven, dedicated space for a double-door fridge, and a functional breakfast nook. An abundance of cupboard space is complemented by a separate, practical scullery.

Living & Dining: Generous lounge and dining areas that flow seamlessly, ideal for hosting guests.

Convenience: Includes a guest toilet, under-stair storage space, and a double automated garage.

Outdoor Living: Step outside to a sparkling swimming pool and a meticulously maintained garden. Added security and accessibility are provided by two side pedestrian gates.

Staff Accommodation: Includes private en-suite staff quarters at the rear of the property.

Upper Level: Private Retreat

En-suite Bedrooms: Four well-sized, light-filled bedrooms, each featuring its own private en-suite bathroom.

Lifestyle Spaces: Includes a dedicated study for remote work and a cozy pyjama lounge for family relaxation.

Finishes: The entire upper level is finished with high-quality, durable laminated flooring.

Estate Amenities

Living in this secure estate offers more than just a home it offers a lifestyle. Residents have exclusive access to:

A communal clubhouse.

A sparkling swimming pool.

Multiple dedicated play areas for children.

Top-tier 247 security for complete peace of mind.

Prime Location

Situated in the heart of FourwaysBeverley, you are perfectly positioned near top-tier infrastructure:

Shopping & Leisure: Moments from Fourways Mall, Montecasino, and a variety of boutiques, salons, and restaurants.

Education: Close proximity to leading schools and colleges.

Connectivity: Easy access to major arterial routes, including Winnie Mandela Drive, ensuring seamless travel to surrounding areas like Dainfern Valley.
