House Under Offer in Fourways Sandton

By appointment only

EXCLUSIVE MANDATE - Step into a world of timeless Tuscan elegance with this magnificently appointed five-bedroom residence, where refined luxury and warm sophistication blend seamlessly to create a home unlike any other. Beyond the grand wooden entrance doors, a breath-taking glass atrium welcomes you with a tranquil water feature and lush greenery, instantly setting a tone of serenity and opulence. Designed for effortless entertaining, the home offers an expansive formal lounge, an intimate dining room enhanced by a cosy fireplace and striking Tuscan-inspired water feature, and a relaxed TV lounge warmed by a double-sided fireplace. Stacking doors open onto a covered patio complete with a built-in braai, overlooking a sparkling pool and beautifully curated entertainment area the ultimate setting for unforgettable gatherings with family and friends. At the heart of the home lies a spectacular chefs kitchen, boasting a gas cooker, extractor, central island with prep sink, granite countertops, abundant cabinetry, a separate scullery, and space for a double-door fridge, all thoughtfully designed for both functionality and style.

The lower level accommodates two versatile bedrooms, including a generously sized guest suite with a full en-suite bathroom, while the second room offers the flexibility of a private study or additional bedroom and cloak room close by. Upstairs, two beautifully appointed en-suite bedrooms feature air conditioning for year-round comfort, while the lavish main suite is a true sanctuary, complete with a fireplace, walk-through closets, and an indulgent full bathroom with double vanities and a striking oval bath for luxurious winter evenings. Adding to the homes exceptional appeal is a private cinema room tucked away above a charming wooden staircase, featuring plush recliner seating, a state-of-the-art projector, and surround sound for an immersive movie experience. Completing this extraordinary residence are three garages, ample storage, and parking for seven additional vehicles and a staff accommodation encouraging an easy lifestyle. Nestled within an exclusive and intimate security estate, this remarkable Tuscan-inspired masterpiece offers an unparalleled lifestyle of elegance, comfort, and prestige a rare hidden gem perfectly suited to the discerning family seeking a home in a class of its own.
