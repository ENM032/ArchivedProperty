3 Bedroom House in Fourways

16 sagewood road
3 2.5 4 800 m²

Timeless Elegance in Exclusive Estate. Nestled within the prestigious sanctuary of Fernbrook Estate, this magnificent home blends elegance with seamless classic luxury. The grand journey begins at the dramatic, double-volume entrance hall, where soaring ceilings and abundant natural light create an immediate sense of arrival. Downstairs, the home unfolds into three separate, fluid reception rooms, featuring 2 lounges and a formal dining room that offer versatile spaces for intimate family moments or grand gatherings. A discreet guest loo adds a touch of convenience to this beautifully curated ground level.

Designed as an entertainer''s paradise, these sweeping living areas open effortlessly to a massive, private covered patio. This exceptional outdoor space serves as a true al fresco luxury retreat, complete with a built-in bar and tv connectivity for unforgettable celebrations. The gourmet kitchen, boasts striking granite countertops, a premium gas stove, and a separate scullery.

Upstairs, a sweeping staircase leads to a private sanctuary of elevated retreats. 3 sun-drenched bedrooms each open onto their own private balconies, offering peaceful, scenic views of the estate. The opulent main suite stands out as a true master oasis, featuring a boutique-style dressing area, a full bathroom, and a tranquil outdoor shower under the stars. A beautifully appointed 2nd full bathroom services the remaining family bedrooms, while a clever study nook on the landing creates the perfect work-from-home corner.

The property is completed by practical elements that ensure total ease of living. A double automated garage provides secure parking alongside ample additional driveway space for guests, while an external staff bathroom equipped with a shower and toilet offers added functionality.

Embracing a lifestyle of modern sustainability, the home is fully equipped with cutting-edge solar power and a high-capacity JoJo water storage tank for complete peace of mind.

This home is nestled within the prestigious Fernbrook estate. Here, families enjoy elite, state-of-the-art security alongside world-class amenities, including a riverfront clubhouse, vibrant kids'' parks, and manicured sports fields
