3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 2 6 908 m² 250 m²

Exclusive Mandate!

Nestled within the prestigious Fairfield Estate, renowned for its exceptional security and 24-hour armed response, this captivating property offers a truly remarkable living experience. As you step through the welcoming enclosed patio, you''ll be greeted by a spacious open-plan lounge and dining room, boasting double-volume ceilings that create a sense of grandeur and elegance.

The thoughtfully designed kitchen is a true culinary haven, equipped with a gas stove, ample storage space, and room for two appliances. With direct access to the court yard and staff accommodation, this kitchen seamlessly blends functionality and convenience.

The Master Bedroom, complete with an en-suite bathroom and generous cupboard space, provides a private sanctuary with direct access to the garden, while the two additional good sized bedrooms share a family bathroom, ensuring comfort and privacy for all.

Complementing the indoor living spaces, the property features a large, well-maintained garden, ripe with endless possibilities, including space for a pool, expansion, and is perfect for children to play and explore. The automated double garage, with direct access to the house, offers secure parking while the expansive driveway provides additional space for guests. Crafted with meticulous attention to detail and designed to cater to your every need, this exceptional property is the perfect blend of comfort, security, and convenience.

The bedrooms have laminate, and the rest of the house is tiled throughout with porcelain tiles.

There is also a solar system that is currently being rented by the tenants, and can be taken over by the new owners.

Fairfield Estate has 247 armed response. There is also a park and the Fourways Junior College within the estate. Let the children ride their bicycles and be safe.

Fairfield Estate is close to Fourways Mall, top private and government schools, the Life Hospital, and other shopping centres.
