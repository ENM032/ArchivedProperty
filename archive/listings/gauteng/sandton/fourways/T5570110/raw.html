3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Modern Family Living at Its Finest

Welcome to this beautiful and modern three-bedroom home, thoughtfully designed for comfortable family living and effortless entertaining. Situated in a well-managed, secure complex, this exceptional home offers generous living spaces, quality finishes, and an outstanding location.

As you step inside, you are welcomed by a spacious open-plan lounge and dining area that flows seamlessly into the modern kitchen. The kitchen boasts ample cupboard space, a stylish centre island with a gas stove, and a separate scullery, offering both practicality and style.

The lounge opens onto a private covered patio overlooking the sparkling swimming pool, creating the perfect setting for relaxing or entertaining family and friends. The entertainment area is complete with a built-in braai and a built-in pizza oven, making outdoor living a true pleasure. A designated washing line area adds to the home''s practicality, while a guest toilet is conveniently located downstairs.

Upstairs, you''ll find three exceptionally spacious bedrooms filled with natural light. The family bathroom is beautifully appointed with both a bath and shower, complemented by double basins. The luxurious main bedroom offers a walk-in closet and a full en-suite bathroom featuring both a bath and shower, as well as double vanities for his and hers.

Additional features include a double automated garage, prepaid electricity, a sparkling swimming pool, and a private outdoor entertainment area designed for year-round enjoyment.

The complex is exceptionally well maintained and offers 24-hour security, providing peace of mind for you and your family. Perfectly positioned within the estate, the home enjoys easy access to major routes, public transport, excellent schools, and a variety of shopping centres, with Fourways Mall just a short drive away.

This is a home that effortlessly combines modern elegance, generous living spaces, and exceptional convenience. Whether you''re looking for a place to entertain or simply enjoy quality family time, this beautiful home has it all.

Contact us today to arrange your private viewing and experience everything this outstanding property has to offer.
