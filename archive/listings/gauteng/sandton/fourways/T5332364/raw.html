4 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
4 3 2 620 m²

Spacious 4-Bed Family Home in Secure Complex with Pool & Garden Oasis

Step through the striking solid-pivot front door into a welcoming hallway, then into a luminous open-plan lounge and dining area the heart of this family home. The lounge and dining rooms flow effortlessly through large glass doors onto a private, undercover patio, garden, and pristine swimming pool perfect for summer afternoons and entertaining.

The chefs kitchen is a highlight: stone countertops, a built-in oven with glass-top stove, and a double-door fridge set seamlessly into custom cabinetry. A built-in display cupboard adds character ideal for showcasing your crystal glassware.

Down the hall are four generously sized bedrooms:

Two secondary bedrooms with laminate flooring and built-in cupboards overlook the pool bright, fresh, and ready for kids or guests.

The third bedroom offers an en-suite bathroom with shower, basin and toilet a practical plus.

The master suite features built-in cupboards, a walk-through dressing room, and a full en-suite bathroom, plus double French doors opening directly onto the garden for serene morning or evening views.

Outside, the landscaped garden is immaculately maintained and features a sparkling pool with a decorative water feature as its centrepiece. A handy outside storeroom adds practical storage space. Inside, double automated garages allow secure, direct entry into the hallway ideal for ease and safety.

This home is the perfect blend of style, comfort, and practicality a turnkey option for families seeking tranquillity, security and a ready-to-enjoy lifestyle.

The secondary, pre schools and high schools both government and private are within minutes away. The Fourways precinct and Monte Casino are 5min away . Design Quarter boosts a brand new look with a checkers , Clicks Pharmacy and many coffee shops and restaurants. All Major roads and N1 Freeway are one minute away. Golf Driving range can be found within Magaliessig for the golfer as well as a Doggie Day Care centre for short term and long term boarding.
