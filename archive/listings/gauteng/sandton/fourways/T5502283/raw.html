Luxury Living Meets Family Comfort in Broadacres

OWNER ASKING: R3 799 000

OFFERS FROM: R3 399 000

Nestled within a highly sought after estate in the heart of Broadacres, this beautifully designed home combines modern luxury with effortless family living. From the moment you step inside, the expansive open plan interiors create a warm and inviting atmosphere, perfectly suited to both relaxed everyday living and stylish entertaining.

The generously extended lounge offers exceptional space and flow, centred around a sophisticated Calore electric fireplace that adds both charm and comfort to the home. Designed with both functionality and elegance in mind, the gourmet kitchen features a striking centre island, gas hob, walk in pantry, and an abundance of custom cabinetry. A separate scullery accommodates three appliances as well as a double door fridge, ensuring practicality without compromising on style.

Upstairs, four beautifully appointed bedrooms provide comfortable accommodation for the whole family, while the bathrooms are finished with quality fittings and refined finishes throughout.

The covered patio has been thoughtfully extended to create the ideal outdoor entertainment area, complete with a built in braai and views over the manicured private garden, offering a peaceful space to unwind or host guests.

Additional features include a back up power system, shutter and roller blinds fitted throughout most of the home, energy efficient underfloor heating in the main bathroom, an enlarged courtyard area, built in garage storage, excellent estate security, and access to a communal pool within the complex.

This immaculate home delivers the perfect balance of comfort, convenience, and contemporary living in one of Broadacres'' most desirable locations.
