4 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
4 2.5 6 2 503 m²

Family Retreat with Tennis Court and Exceptional Entertainment Spaces

Set on expansive grounds in the heart of Fourways is this lovely home with so much to offer. The home features two spacious living areas with a cozy fireplace, as well as a separate dining room ideal for family gatherings and entertaining. The second living area opens seamlessly onto a covered patio overlooking the swimming pool and lush backyard, creating an inviting indoor outdoor lifestyle. The well-appointed kitchen is designed for functionality and style, featuring a gas stove, extractor fan, breakfast nook, veggie sink, ample cupboard space, eye-level grill and built-in microwave. A separate scullery provides space for a washing machine, dishwasher and tumble dryer. Additional outdoor features include a tennis court, staff accommodation and a separate cottage that can easily be utilized as a private office or work-from-home space. The home offers three generously sized bedrooms sharing a full family bathroom. The third bedroom includes additional space ideal for a study or home office. The spacious main bedroom boasts a walk-in closet, air conditioning, a full en-suite bathroom and direct access to the backyard. Further features include a guest toilet, linen cupboard, inverter with solar panels, top of the range security cameras and an alarm system for complete peace of mind. Parking is well catered for with a double garage and a large carport accommodating up to four vehicles. This exceptional property combines luxury, practicality, and secure family living in one of Fourways most sought-after locations.

Do not miss this opportunity to secure your perfect family sanctuary.
