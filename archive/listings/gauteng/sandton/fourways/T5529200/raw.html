4 Bedroom House in Fourways

47 robin drive
4 2.5 6 1 500 m²

The perfect family haven with a cottage, in a secure boomed area and in walking distance to school.

Negotiating from R3 399 000

This spacious and secure four-bedroom family residence welcomes you with a charming entrance and delightful curb appeal, offering a glimpse of the warmth and comfort that await inside.

Designed with both style and practicality in mind, the home features four bedrooms with laminate flooring, including a versatile study that can easily serve as a fifth bedroom. A conveniently located cloakroom adds to the home''s functionality for both family and guests. Three generous reception areas provide ample space for everyday living and entertaining.

The spacious main bedroom offers a peaceful retreat, complete with a modern full en-suite bathroom featuring a freestanding bath your own private oasis.

A true delight for culinary enthusiasts, the gourmet open-plan kitchen is the heart of the home. Boasting generous counter space, a central island with a gas hob and extractor fan, a pantry, scullery, and stylish subway-tile finishes, it is designed for both effortless family living and entertaining.

The open-plan layout flows seamlessly into the large dining room, inviting family room with its welcoming fireplace, and private lounge, creating an ideal setting for hosting guests and making lasting memories.

Outside, the spacious covered patio overlooking the manicured garden, sparkling swimming pool, and trampoline create a serene space where family and friends can relax and enjoy the outdoors together.

Adding exceptional value to this property is a separate Airbnb-friendly studio cottage, staff accommodation, two solar geysers, prepaid electricity, fibre connectivity, two automated garages, 4 carports, and an electric fence, all contributing to a secure and comfortable lifestyle.

More than just a home, this is a private family sanctuary where every generation can come together and enjoy life to the fullest. Asking R3 485 000. JUST WHAT YOU WANT.
