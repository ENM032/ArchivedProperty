3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 3 2 1 689 m² 302 m²

Three bedroomed home, with a separate flatlet - fourth bedroom

Nestled in one of Douglasdale''s sought-after boomed areas, this beautifully renovated freestanding home offers the perfect blend of style, comfort, and exceptional entertainment spaces.

Step inside to a light-filled open-plan living area that flows effortlessly onto the sparkling pool and covered entertainment lapa, creating the ideal setting for relaxed family living and entertaining. The gourmet kitchen is both stylish and functional, featuring ample cupboard space, a gas hob, gas oven and stunning designer pendant lighting.

A cosy TV lounge provides the perfect space to unwind, while the home offers three generously sized family bedrooms serviced by a beautifully appointed bathroom. The spacious main suite is a true retreat, boasting abundant cupboard space and a luxurious en-suite bathroom with a separate toilet.

Adding to the home''s versatility is a fully self-contained flatlet with its own bathroom - ideal for extended family, guests, or rental potential. A separate laundry room adds further convenience.

The beautifully landscaped garden is a standout feature, complete with a sparkling pool, a well-positioned entertainment lapa, and plenty of space for children and pets to enjoy. At the rear of the property you''ll find a Zozo hut, a fully fitted greenhouse, rabbit and bird enclosures, and a thriving vegetable garden.

This exceptional home is packed with premium extras, including:

30,000-litre JoJo water storage system

Solar panels

Pool heating panels

Morso fireplace in the lounge

Air conditioning in the lounge and main bedroom

8kVA inverter

Security camera system

Double automated garage

Beautifully maintained and thoughtfully upgraded throughout, this is a rare opportunity to own a truly special home in one of Douglasdale''s most desirable neighbourhoods. Don''t miss your chance to view this exceptional property.
