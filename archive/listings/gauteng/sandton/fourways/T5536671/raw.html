SERENE & TRANQUIL FAMILY HOME. Discover this stunning, newly renovated family oasis perfect for a blended family seeking versatility and comfort. The main house offers two luxurious en-suite bedrooms - one on each level - providing privacy and convenience. Step outside to an expansive entertainment patio, where you can relax and enjoy views of the beautifully landscaped garden and sparking self filling jacuzzi pool. For those who love to entertain, a separate bar and boma area is nestled at the garden''s end, offering a tranquil retreat. This property also includes two charming one-bedroom cottages, each with its own kitchen, bathroom, and lounge area.
