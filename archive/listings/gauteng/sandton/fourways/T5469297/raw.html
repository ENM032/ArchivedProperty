3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 2 4 850 m² 300 m²

Discover your dream home nestled in the heart of the picturesque Fairfield Estate, a secure estate celebrated for its exceptional 24-hour security and serene surroundings. As you step through the charming front garden and front door, you will be welcomed into a spacious first lounge, which can also be used as a games room, making it the perfect family retreat.

The second lounge seamlessly transitions to a covered patio, which overlooks the beautifully manicured back garden, where you can unwind by the sparkling pool, creating an ideal setting for entertaining or simply enjoying a quiet afternoon in the sun.

Right by the living area is an open-plan dining area, which effortlessly connects to a modern kitchen featuring elegant Caesar stone countertops and a gas stove, perfect for culinary enthusiasts.

A separate scullery provides ample space for all your appliances and conveniently opens to the front garden, enhancing your living experience.

With two sunny bedrooms sharing a full bathroom, and a luxurious Master Bedroom boasting an en-suite, generous cupboard space, and air conditioning, this home is designed for comfort and convenience. The laminate flooring in the bedrooms adds a touch of warmth, while the tiled areas throughout the rest of the house ensure easy maintenance.

Living in Fairfield Estate means enjoying access to a park for the children and proximity to top-rated schools, including the esteemed Fourways Junior College. With the Fourways Mall and Life Hospital just a stone''s throw away, youll have everything you need right at your fingertips.
