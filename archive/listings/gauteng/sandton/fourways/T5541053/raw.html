3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
3 4 6 932 m²

Rare exclusivity in highly sought after Waterford Estate

Perfectly positioned to embrace complete privacy and breathtaking natural surroundings, this architecturally designed residence overlooking the garden and adjacent forestgreenbelt and river, creates a true sanctuary from urban life.

Designed with an emphasis on light, space, and seamless indoor-outdoor living, the home showcases timeless architecture complemented by refined finishes and exceptional attention to detail. Every space has been carefully considered to maximise comfort, functionality, and effortless entertaining.

The home features a beautifully appointed kitchen with separate scullery, flowing into the open plan living area which opens onto a spacious covered patio. The elegant double volume dining room and family room are anchored by a striking double-sided gas fireplace, creating a welcoming atmosphere while seamlessly extending to the outdoor entertainment area overlooking the swimming pool and garden.

For those who work from home, the private study offers a peaceful retreat with private entrance which could also be used as a 4th bedroom with modern guest bathroom with shower downstairs.

Upstairs, the home features 3 spacious bedrooms, each complemented by its own beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom. Two of the bedrooms open onto an expansive balcony, perfectly positioned to capture uninterrupted views across the lush greenbelt.

Additional amenities include 2 garages, irrigation system, generator with switch over and pool heat pump, catering to every need.

This property is an ideal sanctuary for those who value unparalleled luxury and convenience in a prestigious Estate. Waterford Estate provides the best of both worlds within a secure estate with peaceful family-orientated living.
