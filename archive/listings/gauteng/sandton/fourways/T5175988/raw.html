4 BED 4 BATH CLUSTER HOME FOR SALE IN FOURWAYS

Welcome to your new home within the stunning Brendavere Estate in Craigavon, Fourways.

The architecturally designed cluster home offers three spacious bedrooms upstairs with ensuite bathrooms, a showroom quality kitchen fitted with appliances, and a generously large scullery.

Upon entrance you greeted with a seamless flow of open plan spaces, leading to the lounge and dining room.

A 4th bedroom downstairs with its separate ensuite, can be used as a guest room or study.

A generously-sized covered patio leading to a spacious, private garden with swimming pool is welcoming for entertaining and family time.

This corner positioned home, was designed with elegance, style and comfort in mind. Just ready to move in.

The estate offers around-the-clock security and access control with the iconic Fourways Mall a short 2km drive from the estatesentrance.
