3 Bedroom House in Fourways

Where a House Becomes a Home

Tucked away for added privacy, this charming home is a true gem, waiting for the perfect family to make it their own.

Upon entering through the front door, you are welcomed into a spacious open-plan lounge and dining area that flows seamlessly onto a beautiful patio overlooking the garden and pool area the perfect setting for relaxed family living and entertaining.

The home offers the convenience of direct access from the double garage into the house, with a guest toilet conveniently located downstairs.

The generously sized kitchen features ample counter space, plenty of built-in cupboards, and the added benefit of a gas stove, making it both practical and inviting.

Upstairs, you will find all of the bedrooms, along with a dedicated nook that is ideal for use as a small study or home office. An additional bathroom fitted with a shower is also located on this level.

Both bedrooms are spacious and carpeted, creating a warm and comfortable atmosphere. The main bedroom enjoys access to its own private balcony and features an en-suite bathroom complete with both a bathtub and a shower.

Additional features include air conditioning, a double garage, and extra parking space. Situated within a safe and secure estate with security, this home offers peace of mind alongside comfortable and convenient living.
