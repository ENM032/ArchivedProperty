A beautiful three bedroom home

SOLE MANDATE

This beautifully finished three bedroom home is nestled within a secure, intimate estate in Fourways.

The modern kitchen is thoughtfully designed with unique finishes and features a gas hob, electric oven, extractor fan, built-in cupboards, a pantry, and plumbing points for both a washing machine and dishwasher.

The dining area opens up to to the private backyard garden , while the inviting lounge boasts a cozy fireplace for the chilly winter evenings and opens onto a spacious covered top range craftily designed patio with a built -in braai. Overlooking the garden and swimming pool, this outdoor space is ideal for entertaining family and friends.

The generously sized main bedroom features soft carpeted flooring, an elegant built-in headboard, direct access to the backyard and a stylish en-suite bathroom. Two additional well proportioned bedrooms share a modern family bathroom.

Adding to the home''s versatility is a dedicated home office, perfect for remote work or study, as well as staff accommodation upstairs.

The estate also has a designated play area for the children.

Positioned near Fourways High School, this exceptional home enjoys easy access to major transport routes, shopping centres, restaurants, healthcare facilities, and a host of other everyday amenities.
