Stunning Family home in popular complex heart of Fourways

Negotiating from R2 399 000

In a very popular estate in the heart of Fourways in an excellent location with stunning nature reserve next door and with easy access to Fourways mall, Fourways high and a host of amenities.

You are welcomed by a beautiful entrance with a double automated garage. The property has 3 air-conditioned bedrooms, and 2 newly renovated bathrooms with the main en-suite as well as an addition guest cloakroom servicing the garden and pool.

There is a separate granite kitchen which allows separation when entertaining and numerous living areas with air conditioning that can be used in a variety of different ways. There is a patio that leads to large covered pool.

The property has back up water and full sunsynk solar system.
