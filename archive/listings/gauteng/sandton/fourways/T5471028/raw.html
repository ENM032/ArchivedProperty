4 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 2 700 m² 399 m²

4 bedroom Staff Quarters

Experience refined estate living in this exceptional 4-bedroom home, perfectly designed for comfort, style, and modern convenience. Located in the prestigious Helderfontein Estate, this residence offers elegant finishes, spacious interiors, and seamless indoor-outdoor living.

A grand revolving entrance door welcomes you into light-filled open-plan living spaces with soaring ceilings. The designer kitchen features integrated appliances, sleek cream cabinetry, illuminated glass accents, a centre island with gas stove, and a separate scullery.

The dining and lounge areas flow effortlessly onto a sunken patio with foldable doors, creating the perfect entertainment setting. A beautifully positioned swimming pool forms the striking centre-piece of the home.

Downstairs also offers a guest cloakroom, private home office, and a guest bedroom with en-suite bathroom overlooking the pool.

Upstairs, a cozy pyjama lounge leads to three luxurious en-suite bedrooms, including a lavish main suite with walk-in closet and balcony access.

Additional features include:

Double garage

Solar power & inverter system

High-end finishes throughout

Exceptional natural light

Secure estate living

This remarkable property combines sophistication, functionality, and sustainability in one of Gautengs most desirable estates.
