LUXURIOUS SPACE FIT FOR A KING. Offers from R4 999 000

Enter through your stunning double doors into a grand foyer with double-volume ceiling highlighted by magnificent chandelier that sets an elegant tone for this beautiful residence. The home features 3 spacious reception rooms that provide versatile living spaces perfect for family gatherings and entertaining guests.

The expansive kitchen, complete with a separate scullery, offers functionality and style for your culinary adventures.

For entertainment, you''ll love the dedicated section featuring a bar, a braai area, a fire-pit, all enhanced by drop-down blinds that overlook the private swimming pool - ideal for hosting memorable events. A guest suite is conveniently located downstairs providing comfortable space for visitors.

This home offers amazing value and stunning living spaces for the whole family, with staff accommodation, plenty of parking and a sizeable bit bigger than double garage.
