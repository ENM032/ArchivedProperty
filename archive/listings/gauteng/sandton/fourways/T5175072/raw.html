Elegant 4 Bedroom Full Title Home with Pool in Sought After Brendavere in Fourways

Welcome to your dream home a spacious and beautifully maintained 4 bedroom, 3 and a half bathroom full title residence located in the heart of the secure and exclusive Brendavere in Fourways. Designed for both luxurious family living and effortless entertaining, this home offers the perfect balance of style, comfort, and security.

Step inside and be greeted by large open plan living areas, flooded with natural light and thoughtfully laid out to create a warm and inviting atmosphere. The two expansive living rooms provide ample space for both relaxed family evenings and elegant hosting. A guest bathroom conveniently serves the living areas on the ground floor.

The kitchen is a modern delight, boasting ample cupboard space, space for 3 under counter appliances and double door fridge, quality finishes, smeg gas stove and oven, and a functional layout that makes it the heart of the home. It flows seamlessly into the dining and lounge areas, creating an ideal space for both everyday living and entertaining.

Each of the four generously sized bedrooms offers privacy and comfort, with built in cupboards and large windows. The main bedroom is a luxurious retreat, complete with an en-suite bathroom, ample closet space, and views over the garden and pool. 2 bedrooms are served by well appointed full bathroom, with the 4th bedroom also featuring an en suite perfect for guests.

Outside, a sparkling swimming pool invites you to unwind in your private garden sanctuary, while the surrounding entertainment area is ideal for weekend braais and year round family gatherings. The garden is low maintenance but lush, offering both greenery and tranquillity.

Youll enjoy 24 hour security, controlled access, and a peaceful community feel. This estate is perfectly located just minutes from Fourways Mall, Montecasino, top private and public schools, major highways, and a wide selection of restaurants, gyms, and medical facilities.

Key Features:

-4 large bedrooms, 3 and a half bathrooms (2 en suite)

-2 spacious living rooms and open plan kitchen

-Sparkling private swimming pool and garden

-Secure estate with 24 hour access control

-Prime location in Fourways, close to all amenities

This home offers a rare combination of space, security, and prime positioning in one of Fourways most desirable estates. Dont miss the opportunity to make this your forever home.

Viewings by appointment only.
