4 Bedroom House in Fourways

Contact agent for street address
4 2.5 5 803 m² 482 m²

Designer Home in Cedar Lakes Waiting to be Snatched up!

If you are looking for your dream designer home in one of Fourways most sought after estates, look no further! Here you have a property to fall in love with, an architectural masterpiece, boasting character, elegance and dignity. And space ... plenty of it!!

A large glass door welcomes you, facing an artistic koi pond with a relaxing water feature, intriguing your interest. A large living area with a fireplace is pleasantly positioned on the left, and the dining room on the right, graced by a majestic double volume ceiling and a modern staircase that wraps around the sides. Through an angle positioned set of aluminum stacking doors, the living area opens out to the most divine patio, garden, pool and an elevated Japanese Gazebo overlooking the water. The garden is private with tall walls, multiple creepers, trees and bushes, a dream spot and perfect for entertainment and family fun.

The kitchen is well sized and conveniently opens out to the dining area over a breakfast nook, ideal for those on the go meals. It offers plenty of cupboard space, a microwave shelf, room for a double door fridge and a vegetable sink. The scullery hides away the under counter appliances and the double kitchen sink, leading out to the outdoor utility area and the one bedroom staffroom.

Along with the fourth bedroom or possible second study and a guest toilet, a large office room is positioned downstairs overlooking the garden and pool area. Working from home ....what a pleasure!

Upstairs the three bedrooms are positioned around the open stairway landing, filled with plenty of natural light. The master bedroom is a dream! Plenty of space, with a full hallway of cupboards behind the wide bed space. This hallway leads to a large ensuite bathroom with bath, shower, his and her sink. The bedroom gracefully opens out to the EXTENSIVE wooden deck, another entertainers paradise, with stunning views and space enough to host a party. It wraps around the full side of the house, and is accessed by the top landing. Absolutely stunning. Additional to this the upstairs landing leads to a smaller patio with a large Jacuzzi, another entertainment feature of this designer home. What a pleasure. The other two bedrooms are well sized and share the second bathroom with bath and shower and lovely tile features.

The garage is extra length, and offers an extra storage room, workshop space, or possible man cave. A 17 KVA back up power system comes with the house, with all the connections in place. Plenty of extra parking is available in front of the house and on the side as well.

Cedar Lakes is a sought after estate offering the best in the way of lifestyle. Its famous for its fantastic restaurant overlooking one of the 8 lakes, where fishing is permitted on a catch and release basis. Cedar Lakes boast a big swimming pool, kiddies play areas, two tennis courts, multiple family friendly braai facilities, walking trails and a whole lot more.

This home is truly a designer home, and stands out way above the average, waiting to be snatched up by the discerning buyer. Viewings can be arranged by appointment.
