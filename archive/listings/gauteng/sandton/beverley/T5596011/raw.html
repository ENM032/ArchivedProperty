3 Bedroom House in Beverley

Contact agent for street address
3 3.5 2 628 m² 363 m²

Space, Style & Sophistication

A home with genuine individuality, this beautifully appointed three-bedroom residence combines generous proportions, quality finishes and exceptional entertainment spaces. Warm bamboo flooring, extensive double glazing and carefully considered architectural details give the interiors a distinctive contemporary character.

The main living area is light and spacious, incorporating an elegant lounge and dining room framed by plantation shutters and a striking stone feature wall. A fireplace in the lounge adds warmth and atmosphere, while the bamboo floors continue through much of the home, creating a sense of continuity. High-quality doors and windows further reinforce the home''s attention to detail.

The modern kitchen is both stylish and practical, finished with sleek cabinetry, generous work surfaces, integrated appliances and a substantial central island with an induction hob. A particularly attractive feature is the wine cellar cleverly incorporated below the staircase, making excellent use of the space while creating an interesting design feature.

One of the property''s outstanding features is the enormous enclosed entertainment patio. Designed as a genuine extension of the living space, this impressive area features a soaring glazed roof, extensive stacking doors, a built-in bar and ample room for both lounging and large-scale dining. Open the doors, and the entire space connects effortlessly to the garden and swimming pool, creating an exceptional indoor-outdoor entertainment area.

Accommodation comprises three bedrooms and three-and-a-half bathrooms. The bedrooms are notably generous, with the principal suite offering considerably more than simply a place to sleep. Its expansive proportions comfortably accommodate a private lounge area, creating a luxurious retreat, while the adjoining bathroom provides a freestanding bath and contemporary walk-in shower.

A second lounge provides valuable additional living space, while the remaining bedrooms continue the home''s emphasis on individuality, comfort and generous proportions.

Practical features have been given equal consideration. The home is equipped with a sound system throughout, an inverter system and backup water, providing additional convenience and resilience. A double garage completes the offering.

A distinctive home for buyers who value generous living spaces and entertaining, with an enormous enclosed patio and thoughtful combination of style and practicality, setting it apart.
