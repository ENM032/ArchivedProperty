Striking Contemporary Home

Positioned in a sought-after estate renowned for its mature trees and peaceful surroundings, this striking double-storey residence combines contemporary design with warm, inviting living spaces. Large windows throughout the home frame beautiful leafy outlooks, while the seamless indoor-outdoor flow creates an atmosphere of relaxed luxury.

The heart of the home is an impressive designer kitchen featuring expansive stone countertops, a central gas hob, sleek cabinetry, integrated shelving, and a striking suspended extractor. Open-plan reception areas flow effortlessly from the kitchen and dining space into a spacious lounge with a feature fireplace, creating an ideal setting for both comfortable family living and grand-scale entertaining.

A separate family lounge with a built-in pub adds another versatile entertainment space, complete with rich wooden flooring, feature stone cladding, and easy access to the outdoor entertainment areas.

Stacking glass doors open onto a covered entertainment patio with a built-in braai, overlooking a timber deck, sparkling pool, and established garden surrounded by mature trees, offering exceptional privacy and a peaceful natural setting.

Upstairs, the home continues to impress with spacious bedrooms, elegant bathrooms, and elevated views over the surrounding treetops. The luxurious bathrooms feature sophisticated finishes, freestanding baths, walk-in showers, and a refined modern aesthetic. A large balcony extends from the upper level, enhancing the homes connection to the leafy surroundings.

Modern finishes, wood accents, feature fireplaces, quality flooring, and expansive glazing combine to give the home a distinctive contemporary character while still feeling warm and welcoming.

This exceptional residence offers a rare combination of modern architecture, generous entertainment spaces, and serene estate living in a beautifully treed environment.
