3 Bedroom House in Beverley

Contact agent for street address
3 3 2 567 m²

Contemporary Estate Living

Beautifully positioned within a secure estate, this elegant family home combines generous proportions, quality finishes and effortless indoor-outdoor living. Thoughtfully designed for modern family life, the home offers multiple reception areas, spacious bedrooms, a private garden and exceptional entertaining spaces.

At the heart of the home, expansive open-plan living and dining areas flow seamlessly onto a large covered patio through wide sliding doors. Natural light pours into the home, creating warm, inviting spaces that are equally suited to relaxed family living or entertaining friends. The covered patio comfortably accommodates both an outdoor dining area and lounge, overlooking an established, private garden that provides a peaceful setting for year-round enjoyment.

The stylish contemporary kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Featuring sleek white and charcoal cabinetry, Caesarstone countertops, subway-tile splashbacks, and extensive cupboard space, it offers the perfect blend of practicality and sophistication.

The luxurious primary suite is a true retreat. Exceptionally spacious and filled with natural light, it enjoys direct access to the garden and patio, elegant chandeliers, ample space for a seating area or home office, and extensive built-in cupboards. The beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom features a large walk-in shower, contemporary finishes and quality fittings, creating a calm, spa-inspired sanctuary.

Two additional family bedrooms are generously proportioned, each with quality built-in cupboards and easy access to stylish bathrooms finished with modern fixtures. A small study area with custom cabinetry provides an ideal work-from-home environment.

Outside, the beautifully maintained garden offers level lawns, mature landscaping and plenty of space for children and pets to play. The attractive front elevation, paved driveway and automated double garage create excellent street appeal, while ample visitor parking adds everyday convenience.
