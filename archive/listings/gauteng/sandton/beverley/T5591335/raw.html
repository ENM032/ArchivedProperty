3 Bedroom Family home in Beverley

Modern 3-Bedroom Family Home in a Secure Estate

Seeff Exclusive Sole Mandate

This modern three-bedroom family home is set within a highly secure estate and offers stylish finishes throughout, generous living spaces, and excellent indoor-outdoor flow.

The formal living room opens onto the garden and flows seamlessly into an open-plan dining area, which leads out to a private courtyard. The sleek, modern kitchen offers ample cupboard space, marble countertops, a gas stove, and a separate scullery for added convenience.

A spacious informal family room opens directly onto the garden and sparkling swimming pool, creating the perfect setting for relaxed family living and entertaining. Upstairs, a comfortable pyjama lounge leads out onto a balcony overlooking the garden below.

The bright main bedroom enjoys beautiful views, an abundance of natural light, plenty of fitted cupboards, and a modern full en-suite bathroom with a frosted glass door. The two additional bedrooms are positioned down the passage and each features its own en-suite bathroom.

With natural light streaming in from every direction, tiled or laminated flooring throughout, and generous spaces designed for entertaining, this home offers the ideal blend of modern comfort, style, and family-friendly living.
