Standout 4 Bedroom Family Home with Wine Cellar, Solar and Water Backup

Set inside a secure and sought after Beverley estate, this beautifully finished double storey family home offers the perfect balance of style, space and everyday practicality.

From the moment you step inside, the home feels considered. The entrance leads into generous open plan reception spaces, with a warm family lounge centred around a Morso fireplace, a stylish dining area and a striking climate controlled wine cellar that immediately sets the tone for the home.

The kitchen is both elegant and functional, offering granite countertops, a gas hob, eye level ovens, a breakfast counter and a separate scullery with space for your appliances. It is designed for real family living, whether you are entertaining guests or managing the usual weekday chaos with slightly more class than most of us deserve.

The living areas open onto a covered entertainers patio with a built in braai, overlooking the manicured garden and sparkling pool. Itis private, inviting and ideal for relaxed weekends, family lunches or summer evenings outside.

A dedicated work from home office is positioned downstairs and also opens onto the patio, giving you a quiet and practical space without feeling disconnected from the home. A guest cloakroom completes the downstairs layout.

Upstairs, you will find four spacious bedrooms, all filled with natural light and all opening onto the balcony with views over the garden below. The main suite is generous and private, with a walk through dressing area and a full en suite bathroom. The remaining bedrooms are well sized and serviced by a full family bathroom.

One of the biggest advantages of this home is its flexibility. The fourth bedroom, together with the staff accommodation behind it, creates the option for a separate guest suite, granny flat, teenagers pad or additional private living space.

Additional features include a double automated garage, storeroom, workshop, ample visitor parking, partial air conditioning, prepaid electricity, solar and inverter backup, plus water backup tanks with a booster pump feeding the home.

Beverley remains one of Fourways most convenient and secure residential pockets, with quick access to Lonehill Shopping Centre, Fourways Mall, Montecasino, top schools, medical facilities and major routes.

A polished, practical and feature packed family home that offers far more than the usual estate listing.

Private viewings by appointment.
