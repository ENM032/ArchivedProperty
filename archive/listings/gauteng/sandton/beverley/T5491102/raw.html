3 Bedroom Home for Sale in Beverley

This exceptional home offers a rare opportunity to own a truly unique property within this sought-after estate.

Exclusive sole mandate.

Offers from R2,950,000 will be considered.

Designed for modern living, the home features a spacious open-plan lounge and dining area with high ceilings, seamlessly connected to a well-appointed kitchen with a movable island and separate scullery.For those cold winter days an Infinity 13kw closed combustion log fire heats up the lounge and the hall through to the living rooms.

The bedroom wing includes two bedrooms sharing a full bathroom, as well as a generous main bedroom with full on-suite bathroom that opens onto a covered patio.

The outdoor space sets this home apart-featuring a solar-heated jacuzzi, a large covered patio, a manicured garden, and a spacious swimming pool area, ideal for entertaining and relaxed family living.

With seamless indoor-outdoor flow and premium lifestyle features, this property stands out as one of the finest in the estate.

Beverley is close to top schools , shopping centres an pleny more amienaties.
