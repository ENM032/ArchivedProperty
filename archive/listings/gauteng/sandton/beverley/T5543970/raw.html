Elegant Family Living with Exceptional Entertaining in a Prestigious Beverley Estate

Sole mandate.

Nestled within one of Beverley''s most sought-after secure estates, this exceptional four-bedroom residence effortlessly combines sophisticated design, generous proportions and practical family living.

A welcoming entrance hall sets the tone, complemented by a convenient guest cloakroom. At the heart of the home is a beautifully designed open-plan kitchen and dining area, created for both everyday living and effortless entertaining. The kitchen features a central island with a gas hob and prep bowl, ample space for a double-door fridge, and a separate scullery offering a double sink, room for two under-counter appliances and a walk-in pantry.

The dining room is enhanced by striking feature lighting, creating an elegant focal point, while the adjacent TV lounge provides a more intimate space to unwind. A separate study opens directly onto the patio, making it ideal for working from home. An impressive indoor entertainment room with a built-in bar flows seamlessly through stackable doors onto the covered patio, complete with a built-in braai. Overlooking the landscaped garden, sparkling splash pool and beautiful rock feature, this is an outdoor space designed for year-round enjoyment.

Upstairs, all four bedrooms are thoughtfully positioned for family living. Two bedrooms share a stylish full bathroom, while the guest bedroom enjoys its own full en-suite. The luxurious main suite offers a private balcony, an expansive walk-in dressing room and a beautifully appointed full en-suite bathroom with premium finishes.

Completing the upper level is a pyjama lounge that opens onto a large entertainment balcony, providing yet another inviting space to relax or entertain.

Additional features include:

- Double automated garage

- 10kW solar system with inverter and backup battery

- Fibre connectivity

- Excellent security within a highly sought-after Beverley estate

A home that perfectly balances luxury, comfort and functionality, offering an exceptional lifestyle in one of Beverley''s most desirable addresses.

Beverley is a charming and affluent suburb located in the northern suburbs of Gauteng and adjacent to Lonehill.

One of the main draws of Beverley is its peaceful and secure environment. The suburb boasts excellent security measures, including 24-hour patrols, CCTV cameras, and access control at all entrances, managed by a very active Lonehill residents association. This has made it a popular choice for families and professionals looking for a safe and serene place to live.

Beverley is also known for its lush greenery and natural beauty. The suburb is home to several parks and green spaces, picnic areas, and the popular Lonehill park. The nearby Lonehill Nature Reserve is also a popular destination for nature lovers, offering stunning views and a variety of flora and fauna. The suburb is well-connected to other parts of Johannesburg, with easy access to major highways and public transportation. The nearby Fourways Mall and Monte Casino complex provide residents with a wide range of shopping, dining, and entertainment options. Hospitals in the area include Fourways Life and Olivedale Clinic. Excellent schools service the area, Crawford College, Helderfontein College and Heronbridge are a few that come highly recommended. Beverley is a diverse and cosmopolitan suburb, with a mix of modern and traditional architecture. The housing options in the area range from spacious family homes to modern townhouses and apartments, catering to a variety of lifestyles and preferences.

In summary, Beverley is a beautiful and secure suburb that offers residents a peaceful and serene environment with easy access to a range of amenities. Its natural beauty, diverse housing options, and excellent security measures make it a highly desirable place to live in Johannesburg.
