Beautifully Renovated Family Home on 756m² stand in Leafy Beverley, Lonehill

Tucked away in one of Beverleys most desirable leafy streets, this beautifully renovated full-title residence offers the perfect blend of modern farmhouse charm, warm contemporary finishes, and effortless family living on a generous 756m² stand.

From the moment you enter through the gate, youre welcomed by a tranquil outdoor seating area that sets a relaxed, inviting tone. Step inside to a spacious open-plan lounge that flows seamlessly into the heart of the homea stunning modern farmhouse kitchen with quality finishes, perfectly positioned to overlook the dining area and encourage easy everyday living.

A cosy sunken lounge creates the ideal space to unwind, framed by expansive windows that flood the room with natural light while overlooking the sparkling blue pool and lush garden.

Upstairs, two well-sized air-conditioned bedrooms are serviced by a full family bathroom with exciting renovation potential. The oversized main suite is a true retreat, featuring its own lounge or study area with beautiful countryside views, plus a spacious en-suite bathroom ready to be transformed into a luxurious sanctuary.

Warm wooden flooring runs throughout most of the home, adding character and timeless appeal, while practical modern upgrades ensure peace of mind with a large solar power system inverter, batteries and panels and backup water supply.

Outside, the expansive garden and exceptional entertainment space make this home a dream for young familiesoffering plenty of room for children to play, friends to gather, and memories to be made around the pool.

Ideally located just minutes from Leaping Frog Shopping Centre, excellent schools, restaurants, and major routes, this is a rare opportunity to secure a move-in-ready home with space, style, and outstanding future value.
