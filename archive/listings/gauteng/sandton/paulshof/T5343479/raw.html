A Home For The Growing Family -

On show Saturday 15th August 2026 from 09H30 until 11H00, please contact agent for details.

Allow me to present to you this generously sized family home super well located with very close proximity to St Peters Prep, making this an ideal home for a growing family.

This residence encompasses everything you need under one roof.

Key features of the home include:

A double automated garage

Staff accommodation

A corner internal unit within the estate offering quiet and peaceful surroundings and extra security and piece of mind.

The open-plan living areas feature a kitchen with a breakfast nook, a gas hob, space for two under-counter appliances, and ample cupboard storage. The kitchen, dining room, and living area showcase exposed beams, creating a sense of height, while the living room is enhanced by a cozy wood fireplace, perfect for warming up during the winter months.

From the living area, step out onto a covered patio, ideal for entertaining and spending quality time with family. The wrap-around garden is generously sized and includes a sparkling swimming pool, completing the outdoor space.

On the ground floor, you will find three bedrooms and three bathrooms. The primary suite serves as a true retreat, featuring a walk-in dressing area and a modern full bathroom. This bedroom opens up to views of the garden.

The second and third bedrooms both equally spacious share the second full bathroom of the home.

Additionally, a third bathroom with a shower functions as a guest bathroom and serves the fourth bedroom. The fourth bedroom is located upstairs, offering plenty of privacy and the flexibility to be used as a home office.
