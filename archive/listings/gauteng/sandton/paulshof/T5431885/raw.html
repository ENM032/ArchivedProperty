Sophisticated Living Meets Effortless Entertaining in Paulshof

Step into a beautifully renovated sanctuary where elegance, comfort, and modern convenience blend seamlessly. Nestled in the heart of Paulshof, this exceptional 3-bedroom residence offers a refined lifestyle designed for both relaxed family living and stylish entertaining.

From the moment you enter, youre welcomed by expansive, light-filled interiors, enhanced by stunning chandelier light fittings and sleek finishes throughout. The open-plan living spaces flow effortlessly into a designer kitchen, complete with granite countertops, ample cabinetry, and a layout that invites connection.

The home boasts three generously sized bedrooms, all featuring built-in cupboards, with two bedrooms offering direct access to the lush, manicured gardencreating a tranquil indoor-outdoor experience. The main suite is a private retreat, featuring a luxurious en-suite bathroom with a jacuzzi bath, perfect for unwinding in style.

Designed with entertaining in mind, the covered patio showcases skylights and aluminium stacking doors, opening onto a beautifully landscaped garden and sparkling swimming pool. An indoor braai area and cozy fireplace ensure year-round enjoyment, no matter the season.

- Double garage with ample additional parking

- Solar system and inverter for uninterrupted living

- A seamless blend of laminate and tiled flooring throughout
