Entertainers Delight A Unique Home That Has It All!

IMMACULATE HOME! IN A SECURE COMPLEX - PAXOS PLACE!

Serious Seller! Open to Offers!

Situated in a secure and sought-after complex next to St Peters Prep, this stunning family home offers the perfect blend of modern comfort, luxury finishes, and exceptional entertainment space.

Set on 1075sqm of land with 294sqm under roof, this immaculate property welcomes you through grand Meranti double doors into a spacious, light-filled open-plan living area featuring 3m high ceilings, elegant porcelain tiles, and white blinds throughout.

The open layout includes a lounge, dining area, and study, complemented by a fully fitted bar, air conditioning, and ceiling fans ideal for relaxed family living or hosting guests in style.

The modern kitchen is a chefs dream, boasting a glass-top extended stone island, ample cupboard space, and a separate scullery.

Accommodation includes 3 spacious bedrooms:

The main bedroom offers a luxurious en-suite bathroom.

The second and third bedrooms share a Jack & Jill bathroom.

Step through the double aluminium stacking doors to the expansive entertainment patio, where tranquility meets luxury. Enjoy a beautifully landscaped garden, heated swimming pool, built-in braai, and an outdoor stone-top bar setup. Gather around the boma with pizza oven for unforgettable evenings under the stars. Pergola foundation already in place for future extension.

Additional features include:

5KW Sunsynk Solar Inverter with 5KW Battery

JoJo tank with pumped irrigation system

Two Wendy Houses for extra storage

Extended, tiled double garage with laundry area and storage

Motorised front gate and garage doors

WiFi alarm system and ADT surveillance for peace of mind

This home is immaculate and move-in ready nothing to do but unpack and enjoy!

A true entertainers paradise offering lifestyle, comfort, and security in one perfect package.
