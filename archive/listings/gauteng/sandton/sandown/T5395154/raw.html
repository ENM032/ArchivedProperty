Three bedroom family home for sale in Sandown

Welcome to a home that combines thoughtful design, generous proportions, and an inviting indoor-outdoor lifestyle.

A charming approach leads you across composite decking to the front entrance, where a discreet side gate provides access to a storeroom, as well as the side passages and courtyards that run along the home.

Stepping inside, you are welcomed into a striking double-volume dining area that flows seamlessly into the open-plan kitchen. Tastefully renovated, the kitchen is finished with grey granite countertops and crisp white cabinetry, offering both style and functionality. From the main kitchen, you move through to a generous prep area, which serves as a practical extension of the space. From here, there is access to a large walk-in pantry and a separate, laundry room, while a door opens out to the external courtyard, staff accommodation and a second storeroom.

A few steps down from the dining room, the spacious sunken lounge creates a warm and comfortable living space. Large doors open onto a covered patio, inviting you out to an established private garden and swimming pool, perfect for relaxed entertaining. To one side, a sizeable study, work-from-home space, additional lounge or playroom offers versatility, complete with a unique subterranean storage room or wine cellar, and its own access to the covered patio.

The lower level is completed by a luxurious main bedroom suite. Finished with rich wooden flooring, air-conditioning, and direct access to the garden and pool area with its own private patio, this serene retreat is both private and inviting. The bedroom offers a spacious walk-in closet and dressing area, while a separate en-suite bathroom has been beautifully finished in classic black and white, featuring a large bath, walk-in shower, and double vanity.

A feature wooden staircase, positioned between the dining and lounge areas, leads to the upper level where two well-proportioned bedrooms await. Both rooms offer built-in cupboards, wooden floors, and doors opening onto a shared balcony overlooking the garden below.

Additional features include a double garage with built-in storage, as well as two covered parking bays secured behind automated doors, comfortably accommodating up to four vehicles.

Set within a secure estate with a guard and strict access control, the home enjoys a convenient location with easy access to the Sandton CBD, major transport routes, leading schools, places of worship, and a wide selection of Johannesburgs finest shopping and dining destinations.
