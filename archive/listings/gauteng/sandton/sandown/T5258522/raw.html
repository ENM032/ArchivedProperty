Modern Sandown Home: Open-Plan Living, Style and Comfort

SHOW DAY - By appointment only!

Right from the curb, you''ll notice the modern black metal gate and the unique gravel and paver driveway, giving this home a welcoming feel. The white picket fence and lush hedging add to its charm, creating a great first impression.

Step inside to discover a spacious, open-plan living and dining area that just feels airy thanks to those high vaulted ceilings. Its a perfect spot for relaxing or entertaining, complete with a stylish glass-railed staircase that adds a touch of modern flair. The kitchen is a real treat, boasting glossy white cabinets, sleek dark countertops, and a vibrant green subway tile backsplash. You''ll find integrated appliances, plenty of storage, and a dedicated laundry area, making daily chores a breeze. With a separate familyTV room, there''s space for everyone to unwind.

The home offers four comfy bedrooms and three and a half bathrooms, including two ensuites and a convenient guest toilet. Outside, you can enjoy your private patio and the well-maintained garden, perfect for those sunny South African days. There''s a double garage and two additional parking spaces, so parking is never an issue. Plus, your furry friends are welcome here! A self contained flatlet adds to the privacy for guests or staff, with an option for discerning entrepreneurs looking for a BnB opportunity, the space and location positions this home well for both family living or a BnB this is a must see.

Security is well taken care of, with features like an access gate, electric fencing, an alarm system, CCTV, burglar bars, and a security gate, giving you peace of mind. And for staying connected, the home is fibre-ready. This suburban gem in Sandown offers a fantastic lifestyle in a secure community, close to all the amenities Sandton has to offer.
