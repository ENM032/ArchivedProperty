Well-maintained three-bedroom simplex featuring a heated jacuzzi in Strathavon

Designed for effortless, single-level living, this charming simplex is the perfect fit for young families, busy professionals, or those looking to downsize without compromise. Offering a seamless blend of comfort, security, and practicality, it''s a home where everyday living feels easy and inviting.

Set within a sought-after Strathavon neighbourhood with 24-hour CAP patrols, the home offers added peace of mind alongside a strong sense of community.

Step inside to light-filled, open-plan interiors that flow beautifully to the outdoor spaces, creating a warm and welcoming environment ideal for both relaxed living and entertaining

The spacious lounge and dining areas are designed for comfort, featuring a wood-burning fireplace, coal stove, and air-conditioning, perfect for every season. These living spaces overlook a neat, low-maintenance garden complete with a private heated jacuzzi, offering a tranquil retreat for sunny afternoons and quiet evenings.

At the heart of the home, the modern kitchen combines style and functionality, fitted with a four-burner gas stove, double-door fridge, space for under-counter appliances, and ample cabinetry.

All three bedrooms are generously proportioned and bathed in natural light and ample cabinetry. The main suite boasts a full en-suite bathroom, while the two remaining bedrooms are served additional full bathroom.
