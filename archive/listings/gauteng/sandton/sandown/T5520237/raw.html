3 Bedroom House for Sale in Sandown

Sophisticated Cluster Home in Secure Sandown Complex Discover this sophisticated cluster home, ideally situated within a secure complex in the sought-after Sandown area of Sandton. This impressive residence offers three spacious bedrooms and three and a half modern bathrooms, with all three bedrooms benefiting from private en-suite facilities. Spanning 339 square meters of floor space on a 459 square metre erf, this property provides ample room for comfortable living.

The interior boasts an inviting open-plan design, seamlessly connecting the reception area, lounge, and dining room, creating an expansive and versatile living environment.

A dedicated family TV room offers additional space for relaxation. The contemporary kitchen is well-equipped with gas and electric hobs, double eye level oven, and includes a convenient pantry and scullery with space for 3 appliances. A charming gas fireplace adds warmth and character to the main living area.

Step outside to enjoy private outdoor spaces, featuring a balcony, patio, pool and deck. Small garden provides a tranquil retreat, complemented by a built-in braai on the patio.

The house features: a double garage, dedicated staff quarters, and modern additions such as a solar geyser and solar panels.

Ensuring peace of mind, the property is secured within a complex, further enhanced by electric fencing and an intercom system.

Located in Sandown, residents benefit from proximity to key amenities and transport links. With easy access to the highway, Sandton City, Grayston, Benmore and Morningside shopping centers, schools and shops.

Book a viewing to experience this remarkable property.
