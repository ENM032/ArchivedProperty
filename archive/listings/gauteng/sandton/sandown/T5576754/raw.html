4-Bed Spacious Townhouse bordering Sandton

- Within boomed area

Walk Through

Set in a small, exclusive complex, in the secure and private boomed-off part of Sandown, this beautiful freestanding townhouse is the perfect base for modern city living.

Distinguished by an exceptionally spacious open-plan lounge and dining area that opens out to a neat, sun-bathed garden creating the ideal setting for entertaining of relaxed family time.

The kitchen is fitted with granite counters and contrasting white cabinetry, an electric stove and oven with extractor, prep sink and large pantry cupboard. It accommodates a double door fridge.

The scullery area has a double sink and space for a dishwasher. Provision has been made in the garage for the laundry appliances.

The kitchen opens both to the garage as well as to the garden and laundry courtyard around the side of the house.

On the upper level are four bright bedrooms and three bathrooms, two ensuite.

The large, air-conditioned master bedroom opens out to a secluded balcony and enjoys a spacious dressing room and full ensuite bathroom.

The second bedroom is also exceptionally spacious and air-conditioned, enjoying an ensuite with shower. It has an additional, separate entrance to the outside. The room was enlarged by incorporating the staff room into it - this could be re-instated if necessary.

The other two bedrooms are both north facing with garden views and share a full bathroom.

The unit has a double automated garage with secure access directly into the home.

Ideal for a family or as an investment, this home is perfectly positioned to enjoy all that Sandton has to offer, including cafs, restaurants, shopping centres and a selection of premium schools.

Additional features:

- Pet friendly

- Borehole with filters supplying water to all units

A rare find, designed for a relaxed lifestyle in an intimate, well-managed complex, with easy access to major highways, top schools, Sandton CBD, and popular shopping destinations.
