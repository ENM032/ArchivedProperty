4 Bedroom House in Sandown

4 3 4 1 393 m² 440 m²

Modern Family Home with Pool, Garden, and Ample Living Space

Discover this modern two-story residence situated within a desirable gated community in Sandown, Sandton. The property immediately impresses with its contemporary architectural design, featuring a light-colored exterior and a neat tiled roof. A spacious paver driveway leads to a prominent covered entrance with elegant pillars and a large double garage, ensuring ample parking. Spanning 440 sqm, the interior offers generous living spaces designed for comfort and functionality, including three distinct lounges, a dedicated dining room, and a well-appointed kitchen.

This home provides four bedrooms and three bathrooms, including two ensuites and a convenient guest toilet, ensuring privacy and convenience for all residents. The expansive 1393 sqm erf is a true outdoor oasis, perfect for relaxation and entertaining. Central to the outdoor lifestyle is a large, sparkling swimming pool, complemented by a dedicated poolside gazebo with built-in seating ideal for al fresco dining or unwinding. Paved patios and walkways seamlessly connect the outdoor areas, surrounded by a well-maintained, landscaped garden featuring mature trees. An upper-level balcony offers elevated views, while a tennis court provides recreational opportunities.

This property is ideally located within the metropolitan hub of Sandton, offering convenient access to local amenities. The secure environment of a gated community, enhanced by an access gate, security gate, and intercom system, ensures a safe and private living experience. Domestic rooms, outside toilets, and the allowance for pets further enhance the appeal of this family-friendly residence.
