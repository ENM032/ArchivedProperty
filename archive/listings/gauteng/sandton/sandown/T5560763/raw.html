Stylish 3-Bedroom Cluster with Study, Pool & Staff Accommodation in the Heart of Sandown

Rarely do homes become available in this highly sought-after and secure complex in Sandown. Recently updated and competitively priced, this exceptional cluster offers comfortable family living in a prime location.

The spacious open-plan layout features a modern kitchen with granite countertops, ample cupboard space, and a separate drying yard. The kitchen flows effortlessly into the lounge and dining areas, which open onto a large covered patio overlooking the easy-to-maintain garden and sparkling swimming poolperfect for entertaining or relaxing.

Downstairs, you''ll find a versatile study that can easily serve as a work-from-home office or fourth bedroom, with direct access to the garden. A convenient guest toilet completes the lower level.

Upstairs boasts a generous pyjama lounge or playroom with stylish laminated flooring, creating an additional living space for the family. There are three well-proportioned bedrooms with built-in cupboards, a full family bathroom, and a linen cupboard in the passage. The spacious main bedroom opens onto a private balcony and features a full en-suite bathroom with both a bath and shower.

Additional features include:

- Secure complex with 24-hour guarded access

- Double automated garage with direct access into the home

- Staff accommodation

- Easy-to-maintain garden

- Sparkling swimming pool

- Excellent lock-up-and-go lifestyle

Ideally situated in the heart of Sandown, this home is close to excellent schools, shopping centres, gyms, major highways, the Sandton CBD, and Sandton City.

This is outstanding value in one of Sandown''s most desirable complexes. The seller is serious and motivated.
