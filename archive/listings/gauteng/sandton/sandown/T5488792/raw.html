3 Bedroom House in Sandown

Contact agent for street address
3 3.5 2 241 m²

Modern, secure cluster in the heart of Sandown offering stylish, low-maintenance family living!

This well-designed double-storey home features 3 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, open-plan reception areas, and a modern island kitchen with built-in stove, oven, and space for two appliances.

The living areas flow seamlessly onto a private patio and braai area, ideal for relaxed entertaining and family living.

Additional features include the double automated garage, on-site security, back-up power system, excellent natural light, and move-in-ready condition.

Conveniently located close to the Sandton CBD, major business routes, shopping centres, and sought-after schools including Redhill School and Crawford International Sandton College.

A superb opportunity for young families, professionals, or buyers looking for secure, modern living in Sandown.
