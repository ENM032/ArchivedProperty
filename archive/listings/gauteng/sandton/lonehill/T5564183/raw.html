Contemporary Estate Living at Its Finest

Welcome to a home where sophisticated design, generous proportions, and effortless indoor-outdoor living come together in one of the area''s most desirable secure estates.

From the moment you arrive, this beautifully maintained cluster exudes warmth, style, and modern elegance.

Upstairs, the luxurious main suite is your own private sanctuary. Wake up to peaceful views from your private balcony overlooking the manicured garden, while a walk-in dressing room and an elegant full en-suite bathroom complete this exceptional retreat.

A spacious pyjama lounge creates the perfect space for family movie nights, a children''s retreat, or a quiet reading corner. Two further generously proportioned bedrooms with built-in cupboards are serviced by a beautifully appointed full family bathroom.

Designed for both entertaining and everyday family life, the lower level offers flowing open living spaces filled with natural light. A welcoming formal lounge, elegant dining room, guest cloakroom, and expansive family room seamlessly connect to a large covered patio.

Step outside and discover your own private oasis. The sparkling swimming pool, lush irrigated garden, and generous entertainment patio create the perfect setting for relaxed weekends, family gatherings, or summer evenings with friends.

The beautifully renovated kitchen is every home chef''s dream, combining contemporary finishes with practical functionality. Complete with a gas hob, double oven, and separate scullery, it effortlessly caters to both busy family life and entertaining on a grand scale.

Adding exceptional value, the home is fitted with a solar power system and inverter, providing energy efficiency, lower running costs, and reliable backup power when you need it most.

Offering timeless style, exceptional comfort, and the security of estate living, this outstanding residence is more than just a homeit''s a lifestyle waiting to be enjoyed.
