Now You Can Have It All

Yes, you can have it all... Handsome Architecture, borehole and back-up water system, state of the art solar solution, aluminum window frames, exceptional security, contemporary finishes and fittings... and every bedroom complimented by a private en-suite bathroom... this expansive home exceeds all your expectations! Time to enjoy your independence!

Impressive entrance welcomes family and friends to your new home. Here youll discover spacious free flowing living areas comprising elegant lounge, dining room and casual tv-room. The gorgeous free-standing furnace fireplace will definitely keep the winter chills away. Dream fulfilling open plan kitchen with gorgeous counter tops, quality appliances and separate laundary with plenty of appliance space. A private home office completes the ground floor accommodation.

The upstairs comprises 3 cavernous bedrooms complete with 3 beautifully appointed en suite bathrooms. The main en-suite is a delight to the senses - the perfect retreat.

Stacking doors from the lving areas help bring the outdoors indoors and create the perfect for entertaining areas. Theres the expansive wrap around covered patio and bubbling jacuzzi. While the pristine cool blue pool in a manicured garden setting is looking forward to seeing you this summer!

Double garage, staff accommodation, plenty of parking. All perfectly positioned in a secluded part of Lonehill.
