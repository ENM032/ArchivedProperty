4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 3.5 7 1 627 m² 425 m²

S - T - R - E - T - C - H - O - U - T!

Theres room for everyone and everything... this expansive family home deserves to be on your must see list! Master built and lovingly maintained, this home has enjoyed an extreme makeover and features contemporary finishes throughout.

Step inside and be wowed by the feature double volume entrance hall. The accommodation available is impressive and includes Private Lounge and elegant dining room. Gourmet eat-in kitchen cupboards galore, pantry and separate scullery with plenty of appliance space and vast open plan entertainment area that includes tv room with soaring ceilings and fireplace, enclosed patio with built in braai and stacking doors that open onto the outdoor areas.

Guest cloakroom, private study and guest bedroom with with private patio and en-suite bathroom completes the downstairs accommodation.

Upstairs comprises 3 huge bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The main bedroom is en suite and offers cupboards galore and private study area. There are splendid views on offer from the large balcony and a staircase leads you directly to the private park-like garden setting.

Outbuildings include: Triple garage with direct access. Large domestic accommodation, storeroom and plenty of off-street parking.

The monthly running costs: Rates R2 545, Sewerage R1 213, Refuse R548.
