Welcome to a home that effortlessly blends elegance, comfort, and everyday practicality in the sought-after suburb of Beverley. From the moment you step through the welcoming entrance hall, youll feel the warmth and charm that make this property truly special.

Designed for both relaxed family living and stylish entertaining, the home offers a choice of beautifully appointed living spaces, including a formal lounge, dining room, family room, and a private study perfect for working from home or it can be easily converted to a 4th bedroom. A gas fireplace inside the home adds a touch of warmth and ambience on cooler evenings.

The heart of the home is the spacious kitchen, complete with a gas hob, separate scullery, pantry, and additional storeroom, all thoughtfully designed for modern living.

Upstairs, you''ll find 3 generously sized bedrooms, each opening onto its own private balcony, perfect for enjoying a quiet morning coffee or unwinding at the end of the day. Two beautifully appointed bathrooms serve the bedrooms, while a guest cloakroom downstairs ensures convenience for family and guests alike.

Deluxe staff accommodation adds extra flexibility and convenience.

Step outside and discover an entertainers dream: an enclosed patio with a built-in gas braai, a decked outdoor entertainment area, and a pool that invites year-round enjoyment.

Additional features include 3 garages, auto irrigation, fibre connectivity, and a 5kVA inverter for added peace of mind.

As an exceptional bonus - Option to purchase or include all furniture, art and curtains, allowing you to move in and start enjoying this beautiful home from day one.

Set in the prestigious and tranquil suburb of Beverley, this property offers a lifestyle of comfort, convenience, and understated luxury.
