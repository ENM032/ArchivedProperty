5 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
5 5 6 1 260 m²

Elegant Single Storey Family Home with Cottage and Loft

Sole Mandate.

Tucked away in a tranquil, tree-lined boomed enclave, this charming single-storey home captures the essence of comfortable family living. Warm, welcoming, and beautifully maintained, it offers the perfect blend of elegance, practicality, and versatility an inviting space where family memories are made, and everyday living feels effortless.

From the moment you enter, youre greeted by a sense of calm and homeliness. The spacious formal lounge and dining room, both featuring classic Morso fireplaces, set the tone for cosy family evenings and intimate gatherings. The TV lounge flows seamlessly out to a covered entertainment patio overlooking the sparkling pool and lush garden a peaceful retreat ideal for sunny afternoons and weekend get-togethers.

The heart of the home is its beautifully appointed kitchen, complete with both gas and electric hobs, a scullery, and a separate laundry room. Its the perfect space for cooking, entertaining, and connecting with loved ones. The master suite offers a private sanctuary with ample cupboard space and an elegant en-suite bathroom, while two additional bedrooms share a stylish full bathroom. A guest cloakroom with a shower adds a thoughtful touch of convenience.

Adding incredible value is the self-contained cottage featuring two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a modern kitchen and dining area, and its own patio. Perfect for extended family, guests, or as an income-generating opportunity, it offers comfort and independence in equal measure.

The property also includes solar panels with a Sunsynk inverter, JoJo tanks with the option to drill a borehole, staff accommodation, and an extra-length double garage with additional secure parking along the side. A versatile loft space provides endless potential ideal as a home office, studio, or playroom.

This is more than just a home its a lifestyle. Peaceful, secure, and full of character, it offers the perfect setting for a growing family or anyone seeking a home that combines warmth, beauty, and thoughtful design in a sought-after location
