Beautifully renovated

This beautifully renovated family home in one of Lonehill''s most prestigious complexes, Lanzerac, offers the ultimate blend of modern sustainable living and timeless elegance.

With every detail meticulously considered, this home offers effortless living from day one, simply unpack, relax, and start making wonderful new memories.

Cook up a storm on your gas hob while seamlessly entertaining family and guests in the open plan dining area, perfectly complemented by a separate, spacious scullery and a pantry. The layout offers incredible versatility: use the extra living space as a vibrant family room or effortlessly transition it into a spacious 4th bedroom for guests, or a work from home office.

A separate staff accommodation could also be utilised as a guest suite or a work from home space.

Outside, summer entertaining is a dream on the covered patio with its classic built-in braai, while the kids and pets play safely in the beautifully landscaped garden.

Maintaining this lush backdrop is incredibly simple with a manual irrigation system giving you the flexibility to water only when convenient and let nature take over on rainy days for vibrant, year-round greenery.

You will never sit in the dark or lose your fibre internet connection again, thanks to a fully installed 8-panel solar and inverter system. Featuring a double garage, and a double carport, and additional outside ablution facilities, this is secure, premium northern suburbs living at its absolute finest.
