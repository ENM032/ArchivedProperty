Sophisticated Modern Living!

Immerse yourself in the epitome of Lonehill living. This stunning 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home is a masterclass in luxury and practicality, set within a prestigious and secure development that''s perfect for families and entertainers alike.

Downstairs, airy open-plan living areas flow seamlessly from the elegant dining room to the lounge, where air conditioning ensures comfort all year round. The show-stopping designer kitchen features granite countertops, a separate scullery, and is perfectly positioned for effortless entertaining. A guest bathroom adds convenience, while sliding doors open onto a covered patio overlooking a lush, well-maintained garden ideal for relaxed, alfresco living.

Upstairs, four versatile bedrooms offer balcony access and endless possibilities. The indulgent main suite is a retreat in itself, boasting a walk-in closet and luxurious en-suite bathroom. Two additional bedrooms are en-suite with fitted cupboards, while the fourth bedroom can easily be converted into a home office, study, or pyjama lounge.

Move-in ready, secure, and beautifully finished, this home ticks all the boxes for discerning buyers seeking comfort, elegance, and peace of mind. And with its prime location, you''re spoiled for choice with Lonehill''s village atmosphere, exceptional security, and beautifully maintained amenities. Enjoy easy access to Lonehill Shopping Centre, Virgin Active Gym, top restaurants, and excellent schools including Crawford Prep and College, Lonehill International Academy, and various nursery schools.

With its strong investment growth, secure lifestyle, and outstanding family living, Lonehill is the ultimate destination for those seeking the best of Johannesburg''s North.
