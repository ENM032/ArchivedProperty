4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 2.5 2 1 144 m² 310 m²

Your Family Is All That Is Needed to Fill This Fabulous Find

More than just a house... This is home!

Dont procrastinate - this delightful family home is here today but will be sold tomorrow! Immaculately maintained and with added benefit of four bedrooms - this one deserves to go top of the best seller list!

The inviting entrance hall leads to a spacious lounge and elegant dining room. Super open plan kitchen with gorgeous counter tops, separate scullery and breakfast bar open plan to family room. 4 extra spacious bedrooms, study, 2 full bathrooms main en suite and separate toilet.

Covered patio overlooks the cool blue pool and expansive paved entertaining area. Double garage and staff accommodation. Excellent driveway space. Fibre to the home. Easy maintenance facebrick and a solar solution.

All this perfectly positioned on one Lonehills favourite access controlled streets. Just a hop skip and jump from quality nursery schools and Crawford College.

Monthly running costs: Street Levy R1 500, Rates R2 307, Refuse R583, Sewerage R1 346.

Lonehill... the original village in a garden. Here residents get to enjoy an unrivaled security solution, quality educational facilities, convenient shopping centers and picturesque parks all packaged in a pristine house proud environment. If you are looking at a property investment in the Northern suburbs of Sandton... take the time to discover Lonehill.
