4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

6 lewis avenue
4 3.5 2 1 500 m²

Timeless Elegance Meets Modern Comfort

Set on a generous 1,500sqm stand, this classic contemporary home offers space, style, and exceptional indoor-outdoor living. The open-plan kitchen, complete with a large separate scullery, flows effortlessly into the sun-drenched lounge and out onto a covered patio overlooking the sparkling pool perfect for year-round entertaining.

The expansive formal lounge features a cozy fireplace and stacking doors that open to the beautifully maintained garden, creating a seamless connection with nature. Two dedicated studies provide the ideal work-from-home setup, while a guest cloakroom adds convenience for visitors.

The home boasts four well-appointed bedrooms with vinyl flooring, built-in cupboards, and air-conditioning. The main bedroom enjoys a luxurious full en-suite bathroom, complemented by two additional stylish bathrooms. Thoughtful extras include ample storage throughout, full staff accommodation, and a double garage.

Practicality meets sustainability with a full solar system and backup JOJO water tanks ensuring comfort and efficiency in all seasons.

Dont miss this opportunity to own a spacious, secure, and beautifully maintained home that truly has it all.
