Distinguished Lonehill Family Home with Integrated Business Flatlet and Pool

Distinguished Family Residence with Integrated Business Opportunity in Prestigious Lonehill

This exceptional property represents a sophisticated residential sanctuary combined with commercial potential, situated within the exclusive gated community of Lonehill, Sandton. The residence comprises three generously proportioned bedrooms, three well-appointed bathrooms, and a dedicated flatlet, offering versatile accommodation for both family living and entrepreneurial endeavors. The main dwelling encompasses 359 square meters of meticulously designed living space, whilst the expansive 1,963 square meter erf provides substantial grounds for both residential enjoyment and business operations.

The residence features an open-plan living configuration that facilitates seamless entertaining and family interaction. Two fully equipped kitchens accommodate diverse culinary requirements, whilst the formal dining room and lounge provide distinguished spaces for social gatherings. The property incorporates an ensuite bedroom, ensuring privacy and convenience for principal occupants. Outdoor amenities include a swimming pool, patio area, and built-in braai facility, creating an elegant entertainment environment. A dedicated laundry room and storage facilities enhance practical functionality.

Situated within secure gated community with suburban convenience

Security provisions are comprehensive, incorporating 24-hour security monitoring, electric fencing, intercom systems, and access gates, ensuring absolute peace of mind. The property benefits from fibre connectivity, rendering it exceptionally suitable for home-based business operations. Located in the prestigious Lonehill suburb, this residence offers the distinguished combination of secure family living with genuine commercial opportunity.
