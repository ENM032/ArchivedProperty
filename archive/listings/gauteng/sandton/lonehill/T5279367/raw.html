Spacious 5-Bedroom Family Home in Lonehill

This lovely 5-bedroom home with servants'' quarters is set on a large corner stand in one of Lonehills most sought-after boomed-off areas, offering both space and security for the whole family.

As you enter, a generous dining area welcomes you on the left, comfortably accommodating an eight-seater table, while the formal lounge sits to the right. Flowing from there, the spacious family room opens onto the lovely back garden, where a sparkling pool and established trees create a peaceful outdoor setting.

The kitchen is well laid out, with space for three under-counter appliances, a large double-door fridge, a gas hob, and an eye-level double oven. Just off the kitchen, the expansive covered patio overlooks the beautiful garden perfect for relaxed gatherings or quiet mornings with a cup of coffee.

From the kitchen, youll find a flatlet complete with a lounge area that opens onto the garden, a separate bedroom, and an en-suite bathroom with a shower ideal for a teenager, extended family, or even as a home office. Downstairs also includes a guest toilet with handy storage space beneath the stairs.

Upstairs are four spacious bedrooms and three bathrooms. The main bedroom features an en-suite with a double vanity and shower, while the other bathrooms include one full and one with a shower only. Two sets of linen cupboards upstairs provide excellent additional storage.

With ample parking, a double garage, and a layout designed for comfortable family living, this home has everything you need. With a few personal touches and upgrades, it will become your perfect family haven.

Lonehill remains one of Johannesburgs most desirable suburbs, known for its strong community spirit, secure environment, and proximity to top schools, shopping centres, and parks.
