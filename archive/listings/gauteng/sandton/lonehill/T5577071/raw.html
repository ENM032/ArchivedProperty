4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

17 monte solo, 17 eston road
4 4 8 473 m²

Move in Ready Four Bedroom Four Bathroom Estate Home in Lonehill. Welcome to a fully renovated residence delivering every feature you have been seeking.

This exceptional double-storey home is ideally situated within a secure and highly sought-after Estate in Lonehill. From the moment you arrive, the property conveys sophistication, beginning at the gated driveway leading to a triple garage with generous additional parking provided by the expansive driveway.

Upon entering, a welcoming entrance hall divides the living areas and includes a separate self-contained office. The office is air-conditioned and includes elegant built-in cupboards that provide substantial storage.

The first major living area is a generous dining room featuring additional built-in cupboard space located beneath the staircase. This dining area adopts an open-plan design that flows effortlessly into the kitchen and lounge. At the heart of these spaces sits a central air-conditioning unit and a wood-burning fireplace, ensuring year-round comfort regardless of the season.

The substantial granite kitchen includes a dedicated breakfast nook, a gas hob, and a discreet scullery finished to a high standard. The kitchen further provides extra seating and extensive built-in cupboard space for everyday convenience.

Adjacent to the lounge reception is an indoor braai room fitted with stacker doors. These doors create a seamless indoor-outdoor lifestyle while overlooking a sparkling blue swimming pool and manicured garden. This indoor braai room also offers the perfect location for an enclosed entertainment room courtesy of its versatile size.

A beautifully appointed guest bathroom enhances practicality on the ground floor. Completing the lower level of this impressive home is a spacious modern guest suite with its own en-suite bathroom.

Ascending the staircase, natural light floods the passage. On the second floor, two spacious secondary bedrooms share a well-appointed family bathroom. The main bedroom is exceptionally sized, bright and airy, and includes a luxurious en-suite bathroom with premium finishes.

The home incorporates valuable sustainable features, including a large 24Kw Solar Inverter system that generates sufficient power to operate the property off-grid, together with a backup water system with a 500L jo-jo tank equipped. Maintenance is minimal thanks to further notable specifications such as aluminium doors and windows throughout, American shutter blinds, and a low-maintenance landscaped garden. Additional benefits include self-sufficient solar lighting and a solar-heated pool.

Lonehill offers a strong community spirit and residents who value the area, complemented by abundant natural greenery at the well-known Lonehill Park. The location provides convenient access to major roads and a wide selection of shopping centres and restaurant options.

Do not miss this immaculate home. Contact us today to arrange a private viewing of the property.
