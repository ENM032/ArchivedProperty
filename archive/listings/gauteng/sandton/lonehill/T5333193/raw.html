3 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
3 3 4 405 m² 220 m²

Welcome to luxurious estate living in one of Lonehill Easts most desirable boutique estates. This beautifully maintained home offers the perfect balance of comfort, style, and modern convenience ideal for first time home owners or young families looking for a secure and tranquil lifestyle.

Spacious Open-Plan Living

The home welcomes you with a light-filled open-plan layout that seamlessly connects the lounge, dining area, and modern kitchen. The kitchen features sleek granite countertops and a stylish built-in bar, creating the perfect space for both everyday living and effortless entertaining.

Enclosed All-Season Patio

A generously sized enclosed patio with stack doors and a built-in braai and or fire place extends the living space beautifully. Overlooking a manicured private garden, this versatile area can be enjoyed throughout the year and is perfect for hosting friends and family.

Downstairs Accommodation

The lower level offers:

Two well-sized bedrooms and two bathrooms, One bedroom is en-suite.

A private study, ideal for working from home

A convenient storage room beneath the staircase

Upstairs Private Retreat

Upstairs, the impressive main bedroom provides a peaceful sanctuary designed for comfort and privacy. This luxurious space features:

A sunlit enclosed patio

A spacious dressing room

A beautifully appointed full en-suite bathroom with double vanities

Additional Features

Inverter system ensuring uninterrupted power

Double automated garage

Beautifully maintained communal areas with a tranquil koi pond

Secure, family-friendly estate living

Prime Location

Ideally located close to top schools such as St Peters College and Crawford International Lonehill, with convenient access to shopping centres, parks, and major transport routes.

Your Ideal Family Home

Move-in ready and impeccably maintained, this exceptional property offers stylish, comfortable living within a truly charming estate.
