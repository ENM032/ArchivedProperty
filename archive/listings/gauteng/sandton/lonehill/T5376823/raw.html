Lonehill Luxury Living : 5 Bedroom Oasis with heated pool & ultimate security

Expansive double-story gem in Lonehill, a premier northern suburb known for scenic parks and nature. State-of-the-art CCTV, alarm, and four aircons main, 2 beds, gym. Upstairs: a spacious master suite with walk-through dresser and stunning en-suite. Downstairs: two double bedrooms with fans, a full bathroom, plus a versatile home office suite.
