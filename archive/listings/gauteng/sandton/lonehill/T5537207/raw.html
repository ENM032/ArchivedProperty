This Deserves To Go Straight To The Top Of Your 'Must See - Must Own' List!

You cant help but be impressed by our newest offering! This Mediterranean inspired family home is bound to capture your interest.

An inviting entrance looks forward to welcoming your family and friends to your new home. Discover free flowing living areas comprising extra spacious lounge, dining room and tv-room. The gorgeous free-standing furnace fireplace will definitely keep the winter chills away.

Fabulous kitchen that really is better than great! Gorgeous counter tops, bespoke cabinetry, quality appliances and space for double door fridge. Separate laundry provides space for dishwasher, washing machine and dryer.

The downstairs bedroom wing comprises 3 extra spacious bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms including guest bedroom with en-suite shower room. Upstairs the cavernous main bedroom enjoys a private en-suite bathroom and oodles of built in cupboard space. It really is the perfect private sanctuary.

Perfect for entertaining the expansive covered patio overlooks the cool blue pool and established garden setting.

The list of outbuildings and extras is extensive. Double garage, staff accommodation, double carport and large garden shed. Victron inverter and lithium batteries and much more. All this perfectly positioned in one of the neighbourhoods prime street enclosures.

The running costs: Rates R2 450, Refuse R548, Sewerage R1 213. Levy R1 250

Here residents get to enjoy an unrivalled security solution, quality educational facilities, convenient shopping centres and picturesque parks all packaged in a pristine house-proud environment.
