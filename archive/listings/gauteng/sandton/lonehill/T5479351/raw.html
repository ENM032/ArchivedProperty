4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 3 2 458 m²

4 bedroom, 3 bathroom modern family home

Beautifully renovated and designed with modern family living in mind, this exceptional home seamlessly combines style, comfort, and functionality. Situated in one of Lonehills most sought-after estates, Lanzerac, renowned for its exceptional security and strong community feel, this home offers the perfect balance between lifestyle and peace of mind.

A beautifully landscaped garden welcomes you with ample space to add a splash pool, while the covered patio with built-in braai creates the perfect setting for effortless entertaining. Double doors lead into a spacious family room that also opens out to the back garden, enhancing the indoor-outdoor flow.

The open-plan dining area and stunning modern kitchen form the heart of the home. Thoughtfully upgraded, the kitchen offers a gas stove and oven, space for three appliances, a double-door fridge, and a central island that doubles as a breakfast nook.

The home is cleverly designed with two separate accommodation wings. One section offers two bedrooms and a study, serviced by a stylish full bathroom, while the main bedroom enjoys a modern en-suite bathroom with shower and direct access to the garden.

A separate wing features an additional two generously sized bedrooms sharing a sleek bathroom with shower, as well as a private study nook ideal for extended family, teenagers, or work-from-home flexibility.

Additional features include a secure tandem carport and an off-the-grid electrical system complete with solar panels, offering both convenience and energy efficiency.

Lonehill... known for its village feel, is a highly sought after suburb in the northern suburbs of Sandton. Its state of the art security, thanks to the Lonehill Residents Association is unsurpassed. The LRA is also responsible for the upkeep of the beautiful Lonehill Park and dam, other childrens parks, pavements and walkways. The Lonehill Shopping Centre offers a total shopping experience, a Virgin Active Gym and a range of top class restaurants. Schools in this upmarket suburb include Crawford Prep and College, Lonehill International Academy, numerous Nursery schools and Creches.

Close to Monte Casino and the new Fourways Precinct, Lonehill is perfectly positioned in the Northern suburbs for investment growth, a secure lifestyle and family living.

Experience the Lonehill lifestyle!
