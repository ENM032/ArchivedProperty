4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 3 1 173 m²

Sole mandate !

Position and location in one of Lonehills best boomed enclosue- this solid double-storey home is close to all amenities in and around lovely Lonehill.

Walk to school and unwind at home. The welcoming entrance flows onto an open plan bright and light formal lounge, fitted pub, dining area plus enclosed patio with a wood burning braai - these spaces open onto the wrap-around garden and sparkling swimming pool. The open-plan dining area is situated adjacent to the kitchen making dinner parties and family gatherings a breeze. Massive kitchen with granite countertops, breakfast nook, prep bowl, huge walk-in pantry, gas hob with electric oven plus extractor fan, double door fridge space, separate scullery area that can accommodate all your appliances, plus ample built-in cupboards. The scullery area opens onto a courtyard, a perfect spot for your homegrown fruit and veggies. The downstairs floor plan is complete with a family lounge, guest bedroom wing and bathroom plus a study or 5th bedroom option.

The upstairs floor plan boasts 3 sun-soaked bedrooms and 3 bathrooms MES. The master bedroom is a sizable space boasting a dressing area and a full bathroom with his and hers vanities. The master bedroom opens up onto a beautiful balcony, overlooking the treetop views of the established garden below.

This family treasure trove offers the following: Triple automated garages, ample parking space for your visitors, staff accommodation, Wendy house plus the home is fibre ready.

The established garden is set on a 1173m2 and is your own little piece of paradise, boasting plenty of space for your children and furry friends.
