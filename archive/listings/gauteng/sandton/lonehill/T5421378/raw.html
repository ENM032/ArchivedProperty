Welcome to this Cape Dutch style home that exudes elegance, warmth, and timeless charm. From the moment you step into the entrance hall, you feel embraced by light-filled spaces and graceful design. The lounge and dining room flow effortlessly, creating a perfect setting for family moments or entertaining friends.

The kitchen is a chefs delight, featuring a 6-burner SMEG hob, elegant finishes, and a scullery. Upstairs, the pyjama lounge offers a cozy retreat and a perfect space to unwind, read, or enjoy quiet family time.

This home offers four spacious bedrooms and four beautifully appointed bathrooms, including a luxurious en-suite. The main bedroom opens onto a sunlit balcony, inviting the outdoors in. A guest cloakroom adds convenience for visitors, while a work from home provides a serene workspace.

Step outside to the covered patio, where you can relax while overlooking the sparkling pool, enclosed within charming picket fencing. The landscaped garden is inviting and effortlessly maintained with an automatic irrigation system.

Additional features include staff accommodation, a loft , air-conditioning, a fireplace, electric fence, and generous living spaces throughout. Practicality meets luxury with a double auto garage, a home office above the garage, and a double carport. Energy-conscious living is supported by a 5kVA inverter with 8 panels and 1 battery.

Filled with natural light, clean lines, and an airy, open feel, this home is not just a residence, its a sanctuary. Every detail, from the laminate floors to the elegant bathrooms, invites comfort and a sense of belonging. Here, youll find a warm, welcoming haven where style, elegance, and family life beautifully converge.
