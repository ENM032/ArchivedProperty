REFINED LUXURY LIVING IN LONEHILL WITH EXCEPTIONAL SECURITY AND SUPERB ENTERTAINING APPEAL.

This exceptional residence is perfectly positioned in the heart of Lonehill, offering a rare combination of luxury, space, and top-tier security. Enjoy complete peace of mind with 24-hour security patrols throughout the area, ensuring a safe and tranquil lifestyle.

A true standout in the neighbourhood, this magnificent home is among the largest and most impressive properties available. It features a triple automated garage and extensive additional parking, comfortably accommodating up to 16 vehicles ideal for entertainers and large families.

Designed with both comfort and sophistication in mind, the home offers five generously sized bedrooms upstairs, along with a sixth bedroom on the ground floor, perfect for guests or multigenerational living.

The expansive living areas include three elegant lounges, seamlessly blending style and functionality, complemented by a dedicated bar area perfect for hosting and entertaining in style.

Two spacious, well appointed kitchens provide ample cupboard space, generous countertops, a scullery, and a breakfast counter, making them both practical and inviting. High quality finishes flow throughout the home, with bright tiled flooring enhancing the living spaces, while laminate flooring adds warmth and elegance on the ground floor.

With approximately 750m2 under roof, this residence offers unparalleled space and comfort. The estimated rebuild value is in the region of R11 million, highlighting the outstanding value on offer.

Additional features include no monthly levies and municipal rates of approximately R3,000 per month. The property is ideally located close to a selection of prestigious private schools.

Complete with staff accommodation, this home truly ticks every box.

An extraordinary opportunity not to be missed, schedule your private viewing today.
