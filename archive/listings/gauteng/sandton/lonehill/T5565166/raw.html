Step into a home that perfectly blends timeless elegance, exceptional comfort, and effortless entertaining. Situated in one of Lonehill''s most sought-after and secure boomed road enclosure, this magnificent family home has been thoughtfully designed to accommodate every stage of family life while offering the luxury features buyers will appreciate.

A grand entrance hall welcomes you into a selection of beautifully appointed living spaces, including an elegant lounge, formal dining room, spacious family room, an enclosed patio with stacking doors, and a versatile study that can easily serve as a fifth bedroom. At the heart of the home lies a gourmet kitchen featuring three ovens, a built-in coffee machine, and a separate scullery with space for an additional fridge, perfect for busy family living and effortless entertaining.

The home offers four generously proportioned bedrooms, all fitted with air conditioning, complemented by two full bathrooms. The luxurious main en-suite boasts underfloor heating and heated towel rails, creating a private sanctuary of comfort. A guest cloakroom downstairs adds further convenience.

Designed with connection and entertaining in mind, enjoy memorable gatherings in the stylish bar area, relax beside the cosy gas fireplace, or open the stacking doors to reveal a spectacular covered patio overlooking the sparkling pool, tranquil koi pond, and beautifully established garden. An upstairs balcony provides the perfect spot to enjoy your morning coffee or unwind as the sun sets.

Practicality is equally impressive, with staff accommodation, a storeroom, two additional storage huts, and four fully tiled garages complete with extra storage space. A generator ensures uninterrupted comfort, while the alarm system and location within a boomed-off area provide exceptional peace of mind.

This is more than just a home, its a lifestyle of luxury, security, and sophisticated family living in the heart of prestigious Lonehill. The road enclosure has its own private park and play area.

Come home to where every detail has been designed to impress.
