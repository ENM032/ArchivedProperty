3 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
3 2 3 1 200 m² 306 m²

Sole mandate!

Low maintenance single story living in an outstanding location.

This beautifully presented home with flow being a key element boasts an inviting entrance which leads you onto a private formal lounge, opening up with double doors onto a section of the private garden. A spacious open plan dining and family lounge area with a feature wood burning fireplace, spills onto a fabulous covered entertainers patio, overlooking the sparkling solar heated swimming pool and manicured garden. Open concept kitchen with granite counter tops, gas hob with electric eye-level oven plus extractor, breakfast bar, prep bowl, separate scullery area that can accommodate all your appliances, double door fridge space, plus ample built-in cupboards. Fitted air-conditioned study work from home space is neatly tucked away in a quiet space.

3 Delightful sized bedrooms all have a leafy outlook of the garden plus 2 full bathrooms MES. The master bedroom is sun soaked and a luxurious space boasting big windows, allowing loads of natural light to stream in, it has a walk-in dressing area and a full en-suite bathroom.

The irrigated garden is meticulously cared for, and a great space for your children and furry friends to run around and enjoy the outdoor space this home has to offer.

This family treasure trove offers the following: Double automated garages with direct access into the scullery area, undercover caravan and trailer space plus ample parking for your visitors, partial air-conditioning, staff accommodation, solar geyser, Wendy house for extra storage, security alarm system and perimeter electric fencing, plus the home is fibre enabled.

Lonehill Shopping Centre is very easily accessible for all residents to benefit from showcasing numerous well-known anchor tenants such as Woolworths, Pick n Pay, Clicks as well as an elite Virgin Active gym. The Lonehill Residents Association takes great initiative in making sure to improve all aspects of their community. Lonehill environment is manned 247 with dedicated and well trained security encouraging residents to run, walk dogs and push prams. Come get involved in the strong family driven community that Lonehill has to offer!
