Immaculate, Low-Maintenance Home with Quality Finishes & Swimming Pool

SOLE MANDATE

Situated in Widcombe, a well-run, financially sound complex with 247 access control and monitored electric fencing around the perimeter, this beautifully maintained home offers the perfect balance of comfort, style, and security.

Step through a welcoming entrance hall into open-plan living areas, a spacious lounge and dining room opening onto a covered patio overlooking a sparkling solar-heated swimming pool and neat, low-maintenance garden. The pool has recently been fully renovated and fitted with a quiet, energy-efficient motor. A private, fully paved back courtyard adds extra outdoor space with minimal upkeep.

The kitchen is pristine, featuring quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and full floor-to-ceiling tiling. The separate scullery includes a pantry and quartz finishes, while the laundry room offers granite countertops for a practical and polished touch.

There are 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, including a main en-suite with doors leading directly to the garden. Both bathrooms are fully tiled to the ceiling, with quartz countertops in the main bathroom. Throughout the home, youll find quartz windowsills, decorative cornices, solid wooden skirtings, and block-out blinds and curtains where needed. These are details that reflect quality and care.

Additional features include tiled and laminate flooring throughout, underfloor heating, aluminium window and door frames, a double tiled garage, outside toilet, fibre-optic internet connection, DSTV satellite dish, and a three-phase power supply ensuring steady, efficient electricity. Convenient combined plug points 2x3-pin and 2x2-pin are installed throughout.

Pet-friendly and move-in ready, this home offers peace of mind, easy living, and timeless appeal within a secure, well-managed estate.

A meticulously maintained home designed for comfort, efficiency, and everyday ease.

Lonehill is an upmarket residential suburb located in the northern part of Johannesburg, adjacent to Fourways. Known for its well-maintained complexes and lush gardens, Lonehill offers a peaceful living environment that appeals to families, professionals, and retirees alike.

The suburb benefits from a strong and active residents association, which provides comprehensive security services. These include a dedicated control room, permanent armed response teams, and regular street patrols, ensuring the neighborhoods safety.

Lonehill is home to several prestigious schools, well regarded for their academic excellence, making it ideal for families with school-aged children.

Residents have easy access to a variety of shopping centers offering diverse retail, dining, and service options.

The area is well-served by nearby medical facilities.

Overall, Lonehill offers a tranquil and secure suburban lifestyle with convenient access to essential amenities, making it a highly desirable place to live in the Sandton area.
