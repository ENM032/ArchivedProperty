4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 4 6 1 650 m² 380 m²

Rarely does one discover a property overflowing with charm and character as this picture book Tudor inspired family home does.

Tucked away at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, the attractive entrance and driveway tells you that youve arrived at a truly special home.

From the picturesque entrance youll find a private study, intimate lounge, guest cloak room and elegant dining room with fireplace. The country inspired kitchen comes with plenty of cabinetry, gorgeous counter tops and a separate scullery. Theres an adjacent breakfast room with stacking doors that open onto the covered patio.

The family bedroom wing comprises 2 large double bedrooms and family bathroom. The main bedroom offers plenty of cupboard space and full bathroom en-suite.

The family sized living room opens onto the most special of outdoor living areas. Theres space for the 10 seater patio table and a casual seating area.

Upstairs: A private lounge opens onto the roof terrace affording spectacular horizon views. The guest bedroom enjoy an en-suite shower room.

The manicured parklike garden complete with colourful plantings, established trees and rolling lawn is worth the viewing on its own. Theres a custom designed vegetable garden to bring out the suburban farmer in you.

Double garage, loads of driveway space, domestic accommodation, irrigation, electric fence, state of the art security, FTTH and much more. 8kva Inverter with lithium battery rental through Gosolr at R2 950pm.

Set in the heart of Lonehill Notten Village is a secure road closure comprising 60 homes. The monthly running costs: Levy R1250, Rates R2 787, Sewerage R1 213, Refuse R548.

Lonehill... the original village in a garden. Here residents get to enjoy an unrivaled security solution, quality educational facilities, convenient shopping centres and picturesque parks all packaged in a pristine house proud environment. If you are looking at a property investment in the Northern suburbs of Sandton... take the time to discover Lonehill.
