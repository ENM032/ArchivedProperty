Stunning Riverfront Family Home in a Secure Estate

Experience exceptional family living in this beautifully positioned riverfront home, offering breath taking views and an enviable lifestyle within a secure estate. Set on a large stand, the property boasts an expansive, level lawnperfect for family football and cricket matches, outdoor entertaining, or simply enjoying the peaceful surroundings.

The ground floor features a well-appointed kitchen with a gas hob and electric oven, complemented by a separate scullery with space for three under-counter appliances. The spacious open-plan living and dining area flows seamlessly through stacking doors onto a covered patio, creating effortless indoor-outdoor living.

Designed for entertaining, the covered patio overlooks a sparkling swimming pool and features a built-in braai area. An additional separate lounge also opens onto the patio, providing versatile living spaces and excellent flow to the outdoor entertainment area.

Upstairs, the home offers three spacious en-suite bedrooms, all fitted with built-in cupboards and stylish laminate flooring. Two of the bedrooms share a balcony with spectacular views over the river, while a PJ lounge or home office provides additional space for relaxation, study, or remote working.

Additional features include a double automated garage, solar power system, and the peace of mind that comes with secure estate living.
