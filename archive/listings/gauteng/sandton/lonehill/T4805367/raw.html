4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address
4 3 9 1 870 m²

4 Bedroom home offering comfort and luxury.. This stunning 4 bedroom, 3 bathroom house is a perfect blend of modern elegance & comfortable living. Nestled in the serene Lonehill neighborhood, this property offers ample space for families or those who love to entertain. With its thoughtfully designed layout & high-end finishes, this home is sure to impress.

The four bedrooms are designed to provide tranquility & privacy. Each bedroom features large windows that allow natural light to flood in, creating a warm & inviting atmosphere. The main suite includes a his and hers walk in closets, a ceiling fan and air-conditioning an en-suite bathroom equipped with luxurious fixtures, a soaking jacuzzi bathtub, & a separate shower & loo. The additional three bedrooms are equally well-appointed, providing plenty of space for family members or guests. One of the additional bedrooms has a en-suite bathroom offering you a shower, single vanity and loo.

Two secondary bedrooms are serviced by a well appointed bathroom offering you a bathtub, shower over the bath, single vanity & loo.

For those who enjoy entertaining guests, the dedicated bar area adds an extra touch of luxury to this home. Equipped with custom cabinetry & stylish bar stools, it provides an inviting space for mixing cocktails or enjoying wine tastings with friends. This area can easily transition from casual gatherings to more formal events.

The kitchen is well equipped with a stand alone gas stove and oven, ample granite counter space, space for 2 under counter appliances plenty of built in cupboards & a walk in pantry.

Adjacent to the kitchen is an open-plan lounge & dining room that promotes seamless interaction between spaces with air-conditioning central to the reception areas. The spacious lounge area features comfortable seating arrangements central to the home , making it an ideal spot for relaxation or socializing. The dining room accommodates a large table for family meals or dinner parties, enhanced by elegant lighting fixtures that add sophistication to the space.

The dining room ventures out onto a copious covered patio perfect for entertaining with friends and family or having a relaxing afternoon. The patio overlooks its stunning manicured garden. Lush greenery surrounds the house, providing privacy and enhancing curb appeal. The garden features well-maintained flower beds, mature trees offering shade, and pathways leading to seating areas where one can relax outdoors. This outdoor oasis is perfect for hosting summer barbecues or enjoying quiet evenings under the stars.

The property offers you a sauna, borehole, 2 automated garages, 1 manual garage a double covered carport & a staff accomodation.

The suburb of Lonehill maintains its position as one of the safest suburbs in the Fourways area. The residents enjoy a strong sense of community and wonder at the natural beauty the suburb offers.
