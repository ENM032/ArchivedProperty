Sophisticated Five-Bedroom Sanctuary in Exclusive Lonehill Gated Community

Distinguished Residence in Prestigious Lonehill Complex

This exceptional five-bedroom residence, situated within an exclusive gated community in Lonehill, Sandton, represents a sophisticated sanctuary for discerning homeowners. The property comprises generously proportioned living spaces, meticulously appointed throughout, offering an elevated standard of residential excellence. With four elegantly appointed bathrooms, including two ensuites, the residence facilitates contemporary family living whilst maintaining refined privacy and comfort.

The expansive floor plan encompasses four distinct lounges and two formal dining areas, providing versatile entertaining spaces for both intimate gatherings and substantial social occasions. The residence features a fully equipped kitchen, complemented by a dedicated pantry and scullery, ensuring comprehensive culinary functionality. Additional refinements include a family television room, braai room with built-in braai facilities, and a sophisticated bar area, establishing this property as an ideal venue for sophisticated entertaining.

Key Features

Five generously proportioned bedrooms with two ensuites

Swimming pool and scenic garden sanctuary

Four-car garage with six parking spaces

24-hour security, CCTV surveillance, and alarm systems

Air conditioning throughout

Electric fencing and secure access gate

The property encompasses 1,392 square metres of meticulously maintained grounds, featuring a swimming pool, manicured gardens, and paved entertainment areas. Outdoor amenities include a balcony with special features, awning, and scenic vistas. Security provisions are comprehensive, incorporating 24-hour surveillance, electric fencing, alarm systems, and gated access. The residence accommodates four vehicles within the garage, with six additional parking spaces available. This distinguished property invites inspection from qualified purchasers seeking an exceptional residential investment in one of Sandton''s most coveted addresses.
