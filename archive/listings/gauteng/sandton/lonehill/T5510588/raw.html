4 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Contact agent for street address

Your Family is All That's Needed... To Fill This Happy Home!

Set in the heart of Lonehill, the neighbourhood you dreamed of, this much loved family is waiting for you. Welcoming entrance leads you to the free flowing lounge, dining room and study. Large TV room open plan to family sized kitchen with granite counters, plenty of cupboard space and scullery area.

The bedroom wing comprises 4 spacious bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms and guest cloak room. The main bedroom enjoys a contemporary en-suite bathroom and dressing room with cupboards galore.

Ouside theres something special for everybody... theres a extra length covered patio, cool blue swimming pool, custom built fire pit, large level lawned area for kids and furry friends to play, a jungle gym and wendy-house.

Outbuildings and extras include double auto garage with direct access, staff accommodation, carport. Excellent secuirty - alarm, electric fence and cc tv.

The running costs: Rates R2 443, Refuse R548, Sewer R1 213

Call today to see where your familys going... to Lonehill the village in a garden. Here residents get to enjoy an unrivalled security solution, quality educational facilities, convenient shopping centres and picturesque parks all packaged in a pristine house proud environment. If youre looking at a property investment in the Northern suburbs of Sandton take the time to Discover Lonehill.
