Waiting for new chapter to begin and a new family to create cherished memories and fun filled days. This beautiful home welcomes you with a bright entrance hall that leads into spacious, airy living areas: a formal lounge with a wood burning fireplace, elegant dining room, and a lovely family room perfect for quiet moments and joyful gatherings.

The solid maple wood chefs kitchen is thoughtfully designed with a gas hob, a scullery, and a pantry ideal for preparing family meals with ease. Four generously sized bedrooms, ensure everyone has their own personal retreat. Two full bathrooms and a guest cloak add convenience for family and visitors alike.

For those who help make life run smoothly, theres staff accommodation, which can alternatively be used as a home office with its own entrance. The covered patio overlooking the gorgeous, landscaped gardens invites relaxed weekends and summer evenings. The irrigation systems ensures all year-round greenery. The double auto garage has direct access into the home. Additional features include alarm system, outdoor beams, irrigation auto and manual stations, two geysers, and fibre connectivity.

This home is filled with character, from the wooden banisters and window frames to the large airy rooms flowing beautifully into the lush expansive garden. A space waiting for joyful play, peaceful reflection and unforgettable memories. All of this in the much sought after boomed off Hoogenhout Crescent - This home could be JUST WHAT YOU WANT.
