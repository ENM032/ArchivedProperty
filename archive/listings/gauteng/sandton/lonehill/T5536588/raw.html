3 Bedroom House in Lonehill

Largest Stand in the Estate | Private Family Living | Walk to Crawford

Some homes impress with finishes. Others impress with size. This home offers something far more difficult to find... space.

Positioned on the largest stand in the estate, this beautifully maintained family home combines generous proportions, exceptional privacy and a location thats hard to beat. With approximately 410m2 under roof on an expansive 889m2 stand, it offers the perfect balance between elegant family living and outdoor lifestyle.

Designed with everyday living in mind, the home features three spacious reception areas that flow effortlessly to a covered patio, sparkling pool and landscaped garden, creating an ideal setting for entertaining or relaxed family weekends.

The granite kitchen is complemented by a separate scullery, while the flexible layout includes a downstairs en-suite bedroom and two exceptionally spacious upstairs bedroom suites with private balconies and excellent separation for family living. Marble flooring, quality finishes and generous accommodation create a home that is both practical and refined.

Additional features include:

3 Spacious Bedrooms, all en-suite Guest Cloakroom 3 Reception Areas Study Granite Kitchen with Separate Scullery Covered Patio overlooking the Pool Triple Automated Garage Staff Accommodation Private Landscaped Garden 24-Hour Access Controlled Security

Perfectly positioned just moments from Crawford International, Lonehill Shopping Centre, Fourways, major transport routes and a variety of restaurants and amenities, this is a home designed for families looking to settle in for years to come.
