3 Bedroom House in Sunninghill

Contact agent for street address

Accepting offers from R3 799 000, SELLER ASKING MORE. This lovely, fully renovated family home offers comfort, space, and modern living. The home features three spacious bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms, making it ideal for a growing family.

Downstairs, youll find two inviting lounge areas a formal and an informal living space both with charming wood-burning fireplaces, perfect for cozy winter evenings. The living areas flow effortlessly onto a covered patio with stacking doors, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor feel. A truly warm and welcoming home designed for easy family living and effortless entertaining.
