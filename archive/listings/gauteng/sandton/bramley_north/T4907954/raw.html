Solar-Powered Family Home in Prime Boomed Enclave with Massive Parking Capacity

Solar-Powered Family Home in Prime Boomed Enclave with Massive Parking Capacity. Motivated seller relocating - inviting offers from R2.15m, asking R2.3m.

Set on an expansive 1487 m² stand, this move-in-ready family home offers an easy flow, generous living space and excellent illumination across the street frontage, front garden and back garden, complemented by top-tier security including an alarm system, CCTV and electric fencing.

The covered patio opens onto three sunlit reception rooms and a study. These spaces flow seamlessly into a chefs eat-in kitchen featuring gas hobs and a separate scullery. Accommodation also includes 4 spacious bedrooms and 2 full bathrooms.

The outdoor areas are equally impressive, with a large front garden that doubles as extensive visitor parking, accommodating 20 30 vehicles, while the manicured back garden overlooks a sparkling swimming pool.

Enjoy peace of mind with practical off-grid capability, including municipal gas connection and a solar power system with inverters and lithium-ion battery backup, ensuring continuity during power outages. Additional features include air conditioning, fibre internet, a single automated garage, and staff accommodation with cottage potential.

Centrally positioned with convenient access to major highways and key lifestyle and business nodes including Melrose Arch, Blu-Bird Centre, Rosebank and Sandton.

If you''re looking for a secure, spacious family home with alternative energy solutions and exceptional versatility, this property is not to be missed.
