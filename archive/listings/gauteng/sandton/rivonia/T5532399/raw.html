4 Bedroom House in Rivonia

18 ballich way
4 4 5 1 956 m² 390 m²

This lovely, spacious family home is set in a secure boomed-off area in Rivonia, offering comfort, space, and value, as well as a self-contained bachelor flat. The property sits on 1,956 sqm of land and features four sizeable bedrooms two en-suite and two additional bathrooms, including a guest cloakroom. The living areas are open plan and accommodate two lounges and a dining room, along with a spacious kitchen and a separate laundry and pantry. The dining room opens up to a large patio, part of which is undercover. The bedrooms have laminate flooring and offer fitted blinds, with the main bedroom featuring a study area and a walk-in closet, as well as a full en-suite bathroom.

Indoors and outdoors flow seamlessly, with a large garden ideal for kids and pets, already equipped with a built-in trampoline, tree house, and jungle gym, as well as a large swimming pool. The outside flatlet includes a bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom, providing a good income opportunity. Parking is accommodated with three carports and a double automated garage, along with ample additional storage in the garage and extra parking.

Extras of this spectacular home include:

- Backup power with solar panels

- Backup water with Jojo tanks

- Full alarm system

- Wood and gas fireplaces

- Fitted blinds

Rivonia and the security enclosure offer numerous amenities nearby, such as gyms, shopping centers, schools, and main arterial routes. Not to be missedget in touch for a viewing!
