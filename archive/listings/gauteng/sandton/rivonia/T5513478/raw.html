5 Bedroom House in Rivonia

30 eland avenue
5 3 2 1 520 m²

Charming family home with income producing cottage in secure boomed area. Situated within a sought-after, secure boomed enclosure, this lovely 3-bedroom family home offers comfort, space and exceptional value complete with a separate 2-bedroom income-producing cottage.

The main residence welcomes you with warm and inviting living spaces designed for easy family living. Three well-sized bedrooms are serviced by neatly appointed bathrooms, while the reception areas flow effortlessly into the garden, creating a relaxed indoor-outdoor lifestyle.

The spacious garden is perfect for children and pets to enjoy, complemented by a sparkling swimming pool ideal for entertaining family and friends.

A standout feature of this property is the separate 2-bedroom cottage perfect for extended family, guests, a work-from-home setup, or generating rental income to offset your bond.

Additional features include:

Double garage

Staff accommodation

Secure boomed area with controlled access

Generous garden space

This is a fantastic opportunity to secure a versatile family home with added income potential in a well-established, secure neighbourhood.

A property that truly offers lifestyle, flexibility and value all in one.
