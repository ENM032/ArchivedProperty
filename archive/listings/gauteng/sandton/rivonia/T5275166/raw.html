Fabulous Duplex Cluster

This spacious duplex is full of character. With solid bones and a generous layout, its the perfect canvas to create your dream home.

Key Features:

- 3 Generous Bedrooms

All with built-in cupboards. The main bedroom features extra storage and shelving, ideal for organisation lovers.

Both the main and second bedrooms are north-facing, with sliding doors opening onto a tiled outdoor areaperfect for enjoying seamless indoor-outdoor living.

- 2 Full Bathrooms plus guest cloakroom

Each includes both a shower and a bathtub, offering comfort and convenience for families or guests.

- Versatile Living Spaces

A welcoming entrance hall leads into a spacious dining room, which flows effortlessly into an enclosed patio. The large lounge area also opens to the patio, creating a bright and inviting space for relaxing or entertaining.

- Upstairs Retreat

Enjoy a cosy family room with a fireplace, a separate study, and another enclosed patioperfect for a home office, hobby room, or quiet reading space.

- Very secure with trellis doors throughout, alarm, beams and electric fence

Benefit from your own private entrance, additional gate, and paved off-street parking.

Includes a kitchenette, lounge, bedroom, and showerideal for live-in staff, a guest suite, or rental income potential.
