5 Bedroom House in Rivonia

6 winston avenue
5 3 2 2 424 m²

Stylishly Refurbished Family Home. This exceptional, ultra-spacious family residence has been beautifully refurbished to the highest standards, showcasing premium finishes throughout. Thoughtfully modernised, all original steel windows and doors have been replaced with sleek anodised aluminium, enhancing both style and functionality.

Designed for effortless family living and elegant entertaining, the home offers a seamless flow between the sophisticated lounge with feature fireplace, formal dining room, and a superb chefs kitchen complete with undercounter oven and generous pantry space.

Outdoors, the property truly shines with a private pool area, charming lapa, and expansive garden, creating the perfect setting for relaxed family living and unforgettable poolside entertaining.

The luxurious bedroom wing comprises five generously proportioned double bedrooms, complemented by three beautifully appointed bathrooms, including a sumptuous main ensuite. Ample built-in cupboards ensure excellent storage throughout.

Additional features include a double garage, staff accommodation, and extensive guest parking.

Set on an impressive 2,424m² stand, this timeless and elegant home is surrounded by rolling lawns, mature trees, and lush landscaped gardens. Perfectly positioned alongside the renowned Liliesleaf Heritage Site, this prime location offers exceptional privacy, prestige, and tranquility.

Enjoy peace of mind with excellent 24-hour boomed security, while benefiting from easy access to top schools, shopping centres, fine dining establishments, and major transport routes.

A rare opportunity to own a distinguished family home where luxury, space, and location combine seamlessly.
