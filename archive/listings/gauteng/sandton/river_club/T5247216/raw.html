4 Bedroom House in River Club

Contact agent for street address
4 3 2 738 m²

It''s a lifestyle. Family friendly cluster in small estate within close proximity to top Sandton schools and CBD. From the moment you drive into this well kept small estate you immediately feel a sense of calm, it''s secure, quiet and private. The spacious home offers easy living, great entertaining spaces and a larger than average cluster garden complete with undercover patio and pool. Downstairs offers a welcoming entrance hall, 3 living areas and a spacious kitchen with scullery and walk in pantry. Both the formal lounge and TV room flow seamlessly onto the under cover patio. The dining room has a large slide back window which overlooks the patio. There is also a guest cloakroom and large under stair storage closet on the ground floor. Upstairs offers 3 spacious bedrooms, the primary complete with study, large dressing room and his and hers bathrooms. There is also a full family bathroom. All 3 bedrooms lead out onto a large sunny balcony overlooking the treetops and garden. There is staff accommodation which is currently integrated into the home and serves as a 4th bedroom en-suite and which can easy be converted back into staff accommodation. Borehole for garden and pool and solar powered inverter for electricity backup.
