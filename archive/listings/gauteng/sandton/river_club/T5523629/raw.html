Architecturaly designed modern home in Morningside Ext 40 Security Enclave.

Set within an exclusive gated enclave in Morningside Ext 40, Sandton, this fully renovated modern residence delivers a rare combination of architectural design, security, and functional family living.

The home is designed around a striking central swimming pool, with all principal living spaces oriented to maximise natural light and seamless indoor-outdoor flow.

Living & Entertaining

A wide entrance hall opens into a vast open-plan living environment combining lounge, dining, and a high-end kitchen. Large sliding doors extend onto a covered patio, creating a continuous entertainment space overlooking the pool and garden.
