An Icon of Sophisticated Luxury Living . An Icon of Sophisticated Luxury in Morningside

4 stylish bedrooms,1 guest bathroom, 3 bathroom with 2 of which is on-suite.

A residence of rare distinction, timeless elegance, and uncompromising luxury this extraordinary Morningside Stately home has been masterfully curated for the discerning buyer who appreciates refined living on a grand scale.

Privately positioned behind imposing double-volume walls and regal automated gates, this stately home immediately commands attention with its striking architectural presence and exceptional craftsmanship.

The arrival experience is nothing short of impressive. Grand double doors open into an elegant entrance hall where sophistication and warmth blend effortlessly. To the left, a beautifully appointed guest cloakroom serves visitors with understated luxury, while to the right, a bespoke executive study offers the ideal private workspace for todays accomplished professional.

Designed with both lavish entertaining and intimate family living in mind, the residence unfolds into an exquisite collection of reception spaces adorned with sleek porcelain flooring, bespoke finishes, and seamless indoor-outdoor flow.

Here, the ultra-contemporary gourmet kitchen takes centre stage impeccably designed with premium finishes, an elegant breakfast nook, and a discreetly positioned scullery complete with space for 4 appliances walk-in pantry.

The open-plan entertainment wing has been curated to deliver an unparalleled lifestyle experience, effortlessly integrating:

Sophisticated :

- An elegant dining space with a designer imported marble slab table built exclusively for the space.

- A luxurious leisure lounge

- A temperature-conscious, fully fitted wine cellar accommodating in excess of 300 bottles

Floor-to-ceiling stacking glass doors dissolve the boundaries between interior and exterior living, revealing an entertainers paradise overlooking a magnificent heated swimming pool and exquisitely landscaped gardens.

This private sanctuary further boasts:

- A bespoke pizza oven

- Designer braai and entertainment pavilion

- An enchanting walk-in doll house nestled within the gardens, creating an idyllic family home.

Luxurious Bedroom Suites

The residence offers four beautifully appointed bedroom suites, each finished with premium wooden flooring and exceptional attention to detail. Three of the suites are fitted with air-conditioning, while two offer expansive proportions with luxurious en-suite bathrooms.

The primary suite is a statement of indulgence elegance and comforting serenity.

Every bathroom within the home has been finished to the highest standards
