6 Bedroom House in Morningside

Contact agent for street address
6 6.5 2 3 968 m²

Sometimes art is created with brick and mortar and becomes so much more than just a home. This home has done just that!. As you enter this abode you are immediately transported to another world abundant with greenery as far as your eyes can gaze. Authentic and possessing a remarkable probity, this gorgeous home is a showstopper. A place to live and breathe in. Ensconced on an acre of rolling lawns and established trees sequestered deep within a guarded boomed enclosure, it''s hard to find a reason not to own it at once.

The home beckons entry through a grand entrance hall with high ceilings and beautifully textured walls leading through to various open-plan reception rooms for every occasion. There is a space for everyone and everything, and the home demands that you immediately relax and soak in the glorious views of the sprawling gardens through double glazed black aluminum glass windows which prevail throughout and flow out to the undercover patio with separate covered braai area and sparkling pool.

Entertainment is an absolute requirement of this home evident in the interplay between indoors and out. Top quality finishes and textures, and abundant natural light create a foil in which to envelope yourself and entertain lavishly. Only a visit can confirm the superlatives used to describe the indescribable. From its very design outset, quality and fastidious attention to detail were demanded and executed. Extensively insulated the temperature will seldom dip below 17 degrees.

Accommodation is ideal and caters perfectly for the larger family with 4 bedroom suites including a fabulous main bedroom with dressing room, lounge and balcony with breath-taking views of Bryanston Ridge rich with ghost oak wooden floors and air-conditioned rooms throughout. Plus, a 2 bedroom suite flatlet with kitchenette, lounge and separate entrance. A full solar inverter system, state-of-the-art security systems, study, library, 2 staff rooms, garaging for 2 cars and more than ample guest parking.

We could go on and on, but might we suggest a private viewing
