Family Cluster Home in a Secure Gated Morningside Community with Cottage

Situated within a secure gated community in the highly sought-after suburb of Morningside, Sandton, this spacious three-bedroom cluster home presents an excellent opportunity for buyers looking to add their own touch and unlock its full potential. Offering approximately 250 sqm under roof on a 425 sqm erf, the property provides generous accommodation, a practical layout, and a wrap-around garden that creates a peaceful outdoor setting.

While the home would benefit from some maintenance and cosmetic updating, it offers a solid foundation and exceptional value in a prime location.

The home features two separate reception areas, ideal for both family living and entertaining, complemented by a cosy gas fireplace. At the heart of the home is a spacious open-plan kitchen fitted with a gas hob and double eye-level ovens, offering ample space for cooking and gathering.

The upstairs main bedroom suite is a standout feature, complete with a generous dressing room and separate his-and-hers bathrooms, one with a bath and the other with a shower.

Downstairs, there are two additional bedrooms. One enjoys the convenience of an en-suite bathroom with a shower and toilet, while the second is serviced by a family bathroom featuring both a bath and shower.

A cottage situated above the garage provides flexible additional accommodation and would be ideal for a teenage retreat, guest suite, home office, or staff quarters.

This is a wonderful opportunity to secure a well-sized home in a desirable Morningside location and add value through thoughtful improvements. With its spacious accommodation, secure setting, and versatile cottage, this property offers the perfect canvas to create a truly special family home.
