4 Bedroom House in Morningside

Contact agent for street address
4 3 8 3 775 m²

Easy Living Morningside Gem On A Large Stand

Nestled in the sought-after neighborhood of Morningside, Sandton, this lovely single-storey is the perfect family home.

The main house has four spacious bedrooms overlooking the garden and three modern bathrooms.

This residence is designed for both relaxation and entertaining. The kitchen is the heart of this open plan home, it seamlessly flows into the dining room, and living room creating an inviting atmosphere for family meals and gatherings.

Outside is a stunning covered patio that overlooks a sparkling pool, perfect for enjoying sunny afternoons or hosting weekend braais The large sloped garden is perfect for the family and pets to enjoy.

The private tennis court, needs some care to get it back to its former show room condition, making it the perfect place for ball sports.

The entrance to this home, is currently on Summit Road, but can easily be change to the boomed area of Aberfeldy Avenue, creating even better security. There is plenty of off road parking, two garages with automated doors, parking is hassle-free. The property is fully walled with an electric fence and gate, ensuring security and peace of mind.
