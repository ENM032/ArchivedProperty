Unforgettable and Unique Cluster Home with Bespoke Charm and Character.

Unforgettable and Unique Cluster Home with Bespoke Charm and Character.

Situated in a small and exclusive complex of only 9 units this family home is the ultimate in luxurious living. Double volume entrance hall, modern design and natural light. Open plan living spaces enhanced by an effortless indoor outdoor flow. Entertainment area opens out onto covered patio and secluded garden.

Designer Kitchen with integrated appliances, breakfast area and separate scullery and laundry. Beautiful finishes throughout. Further along the ground floor is the guest bedroom offering just the warmth and comfort guests would welcome. Up a beautiful flight of stairs 3 well appointed en-suite bedrooms, study or 4th bedroo and comfortable pyjama lounge. The main bedroom has a dressing area and a modern bathroom.

Extras include:

- Water backup: JoJo tank

- LED lights

- Ducted Air Conditioner allows to cool or heat each room individually

- Underfloor heating in the bathrooms

- Stacking doors

- Storage space

- Staff accommodation

- Alarm system

- Security beams

- Double automated garage with direct access to the house

- 24-hour security boomed off area.

PRIME LOCATION

Central to both Sandton and Rivonia with easy access to the major freeways and conveniently close to many top amenities including:

RETAIL: Rivonia Village, The Wedge, Morningside Shopping Centre, Sandton City and Nelson Mandela Square

FITNESS: Virgin Active, Planet Fitness, BODYTEC, Sandton Action Sports, Morningside Country Club

TRANSPORT: Gautrain, Rea Vaya Bus Services, Uber, Bolt

HEALTH CARE: Morningside Mediclinic, Intercare Sandton, Sandton Surgical, The Wedge Medical Suites

EDUCATION: Redhill School, The French School Lyce Jules Verne, Crawford Sandton, Crawford Rivonia, Rivonia Primary, Manor Pre-Primary, Curro Rivonia, Richfield College, Regenesys Business School, IIE Varsity College
