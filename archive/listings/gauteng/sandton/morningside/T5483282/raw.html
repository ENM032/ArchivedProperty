3 Bedroom House in Morningside

Contact agent for street address
3 3.5 2 290 m²

This exceptional single-level residence offers a rare combination of contemporary design, advanced security, and complete energy independence in one of Morningsides most established pockets.

Set within a low-density complex, the 267m2 home features three spacious en-suite bedrooms, a versatile playroom or home office, and seamless open-plan living centred around a stylish gas fireplace. The living areas flow effortlessly onto a covered patio with a built-in braai and a beautifully manicured, maintenance-free private garden.

Included in the purchase price is R850,000 worth of bespoke cabinetry and custom feature walls, creating a home that feels refined, complete, and move-in ready.

Designed for modern living, the home is equipped with a full solar and backup power solution, Samsung air-conditioning throughout, fibre connectivity, and a dedicated cellphone signal booster.

Security is a standout feature, with facial recognition access, Ajax wireless alarm systems, perimeter beams, HD camera surveillance, a secure strong room, and 24-hour CAP monitoring offering peace of mind at every level.

Ideally positioned just minutes from Sandton CBD, top schools, major shopping centres, and main arterial routes, this is a sophisticated lock-up-and-go home perfectly suited to executives, families, and frequent travellers.

Arrange your private viewing today and experience a level of luxury, premium security, and ultimate convenience rarely found in Sandton.
