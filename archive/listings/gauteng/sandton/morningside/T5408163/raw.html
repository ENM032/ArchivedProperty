4 Bedroom House in Morningside

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 2 2 000 m²

Offers from R10.5 million. Asking R10.950 million. Exquisite is the defining word that truly captures the essence of this remarkable residence. . Set on a 2000m² stand in the most coveted position in Morningside, this exceptional property offers a rare combination of grandeur, serenity, and uncompromising quality. Surrounded by a park-like garden rich in birdlife, the home feels like a private sanctuary tranquil, secure, and completely secluded from the bustle of city life, yet minutes from Sandtons finest amenities. Designed and built to exacting standards, the home showcases a seamless, flowing floor plan that effortlessly blends elegant entertaining with relaxed family living. Vast picture windows flood the interiors with natural light and frame breathtaking views of the manicured gardens, creating a seamless blend between indoors and out.

Expansive, interleading living spaces allow for grand-scale entertaining while still offering intimate corners for quieter moments. The formal dining room open to a library room conversation area with feature wine cellar is refined and atmospheric, ideal for elegant dinner parties. The generous family room flows through onto an enclosed patio with stacking doors complete with a built-in bar and braai. At the heart of the home lies a gourmet, eat-in kitchen designed for both function and flair. Upstairs accommodation is ideal with 4 luxurious bedroom suites offering comfort, privacy, and beautifully appointed bathrooms. The main suite is truly magnificent with serene garden views and his-and-hers dressing rooms. A cosy pajama lounge provides an additional relaxed living area for the family. Plus, a study, feature pool, borehole, full load generator, double garaging, ample guest parking, double staff accommodation and top-notch security features.

Positioned within walking distance to Redhill School & Sandton Shul and close to Crawford College and The French International School.

You owe it to yourself to make this a definite stop on your journey to find your dream home.
