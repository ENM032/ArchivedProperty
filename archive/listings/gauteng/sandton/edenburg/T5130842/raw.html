Spacious Family Home with Income-Generating Flatlet and Stylish Outdoor Living

Nestled in a quiet, family-friendly neighborhood, this versatile home offers a harmonious blend of comfort, functionality, and investment potential.

The main residence features three spacious bedrooms and two bathrooms, including a master en-suite for added privacy. The well-appointed kitchen flows seamlessly into the open-plan living and dining areasperfect for both daily living and entertaining. A dedicated study provides the ideal work-from-home setup, while the double garage ensures secure parking and extra storage space.

Step outside to a sun-drenched patio, ideal for morning coffee or weekend braais.

A standout feature of this property is the self-contained flatlet, complete with one bedroom, one bathroom, and a private kitchenperfect for extended family, guests, or as a rental income opportunity.

This home effortlessly combines modern living with practicalityoffering room to grow, relax, and create lasting memories.
