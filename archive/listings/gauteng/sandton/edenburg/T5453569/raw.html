4 Bedroom House in Edenburg

11 east pont road
4 2.5 8 1 500 m² 370 m²

Exclusive Mandate. Discover this impressive single-storey freestanding residence situated on a substantial 1500 m² erf in sought-after Edenburg. This a very quiet surburb close to a lot amenities, stone throw away from Rivonia Village, and closer to Morningside, Sandton Square and Bryanston.  You are really spoiled for choice. This well-appointed home features an enduring slate roof and is positioned on a level road, ensuring easy access. Designed for comfortable living, the interior boasts two spacious lounges, a functional kitchen, and a dedicated, generously sized studyoffice.

Stepping inside, you will find a home full of charm, through the entrnce hall with tiled flooring you are greeted by the expansive living area designed for family living and entertaining.

Accommodation includes four spacious bedrooms, each fitted with built-in cupboards, with the main bedroom offering the luxury of a full en-suite bathroom and a convenient walk-in closet. A further 1.5 bathrooms cater to family and guests. Practical amenities abound, including a domestic room, a double garage with internal access into the home, and ample additional parking for up to six vehicles. The property is pet-friendly, welcoming all members of the family, and enhanced by an electric fence for peace of mind. Outside, a refreshing swimming pool promises endless leisure and entertaining possibilities within the expansive grounds. This Edenburg property presents a rare blend of generous space, practical features, and a desirable lifestyle. Don''t miss the opportunity to make this your new home.
