Beautiful 3-Bedroom Family Home with Solar Power, Pool & Staff Accommodation

Welcome to your oasis in Edenburg

This well-maintained home offers 3 spacious bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, including a generous main en suite featuring a separate dressing room. The main bedroom has sliding doors to a covered patio. All bedrooms have beautiful wooden floors, ample built-in cupboards, and plenty of natural light. Aircons in 2 bedrooms.

The inviting, spacious lounge opens onto a covered patio with a fireplace and built-in braai, creating the perfect space for entertaining or relaxing while overlooking the lovely garden and sparkling swimming pool.

A versatile prayer room can easily be converted into a formal dining room, offering flexibility to suit your family''s needs.

The kitchen provides space for two appliances and has ample cupboard space.

Additional features include:

Single garage

Two covered carports

Staff accommodation

Solar panels with an inverter for energy efficiency and backup power

Air conditioning

Beautiful, well-established garden

Sparkling swimming pool

Very secure, with alarm and internal and external beams

This home combines comfort, practicality, and energy efficiency, making it an excellent choice for families seeking a move-in-ready property.

Close proximity to all amenities, highways, shopping centres, top schools, and hospitals.
