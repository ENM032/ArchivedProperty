Exquisite Fully Furnished Luxury Residence | A Masterpiece of Design, Comfort & Sophisticated Living

Experience the pinnacle of luxury living in this exceptional fully furnished residence, where timeless elegance, contemporary design and uncompromising quality come together to create a home unlike any other. Beautifully curated with premium furnishings, designer finishes and world class amenities, this remarkable property offers a seamless turnkey lifestyle for the discerning purchaser.

Perfectly positioned in the sought after suburb of Edenburg, this magnificent family residence enjoys close proximity to leading schools, Sandton''s business district, premium shopping destinations and major transport routes while offering complete privacy and tranquillity.

From the moment you arrive, the beautifully landscaped gardens, mature citrus trees and impressive faade set the tone for what awaits inside. Two spacious double automated garages with quality tiled flooring and custom built storage provide secure parking for four vehicles while offering exceptional practicality.

The welcoming entrance hall introduces a home defined by sophisticated interiors, elegant wallpaper finishes and generous proportions. At its heart lies a spectacular designer kitchen featuring sleek white gloss cabinetry, luxurious granite countertops, integrated Bosch appliances, extensive storage and a statement centre island fitted with an induction gas hob, creating the perfect balance between everyday family living and effortless entertaining.

Complementing the main kitchen is a fully equipped chef''s kitchen complete with a Whirlpool integrated oven, gas stove, generous pantry, dedicated scullery and additional preparation space, allowing entertaining on any scale with complete ease.

Flowing seamlessly from the kitchen is the impressive open plan dining room, enhanced by soaring double volume ceilings, beautiful timber flooring and a feature fireplace that creates warmth and ambience throughout the cooler months. The elegant sunken lounge extends effortlessly through expansive doors to the spectacular indoor heated swimming pool and covered entertainment pavilion, creating an extraordinary indoor outdoor lifestyle designed for year round enjoyment.

Accommodation is both generous and luxurious.

The home offers six beautifully appointed bedrooms, each individually designed with premium finishes, air conditioning, bespoke cabinetry and elegant en suite bathrooms.

Three spacious en suite bedrooms are positioned on the lower level, all opening onto the beautifully landscaped gardens through elegant American shutters and offering exceptional comfort for family and guests alike.

Upstairs, an expansive pyjama lounge and cinema room overlook the main living spaces below while a dedicated study area with custom fitted desks creates the ideal work from home environment.

The magnificent primary suite is a private sanctuary featuring automated curtains, integrated home theatre system, luxurious his and hers walk in dressing rooms and an opulent spa inspired bathroom complete with double vanities and an oversized walk in shower.

Designed for effortless family living, the residence also offers multiple guest cloakrooms, dedicated study areas and extensive storage throughout.

The property further benefits from two fully self contained staff suites. One includes its own private lounge, kitchen, generous bedroom and full en suite bathroom, while the second offers open plan accommodation with kitchenette and private bathroom. An additional outdoor guest bathroom with shower further enhances the home''s functionality.

Exceptional sustainability features include two boreholes, a 10 000 litre JoJo water storage system supplying backup water, automated irrigation servicing the entire garden and beautifully established landscaped grounds that remain lush throughout the year.

Every element of this residence has been thoughtfully considered and meticulously maintained, creating a home that effortlessly combines luxury, comfort and functionality. Offered fully furnished with carefully selected designer pieces and premium appliances, this is a rare turnkey opportunity for buyers seeking an exceptional lifestyle without compromise.

Whether enjoyed as a distinguished family residence or an executive entertainer''s home, this remarkable property represents luxury living at its finest.
