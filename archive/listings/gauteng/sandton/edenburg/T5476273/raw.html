Welcome to your new home located in Edenburg.

This stunningly large and spacious is apart of a secure and gated community.

When you step into the house you are greeted by a beautiful foyer entrance lounge. Connected to the foyer is a beautiful lounge that holds more than enough space for both a dining room and living room. Connected to your lounge is a modern kitchen with clean and beautiful touches. The outdoor area greets you with a large patio that is not only beautiful but also perfect for hosting. When you step down the patio stairs you enter a large garden with a pool. This property is a double story, so you will find bedrooms both downstairs and upstairs, all the bedrooms are spacious and beautiful. 6 bedrooms and 6.5 bathrooms. 5 of the bedrooms are en-suite. The second floor of this home takes you into a whole new world with features such as an in house, a dininglounge area, and a massive balconyoutdoor area.
