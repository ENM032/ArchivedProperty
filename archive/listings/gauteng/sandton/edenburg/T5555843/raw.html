3 Bedroom House in Edenburg

Panhandle 3 bed home on 1983sq m in EdenburgRivonia

This is what you have been waiting for, space, lots of light and income potential.

Large living areas: Huge entrance hall leading to lounge that is wrapped with an L-shaped covered patio overlooking wonderful garden and pool. Kitchen with separate scullery has a serve through to a private little awesome patio. Dining room that easily accommodates a 10 seater. All of these mentioned rooms have exceptional wooden flooring.

Bedrooms: Three spacious bedrooms. The main bedroom is en-suite with full north facing bathroom looking onto pool. Huge dressing room. The other two bedroom are inter-linked with a bathroom.

Downstairs: Bonus flatlet, perfect income generator or WFH which is on the lower level of the home consisting of a lounge, separate bedroom, bathroom and kitchen with stove. Private entrance and parking.

Entertainment: Lower level has a huge family room which accommodates a billiard table or can be used as the perfect TV room. This leads to a fitted bar.

Patio-Garden-Pool: Fantastic L-shaped patio with pool, huge garden with established trees lawn and garden flower beds. Kids and pets will love this garden.

Location: Sought after family area, close to excellent schools, shops and main routes. Double auto garage, staff quarters and lots of off road parking.
