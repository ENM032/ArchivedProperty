4 Bedroom House in Edenburg

15 5th lane
4 3.5 5 3 971 m²

Exceptional luxury family estate with world class features in secure boomed enclosure. This expansive and beautifully appointed 4-bedroom residence offers an outstanding combination of luxury, comfort, and self-sufficiency, all tucked away within a secure 24-hour manned boomed area. Designed for effortless family living and exceptional entertaining, the home delivers impressive proportions, premium finishes, and world-class outdoor amenities.

The accommodation includes four generously sized bedrooms, three full bathrooms, and a guest cloakroom. Central air conditioning serves the bedroom wing and study, while underfloor heating throughout the main house ensures year-round comfort. Elegant wooden laminate flooring flows through the living areas, with plush carpeting in the bedrooms adding warmth and sophistication.

Three large reception spaces including a fitted modern TV lounge provide versatile living for both formal and relaxed occasions. A Morso fireplace creates a cosy focal point during winter months. The gourmet kitchen, complete with appliances and a separate scullery, is thoughtfully designed for both everyday convenience and entertaining on a grand scale.

Adding further value and versatility to the property is a separate cottage with its own private entrance, ideal for extended family, guests, or as an income-generating opportunity. The cottage offers two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchenette, and comfortable lounge area, along with its own double carport, ensuring privacy and independence from the main residence.

The outdoor offering is truly spectacular. Expansive patios overlook a breathtaking landscaped garden featuring decked walkways, tranquil ponds, and a stunning pool area. The garden was featured in Cond Nast magazine in 2008 and continues to provide a serene, resort-like environment. Additional lifestyle features include a private tennis court with entertainment deck, a fully equipped Wendy house, jungle gym, trampoline, and childrens playhouse creating the ultimate family sanctuary.

Vehicle accommodation is exceptional with triple automated garages, a double carport, two automated entrances, and a fully fitted home gym within the garage.

Energy & Water Independence

3-phase 50kVA electricity supply

5kVA inverter system with 10 solar panels

20kWh UPS backup battery system with online management

Super-flow borehole with two 5,000L water tanks

Fully automated irrigation system

State-of-the-Art Security

Dual alarm systems interior and exterior

Electric fencing and perimeter beams

13-camera Hik-Connect surveillance system

Industrial fire alarm system throughout the home

Combining luxury, sustainability, recreation, and security plus the added benefit of a private cottage this remarkable property offers a rare lifestyle opportunity for the discerning buyer seeking space, privacy, and uncompromising quality.

A truly one-of-a-kind family estate.
