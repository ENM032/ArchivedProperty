3 Bedroom House in Edenburg

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 2 403 m²

Beautiful Simplex Cluster

As rare as hens teeth, this fabulous simplex cluster is nestled within an exclusive complex of only a few units, offering privacy, security, and refined living.

Enter a spacious entrance hall with views of a beautiful water feature just outside the front door, creating an immediate sense of tranquillity and elegance. The expansive open-plan living area boasts a stunning double-sided fireplace that seamlessly connects to a cozy secondary sitting room. Two sets of doors open onto a covered patio overlooking a charming garden part indigenous and part fully landscaped with an array of beautiful plants.

The open-plan kitchen is both stylish and functional, complete with a breakfast nook, Caesarstone countertops, and ample cupboard space. A separate scullery offers space for three appliances, additional storage cupboards, and leads to an outdoor paved utility area.

The bedroom wing comprises three spacious, carpeted bedrooms, all with generous built-in cupboards and overhead fans. The second bathroom includes both a bath and a shower. The large main bedroom opens directly onto the garden and features abundant cupboard space and a full en-suite bathroom.

Additional features include:

- Fully alarmed with exterior beams

- Inverter system servicing the entire cluster

- Beautiful water feature outside front door

- Secure boomed enclave with 247 security

- Double automated garages direct access to the entrance hall

A rare opportunity to own a beautifully maintained home in a highly sought-after, secure setting

Close to all amenities, highways, top schools, shopping centres and hospitals
