3 Bedroom House in Magaliessig

38 cornmarsh
3 2 2 400 m² 197 m²

Stunning cluster with low maintenance garden low levies too!

Nestled within a very desirable estate, this home offers a serene and peaceful retreat, set back from the hustle and bustle. Recently renovated from top to bottom, it boasts a practical and functional layout, making it perfect for both growing families and empty nesters.

As you step inside, you are welcomed by an abundance of natural light and spacious open-plan living areas that include:

- An entrance hall with space for coffee bar bar counter informal eat in area with aluminium sliding doors which lead out to the outside entertainment area

- A TV room with space for a study nook

- A versatile Family Room, which can easily be converted into a fourth bedroom

- A Dining Room that seamlessly connects to the open-plan kitchen, featuring a central island with a gas hob. Dining room can easily accommodate a 12 seater table. Fan.

The kitchen is equipped with a separate scullery that offers double sinks, ample cupboard space, an additional gas hob, a gas oven, an extractor fan, granite countertops, and provisions for two under-counter appliances. A stylish wood fireplace adds a cozy touch for those chilly winter evenings.

Step outside from the living areas to discover a private entertainment space, ideal for unwinding with friends and family and beautifully maintained private low maintenance wrap around garden.

The sleeping zone comprises of three generously sized bedrooms and two bathrooms. The main bedroom is super spacious, features its own en-suite bathroom complete with a shower, while the second and third bedrooms are equally spacious.

The second bedroom is spacious to fit a king size bed and the third bedroom to fit a queen size bed.

The second bathroom of the home is modern and boasts both a shower and bathtub.

Additional features of this home include:

- Solar electric geyser which keeps your Eskom bill nice and low combined with the gas hobs and gas oven and wood fireplace, makes this home very energy efficient. For the overcast rainy days the geyser will kick in via electricity always ensuring hot water.

- Ceiling fans in all bedrooms and the dining room

- Irrigation in the garden

- Aerolite ceiling insulation

- Movable wardrobes in the bedrooms for flexible arrangement

- A movable kitchen island

- A double garage

- Internal home for added peace of mind and quiet away from the hustle and bustle.

- A wrap-around garden perfect for families and pets

This home truly combines comfort and style, making it a must-see!

Location is excellent making this estate an ideal choice for families and professionals looking to live in a friendly and security conscious community with the proximity to many brilliant schools in the area such as:

St Peters Prep, Bryanston Parallel Medium, Crawford Prep and College, Fourways High and more!

N1 highway, Bryanston Drive, Winnie Mandela Drive and Witkoppen are a stone throws away.
