Spacious Family Townhouse in Secure Magaliessig Complex

Spacious Family Living in a Secure Magaliessig Complex

NEGOTIATING OFFERS FROM R1 999 000

SELLER ASKING R2 250 000

Discover exceptional space and comfort in this inviting 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom townhouse, ideally situated within a small-density security complex in Magaliessig. Offering classic charm, this home provides a generous 199 sqm of living space, a rare find.

An expansive entrance hall leads to a well-appointed kitchen, featuring space for 2 appliances, abundant cupboards, and extensive workspace. Three distinct reception areas flow seamlessly, with two opening directly onto a manicured garden and private patio, perfect for relaxation and entertaining. A dedicated family TV room and guest toilet enhance functionality.

Upstairs, three large bedrooms offer comfortable retreats. The main bedroom includes a private en-suite, while both the main and second bedrooms benefit from their own mini balconies. Storage is a significant advantage, with wall-to-wall cupboards upstairs, a large area under the staircase, and practical loft storage within one of the two automated garages.

As a desirable corner unit, the property features a stunning wrap-around garden, complete with an irrigation system and Jojo tank. Enjoy direct, exclusive access to the communal pool area. Additional features include air conditioning, pet-friendly policies, and robust security measures such as an access gate, security gate, burglar bars, and a fully walled perimeter, offering peace of mind in this sought-after suburb.
