Immaculate Renovated Townhouse in Secure Magaliesig, Sandton

Move-in ready, with affordable levies, this immaculate renovated freehold townhouse in Magaliesig offers a standout blend of modern comfort, exceptional neatness and secure living. The complex is recognised for its wellkept grounds, clean communal areas and strong focus on resident safety, creating a calm and orderly environment throughout.

The home features three beautifully tiled bedrooms and two pristine bathrooms, all finished with care and attention to detail. The open plan lounge and dining room connects seamlessly to a modern kitchen fitted with a gas stove, forming a warm and contemporary living space. A sunny covered patio extends the homes living area and overlooks the sparkling pool, providing an inviting setting for both relaxation and entertaining.

The property includes a double automated garage and a covered secure carport positioned within the full perimeter wall. Visitor parking is conveniently available, offering added ease for guests.

This townhouse delivers immaculate presentation and secure, comfortable living in one of Magaliesigs most neatly maintained complexes. It is an ideal choice for anyone seeking a modern, safe and refined home in a prime location.
