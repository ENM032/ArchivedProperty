3 Bedroom House in Magaliessig

16 syntagma crescent
3 2 8 669 m² 211 m²

Spacious Family home with study, solar, water and security

VIEWINGS BY APPOINTMENT ONLY

Welcome to this warm and inviting family home, offering generous proportions, excellent entertainment spaces and a wonderful

combination of comfort, practicality and modern conveniences, all set within a secure and sought-after complex.

As you enter, a welcoming entrance hall leads into a spacious sunken lounge where natural light streams through overhead skylights,

creating a bright and airy atmosphere. Glass doors open onto a covered patio with built-in braai, overlooking the established garden - the ideal space for

relaxed family gatherings and entertaining.

The dining room forms part of the open-plan living area and offers ample space for larger family occasions, comfortably

accommodating a large dining suite. Sliding doors provide additional access to the garden area, enhancing the home''s easy indoor-outdoor flow.

The well-designed kitchen offers abundant cupboard space, a walk-in pantry and separate scullery with space for appliances. Complete with a

gas hob, electric oven and room for a double-door fridge, this kitchen has been designed with both practicality and functionality in mind.

The home offers three sunny bedrooms with built-in cupboards and comfortable proportions. The spacious main bedroom accommodates a

king-sized suite with ease and boasts a walk-in dressing room with skylight, as well as direct access to the patio and garden. The en-suite bathroom is

complete with bath, shower and vanity, while the second bathroom services the remaining bedrooms.

A north-facing study provides the ideal work-from-home space, while a guest cloakroom adds additional convenience.

Adding to the home''s appeal is a Jacuzzi opening onto its own secluded patio - creating the perfect space to unwind after a long day.

Additional features include:

- Basement area ideal as a wine cellar or additional storage

- 5kW inverter with two lithium batteries

- 10 solar panels

- Four JoJo tanks with approximately 8,000 litres of water storage capacity

- Irrigation system servicing both the landscaped garden and rooftop vegetable garden

- WiFi ready

- Pet-friendly complex

Security has been thoughtfully considered, with security gates fitted to external access points and skylights, outdoor infrared

detectors and panic buttons linked directly to the guardhouse.

Completing the home are two double automated garages with one tandem to the rear garden, together with ample off-street parking

for visitors.

Ideally positioned within family-friendly Magaliessig, close to excellent schools, Fourways Precinct, Montecasino, Design Quarter, major

routes and all amenities.

A home offering space, warmth and exceptional value, with thoughtful extras seldom found in one property.
