4 Bedroom House in Magaliessig

Contact agent for street address
4 3.5 8 869 m² 325 m²

The Best of Both Worlds Freestanding living with Estate like security with this pan handle home

Welcome to a truly exceptional family home, uniquely positioned on a spacious stand with an abundance of indoor living space.

One of the standout features of this residence is its impressive security. Nestled within a panhandle, it benefits from a double gate leading from the road, through the panhandle, and to your private entrance. Although surrounded by neighboring homes, the property offers unparalleled privacy.

This double-storey home features three generously sized bedrooms on the upper level, along with two bathrooms. The fourth bedroom is particularly versatile, boasting its own separate entrance, making it ideal for staff accommodation, a teenage retreat, or a dedicated workspace.

The home showcases expansive living areas, including an open-plan living room, a dining area, and a separate TV lounge, all tiled throughout the ground floor. The dining room seamlessly connects to the well-equipped kitchen, which offers ample cupboard space, granite countertops, a walk-in pantry, a prep sink, an electric hob, an oven, an extractor fan, and a cozy breakfast nook for effortless morning meals. Additionally, a separate scullery provides space for three under-counter appliances, a double sink, and extra storage.

Both the TV lounge and formal living area open through sliding doors to a covered patio, complete with a built-in braai, overlooking the swimming pool and expansive garden. A convenient guest toilet is also located on the ground floor.

Upstairs, you''ll discover a study nook, perfect for a home office or quiet reading corner. All bedrooms are spacious and suitable for families with children of all ages. The main bedroom serves as a true sanctuary, featuring a private balcony, a dressing area, and a beautifully designed bathroom with a double vanity, bath, and shower. The second and third bedrooms are equally spacious, with the second bedroom also providing access to the balcony. All three bedrooms are adorned with laminate flooring and are flooded with natural light.

The second and third bedroom shared the second modern full bathroom of this home

The fourth bedroom separate from the main house is ideal as staff accommodation, teeange pad or work from home space. It comprises of a kitchenette, bathroom with shower and bedroom.

Additional features of this remarkable home include:

- A double garage with internal access

- Ample parking space for an additional six vehicles

- Fibre readiness for high-speed internet

- Internal home alarm system

- Electric fence for added security

- Trelly doors and burglar bars for peace of mind

This family home truly offers the perfect blend of comfort, security, and spacious living.
