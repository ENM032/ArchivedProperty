Exceptional Dual-Living Home in Magaliessig.

Located in a highly sought-after boomed-off area of Magaliessig, this expansive property offers an exceptional combination of space, versatility and opportunity, with the added benefit of business rights.

The property is uniquely configured as two homes joined together, offering a total of 7 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms. The original residence boasts 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, while the newer main home offers a further 4 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, making this an ideal property for a large family, extended family or those seeking flexible living arrangements.

The spacious living areas are complemented by a large wrap-around garden, creating an inviting outdoor environment for children, pets and entertaining. The property also boasts a swimming pool and jacuzzi, perfect for relaxing and enjoying time with family and friends.

Adding to the appeal is the property''s business rights, opening up a range of possibilities for those looking to combine their home and business or take advantage of the property''s excellent potential.

Practical features include staff accommodation, solar panels, a double garage, an additional single garage and a carport, ensuring ample parking and convenience.

With its unique dual-home configuration, business rights, generous accommodation and fantastic outdoor spaces, this is a rare opportunity to secure a versatile property in one of Magaliessig''s most desirable areas.

Whether you''re looking for an exceptional family home, dual-living opportunity or a property with business potential, this one is not to be missed!

Magaliessig, is one of the most sought-after suburbs in Johannesburg North known for its safe family living, with quick access to William Nicol off-ramp and major roads. Monte Casino is a few minutes away and has an abundance of restaurants, movie houses, theatres and an amazing bird park. There is something here for everyone. Schools in and near the area include Minnieland Creche & Pre-School, Crawford Prep and College. Brescia House, St Peters Prep, St Stithians, Fourways High and Bryandale. Boutique Home school facilities are also available in the area.
