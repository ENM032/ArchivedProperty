2.5 Bedroom House in Magaliessig

Contact agent for street address
2.5 2.5 2 559 m²

Spacious Single-Level Home with Exceptional Indoor-Outdoor Living

Offering generous proportions, versatile living spaces and an inviting connection to the outdoors, this three-bedroom, three-bathroom home provides comfortable family living with plenty of room to entertain.

One of the standout features of the property is its four spacious open living areas, including two lounges, providing excellent flexibility for both everyday living and entertaining. A separate dining area complements the main lounge, while the additional lounge lends itself equally well to a family room, TV lounge or work-from-home space.

The kitchen is well-proportioned, with ample cabinetry, generous work surfaces, and a convenient breakfast counter, creating an informal space for casual meals while remaining easily accessible to the adjoining living and dining areas.

A particularly appealing feature is the enclosed patio, which effectively extends the home''s living space. Fitted with wide folding glass doors, it can be opened almost completely to the garden, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor entertainment area. From here, the home flows onto a private, established garden and the sparkling swimming pool, creating a relaxed setting for entertaining and enjoying Johannesburg''s warmer months.

Accommodation comprises three bedrooms and three bathrooms. The generously sized main bedroom enjoys direct access towards the pool area and is complemented by an exceptionally spacious walk-in dressing room with extensive fitted cupboards, as well as a large en-suite bathroom with both bath and shower.

The additional bedrooms offer comfortable accommodation for family or guests, while the home''s three bathrooms add further practicality.

Natural light is another feature throughout the property, enhanced by large windows, sliding doors and strategically positioned skylights. Multiple air-conditioning units provide additional comfort.

Outside, the established garden surrounds the entertainment areas with mature planting and greenery, giving the property a private and tranquil feel. The swimming pool and paved entertainment area form an attractive focal point, while a separate utility courtyard provides practical space away from the main garden.

A comfortable and wonderfully versatile home where generous living spaces, an enclosed entertainment area and a private pool garden combine to create an excellent setting for relaxed family living.
