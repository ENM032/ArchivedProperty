Spacious 2 bedroom Home in sought after area

2-Bedroom Home with Separate Flatlet in Secure Magaliessig Complex

Situated in a highly sought-after and secure complex in Magaliessig, this full-title home offers the perfect blend of comfort, convenience, and value.

The main house features 2 well-sized bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, complemented by a beautifully renovated kitchen with modern finishes. The spacious living area flows effortlessly, creating an inviting space for both relaxing and entertaining.

A standout feature of this property is the external, fully equipped flatletideal for extended family, guests, or generating rental income.

Additional highlights include:

- Double garage with ample parking

- Generous garden space, perfect for outdoor living and pets

- Solar panels, offering energy efficiency and reduced electricity costs

- Very low levies, adding to the overall affordability

Located within a popular, secure complex, this home provides peace of mind while being close to key amenities, schools, and major routes.

Magaliessig, is one of the most sought-after suburbs in Johannesburg North known for its safe family living, with quick access to William Nicol off-ramp and major roads. Monte Casino is a few minutes away and has an abundance of restaurants, movie houses, theatres and an amazing bird park. There is something here for everyone. Schools in and near the area include Minnieland Creche & Pre-School, Crawford Prep and College. Brescia House, St Peters Prep, St Stithians, Fourways High and Bryandale. Boutique Home school facilities are also available in the area.
