4 Bedroom House in Magaliessig

59 cornmarsh way
4 3.5 2 405 m² 240 m²

Cluster in sought after estate

Welcome to this stunning double-storey home well located on the Bryanston East border with Magaliessig and situated in a secure and family-friendly estate with low levies, ideal for those seeking a close-knit community where children can safely play in the streets.

As you step inside, youll be welcomed by an abundance of natural light and a seamless flow of open-plan living that beautifully connects indoor and outdoor spaces.

The inviting family room features a cozy wood-burning fireplace, while the spacious dining area is perfect for entertaining. The open-plan kitchen is fully equipped with a gas hob, ample cupboard space, a double fridge, a breakfast nook, and a separate scullery with a double sink and space for two under-counter appliances, plus additional storage.

Conveniently located on the ground floor is a guest loo.

Venturing outside, sliding doors from both the family room and dining area open up to a beautifully landscaped garden. Here, youll find a perfectly sized swimming pool to help you cool off on hot summer days, a covered patio ideal for easy living and entertaining, and a wrap-around garden thats perfect for family gatherings and your furry companions.

Upstairs, youll discover three well-appointed bedrooms and two bathrooms.

The main bedroom is a true sanctuary, featuring large windows, a sliding door that opens to a Juliet-style balcony overlooking the pool and garden, air conditioning, a ceiling fan, and a walk-through dressing area with glass sliding doors leading to a full en-suite bathroom.

The second bedroom also boasts sliding doors balcony style and includes glass sliding door wardrobes.

The third bedroom is thoughtfully positioned within the home, offering breathtaking views and air conditioning.

Additionally, there is a fourth bedroom located outside, which is perfect for a teenage retreat, accommodation for an older parent, staff quarters, or a dedicated workspace.

Key features of this home include:

- Automated double garage

- Electric fencing surrounding the home the estate also has perimeter electric fencing

- Fibre-ready connectivity

- Gas hob

- Wood-burning fireplace

This sought-after estate is ideal for families, offering a strong sense of community, outstanding security, and close proximity to excellent educational institutions such as St. Peter''s Prep, Crawford Schools, Brescia House, Michael Mount, Bryanston Parallel Primary, Fourways High, and Bryanston High.

Enjoy easy access to major roads including Winnie Mandela Drive, Bryanston Drive, Witkoppen, and the N1 highway.
