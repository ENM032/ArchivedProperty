Beautiful 3 bedroom 2 bathroom home for sale in Magaliesig

Situated in a secure estate in Magaliesig this lovely home features a sunroom, large private garden and swimming pool.

The open plan kitchen has ample cupboard & counter space and boasts an eye level oven, gas stove top, a breakfast nook and seperate scullery area with connections for two under counter appliances. A door leads directly into the double garage which has an automated door.

The kitchen opens into the spacious living room and seperate dining room, both have wooden flooring and the adjacent sunroom has large windows and sliding doors.

The covered patio is accessed from either the sunroom or living room and overlooks the front garden space and swimming pool.

A passage way leads to the three bedrooms which all feature tiled floors and full height built in cupboards.

The spacious main bedroom has a dressing area that leads to the full ensuite bathroom. The second and third bedrooms are serviced by the second bathroom which has a bath, toilet and vanity basin.

The garden wraps around the back of the house and a pan handle driveway ensures your privacy with plenty of space for visitors parking

The Estate has 24hr security and intercom access control and is pet friendly, Fibre is installed to the home
