4 Bedroom House in Magaliessig

2 hiskett avenue
4 3.5 4 570 m² 272 m²

Immaculate Cluster Home i Sought After Estate

This stunning residence embodies luxury and contemporary elegance!

Nestled in one of Magaliessig''s most desirable estates, celebrated for its picturesque parks and vibrant community spirit, this home awaits new owners who appreciate peaceful living paired with modern, high-end finishes.

For added tranquility and peace of mind, this is an internal home within estate and super close to one of the beautiful parks within the estate.

This property is move-in ready, having undergone extensive maintenance and renovations, most of which were completed within the last two years. Highlights include a recently top to bottom renovated kitchen with high quality finishes, a covered outdoor patio, new tiling around the property, and a pool that is just 4 years old, among other improvements.

Upon entering, you are welcomed by an abundance of natural light and a seamless flow throughout the space. The dining room and TV room feature high ceilings and open up to the garden, a beautiful kitchen and sunken family room, complete with a wood-burning fireplace, flow out to the covered patio through sliding doors.

The kitchen is a chefs paradise, featuring Caesarstone countertops, ample cupboard space, soft-close drawers, a built-in water purifier, waterproof vinyl flooring, a central island, an eye-level oven, and a separate scullery with space for two under-counter appliances and a stone sink.

This home offers four bedrooms, three bathrooms, and a guest loo, or it can be configured as a three-bedroom home with staff accommodation. The fourth bedroom has a separate entrance and is connected to the house via the garage, providing versatility.

The primary suite serves as a true retreat, featuring a walk-in dressing area that leads to a modern and exquisite bathroom, with the sunny bedroom opening to the exterior through sliding doors.

The second and third bedrooms are equally spacious and share the second full bathroom, complemented by a convenient guest loo.

The fourth bedroom, accessible from the garage, includes air conditioning, a built-in desk, blinds, an en-suite bathroom, and its own entrance, making it suitable for staff accommodation.

Ideal for entertaining, the covered patio boasts a built-in braai and serving counter with under-counter storage, overlooking a beautifully landscaped garden and pool, perfect for warm summer days.

- Double garage with internal access and built-in cupboards for additional storage garage door motor is just 8 months old

- Irrigation system with a timer

- Linen cupboard

- Blinds in most rooms

- Wi-Fi booster for enhanced connectivity

- Fiber-ready

The estate fosters a strong sense of community, featuring beautiful parks with playgrounds and braai area along with being very well run and excellent security this is a home you don''t want to miss out on.
