Spacious Five-Bedroom Family Home with Pool, Established Garden and Multiple Living Areas

Set on a generous, tree-filled stand, this substantial Mediterranean-inspired home offers generous family accommodation and multiple living areas and a private garden setting.

The home welcomes you with a distinctive entrance area and built-in bar, creating an inviting first impression. Multiple reception rooms provide excellent family and entertainment options, including a spacious lounge with fireplace, a separate family room overlooking the garden and pool, and a dedicated dining room.

The large kitchen offers ample cupboard and counter space, a central breakfast area, pantry, and practical access to the service areas. While some finishes reflect an earlier style, the home has been well maintained and offers excellent potential for buyers wishing to update and modernise over time.

Accommodation comprises five well-sized bedrooms and three and a half bathrooms. The main bedroom is generously proportioned and features a dedicated dressing area, ample built-in cupboard space, direct access to a private enclosed courtyard, and an en-suite bathroom with a bath, toilet and vanity.

Upstairs, an additional en-suite bedroom offers timber ceilings, arched windows and built-in cupboards, making it ideal as a guest suite, private teen bedroom or second main-style bedroom. The remaining bedrooms are well proportioned and offer flexibility for family, guests or work-from-home use.

Outdoor living is a major feature of the property. The established garden is private and leafy, with mature trees, generous lawned areas, a swimming pool and a thatched entertainment area ideal for relaxed weekends, family gatherings and entertaining.

Additional features include domestic accommodation, laundry room, two storerooms, a double automated garage and a double carport.

This is a spacious, character-filled home offering substantial accommodation, excellent entertainment potential and a beautiful garden setting.
