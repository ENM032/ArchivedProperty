Situated in the Prime Location of Bryanston: A Spacious and Elegant Home Awaits

Nestled in a highly sought-after and secure enclave within the vibrant neighbourhood of Bryanston, this elegant and spacious home offers an unparalleled opportunity for discerning buyers.

Located just minutes from major roads and essential amenities, the property''s central position emphasizes its prime real estate investment value.

Upon entering through the grand front gates, you''ll be greeted by a sizable front courtyard, providing ample parking space and a carport for two vehicles and two garages for the residence. An aviary equipped with ultraviolet heating lights offers a unique feature in the meticulously manicured front garden.

The majestic double-volume entrance boasts breathtaking views of the lush garden and elegantly divides the house. To the right, you will find the family room and a generously equipped kitchen featuring ample cupboard space, a pantry, scullery, a fireplace, two built-in ovens, a steamer, a Miele microwave, and Siemens gas and electric stove. To the left, enormous living spaces unfold, including formal dining and entertainment rooms, marked by double-volume ceilings and leading into an enclosed patio with a built-in braai, pool room, and more.

Rich in character, the double-sided fireplace and underfloor heating throughout the house except in the passages add warmth, while air conditioning in every room ensures comfort. A custom-built wine cellar is a unique addition, and a special catio on the ground floor will delight your furry friends.

Safety is paramount, including a safety gate to secure the top floor at night, electric fencing, and complete security. The property''s north-facing aspect further enhances its appeal.

On the second floor, three more bedrooms await, including the master suite with a steam shower and outdoor shower. The opulent marble tiling in the bathrooms, double-glazed windows, and 30 solar panels add to the luxurious feel. Remarkably, the area does not experience load shedding, and the house is partially off-grid.

The stunning garden, graced with mature trees, affords privacy and features a massive, inviting pool. Additional amenities include a domestic room, staff bathroom, and extra storage.
