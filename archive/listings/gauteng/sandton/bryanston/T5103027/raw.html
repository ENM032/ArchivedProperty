3 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
3 3 2 212 m²

Exceptional Modern Top-Quality Home

This 3 Bedroom, 3.5 Bathroom European-style designer townhouse exudes excellence with top quality fittings and fixtures. Offering so many extras including automated roll-down security shutters perfect for lock-up-and-go, security cameras, European window designs with intelligent multi-directional opening and closing system, privacy shutters, High-gloss porcelain tiling throughout, centralised vacuum system, and so much more.

Spacious open-plan living at it''s best. The modern designer kitchen boasts high-gloss cabinetry, with Caesarstone countertops and a feature waterfall island with LED lighting. Space for a double fridge, all SMEG appliances including an induction stove and oven, and 3 under-counter SMEG appliances which are all included. The kitchen flows to the open-plan living area consisting of a lounge area and dining room. These areas open out through glass sliding doors to a small no-maintenance garden area. Completing downstairs is a guest toilet.

The floating staircase is a feature in it''s own right. Upstairs you will find 3 bedrooms luxuriously carpeted, all with ensuite modern bathrooms. The spacious main bedroom also boasts a walk-through dressing room with ample closet space and a centre island.

The property also offers a double garage. Situated within a sought-after upmarket complex with only 15 units, which offers good security and perfect location.
