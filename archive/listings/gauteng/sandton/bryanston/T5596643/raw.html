4 Bedroom House in Bryanston

14 cypress avenue
4 2 6 1 997 m² 375 m²

Elegant Family Entertainer with Pool, Garden & Backup Power

A beautiful blend of space, style and effortless indoor-outdoor living

Welcome to a home designed for comfortable family living and unforgettable entertaining. This beautifully presented four-bedroom residence offers generous proportions, stylish finishes and a seamless connection between its inviting interiors and lush outdoor spaces.

The heart of the home is the spacious open-plan living area, creating an easy flow between the lounge, dining space and kitchen. Large openings lead effortlessly outdoors to a covered entertainment patio with a built-in braai, overlooking the decked swimming pool area and beautifully manicured garden, the perfect setting for relaxed weekends, summer gatherings and entertaining family and friends.

The modern open-plan kitchen is both stylish and practical, featuring sleek granite countertops, a gas stove with extractor and a separate scullery with space for three appliances. Everything has been thoughtfully designed to make everyday living as effortless as possible.

Accommodation comprises of four generously sized bedrooms and two spacious full bathrooms, complemented by a separate guest toilet. The generous layout provides plenty of room for the whole family while offering comfortable privacy when guests come to stay.

Adding to the home''s versatility is staff accommodation with its own en-suite bathroom, which could easily be transformed into a private work-from-home office, studio, guest suite or additional living space to suit your lifestyle.

Outside, the home truly comes into its own. The lush, manicured garden provides a beautiful private backdrop, while the decked swimming area creates an inviting resort-style atmosphere. The covered patio and built-in braai make outdoor entertaining effortless, whatever the occasion.

Practicality and security have also been carefully considered, with a double automated garage, electric fencing, secure perimeter wall and backup power providing peace of mind and uninterrupted convenience.
