This stunning modern home prestigiously positioned in Bryanston within convenient access to excellent private schools, upmarket shopping facilities and all major arterials. Perfectly blending contemporary design with exceptional functionality, this residence offers an unparalleled lifestyle of comfort, elegance, and convenience.

ACCOMODATION

The welcoming entrance sets the tone of tranquillity with a water feature. Upon entering, you are greeted by a striking double-volume entrance area, where natural light pours in through a full-length window, creating a bright and airy atmosphere. This space seamlessly flows into the sunken formal lounge, featuring a cozy fireplace that adds warmth and charm, while the stacker doors open up to a spacious patio with braai area, beautiful garden with pool, making this home ideal for entertaining.

The open plan kitchen is a chefs dream, showcasing high end furnishes and appliances, including a 5 burner Smeg gas stove ans Smeg Ddoucble ovens. A large scullery with space for appliances and a spacious pantry making it both stylish and functional.

The living area is equally inviting adjacent to the dining room features an exquisite double sided gas fireplace and a wine cellar glass enclosed under the staircase.

The home''s thoughtful layout includes a spacious study, which can also serve as a guest bedroom. Upstairs, the 4 bedrooms are generously sized, with the main bedroom featuring a luxurious en-suite bathroom with a soaking tub for the ultimate relaxation experience.

Additional highlights include a domestic quarter, excellent security features with CCTV and parameter electric fencing, and the convenience of being part of a secure, well-maintained complex. Enjoy purified drinking water at all times - A built in water filtration system, ensures that every shower, tap and appliances use filtered water.

Every detail in this home has been carefully considered and designed for comfort and luxury.
