3 Bedroom House in Bryanston

11 andrew murray road
3 3 2 2 481 m² 518 m²

Immaculate Home with separate income-producing two bedroom cottage. This fully renovated home features exceptional finishes throughout. Boasting a private guardhouse at the main gate and surrounded by high walls with electric fencing, this home is a haven for the safety-conscious.

A portico entrance, a solid swivel door in carved wood and high ceilings welcome you to this home, meticulously cared for and renovated by its owners.

Living rooms open onto an expansive, lush garden, an entertainer''s gazebo, a fish pond and a feature pool. Reception rooms include a formal lounge with a wood fireplace, a dining room boasting high ceilings and TVFamily room. The bar room has granite tops, ample cupboards, and a fireplace shared with the lounge.

The kitchen boasts tiled flooring, granite tops, a pantry cupboard, and a breakfast counter. A large, separate laundry cupboard next to the kitchen houses 4 appliances, ensuring ease of movement and practicality.

The main bedroom is en suite with a dressing area and full bathroom. There are three further well-sized, light-filled bedrooms. The fourth bedroom could be used as a study. 2 Full bathrooms. 3-Phase, prepaid electricity. Borehole.

BONUS: Completely detached from the home, with a separate driveway entrance and fully walled, is a spacious 200 m² cottage. Carport, loungedining room, kitchen, two bedrooms, one bathroom with a shower. This income-producing property, with independent water and prepaid electricity meters, potentially generates R120 000 per year.
