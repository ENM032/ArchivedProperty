5 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address

Newly Renovated Family Home with a Magical Indoor-Outdoor Lifestyle

Asking Price: R5,5m

Stand: 1,200 sqm | Under Roof: 350 sqm

Tucked away from the road behind a wide, double-gated driveway, this beautifully renovated family home combines contemporary style with relaxed, effortless living. Designed to embrace its tranquil surroundings, the home offers a seamless indoor-outdoor lifestyle, with light-filled receptions flowing to an exceptional entertainment patio overlooking the sparkling pool and landscaped gardens.

At the heart of the home is a stunning open-plan designer kitchen featuring a generous centre island, wide gas stove and quality finishesperfectly positioned to connect with the living spaces and outdoor entertainment areas.

Offering three spacious reception rooms and four generous bedrooms 2 en suite and 2 sharing a bathroom, the flexible layout easily adapts to your lifestyle. The fourth bedroom, together with an adjoining reception, creates the ideal work-from-home office, teenage retreat, guest suite or self-contained granny cottage.

The expansive covered entertainment area is designed for memorable gatherings, complete with extensive serving counters, space for fridges and a gas braai, all overlooking the pool and gardens on both sides of the home.

An outdoor studio provides the opportunity to create a staff suite, guest accommodation or additional work-from-home space. Currently no plumbing and electricity
