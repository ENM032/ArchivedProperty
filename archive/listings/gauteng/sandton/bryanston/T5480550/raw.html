Spacious 4 Bedroom Bryanston Residence | 280m² Boutique Living.

Introducing a new level of space and functionality within The Merlin, Bryanston. A BRAND NEW boutique full-title development now launching only 10 units!

Offering 280m² of living space, this 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home is designed for families who value both comfort and flow. The expansive open-plan living area connects effortlessly to a large covered patio, creating an ideal setting for entertaining and everyday living.
