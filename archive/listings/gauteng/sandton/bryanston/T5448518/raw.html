4 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 3 1 800 m² 970 m²

Exclusive Mandate - Sophisticated Bryanston Living in a Stunning Georgian-Inspired Home

Seller will consider offers from R9 500 000.

Set within a beautifully landscaped garden, this exceptional Georgian-inspired residence offers timeless elegance, generous proportions, and refined living in one of Bryanstons most desirable locations.

Upon entering, you are welcomed by a grand entrance hall that immediately draws your eye through the home to the lush garden beyond, creating a sense of space, light, and tranquillity. The home has been thoughtfully designed with expansive living areas that flow seamlessly from one space to the next, balancing sophistication with effortless comfort.

At the heart of the home lies a magnificent chefs kitchen, appointed with integrated fridge and freezer units, premium Siemens appliances, a substantial centre island, and a spacious scullery and pantry. The kitchen transitions gracefully into a warm and inviting lounge featuring a gas fireplace and lovely views across the manicured garden and covered patio.

The formal dining room sits elegantly just off the kitchen, offering a striking setting for entertaining while maintaining its own unique character and charm.

Further along the home, a refined formal lounge with beautiful built-in cabinetry provides an additional space for relaxed yet sophisticated living. The ground floor also includes a guest cloakroom and access to a beautifully appointed private cottage or teenage suite. This self-contained space offers its own lounge, separate kitchen, a spacious bedroom with a full en-suite bathroom, private entrance, and its own garage ideal for guests, extended family, or independent living.

Ascending the graceful staircase, the upper level reveals an equally impressive private living area. Here you will find abundant cabinetry, a generous study, a gym, and three beautifully proportioned en-suite bedrooms. Each bedroom enjoys tranquil views over the garden and features full bathrooms.

The main suite is a serene private sanctuary, complete with a charming fireplace, an expansive walk-in dressing room, and a luxurious bathroom that flows seamlessly from the dressing area creating a truly indulgent retreat.

The additional two bedrooms are equally spacious and both feature walk-in closets. The study is perfectly positioned for working from home, overlooking the entrance to the property, while the gym offers versatile space that could easily serve as a pajama lounge or recreational room.

The outdoor living spaces are designed for effortless entertaining and relaxation. A large covered patio overlooks the lush garden and sparkling swimming pool, while a built-in braai creates the perfect setting for gatherings and leisurely afternoons.

Completing this remarkable home are three garages and staff accommodation comprising two bedrooms, a kitchen, and a bathroom.

This distinguished residence offers classic architectural beauty combined with modern comfort an exceptional opportunity to enjoy elegant living in the heart of Bryanston.
