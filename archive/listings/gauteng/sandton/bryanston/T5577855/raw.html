5 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address

MOVE-IN-READY FIVE-BEDROOM FAMILY HOME WITH SOLAR, WATER BACKUP AND SUPERB ENTERTAINMENT SPACES

Set within one of Bryanston''s most sought-after secure estates, this beautifully maintained five-bedroom residence offers generous proportions, excellent indoor-outdoor living and all the modern conveniences required for an effortless family lifestyle.

Thoughtfully upgraded and ready to move into, the home combines elegant, light-filled interiors with a private landscaped garden, sparkling swimming pool, solar power and a comprehensive water-backup system.

LIGHT-FILLED LIVING AND ENTERTAINMENT

Designed for relaxed family living and sophisticated entertaining, the home offers multiple reception areas, including an expansive open-plan lounge and dining room, as well as a separate familyTV lounge opening directly onto the pool area.

The central living spaces are filled with natural light and flow seamlessly onto a covered entertainment patio with a built-in braai.

Enclosed by stylish aluminium stacking doors, the patio becomes a warm and inviting additional living space during the cooler months. In summer, the doors open completely to connect the interior with the swimming pool and beautifully landscaped garden.

Whether hosting family and friends or enjoying a quiet afternoon beside the pool, this versatile entertainment area can be enjoyed throughout the year.

CONTEMPORARY OPEN-PLAN KITCHEN

Positioned at the heart of the home, the elegant open-plan kitchen offers generous cabinetry, a preparation sink, a four-plate Smeg gas hob with a dedicated 19kg gas installation, and space for a large refrigerator.

A separate scullery provides a double sink and space for three appliances, while the additional laundry room offers further practical convenience.

FIVE SPACIOUS BEDROOMS UPSTAIRS ACCOMMODATION

All five bedrooms are located upstairs and feature Samsung aircons and quality laminated flooring, ensuring year-round comfort.

The luxurious main suite is a peaceful private retreat with ample built-in cupboards, a full en-suite bathroom featuring a spa bath, and its own balcony overlooking the beautifully landscaped garden.

The spacious second bedroom includes built-in cupboards and a private en-suite bathroom with a shower.

Bedrooms three and four are generously proportioned and offer abundant cupboard space-ideal for children, extended family or guests.

The bright and spacious fifth bedroom enjoys direct access to a balcony overlooking the immaculate garden.

EXTENSIVE RECENT UPGRADES

One of this home''s greatest advantages is that the major improvements have already been completed, allowing its new owners to simply move in and enjoy.

Recent upgrades include:

- Premium porcelain floor tiles in the kitchen, lounge and patio

- Aluminium stacking doors enclosing the entertainment patio

- Aluminium windows and exterior doors on the ground floor

- Smeg four-plate gas hob with a dedicated 19kg gas installation

- Laminated flooring throughout all five bedrooms

- Exterior gates operated by two Centurion remote-controlled motors

SOLAR POWER AND WATER BACKUP

The home is equipped for greater energy efficiency and everyday peace of mind with:

- 5kW Deye inverter

- 5kW Deyness lithium battery

- 8 solar panels

- Two 1,000-litre JoJo water tanks

- Intelligent automatic pressure pump designed to maintain strong, consistent water pressure comparable to the municipal supply

ADDITIONAL FEATURES

- Underfloor heating in the lounge, dining area and 2 main bedrooms

- Gas fireplace

- Separate laundry room

- Guest cloakroom

- Outside toilet

- Linen cupboard

- Under-stair storage with potential for conversion into a wine cellar

- Alarm security system

- Double automated garage

- Automated irrigation system

- Sparkling swimming pool

- Manicured private garden

- Camera system inside and outside the house

- Safely located inside the estate away from the boundary walls

- Fibre connectivity

PRIME BRYANSTON LOCATION

The estate is conveniently positioned close to many of Bryanston and Sandton''s leading schools, shopping destinations, medical facilities and recreational amenities.

RETAIL

Bryan Park Shopping Centre, Nicolway Bryanston, Bryanston Shopping Centre, Hobart Grove Centre, On Nicol, Sloane Square, Epsom Downs Shopping Centre, Sandton City and Nelson Mandela Square.

FITNESS AND RECREATION

Bryanston Country Club, Bryanston Sports Club, Virgin Active, Planet Fitness, Field and Study Recreation Centre, F45 Training, BODYTEC, ProForm Wellness, ATG CrossFit, Bryanston Fight Club, Ultimate Athlete SA and BODY20.

HEALTHCARE

Mediclinic Sandton, NHC Medical and Dental Centre Bryanston and Bryanston Medical &amp Dental Centre at Nicolway.

EDUCATION

St Stithians College, Curro Bryanston Preschool, Bryanston Parallel Medium School, Bryanston Primary School, Bryneven Primary School, Bryandale Primary School, Infinity College, Crawford International, Sandton School Group, Brescia House School, The King''s College and Preparatory School, Bryanston High School, Michael Mount Waldorf School and O''Summit International School.

TRANSPORT

Convenient access to the Gautrain, Rea Vaya bus services, Uber, Bolt and local tuk-tuk services.
