Airy 3-Bedroom Loft Duplex Nestled Among Forest Flowers in Leafy Estate

Tucked away in a tranquil, greenbelt corner of a secure, well-maintained complex, this unique loft-style duplex townhouse offers a harmonious blend of space, light, and modern comfort. Surrounded by lush greenery and just steps from the complexs sparkling communal pool, the home enjoys an enviable position perfect for those who appreciate nature and a peaceful atmosphere.

Step inside to a spacious open-plan living area that comfortably accommodates both lounge and dining spaces. Large glass doors lead out onto a private patio and generous garden ideal for entertaining or simply enjoying a quiet moment outdoors. The modern kitchen features sleek cabinetry, ample workspace, and a gas hob, making it as practical as it is stylish.

Downstairs you''ll find two well-proportioned bedrooms that share a full guest bathroom, perfect for family living or hosting visitors. The upstairs level is a standout feature a sprawling main bedroom with enough space for a cozy pyjama lounge, home office, or reading nook. A private bathroom completes this upper-level retreat, offering privacy and comfort in equal measure.

Additional features include a single lock-up garage and access to forest and riverside walking trails nearby. With 24-hour security, beautifully maintained grounds, and an unbeatable location within the complex, this home is an ideal choice for those seeking a blend of convenience, style, and natural beauty.
