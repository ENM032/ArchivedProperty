Modern Contemporary 3 Bedroom 3 Bathroom Exclusive Home in Bryanston

Situated in the highly sought-after suburb of Bryanston, this exceptional 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom residence with an additional guest bathroom offers a rare opportunity to own one of the most unique properties within a secure estate environment.

Designed with modern contemporary finishes throughout, this home welcomes you into a spacious open-plan layout that effortlessly blends comfort with stylish living. The well-appointed kitchen features ample cabinetry and workspace, perfectly positioned to flow seamlessly into the dining area, creating an inviting setting for both everyday family living and entertaining guests.

The generously sized lounge area extends out onto a large covered patio, complete with fitted roller blinds to provide additional privacy when required. This versatile outdoor living space allows for year-round enjoyment, whether hosting gatherings or simply unwinding in a peaceful and private setting.

Two of the three well-proportioned bedrooms offer full en-suite bathrooms, providing convenience and comfort, while air-conditioning installed in both the bedrooms and living area ensures a comfortable indoor climate throughout all seasons.

This home truly distinguishes itself from the rest within the estate by offering exclusive access as well as three full floors dedicated entirely to this property a rare feature that enhances both privacy and functional living space. The upper level boasts a stunning upstairs patio that showcases scenic views across the Bryanston landscape. During both the early morning hours and late afternoons, the surrounding skyline comes to life, offering a tranquil backdrop ideal for relaxation or entertaining.

The property is located within a secure estate featuring 24-hour security, electric fencing, an intercom system, and motion-detection CCTV surveillance along the boundary walls, providing residents with peace of mind and a secure lifestyle.

Bryanston remains one of Johannesburgs most desirable residential suburbs, known for its established infrastructure, tree-lined streets, and close proximity to a wide range of amenities. Residents enjoy convenient access to leading schools, shopping centres, fine dining restaurants, fitness facilities, and major business hubs such as Sandton, making it an ideal location for both families and professionals alike.

With easy connectivity to main arterial routes and nearby commercial districts, this home combines the tranquillity of estate living with the convenience of urban accessibility.

A truly rare and exclusive opportunity to secure a distinctive multi-level home in one of Bryanstons most sought-after estate settings.
