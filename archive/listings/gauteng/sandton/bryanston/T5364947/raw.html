Smart, State-of-the-Art Bryanston Oasis Renovated Home plus Cottage

Calling nature lovers. Tucked away in a tranquil, private setting, this young, trendy and beautifully renovated Bryanston designer home is surrounded by mature trees and abundant birdlife, offering a rare sense of peace with the convenience of city living.

A striking double-volume entrance with high ceilings welcomes you into a light-filled, open-plan living space.

The dining area flows seamlessly into the eat-in gourmet kitchen, while the spacious main reception opens outdoors to a super-sized swimming pool with automated cover, perfect for effortless entertaining and relaxed family living.

The luxury main suite enjoys garden views and a designer bathroom, creating a serene private retreat. Downstairs accommodation includes three well-proportioned bedrooms sharing a stylish designer bathroom with a shower, as well as a guest cloakroom.

The gourmet kitchen features a gas stove and quartz countertops, overlooks the garden, and opens to a private courtyard leading to a separate laundry and staff cloakroom.

Adding exceptional value, the separate cottage offers:

- Upstairs: One bedroom, kitchenette and bathroom
