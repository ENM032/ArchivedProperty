Spacious North-Facing Family Home on a Prime Bryanston Stand

Positioned on a prime stand in Bryanston, this spacious north-facing family home offers an exceptional opportunity to create your dream residence. Filled with natural sunlight and boasting generous proportions throughout, the home welcomes you with a large entrance hall, perfect for displaying feature artwork and creating a lasting first impression.

A dedicated home office or study provides the ideal work-from-home space, while the expansive open-plan living and dining area flows effortlessly onto a sun-drenched patio, creating an inviting setting for family living and entertaining.

A separate TV room also opens onto the patio and established garden, overlooking the swimming pool and offering additional space for relaxation. The well-appointed kitchen features granite countertops, ample built-in cupboards, a double oven, and both gas and electric hobs.

A separate scullery and laundry area ensure practicality, while the adjoining breakfast room provides the perfect spot for casual family meals. The bedroom wing comprises three generously sized north-facing bedrooms and two bathrooms. The impressive master suite is exceptionally spacious, offering abundant built-in cupboards and a large en-suite bathroom.

The property also boasts a double garage, covered carports for six vehicles, and a long driveway that provides ample visitor parking while offering children plenty of space to ride bicycles safely. The beautiful garden and sparkling swimming pool create the perfect outdoor setting for family enjoyment and entertaining.

This generously sized home offers endless potential, and with a little TLC, it can be transformed into an outstanding family residence in one of Bryanstons most sought-after locations.
