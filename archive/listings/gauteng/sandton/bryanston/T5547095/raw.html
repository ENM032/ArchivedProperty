A Timeless Bryanston Estate | Grand Family Living on Prestigious Shepherd Avenue

A residence of exceptional quality and enduring elegance, envokes the classic sophistication of Cape Towns Bishops Court.

Set on approximately 2,000m² of beautifully landscaped gardens, the home has been meticulously maintained and showcases a level of craftsmanship and construction rarely found today. Every space has been thoughtfully designed to offer effortless family living while providing an exceptional setting for entertaining.

Elegant reception rooms flow seamlessly to an expansive covered patio and a striking infinity pool, creating a natural connection between indoor and outdoor living.

The accommodation comprises four spacious upstairs bedroom suites, each with its own en-suite bathroom, while a beautifully appointed, self-contained cottage offers a kitchen, living area and French doors opening onto a charming Juliet balconyideal for guests, extended family or a private work-from-home space.

Additional features include four automated garages, a fully equipped gym, a separate scullery, extensive staff accommodation for two, and 3 gas fireplaces that bring warmth and character throughout the home.

This is a residence defined not only by its scale and elegance, but by its integrity. Beautifully proportioned, impeccably maintained and built to an uncompromising standard, it is a home designed to be enjoyed for generations.

A rare opportunity to acquire a truly distinguished family estate in the heart of Bryanston.
