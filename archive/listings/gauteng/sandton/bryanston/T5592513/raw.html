5 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
5 5.5 6 1 000 m² 600 m²

Elegant 5-Bedroom En-Suite cluster in Exclusive 24-Hour Guarded Estate

EXCLUSIVE SOLE MANDATE

Viewings by appointment on Saturday 22nd August between 14:00-16:00. Please contact the agent to arrange.

Welcome to an exceptional five-bedroom en-suite residence, set within one of Bryanston Wests most exclusive and well managed estates.

Comprising just 25 homes, with 24-hour guarded security, this upmarket complex offers a sense of privacy and tranquility in a prime Bryanston West location, ideally positioned for Kings College, St Stithians and Brescia House.

Set on approximately 1000 square metres, with an impressive - 600 square metres under roof, this north-facing residence is flooded with natural light, while the beautifully established and meticulously maintained garden creates a tranquil backdrop to the home.

From the moment you enter, the double-volume entrance hall, framed by expansive glass windows, creates an immediate sense of space, light and understated elegance.

The living spaces have been thoughtfully designed for both relaxed family living and effortless entertaining. The lounge and TV room open directly onto the expansive enclosed patio, while the dining room enjoys a connection to a private courtyard. The heart of the home is undoubtedly the exceptionally large enclosed patio, where stacking doors open completely to the garden, creating a seamless transition between the interior and exterior spaces. With dedicated dining and lounge areas, a serving counter and wood-burning braai, it is an inviting setting for relaxed gatherings and year-round entertaining.

The kitchen is stylish and beautifully appointed, featuring Caesarstone countertops, AEG appliances, a combination gas and electric hob, and a separate scullery with space for three appliances.

Accommodation is exceptionally versatile, offering four luxurious en-suite bedrooms plus a study, or the option of five en-suite bedrooms. The private guest suite is positioned on the lower level and features its own dressing room, en-suite bathroom and direct access to the garden.

Upstairs, the secure bedroom level offers a generous main suite with a walk-in dressing room, full bathroom and doors opening onto a private balcony. The additional bedrooms are beautifully appointed, each with an en-suite bathroom and generous dressing facilities, with two enjoying access to a shared balcony with views.

Behind the elegance, the home has been thoughtfully equipped for modern living, with underfloor heating throughout and an energy-backup system comprising a Sunsynk 8kVa hybrid inverter, two 4.95kW Greenrich batteries and twelve 500W JA Solar panels.

A triple garage, with a roller-shutter door providing drive-through access to a private rear courtyard and additional parking, together with staff accommodation, gardeners ablution facilities, storage and excellent security, completes this exceptional offering.

Beautifully presented, wonderfully private and exceptionally versatile, this distinguished residence offers an elegant Bryanston lifestyle within an intimate and secure estate.

A home of substance, warmth and understated sophistication where generous proportions, beautiful gardens and effortless indoor-outdoor living come together.

Viewings by appointment. Please contact the agent to arrange.
