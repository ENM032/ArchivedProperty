Modern Spacious Cluster Home within Secure Estate

Seller Considering All Serious Offers.

The exterior has been freshly painted.

Welcome to the modern and luxurious world of this upmarket cluster, situated in a secure complex in the sought-after area of Bryanston. With its contemporary design, this property offers a lifestyle of comfort and sophistication.

Upon entering, you are greeted by an open-plan layout that creates a seamless flow throughout the home. The spacious living areas provide the perfect space for entertaining guests. The beautifully designed kitchen is a chef''s dream, boasting a gas stove, ample cupboard space, and a designated area for a double fridge. The scullery is conveniently equipped to accommodate three under-counter appliances, ensuring a clutter-free and efficient workspace.

The ground floor encompasses the dining room, kitchen, and a TV room, providing a variety of areas to relax and unwind. For those tranquil mornings, an intimate atrium awaits, offering an ideal spot for enjoying a cup of coffee or reading a book.

Step out from the dining room onto a spacious covered patio, adorned with American blinds that can be effortlessly folded open.

Moving upstairs, you will discover three well-appointed bedrooms. The first bedroom boasts an en-suite bathroom complete with a bath, toilet, and basin. The second bedroom features its own shower, toilet, and basin, and it opens onto a shared balcony, allowing you to relish the outdoors from the comfort of your room. The main bedroom exudes elegance and offers a walk-through closet and a full en-suite bathroom with double basins, providing a private sanctuary for relaxation and rejuvenation. Sharing the balcony with the second bedroom is a spacious pyjama lounge, which can serve as a cozy retreat or an additional entertainment area.

The complex itself is designed with your safety and peace of mind in mind, offering 24-hour security. Furthermore, its convenient location places you just minutes away from Sandton City, the bustling hub of premier shopping, dining, and entertainment.

In summary, this modern, upmarket cluster in a secured complex in Bryanston presents a contemporary lifestyle coupled with exceptional features. With its open-plan layout, beautiful kitchen, ample storage, covered patio with garden views, three bedrooms all with en-suite bathrooms, and the added benefit of 24-hour security, this property is an exquisite gem.
