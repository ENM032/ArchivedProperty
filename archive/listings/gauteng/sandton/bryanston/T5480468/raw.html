Boutique Bryanston Living | 3 Bed | 248m² | Private Garden

The Omega offers a refined entry into The Merlin, a boutique collection of just 10 exclusive homes in Bryanston.

This 248m² residence features 3 spacious bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and a well-balanced layout designed for comfortable, modern living. The open-plan kitchen, dining, and lounge area flows seamlessly onto a covered patio, creating a natural indoor-outdoor lifestyle ideal for entertaining.

Upstairs, the master suite includes a full en-suite bathroom, while additional bedrooms are generously sized and serviced by well-appointed bathrooms.

With a double garage, private garden, and functional scullery, the Omega delivers practical luxury in a limited, high-end development.
