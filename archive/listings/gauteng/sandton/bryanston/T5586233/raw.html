5 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
5 5 7 1 000 m² 596 m²

Some homes simply accommodate a life. This one elevates it.

Tucked discreetly on a private panhandle in Bryanston, this newly renovated contemporary residence offers 596 sqm of refined living across a full 1 000 m2 stand. Five bedrooms, each appointed with its own state-of-the-art bathroom, a dedicated dressing room, and a built-in workstation, create a sense of quiet generosity and considered privacy throughout. The heart of the home is an elegant open-plan kitchen that flows into expansive living spaces, while a separate dining room stands ready for long, unhurried evenings. The drawing room has a beautifully featured gas fireplace for cosy evenings.

Yet it is the outdoor realm that lingers longest in the memory. A broad covered veranda opens onto a still koi pond where the water moves gently beneath the reflections of trees. From here the garden unfolds with deliberate calm a fire pit set for evenings that stretch late into the night. This pool catches the changing light, a jacuzzi for quiet restoration, and sculptural stones placed with intention throughout a landscape designed for stillness. This is not a garden in the ordinary sense. It is a private outdoor world that invites the family to slow down, to gather, and to feel the city recede.

Every modern necessity has been considered with equal care. Comprehensive security, generator and inverter backup, fibre connectivity, automated blinds, aluminium windows, and full air-conditioning ensure the experience remains effortless. The renovation is recent and thorough, executed to a consistently high contemporary standard.

Bryanston continues to reward those who understand its enduring appeal. It remains one of the few Johannesburg suburbs where demand stays both deep and resilient drawn by top schools, genuine privacy, and proximity to Sandtons economic heart without its intensity.

With staff quarters and outdoor living of genuine distinction on a secluded panhandle. In a market where homes of this completeness and calm are increasingly scarce, it represents both a rare lifestyle opportunity and a considered long-term holding.

Private viewings by appointment only.
