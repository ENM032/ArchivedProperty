3 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
3 4 2 816 m²

Luxurious Modern Living in the Heart of Bryanston East

Step into a home where contemporary design meets effortless luxury. As you enter this beautifully crafted residence, youre welcomed by lush, manicured gardens and a striking double-volume glass entrance that sets the tone for the sophistication within.

The ground floor is designed for both refined living and effortless entertaining. A bespoke cinema room with ambient lighting creates the perfect space for family movie nights or hosting guests. A private office offers a quiet work-from-home retreat, while a stylish guest cloakroom adds convenience.

The expansive open-plan formal lounge flows seamlessly into the dining area and designer kitchen, creating a harmonious living space. The kitchen is both functional and elegant, featuring ample storage, a separate scullery accommodating three under-counter appliances, and a central island with a gas hob. A well-appointed breakfast nook completes this inviting culinary space.

The home flows effortlessly onto a covered patio and entertainment area, fitted with aluminium stacking doors, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor experience while overlooking the garden and sparkling pool.

Additional standout features on this level include surround sound throughout, a built-in fish tank, and bespoke lighting elements that enhance the homes modern aesthetic.

Upstairs, the home continues to impress with three generously sized bedrooms, two bathrooms, and a versatile open-plan areaperfect as a PJ lounge, gym, or relaxation space.

The main bedroom is a private sanctuary, complete with air conditioning, surround sound, and a spacious walk-in closet. The luxurious en-suite bathroom offers a full suite of finishes for ultimate comfort. The second and third bedrooms are equally well-sized, featuring stylish lighting and sharing a full bathroom.

Outdoors, a beautifully designed deck surrounds a heated swimming pool, creating a private oasis perfect for both relaxation and entertaining. The pool is fitted with a heat pump and chlorinator for year-round enjoyment, while its position alongside the entertainment area makes it ideal for hosting or enjoying warm summer days. The garden is fitted with an irrigation system, ensuring easy maintenance and year-round greenery.

Additional features include:

Backup power via inverter

Solar geyser for energy efficiency

Double automated garage

Domestic bathroom and separate storage room

This exceptional home offers a seamless blend of style, comfort, and modern convenienceperfectly positioned in sought-after Bryanston East.
