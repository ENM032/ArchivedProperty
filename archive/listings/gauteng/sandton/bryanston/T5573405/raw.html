4 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 3 1 800 m² 588 m²

Secure Cluster In A Boomed-off Enclave

Set within a secure, gated and guarded enclosure, and further within a residential estate of four luxury homes, this exceptional residence occupies 1,800 sqm of beautifully established gardens, offering complete privacy, security, and an enviable lifestyle.

Beautifully designed and recently renovated with a timeless Mediterranean-inspired aesthetic, the home is flooded with natural light, creating warm, inviting interiors that balance elegance with everyday comfort. Solid wood flooring, generous proportions, and sophisticated finishes combine to create a residence that is both luxurious and effortlessly liveable.

Designed for both relaxed family living and effortless entertaining, the home offers expansive reception areas and generously proportioned lounges that flow seamlessly to the outdoor entertainment spaces. Large windows and doors invite the surrounding gardens indoors, while the landscaped grounds provide a tranquil retreat centred around a heated swimming pool perfect for year round enjoyment.

At the heart of the home is a beautifully appointed, spacious kitchen, offering abundant workspace and storage while serving as the natural gathering place for family and friends.

The accommodation comprises four generously proportioned bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom.

Outside, an impressive motor court providing ample secure parking for residents and guests alike. The established gardens are further complemented by a borehole requiring reconnection, while an inverter system provides added convenience and peace of mind.

Combining timeless Mediterranean elegance, spacious living, abundant natural light, and exceptional outdoor amenities, this remarkable residence presents a rare opportunity to acquire an elegant family home within an exclusive four-home enclave, where luxury, privacy, and lifestyle come together effortlessly.
