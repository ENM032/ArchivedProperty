SPACIOUS HOME IN SECURE, SOUGHT-AFTER BOOM ENCLOSURE

SHOWDAY CANCELLED FOR 23 AUGUST 2026

NEGOTIATING BEST OFFER ABOVE R 5 799 000

Set within the sought-after Hamilton Boom enclosed section of Wilton Avenue, this exceptional home offers generous proportions and modern finishes throughout.

Combining security, privacy, and close proximity to Sandton and an abundance of top schools, this is an opportunity not to be missed.

Upon entering, a grand double-volume entrance hall creates an impressive first impression.

The ground floor features spacious open-plan living, including a lounge, dining room, TV room, and a sleek modern kitchen complete with a centre island, scullery, and walk-in pantry. Stacking doors open onto a covered patio overlooking the pool and garden, creating the perfect space for entertaining. Also on this level are a guest bedroom with a full en-suite bathroom, a guest toilet, wine cellar, and direct access to the double garage.

Upstairs offers four spacious bedrooms, three of which are en-suite, while the fourth could easily serve as a home office or gym. Three of the bedrooms enjoy access to a private balcony.

Additional features include staff accommodation, a full solar system, and a rainwater tank, and ample storage throughout.
