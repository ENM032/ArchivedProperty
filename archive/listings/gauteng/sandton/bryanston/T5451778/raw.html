House For Sale in Bryanston, Sandton

Architectural Masterpiece on The River Road | Bryanston East

Owner Asking R 5 999 000

Only Considering Offer Above R 5 499 000

4 Bedrooms | 3.5 Bathrooms | 2 Garages | Separate Flatlet

The Pinnacle of Modern Living

Set within a boutique, high-security enclave on the prestigious River Road, this architectural masterpiece offers the perfect balance of contemporary design, privacy, and secure estate living. Designed for discerning buyers who appreciate volume, light, and seamless flow, this residence delivers a lifestyle that is both sophisticated and effortless.

Exceptional Living Spaces

From the moment you enter through the striking double-volume glass entrance, you are welcomed by expansive open-plan receptions filled with natural light. Soaring ceilings and floor-to-ceiling glazing create a dramatic yet inviting atmosphere, while the seamless indoor-outdoor integration makes this home ideal for both relaxed family living and stylish entertaining.

Gourmet Lifestyle Kitchen

At the heart of the home lies a sleek, high-gloss designer kitchen fitted with integrated Smeg appliances, a generous central island with breakfast bar seating, and clean contemporary finishes. A discreet, fully equipped scullery accommodates all major appliances, keeping the main space beautifully uncluttered.

Effortless Entertaining

Stacker doors open from the living areas onto a covered entertainers patio complete with a built-in gas braai. Overlooking a private, low-maintenance garden and feature swimming pool, this space is perfectly positioned for year-round gatherings and relaxed weekend afternoons.

Luxury Accommodation

Upstairs, the Master Suite is a private sanctuary featuring:

Boutique-style walk-in dressing room

Designer en-suite bathroom

Free-standing statement bathtub

Double vanity and premium finishes

Three additional bedrooms offer generous proportions, bespoke cabinetry, and excellent natural light.

Separate Flatlet Income or Extended Family

A valuable addition to the property is the self-contained downstairs flatlet with its own garden access. Ideal for rental income, extended family, a work-from-home office, or guest accommodation.

Additional Features

Double automated garage with direct internal access

Guest cloakroom

24-hour manned access control and top-tier security

Secure, boutique cluster setting

Location Excellence Bryanston East

Positioned in one of Johannesburgs most sought-after suburbs, this home enjoys exceptional proximity to:

River Club Golf Course

St Stithians College

Redhill School

Nicolway Bryanston

Morningside Shopping Centre

All within minutes of Sandtons commercial hub, elite schools, and premium retail.

This is more than a home it is a statement of modern luxury and refined cluster living in the heart of Bryanston East.
