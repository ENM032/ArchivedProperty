Unmatched Elegance & Skyline Views: Bryanston''s Crown Jewel

Offers from R17 990 000.00 and asking R18 990 000.00.

Welcome to an unrivaled display of modern sophistication in the prestigious suburb of Bryanston. This magnificent opportunity offers 870m2 of luxury, set on 3867m2 of rolling landscape with prestigious views. This newly constructed, three storey architectural wonder seamlessly blends grand proportions with cutting edge design, offering the ultimate luxurious lifestyle for discerning buyers.

Ground Floor Grandeur Indoor and Outdoor Harmony

Step through a dramatic double-volume entrance hall into expansive, sunlit living spaces framed by recessed ceilings and ambient lighting.

Living &amp Dining: Features formal and informal lounges anchored by a cozy wood-burning fireplace.

Gourmet Chef''s Kitchen: Fitted with top-tier integrated Smeg appliances, a sleek center island, a separate scullery, and a dedicated laundry room.

Executive Suite: Includes a private study for work-from-home convenience and a guest bathroom.

Seamless Entertaining: Expansive stackable glass doors flow out onto a spacious covered patio overlooking a manicured landscaped garden, complete with an automated irrigation system, a sparkling swimming pool, and a dedicated outdoor guest bathroom.

Second Floor Private Sanctuary

4 Luxury Suites: Four generously proportioned, all en-suite bedrooms, each featuring direct access to private balconies with sweeping tree top views.

Master Suite: A lavish retreat offering panoramic skyline vistas through oversized sliding windows, an opulent spa style en-suite bathroom, and custom walk-in closet space.

Family Lounge: A casual pajama lounge and outdoor terrace provide the perfect setting for relaxed family moments.

Rooftop Level The Crown Jewel

Designed for world-class entertaining, the expansive rooftop terrace offers breathtaking, uninterrupted 180-degree views of the Johannesburg skyline. Protected by frameless glass balustrades, this space includes a fully equipped serving station ideal for hosting cocktail evenings under the stars.

Additional High End Features

Comfort &amp Efficiency: Ducted air-conditioning throughout and an energy-efficient heat pump for hot water.

Garages &amp Accommodation: Two automated garages, generous visitor parking, and double staff accommodation equipped with a kitchenette.

Elite Security: State of the art protection including a guard house, electric fencing, perimeter beams, and round the clock CCTV surveillance.

An extraordinary residence where luxury meets function a rare opportunity not to be missed. Contact us today to schedule your private viewing.
