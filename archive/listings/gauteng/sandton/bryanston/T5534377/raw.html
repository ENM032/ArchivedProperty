3 Bedroom House in Bryanston

2 ss ledi 39, 39 villa lane
3 3.5 2 254 m²

Exclusive Four-Level Luxury Townhouse with a Private Penthouse-Style Master Suite

Situated in an exclusive complex of just 12 luxury homes, this unique four-level townhouse offers 254m2 of stylish, versatile living.

The home features spacious open-plan living areas with floor-to-ceiling stack doors opening onto a private patio, a modern Caesarstone kitchen with a gas hob and oven, three generous bedrooms, two-and-a-half bathrooms, plus a versatile ground-floor studio with its own bathroom and kitchenetteideal as guest accommodation, a home office, teenager''s pad or domestic accommodation.

What truly sets this home apart is the spectacular top-floor main suite. Occupying its own private level, it opens through stack doors onto a wraparound balcony with beautiful sunset views and glimpses of the Sandton skylinecreating a luxurious penthouse feel rarely found in townhouse living.

Additional features include a single automated garage, storeroom, built-in gas heaters throughout, alarm system, extra parking and 24-hour security.

A truly unique Bryanston home that needs to be experienced in person.
