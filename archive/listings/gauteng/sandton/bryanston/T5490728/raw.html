A Perfect Loft Home in a perfect locaton

Perfectly positioned in the heart of Bryanston, this exceptional property offers convenience, comfort, and versatile living just minutes from St Stithians College and Sandton Mediclinic. Ideal for families and professionals alike, the home combines spacious interiors with modern finishes.

The main house features four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious loft-style master suite complete with its own private en-suite bathroom. The home also includes a private office, ideal for working from home or creating a quiet study space. A large walk-in safe adds an extra layer of security and practicality for valuable storage.

The newly renovated kitchen boasts contemporary finishes and flows seamlessly into the open-plan living spaces, leading into the lounge and dining room and a spacious scullery area perfect for both relaxed family living and entertaining guests.

Additional features include staff quarters, providing added convenience and flexibility.

Separate from the main residence is a stunning one-bedroom loft cottage offering complete privacy. This beautifully designed space includes its own kitchen, lounge area, and a full bathroom, making it ideal for extended family, guests, rental income, or a work-from-home setup.

Set on a spacious and well-maintained yard, the property offers endless possibilities for outdoor living, landscaping, entertainment areas, or future additions. Clean, expansive, and full of potential, this home is a rare find in one of Bryanstons most desirable locations.
