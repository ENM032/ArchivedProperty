Elegant Georgian Home with Timeless Provencal Charm

Designed by the renowned architect Gavin Warburton, this exceptional Georgian-inspired residence beautifully blends timeless elegance with the warmth and character of the French countryside.

Set on an expansive 3,646 m2 stand with approximately 431 m2 under roof, this gracious home is nestled in a peaceful cul-de-sac, offering both privacy and tranquility.

Authentic wooden sash windows and classic architectural details reflect its Provencal influence, creating a home filled with charm, sophistication, and natural light.

Designed with effortless entertaining in mind, the spacious living areas flows seamlessly to the outdoor entertainment spaces, overlooking a heated salt chlorinated pool - perfect for hosting memorable gatherings with family and friends.

From the moment you enter, you''ll be captivated by the elgant reception rooms, offering gracious spaces for both formal entertaining and relaxed family living. At the heart of the home is a beautifully appointed kitchen, thougfully designed with quatilty finnishes and genrerous workspaces.

The luxuirous main suite is a private santuary, complete with a lavish en-suite bathroom. A separate guest suite provides exception comfort and privacy for visitors, and two additonal bedrooms with a shared bathroom.

Adding incredible value and vesatility is a two-bedroom cottage, ideal for extended family, guests, a work-from-home office, or rental income potential.

Thoughtfully designed for both everyday living and effortless entertaining, the home offers elegant reception rooms, a beautifully appointed kitchen

The covered patio overlooks a sparkling heated saltwater pool and magnificent landscaped gardens, creating the perfect setting for relaxed afternoons and memorable gatherings. The property boasts an extensive 24-panel solar system with inverter and battery backup, a connected generator, borehole with filtration and water storage, fibre internet, CCTV, alarm system and intercom, ensuring comfort and peace of mind.
