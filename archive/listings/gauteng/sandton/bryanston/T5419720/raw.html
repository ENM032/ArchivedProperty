3 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
3 2 3 1 564 m² 369 m²

Prime Bryanston East Opportunity Subdivision in Progress, Renovators Dream

Set in a sought-after, community orientated street in Bryanston East close to Sandton business district, this property presents a rare opportunity to secure a large stand and create your ideal home. This property sits on 1564sqm of the original 4,035sqm stand, currently being subdivided into three stands to be sold separately, meaning the final layout will feature neighbouring homes on either side.

The existing 370sqm home offers solid bones and generous proportions, including 3-bedrooms, 2-bathrooms, a closed kitchen, separate dining and living rooms opening onto a covered patio with a pool. Upstairs is a standalone room accessed via a staircase ideal as a home office, TV lounge or for a renovation project. Buyers may prefer to incorporate this space into a larger renovation project by adding a slab to create a functional upstairs area or alternatively removing it to retain a single-storey layout.

The property requires comprehensive renovation and modernisation, making it ideal for buyers looking to invest in location and add value through upgrades or redesign tailored to their needs. This property presents excellent potential for value creation in a PRIME location.
