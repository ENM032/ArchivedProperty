House For Sale in Bryanston, Sandton

SPACIOUS SINGLE-LEVEL FAMILY HOME ON EVER POPULAR DEVONSHIRE AVENUE

OWNER ASKING R 6 499 000

NEGOTIATING BEST OFFER ABOVE R 4 999 000

Set on the highly sought-after Devonshire Avenue, this exceptional home offers the perfect blend of space, comfort, and convenience, with generously sized bedrooms and expansive living areas throughout.

Upon entering, the welcoming entrance hall leads into the impressive open-plan living spaces, comprising a spacious lounge with stacking doors opening onto a large covered patio, seamlessly connected to the dining area, bar, and modern kitchen with sleek finishes and a separate scullery.

The patio overlooks the expansive garden and swimming pool, creating an ideal setting for entertaining. A guest bathroom conveniently serves the living areas, keeping the private bedroom wing separate.

The accommodation wing features four well-proportioned bedrooms. The spacious main suite includes a walk-in dressing room and en-suite bathroom. Bedroom two enjoys a Jack-and-Jill en-suite, while bedrooms three and four are serviced by a full family bathroom. A versatile fifth room can easily function as an additional bedroom or pyjama lounge and offers further expansion potential by removing dry walling separating the adjacent office space. A second dedicated office at the opposite end of the home provides an excellent work-from-home solution.

Adding further value is a self-contained one-bedroom flatlet complete with a lounge, bathroom, and kitchenette. Features which complete this home include a study, a powerful solar system, borehole for garden use, functional underfloor heating, double garage, ample additional parking, staff accommodation, a garden storage room, and perimeter security beams with CCTV cameras for added peace of mind.
