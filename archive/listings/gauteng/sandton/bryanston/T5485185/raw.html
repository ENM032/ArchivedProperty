Charming and Inviting 4 Bedroom Family Home with Exceptional Indoor-Outdoor Living

Full of warmth and character, this beautifully maintained north-facing family home offers a lovely cottage feel with timeless finishes, generous living spaces, and a magnificent established garden with borehole.

A welcoming entrance that leads into a spacious lounge featuring a huge bay window and a cosy fireplace, creating the perfect setting for relaxed family living. The dining room flows seamlessly into a well-equipped modern kitchen with glass doors and a separate laundry area.

Adding to the homes charm is a private study and an entertainment room complete with an original anthracite fireplace. A second lounge opens onto a covered patio overlooking rolling lawns, a sparkling swimming pool, and the lush established garden.

The home is tiled throughout and offers 4 well-sized bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. The spacious north-facing main bedroom enjoys beautiful natural light and features a walk-in dressing room with additional built-in cupboards and a en-suite bathroom.
