Brand New Luxury Family Home in Secure Bryanston Estate

Experience sophisticated estate living in this beautifully designed brand-new home at 18 Devonshire Avenue, Bryanston. Combining contemporary architecture, premium finishes and generous proportions, this exceptional residence offers the perfect balance of luxury, comfort and effortless family living.

A dramatic double-volume entrance leads into expansive open-plan living areas featuring a stylish lounge with a feature fireplace, an elegant dining area and a designer kitchen with Caesarstone countertops, a large centre island, gas hob, space for a double-door fridgefreezer and a separate scullery and laundry.

Stacking doors create a seamless flow to the spacious garden, making this home ideal for entertaining and relaxed indoor-outdoor living.

Upstairs, the luxurious main suite offers a private retreat with a walk-in dressing room, built-in vanity, a beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom with double vanities, freestanding bath and oversized shower, and sliding doors opening onto a private balcony.

Three additional spacious bedrooms with built-in cupboards share access to a balcony and are serviced by two stylish bathrooms. A versatile upstairs pyjama lounge is perfect as a family TV room, home office, study or children''s playroom.
