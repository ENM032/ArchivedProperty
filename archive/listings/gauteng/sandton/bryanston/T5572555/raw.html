Modern Contemporary 5-Bedroom Designer Home An Entertainers Paradise

Asking Price: R6,000,000

Erf Size: 645 sqm

Under Roof: 487 sqm

Exclusive estate of only 8 homes

Recently built and showcasing striking contemporary architecture, this sophisticated five-bedroom designer residence combines generous proportions, quality finishes and exceptional indoor-outdoor living in a sought-after Bryanston location.

A dramatic double-volume entrance hall creates an impressive welcome, with a floating staircase that serves as a striking architectural feature. The second reception leads to the private cinema and into the expansive main living and entertainment area.

Designed around entertaining, the receptions open onto three sides, flooding the home with natural light and fresh air and creating a seamless connection to the outdoor entertainment paradise.

The entertainment area has something for everyone, featuring a swimming pool, jacuzzi, fire pit, bar and built-in braai area with generous counters, together with space for table tennis, a pool table, mounted TV and relaxed outdoor seating. Feature bulkhead ceilings and carefully considered lighting continue both inside and outdoors, creating a spectacular atmosphere after dark.

At the heart of the home is the designer open-plan kitchen, centred around an impressive Caesarstone island and fitted with a full complement of Smeg appliances, including an integrated fridge and deep freeze, two ovensone a combination microwaveand a wide hob.

The floating staircase leads to a massive upstairs landing overlooking the double-volume entrance hall, flowing to a generous multipurpose area ideal as a pyjama lounge, study or home gym.

Accommodation comprises five en-suite bedrooms, all with excellent cupboard space. Four suites feature full bathrooms with both bath and shower, while the downstairs guest suite has a shower bathroom.

The luxurious main suite is a destination of its own, opening onto both north- and south-facing balconies and featuring a statement bulkhead ceiling, exceptionally spacious dressing room and beautifully appointed designer bathroom.

The second upstairs bedroom enjoys access to a wraparound balcony opening to both the north and south, adding to the home''s wonderful sense of space and openness.

Additional accommodation includes a staff suite with shower bathroom, guest cloakroom and double garage
