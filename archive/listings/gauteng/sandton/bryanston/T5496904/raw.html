Affordable Luxury Simplex Awaits!

Set within an exclusive estate of only four homes and positioned in a secure 24-hour-guarded boomed enclosure, this magnificent simplex cluster offers luxurious, easy living in one of Bryanston Easts most desirable locations.

Designed as the ideal forever home, this spacious residence combines elegant open-plan living with the practicality of single-level convenience perfect for families with young children or those seeking a home without stairs.

The welcoming entrance flows into expansive reception areas with impressive high ceilings, creating a wonderful sense of light and space. Living areas open seamlessly outdoors to a gorgeous private garden, ideal for entertaining, relaxing, or simply enjoying the tranquillity of this quiet setting.

The gourmet open-plan kitchen is beautifully positioned at the heart of the home and is complemented by a separate scullery for added convenience.

Accommodation includes four en-suite bedrooms, with one currently configured as a sophisticated study or work-from-home office. A luxury staff suite also offers excellent flexibility as an additional work-from-home studio, guest suite, or creative space.

Completing the home are a double garage, excellent security, and ample garden space to add a pool, should you desire.
