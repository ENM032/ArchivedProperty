4 Bedroom House in Bryanston

2a st audley road
4 4.5 2 180 m² 170 m²

BRAND-NEW LUXURY LIVING | 4 EN-SUITE BEDROOMS | EXCLUSIVE ESTATE OF ONLY 25 HOMES

Nestled within an exclusive boutique estate of just 25 residences, this brand-new home presents an exceptional opportunity to enjoy sophisticated modern living in one of Bryanston''s most sought-after locations. Designed with meticulous attention to detail and finished to the highest standards, this elegant residence seamlessly combines luxury, comfort, and contemporary style.

At the heart of the home lies a beautifully designed open-plan living space, where floor-to-ceiling stacker doors create a seamless connection between the interior and the private landscaped garden. A stylish outdoor seating area provides the perfect setting for entertaining guests, enjoying relaxed family gatherings, or unwinding with sunset sundowners while embracing the effortless indoor-outdoor lifestyle.

The sleek, ultra-modern kitchen is a true entertainer''s delight, featuring a gas hob, space for three appliances, ample cabinetry, and a sophisticated breakfast bar. Flowing effortlessly into the dining area and spacious lounge, it creates a welcoming environment ideal for both everyday living and hosting.

The ground floor further offers a guest cloakroom and beautifully integrated living and dining spaces that are filled with natural light, creating an inviting and contemporary atmosphere throughout.

Upstairs, four generously proportioned en-suite bedrooms are thoughtfully arranged around a spacious pyjama lounge, providing both privacy and a comfortable family retreat. The luxurious main suite is particularly impressive, featuring a beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom, a walk-through dressing area with premium cabinetry, and a private balcony where you can enjoy tranquil evenings and breathtaking sunsets. The three additional bedrooms are equally well-appointed, each offering its own en-suite bathroom and ample built-in cupboards.

Additional Features:

Double automated garage with direct access into the home

Private landscaped garden

Staff accommodation with en-suite bathroom

Premium, state-of-the-art finishes throughout

Walking distance to Bryanston Shopping Centre, Bryneven Primary School, and several leading nursery schools

Convenient access to major highways, Nicolway Shopping Centre, and Sandton CBD

Offering the perfect balance of luxury, security, and low-maintenance living, this exceptional home is ideally suited to the discerning buyer seeking contemporary elegance in a prime Bryanston address.

An outstanding residence that must be experienced to be fully appreciated.
