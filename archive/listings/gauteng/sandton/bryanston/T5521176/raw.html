4 Bedroom House in Bryanston

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 2 986 m² 585 m²

Set within a secure road enclosure, just minutes from Bryanston Country Club, this exceptional family home combines generous proportions, modern luxury, and effortless entertaining.

Positioned on 986sqm of land with an impressive 585sqm under roof, the home offers expansive open plan living spaces designed for both comfortable family living and stylish hosting.

At the heart of the home is a beautifully appointed kitchen featuring a large centre island, separate scullery, and a striking glass enclosed wine cellar. An open cocktail bar with serving counter and space for an under counter bar fridge adds to the homes impressive entertainment offering.

Large stacking doors open onto an expansive covered patio overlooking the garden and swimming pool, seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living. A feature wood burning fireplace ensures year round enjoyment, while a separate home office and guest cloakroom complete the ground floor.

Upstairs, a spacious pyjama lounge leads to four oversized en-suite bedrooms, all opening onto a shared balcony. The luxurious main suite offers exceptional space, ample cupboard space, and a beautifully finished full bathroom.

Additional features include staff accommodation, a double garage, and so much more.
