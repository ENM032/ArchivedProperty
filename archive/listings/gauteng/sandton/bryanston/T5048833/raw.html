Elegant Property Investment Dream in Bryanston East Central

Family Home Investment Property with Cottages & Lease Income in Sought-After Bryanston East.

Seller Accepting Reasonable Offers!!

Land Size: 4051 sqm

Bedrooms: 3 | Bathrooms: 2.5

Additional Units: 2 Modern Cottages | Staff Quarters | Office Space | Workshop

Welcome to this exquisite and spacious property located in the heart of Bryanston East, offering a perfect blend of charm, functionality, and income-generating potential.

Upon entry, you are greeted by a welcoming light-filled office space and a stylish cloakroom. The heart of the home boasts a modern kitchen fitted with both electric and gas stoves, space for a double-door fridge, and a generous walk-in pantry with skylight. Elegant wooden floors flow into a spacious dining and lounge area, complete with a cozy fireplace, and extend to a large TV room and entertainment space featuring a second fireplace.

Enjoy seamless indoor-outdoor living on the covered patio that overlooks a serene swimming pool and lush, manicured wrap-around garden framed by beautiful treesideal for relaxing or entertaining.

Upstairs offers 3 sunlit bedrooms, all with ample cupboard space and ceiling fans. The master suite includes a walk-in closet, lounge area, and a luxurious en-suite bathroom with double vanity. The main and second bedrooms open onto a large balcony, offering tranquil views of the garden below.

Additional Features:

Cottage 1: 1-bedroom unit with skylight, open-plan kitchen, en-suite bathroom, and currently tenanted.

Cottage 2: 2-bedroom unit, both en-suite, featuring a spacious kitchen with eye-level oven and central island.

Staff Quarters: 2 rooms with a full bathroom and a laundry room.

Garaging: 3 garages additional garage for up to 4 vehicles.

Workshop Area: Secure, enclosed space ideal for storage or projects.

Separate Office with Bathroom: Can be converted into another cottage or guest suite.

Security: Electric fence, alarm system, CCTV cameras, and located in a secure, highly sought-after neighborhood.

Location: Prime Bryanston East, close to major routes, excellent schools, shopping centers, and more.

Bonus: Significant potential for land subdivision and rental income from the additional units. Contact us today to arrange a private viewing and secure this lifetime opportunity.
