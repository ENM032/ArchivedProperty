Elegant Family Living in a Secure Bryanston Enclave

Nestled on a generous 2,500m² stand in one of Bryanstons most sought-after boomed roads, this beautifully renovated north-facing residence offers an exceptional blend of luxury, comfort, and effortless entertaining.

Bathed in natural light, the approximately 600m² home has been thoughtfully updated and is presented in immaculate, move-in-ready condition. Expansive reception rooms flow seamlessly to the landscaped garden, creating an atmosphere of relaxed sophistication perfectly suited to both family living and entertaining on a grand scale.

At the heart of the home lies a magnificent gourmet kitchen a true gathering place where family and friends naturally come together. Adjacent living areas include a cosy TV lounge with a wood-burning fireplace, providing warmth and ambience during Johannesburgs winter months.

Accommodation comprises three spacious bedrooms within the main residence, a separate en-suite guest room that offers ideal flexibility as a guest room, private work-from-home space, teenage retreat, or accommodation for extended family. There is a study with its own entrance making it a possible work from home space. In addition, the property features exceptionally spacious staff accommodation complete with its own lounge, kitchen, and bathroom.

Outdoor living is equally impressive. A beautifully established garden, nurtured by a high-yielding borehole connected directly to the house, surrounds the sparkling swimming pool. The enchanting rose-covered arched trellises lining the driveway create a memorable arrival experience, while a separate entertainment space featuring a bar, TV lounge and seating area provides the perfect setting for hosting family celebrations and social gatherings.

Further enhancing the propertys appeal are sustainable lifestyle features, including solar power with battery backup currently rented through Versofy Solutions, ensuring peace of mind and reduced reliance on the grid. The property has borehole with a strong yield that feeds to the home.

Set on a quiet, highly private road with excellent security, this exceptional home offers a rare opportunity to enjoy gracious family living in a tranquil setting while remaining close to Bryanstons premier schools, shopping centres and business hubs.
