Modern 3-Bedroom Family Home with Exceptional Entertainment Spaces Bryanston East

Discover family living in this beautifully renovated 3 bedroom home, perfectly positioned on an elevated 1621sqm stand in Bryanston East.

This elegant residence offers 3 spacious living areas with a feature fireplace, a modern open-plan kitchen with a large separate scullery. The main bedroom suite is a peaceful retreat, featuring a jacuzzi bath and ample built-in storage.

Seamless flow to an expansive covered patio ideal for effortless entertaining. The patio overlooks a sparkling pool and the mature landscaped garden, creating a private oasis perfect for family relaxation or hosting guests.

An opportunity to purchase an existing subdivision 1650sqm at the rear of the property for future development or investment potential, on the market to be sold separately at R2,7m.

A rare opportunity to secure a stylish, move-in-ready home with exceptional lifestyle appeal and long-term value.
