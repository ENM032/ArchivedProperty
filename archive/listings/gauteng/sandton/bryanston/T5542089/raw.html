Newly Renovated 4-Bedroom Family Home for Sale in Bryanston

Discover contemporary family living in this beautifully renovated four-bedroom home, perfectly positioned in one of Bryanston''s secure, boomed-off neighbourhoods.

From the moment you step through the front door, the home welcomes you with an abundance of natural light and thoughtfully designed open spaces. The entrance flows seamlessly through large sliding doors onto the expansive garden, creating a wonderful indoor-outdoor connection that extends directly to the sparkling swimming pool.

Designed with both family living and entertaining in mind, the home offers multiple living wings that provide versatile seating and relaxation areas. Just off the entrance is a tranquil lounge flooded with natural light, featuring floor-to-ceiling glass sliding doors that open onto the garden.

To the right, an open-plan family room flows effortlessly into the dining area and modern kitchen, complete with a separate scullery for added convenience. Adjacent to the dining room is a flexible space that could serve as a wine cellar, library, reading room, or additional entertainment area.

At the heart of the home is a stunning living space overlooking the garden. Enhanced by a skylight, this inviting room is bathed in natural sunlight throughout the day, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

All spacious bedrooms are en-suite, ensuring privacy and comfort for every member of the family. Two bedrooms, including the luxurious main suite, feature generous walk-in closets.

An exceptional bonus is the fully self-contained cottagetruly a home within a home. It offers a comfortable lounge, guest toilet, an en-suite bedroom, and an additional room that can easily be converted into a kitchen, home office, studio, or private workspace. Staff accommodation is also included.

The beautifully landscaped garden is an entertainer''s dream, featuring a dedicated braai area with a seating area , a fire pit for cosy evenings, and a sparkling swimming pool that completes the outdoor lifestyle.

Additional features include:

Secure boomed and gated neighbourhood

Alarm system

Staff quarters

Self-contained cottage

Multiple entertainment areas

Beautifully renovated throughout

Family-friendly location

This exceptional Bryanston home combines modern elegance, flexible living spaces, and outstanding security, making it the perfect choice for families who love to entertain while enjoying comfort, privacy, and style
