House For Sale in Bryanston, Sandton

SPACIOUS FAMILY HOME IN BRYANSTON EAST

OWNER ASKING R 5 999 000

CONSIDERING BEST OFFER ABOVE R 4 999 000

Set on over a half-acre in sought-after Bryanston East, just moments from Riverside Centre, top schools and the Sandton business node, this home offers space, flow and effortless indoor-outdoor living in one of the suburbs most desirable pockets.

Designed with entertaining and family life in mind, the home features expansive open-plan living areas comprising a lounge, dining room and kitchen with a separate scullery, as well as a comfortable TV room. The entire living space opens seamlessly through stack doors onto a large covered entertainment patio, creating the perfect setting for year-round gatherings.

From the patio, the home area leads off to a charming fire pit area and cascades into a vast, established garden complete with a sparkling swimming pool. Its the kind of garden where kids can run free and adults can unwind around the fire as the sun sets.

Tucked away off the kitchen and scullery is a private study, which can easily be converted into a guest suite or work-from-home space as well as a utility room.

The bedroom wing offers three spacious, light-filled bedrooms with built-in cupboards and two and a half bathrooms. The family bathroom is fully equipped with a double vanity, bath, shower and toilet, while the main bedroom is exceptionally spacious and features a walk-in dressing area and a luxurious en suite bathroom with spa bath. A separate guest toilet and a full walk-in linen cupboard provide additional convenience.

Additional features include a double garage, ample extra parking, electric fencing, an alarm system, an irrigation system, as well as double-glazed windows throughout ensuring peace and quiet when needed.

A rare opportunity to secure generous land, excellent flow and prime positioning in Bryanston East.
