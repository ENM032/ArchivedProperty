4 Bedroom House in Bryanston

62 devonshire avenue
4 4.5 3 1 031 m² 630 m²

Step into a world of sleek sophistication with this cutting-edge family residence, located in the highly sought-after suburb of Bryanston. Designed with modern living in mind, this home combines luxury, functionality, and exclusivity to deliver an unparalleled lifestyle experience.

4 Spacious en-suite bedrooms all light-filled and stylish, each with premium wooden floors and ample storage. A family lounge is also situated on the second floor offering comfort and relaxation for the family before retiring to their rooms.

One of the rooms is separate and on the ground floor fully equipped and ideal for accommodating older family members or guests.

A state-of-the-art kitchen fitted exclusively with Miele appliances for a seamless cooking experience , a sleek gas stove for precision cooking.Modern cabinetry and finishes for a clean, minimalist aesthetic, an inviting breakfast bar for casual family meals.

The family lounge and formal lounge designed for both casual and refined gatherings

Outdoor entertainment area covered patio overlooking a sparkling pool, surrounded by a beautifully landscaped garden.
