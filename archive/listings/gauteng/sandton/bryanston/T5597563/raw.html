Immaculate 3-Bedroom Executive Townhouse

Immaculate Executive Townhouse in Secure Bryanston Complex

This exceptional three-bedroom executive townhouse offers refined living in one of Sandton''s most sought-after security estates. The property combines contemporary design with practical functionality, featuring a marble staircase entry that sets the tone for sophisticated entertaining. The open-plan reception rooms flow seamlessly across the first level, creating bright, balanced spaces ideal for family living and hosting guests.

The ground floor showcases a fluid layout connecting the lounge, dining room, and family room, all opening onto a sunlit patio and manicured garden with a splash pool. The contemporary kitchen features premium appliances, generous preparation and storage space, complemented by a separate scullery and laundry for convenience. Upstairs, are three bedrooms. The main with a full bathroom and walk in cupboards. The second bedroom is ensuite with a shower, whilst the third bedroom has a closeby full bathroom
