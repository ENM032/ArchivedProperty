Midstream Hill Contemporary Farmstyle Double Storey with Sparkling Pool

Set within the secure and intimate Midstream Hill Estate, this contemporary farmstyle home captures the essence of relaxed family living with a modern edge. From the moment you enter the double gated courtyard, you sense that this is a space designed for both privacy and play. There is ample room for children to run freely outdoors while you enjoy the peace of a beautifully kept, secure environment.

Perfectly positioned near Mediclinic Midstream and with quick access to Midstream Drive and Brakfontein Road, the estate offers effortless connectivity without sacrificing tranquillity. Midstream Hill is known for its neat streetscapes, smaller community feel and strong focus on security and lifestyle, making it an exceptional choice for families who value both comfort and convenience.

Inside, the home unfolds into light-filled, open-plan living areas anchored by a striking white kitchen. Clean lines and a fresh palette create a welcoming heart of the home where cooking, conversation and connection naturally come together. Whether preparing a weekday dinner or entertaining guests, you remain part of every moment as the living areas flow seamlessly onto the patio.

The undercover patio, allows you to shape the space according to the season. Open it up to welcome summer breezes or create a cozy retreat during cooler evenings. Beyond, a generous sparkling pool invites endless summer enjoyment, while the boma area sets the scene for relaxed evenings around the fire. The garden offers generous lawn space, ideal for children and pets to enjoy safely within the estate.

Upstairs, three comfortable bedrooms and two bathrooms form a private family sanctuary. This level is dedicated to rest and retreat, offering a calm contrast to the vibrant living spaces below. Downstairs, a guest cloakroom adds convenience, and a formal study provides flexibility as a work-from-home office or an additional bedroom for visiting family members.

A practical scullery connects to an expansive undercover parking area accessed through a drive-through garage, ensuring ample covered space and functionality. Staff facilities further enhance the thoughtful layout of this well-designed home.

Residents of Midstream Hill enjoy access to the estates green strip and walking trail, perfect for early morning walks or afternoon unwinds in a secure and beautifully maintained setting.

This is more than a home it is a lifestyle defined by security, space and contemporary farmstyle charm. Come and experience the warmth and ease of living in Midstream Hill.

Please be advised that Midstream estates has a set of house rules, it is determined that CATS ARE NOT ALLOWED in the estate. For more information please request a full set of rules to assist with your decision.
