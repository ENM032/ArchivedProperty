Exclusive Tri-Mandate | 3 Bedroom Home in Midstream Meadows Estate.

This beautifully presented home in Midstream Meadows Estate offers a practical layout, modern finishes, and comfortable living spaces designed for everyday ease.

A welcoming entrance hall leads into the heart of the home, where the open-plan kitchen, dining, and living areas create a seamless flow for family living and entertaining. The kitchen is well-equipped with quality cabinetry, a gas hob, electric oven, breakfast nook with prep bowl, and a separate scullery with space for three appliances.

The living area opens onto a shaded patio with a built-in braai, overlooking a neat, low-maintenance garden with synthetic lawn and manual irrigation.

The home offers three spacious bedrooms, all fitted with laminated flooring, built-in cupboards, and ceiling fans. The main bedroom includes a walk-in closet and en-suite bathroom with double vanities and his-and-hers showers. The remaining two bedrooms share a modern full bathroom.
