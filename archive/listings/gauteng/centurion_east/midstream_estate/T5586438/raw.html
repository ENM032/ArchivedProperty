Sought After Address in Midstream Estate - 4 Bedroom House

SOUGHT-AFTER ADDRESS | RENOVATED 4-BEDROOM HOME WITH PRIVATE GARDEN

Midlands Estate

Perfectly positioned in a quiet, family-friendly street, this beautifully renovated 4-bedroom home offers generous living spaces, privacy, and an inviting atmosphere ideal for relaxed family living and effortless entertaining.

The light-filled interiors flow seamlessly from the open-plan living areas to a spacious enclosed entertainment area and established garden. Mature trees provide wonderful shade and privacy, creating a tranquil outdoor setting.

Conveniently situated close to parks and access gates, this home combines a sought-after location with comfortable, versatile living.

Downstairs

- Welcoming entrance leading into a warm and beautifully maintained home.

- Guest bedroom with sliding doors opening directly onto the garden.

- Separate bathroom conveniently doubling as a guest cloakroom.

- Light and airy open-plan lounge, dining room and kitchen.

- Spacious kitchen featuring a marble breakfast island, semi-solid wood cabinetry and a separate scullery with space for three appliances.

- Ceiling fans in the lounge, dining room and entertainment area for added year-round comfort.

- Generous entertainment area with built-in braai, prep bowl and aluminium stacking doors opening seamlessly onto the garden.

- Tranquil water feature complementing the peaceful garden setting.

- Double automated garage with convenient direct access into the home.

- Separate staff accommodation with its own bathroom.

Upstairs

- Cosy pyjama lounge, perfect as an additional family relaxation space.

- Convenient linen cupboard providing additional storage.

- Spacious main bedroom with ample cupboard space, a full en-suite bathroom and sliding doors opening onto a private balcony overlooking the established garden and mature trees.

- Two additional bedrooms sharing a family bathroom.

Additional Features

- Recently renovated and freshly painted inside and out.

- New geyser installed.

- Manual irrigation system.

- Established private garden with mature trees offering shade and seclusion.

- Quiet, family-friendly street.

- Ideally positioned close to a park and access gates.

The Midstream Lifestyle

Living in Midstream is about more than simply owning a home - it offers a secure and convenient lifestyle for the whole family. Residents enjoy excellent security, highly regarded schools from baby care through to Grade 12, a private hospital, shopping facilities, restaurants, sporting amenities, parks and recreational facilities.

A beautifully presented family home offering space, privacy and an exceptional location in one of Midlands Estate''s sought-after streets.
