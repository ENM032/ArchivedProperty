4 Bedroom House in Midstream Estate

Contact agent for street address
4 3.5 2 600 m² 278 m²

Stylish 4-bedroom home with modern finishes, solar and heated pool.

Exclusive Mandate!! On show - by appointment only!

Set in a peaceful street within a secure estate, this stunning modern 4-bedroom family home offers the perfect blend of contemporary style, comfort and effortless family living. Boasting quality finishes, generous entertainment spaces, a beautifully established garden and a sparkling heated pool, it is designed for relaxed living and memorable gatherings. Solar power adds energy efficiency and peace of mind, completing a sophisticated home designed for comfortable family living.

Living in Midstream means more than owning a home its a lifestyle. Residents enjoy world-class security, top-tier schools from baby care through to Grade 12, a private hospital on your doorstep, shopping conveniences, and a variety of sporting and recreational facilities that make everyday life both easy and enriching. This isnt just a property. Its peace of mind, convenience, and community, all in one.

Dont miss out. Book your viewing TODAY and experience the Midstream lifestyle for yourself! Be sure to watch the included full HD video.

- Inviting entrance creating an immediate sense of style and comfort.

- Modern kitchen with quality finishes, a large breakfast island, space for a double-door fridge, gas and electric stove, and electric oven.

- Separate scullery with ample space for three appliances.

- Indoor entertainment area featuring a built-in Cape braai, basin, and cupboards.

- Convenient guest toilet.
