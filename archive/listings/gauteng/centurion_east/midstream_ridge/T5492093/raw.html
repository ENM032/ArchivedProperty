4 Bedroom House in Midstream Ridge

Contact agent for street address
4 3.5 4 600 m² 284 m²

Where Beautiful Family Memories Await Your Arrival

Nestled within the highly secure and prestigious Midstream Ridge Estate, this double-story family home effortlessly blends contemporary style with everyday functionality. The heart of the ground floor features an expansive, open plan layout where a sleek, modern kitchen, complete with premium finishes, an eat-in island, and a gas stove flows seamlessly into the bright dining room and lounge. This level is smartly designed for convenience and guests, hosting a stylish guest toilet and basin, alongside a private downstairs bedroom equipped with its own ensuite bathroom. Sun-drenched floor-to-ceiling glass doors open up completely to merge the indoor living space with a massive, private garden and a dedicated boma area, making it an absolute dream for both relaxed family life and vibrant weekend entertaining.

Upstairs, the property transitions into a peaceful family sanctuary hosting three additional bedrooms and two beautifully styled bathrooms. This includes a luxurious master suite with a standalone tub and a shower, while the other two sunlit rooms share a spacious full bathroom. The upper level layout also boasts a clever landing, perfect for a home office or a music nook. While the home is structurally excellent and move-in ready, it requires a touch of work, a fantastic opportunity for a premium estate property.
