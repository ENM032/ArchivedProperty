3 Bedroom House in Retire at Midstream

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 3 651 m² 295 m²

Immaculate 3-bedroom retirement home with solar and lock-up-and-go ease.

Exclusive mandate - on show, by appointment only!

Tucked away in a prestigious security estate, this immaculate 3-bedroom, single-level retirement home offers the perfect balance of elegance, comfort, and effortless lock-up-and-go living. Thoughtfully designed with beautiful, flowing living spaces, a manicured garden, and premium extras including solar power, this home promises peace of mind without compromising on style. Positioned close to a picturesque park, residents can enjoy tranquil walks and a secure, community-focused lifestyle in one of the estates most desirable settings.

Dont miss out. Book your viewing TODAY and experience the Midstream lifestyle for yourself! Be sure to watch the attached full HD video.

Retire at Midstream offers an exceptional lifestyle where elegant homes, beautifully maintained surroundings, and outstanding security create the ideal environment for relaxed retirement living. Enjoy the convenience of top-tier medical facilities, shopping centres, restaurants, walking trails, and a vibrant community atmosphere, all within one of Midstreams most sought-after estates. This is more than just a home its peace of mind, comfort, convenience, and community combined.

ACCOMMODATION INCLUDES:

Inviting entrance featuring a striking wooden pivot door.

Spacious open-plan layout seamlessly connecting the dining room, lounge, and kitchen.

Comfortable TV lounge with air conditioning and high-quality laminated flooring.

Elegant double-sided gas fireplace creating warmth and ambience between the lounge and dining area.

Dining room fitted with a ceiling fan for added comfort.

Modern kitchen with granite breakfast island, double eye-level oven, gas stove, and space for a double-door fridge.

Built-in water purifier for convenient access to clean drinking water.

Generously sized walk-in pantry offering exceptional storage space.

Separate scullery with space for three appliances.

Enclosed entertainment patio with built-in braai, stylish bar counter, beautiful display cupboards, and aluminium stacking doors opening onto an outdoor patio with an adjustable louvre roof.

Beautifully maintained and manicured garden.

Spacious main bedroom with ample built-in cupboards, air conditioning, and ceiling fan.

Luxurious full en-suite bathroom in the main bedroom.

Two additional bedrooms with carpets and ceiling fans, sharing a modern bathroom.

Guest toilet for visitors convenience.

Versatile study or hobby room with built-in cupboards and high-quality laminated flooring.

Solid wooden doors and wooden architraves add timeless character throughout the home.

Extra-high ceilings enhance the sense of light and space.

Double automated garage with extra height for a caravan.

One garage offering pull-through access to a covered third parking area.

Additional extended garage space ideal for storage or a workbench.

Wheelchair-friendly design with ramps, wider doorways, and no steps.

Staff toilet.

Garden storeroom for additional outdoor storage.

Automated irrigation system for effortless garden maintenance.

Double-sided gate providing convenient access for a trailer.
