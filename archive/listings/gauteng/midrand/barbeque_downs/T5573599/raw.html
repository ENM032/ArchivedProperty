Exquisite Estate Sanctuary in Prestigious Barbeque Downs

Discover an exceptional residence nestled within an exclusive security estate, where sophisticated suburban living harmonizes with unparalleled peace of mind. This meticulously appointed three-bedroom sanctuary offers a refined lifestyle experience, perfectly positioned for discerning families seeking both elegance and security.

The residence showcases thoughtfully designed living spaces across 294 square metres, featuring a sumptuous master suite with ensuite bathroom, two generously proportioned secondary bedrooms, and a second full bathroom.
