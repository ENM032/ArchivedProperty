3 Bedroom House in Barbeque Downs

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 419 m²

Seamless and visually captivating - a true testament to warmth, comfort and practicality.

Ready to move into your dream home Your opportune moment is here in this stunning 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom full title home. This warm and welcoming home not only welcomes family and friends the flow creates spaces that make you feel completely at home and at ease, too. Ticking all the right boxes and priced to move, this lovingly well-kept piece of real estate in Barbeque Downs beckons to a growing family, seeking an ideal setting to embrace their next chapter of life with unparalleled style.

Upon entry to this spacious abode, an open plan layout cohesive with spaces that flow together, seamlessly connects lounge, kitchen and dining room framed by sliding doors opening onto the manicured gardens and glistening swimming pool. A guest cloakroom provides additional convenience.

At the heart of the home is a stunning gourmet eat-in chef''s kitchen, with bespoke cabinetry and counters. Equipped with top-of-the-line appliances, culinary delights await, while seamlessly integrating into the open-plan family room, allowing for effortless entertaining and family gatherings. This area balances style & practicality to inspire culinary creations that nourish & bring families together.

The garage, more than just a parking space, houses the laundry area, replete with fitted storage for all your needs.

Ascending to the upper level, three generously sized bedrooms are each equipped with fitted wardrobes. All bedrooms have access to the expansive private balcony, providing scenic views of the surrounding area. The spacious primary suite, with lots of built-in cupboards, features an en-suite bathroom with dual vanities. The two bedrooms share an additional family bathroom, ensuring the utmost convenience for the entire household.

A blissful setting to create lasting family memories or to entertain on a large scale - the awe-inspiring garden

with pool and a koi pond is an absolute marvel.

When compromise in style and class is not an option, this home is for you.
