3 Bedroom House in Barbeque Downs

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 514 m² 176 m²

Stylish Single-Storey Home with Exceptional Entertainment Features

NEW RELEASE - SOLE MANDATE

Serious seller inviting buyers to view and negotiate.

Step into this lovely single-storey home, where comfortable family living meets effortless indoor-outdoor entertaining. A welcoming entrance leads seamlessly into the inviting living area, creating a warm and relaxed atmosphere from the moment you arrive.

The spacious living area is fitted with air-conditioning and flows beautifully onto an enclosed patio, offering an additional versatile living and entertaining space overlooking the garden and pool.

The home features a well-appointed kitchen with a dual electric and gas hob, together with convenient space for an under-counter appliance. A dedicated studyoffice is positioned just off the living area, providing an ideal work-from-home space.

Accommodation comprises three comfortable bedrooms, all finished with stylish laminate flooring. The spacious main bedroom enjoys air-conditioning and features a full en-suite bathroom, creating a comfortable private retreat. The two additional bedrooms share a second bathroom fitted with a shower.

Outdoor living is a real highlight, with an entertainment deck overlooking the sparkling swimming pool, creating the perfect setting for relaxed weekends, entertaining friends or simply enjoying the outdoors.

Adding significant value and peace of mind, the property is equipped with an impressive 12 solar panels 8kW Sunsynk Inverter and 3 x 5kW Lithium iron batteries, helping to reduce reliance on the grid and provide greater energy independence. A 1,000-litre backup water tank further enhances the home''s practicality and resilience.

For added convenience, the property offers a double automated garage, complemented by additional parking, providing ample space for residents and guests.

With its single-level design, generous living spaces, indoor-outdoor flow, excellent entertainment areas and impressive backup power and water solutions, this is a home that combines comfort, convenience and modern-day practicality.
