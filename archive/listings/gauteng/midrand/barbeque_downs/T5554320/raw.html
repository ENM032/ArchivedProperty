Charming Family Home in Secure Estate

Experience the perfect blend of security, space, and style in this stunning 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom duplex, located within a sought-after, secure residential estate in Sandton. This home offers the ultimate peace of mind with 24-hour security patrols and a controlled access gate, making it an ideal choice for families.

Key Features:

Secure Living: Enjoy the tranquility of living in a well-managed estate with round-the-clock security, perfect for a safe and relaxed lifestyle.

Upstairs Retreat: The master suite features an enormous balcony, offering a private outdoor sanctuary for morning coffee or evening sunsets.

A Private Garden Oasis: Step into your own personal garden unit, complete with a lush, well-maintained garden area that is perfect for outdoor dining and entertaining.

Flexible Layout: This duplex offers 4 spacious bedrooms, with a convenient downstairs bedroom that can be easily transformed into a home office or guest room.

Modern Amenities: Enjoy the convenience of modern finishes throughout, including two stylish bathrooms and a functional kitchen.

Spacious Living: The ground floor offers a welcoming entrance and spacious living area that opens directly onto the covered patio and garden.

Parking & Utilities: The unit includes a double auto garage and features pre-paid electricity for added convenience.

Pet-Friendly: Small dogs are welcome with prior approval from the Body Corporate.

Location, Location, Location:

This home is ideally situated in a prime area of Sandton, with easy access to shopping centers, schools, and major transport links. Enjoy the best of suburban living with the convenience of city amenities.
