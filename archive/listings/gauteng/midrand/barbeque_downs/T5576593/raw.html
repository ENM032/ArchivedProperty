4 Bedroom House in Barbeque Downs

Welcome to this spacious and beautifully maintained 4 bedroom family home in the highly sought after Honey Hills Estate in Barbeque Downs.

Designed for comfortable family living, this home offers four generously sized bedrooms, three well appointed bathrooms, and a dedicated study, perfect for working from home or creating a quiet reading space.

The open and inviting living areas flow effortlessly, creating the ideal setting for both everyday living and entertaining. Step out onto the large balcony and take in the spectacular views, the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, unwind after a long day, or entertain family and friends.

Outside, you''ll find a large private, pet friendly garden where children can play freely, pets can roam, and loved ones can gather. A double garage provides secure parking.
