Three Bedroom Home in Security Estate in Barbeque Downs

SOLE MANDATE

New listing in Woodlands Estate in Barbeque Downs a beautiful single-level family home offering three bedrooms and two bathrooms.

The main bedroom is well-sized, with good natural light, laminate flooring and an en-suite bathroom with shower and bath. The second and third bedrooms have laminate flooring, and good cupboard space. They share a bathroom.

The double garage conveniently leads into the modern kitchen, which is well designed with ample cupboard space, and a scullery. The open plan living area is light and spacious. Step outside onto the covered patio, with a built in gas braai, overlooking the garden and lovely views.

Enjoy a harmonious and secure lifestyle in the sought after Woodlands Estate.
