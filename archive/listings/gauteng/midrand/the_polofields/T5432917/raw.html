3 Bedroom Apartment in The Polofields

602 the polofields waterfall, 1 polofields drive
3 2 2 118.4 m²

Sole Mandate - Three Bedroom ground floor apartment with landscaped garden

This quiet 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom ground-floor apartment has an open, landscaped garden.

The dining area and living room are open to the modern kitchen. The enclosed patio is accessible from the lounge area and includes a fireplace and frameless stacking doors that connect the open-plan dining and living space to a very quiet and private garden.

The kitchen is fully equipped with Smeg appliances, including a refrigerator, freezer, dishwasher, and washing machine. The cabinets extend to the ceiling.

The master bedroom features an air conditioner, built-in cupboards and an en-suite bathroom.

The two other bedrooms also come with built-in cupboards and share a full bathroom.

The apartment also includes an Inverter.

The unit comes with 2 carports.

These amenities are right at your doorstep.

The Lifestyle Centre is state-of-the-art, offering a restaurant, concierge service, laundromats and dry cleaners, convenience store, bar, cinema, childrens play area, squash courts and mini sports fields, swimming pool and yoga deck, Train Science Gym with an instructor Vitality Approved, Camelot Spa, and an eco-friendly car wash.

Enjoy easy access to highways, shopping centres, and schools.
