Spacious Accommodation

Three Generously Sized Bedrooms

Two Contemporary Bathrooms

Meticulously Maintained & Move-In Ready

Stand-alone inverter system

Gourmet Kitchen with Premium SMEG Appliances

Open-Plan Living Designed for Entertaining

The spacious lounge and dining area are flooded with natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere. Sliding doors open seamlessly onto a covered patio and private garden, extending the living space and creating the perfect environment for entertaining guests or enjoying relaxed outdoor living.

Private Garden Retreat
