3 Bedroom Apartment in The Polofields

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 118 m²

Modern 3 Bedroom and 2 Bathroom Ground Floor Garden Apartment Offering Lifestyle, Security and Convenience!

Step into comfort and convenience with this beautifully maintained 3 bedroom ground floor garden apartment, perfectly designed for relaxed family living. The home offers a seamless open-plan layout where the kitchen flows effortlessly into the dining area and TVfamily lounge, creating a warm and inviting space ideal for entertaining or enjoying everyday life.

The kitchen offers a lot of built in cupboards, ample countertop space and all appliances are integrated.

One of the standout features of this home is the covered patio, which extends your living space outdoors. From here, you step into a spacious green garden area with a playground, providing the perfect setting for children to play, relaxing afternoons, or weekend gatherings.

All three bedrooms are generously sized and feature built-in cupboards, ceiling fans, and ample natural sunlight that fills the rooms throughout the day. The property includes two neat full bathrooms, both well-maintained and designed for practical, comfortable living.

The apartment also offers two dedicated parking carports for added convenience.

Residents enjoy access to an exceptional range of lifestyle amenities within the estate, including a communal swimming pool, restaurant, convenience store, fully equipped gym, basketball court, squash facilities, and various recreational spaces designed for the whole family to enjoy.

Security is a top priority, with access-controlled entrance and 24-hour security, ensuring peace of mind and an extremely safe living environment.

Ideally located close to major highways and shopping centres, this home offers effortless connectivity and everyday convenience, making it a perfect choice for families, professionals, or investors seeking a secure and vibrant lifestyle.

Contact the agent today for more info or to arrange a private viewing of this absolute Gem!
