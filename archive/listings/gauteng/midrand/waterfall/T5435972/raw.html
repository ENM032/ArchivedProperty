Modern, Effortless Living in The Polofields

Set within The Polofields, this beautifully appointed 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment offers a seamless blend of modern design, comfort, and convenience which is ideal for both homeowners and investors.

The kitchen is sleek and contemporary, fitted with premium SMEG appliances, including a gas stove, fridge, dishwasher and washing machine, all cleverly integrated into the cabinetry for a clean, streamlined look.

The open-plan dining and living area flows effortlessly onto a private balcony, complete with a built-in braai, as well as a convenient cupboard and countertop for easy entertaining.

All three bedrooms are generously sized, with the main bedroom featuring a full en-suite bathroom with both a bath and shower, finished with striking black fixtures. The main bathroom is equally well-appointed, offering a bath and shower combination with matching modern finishes.

Additional features include:

-2 covered carports

-Contemporary finishes throughout

-Excellent natural light and flow

Perfectly positioned, the apartment is just a stones throw from the shopping hub, Polofields Crossing, adding everyday convenience to its appeal.

Residents enjoy access to the exclusive Lifestyle Centre, which includes:

-Restaurant and bar

-Concierge services

-Cinema room

-Swimming pool and yoga deck

-Gym

-Camelot Spa

-Squash courts and mini sports fields

-Childrens play area

-Laundromat, dry cleaning, and convenience store

-Eco-friendly car wash

This is secure estate living at its finest, combining lifestyle, location, and luxury in one exceptional offering.
