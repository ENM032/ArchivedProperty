3 Bedroom Apartment in Waterfall

the polofields waterfall, 1 polofields drive
3 2 2 118.4 m²

Exclusive Sole Mandate - Landscaped Garden Apartment with Designer Furniture and Kitchen

This 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom ground-floor apartment is a must-have with designer furniture and kitchen appliances.

The living room comes with designer furniture, custom wallpaper and designer light fittings. It also included a TV and media bookshelf.

The dining area comes with a designer table and chairs with a mirrored wallpapered wall finish.

The kitchen features custom marble countertops with Smeg Appliances. A Smeg 4 gas burner hob with an electric oven.

Additional appliances boast a built-in Bar Fridge as well as a built-in Wine Cooler.

The master bedroom features designer furnishings, built-in cupboards and an en-suite bathroom.

The second bedroom is also custom-furnished and comes with built-in cupboards.

The third bedroom has built-in cupboards with a wallpapered mirrored wall head wall.

There is a study nook between the 2nd and 3rd bedrooms

The patio is accessible from the lounge area and has been fully customized to be used as an indooroutdoor dining area, and includes a built-in fireplace, built-in cupboards with a Microwave Oven.

Additionally, the patio is fully furnished with a designer tiled floor.

Moving from the Patio into the landscaped garden, an open space, with privacy is experienced, as the apartment is a front row unit, your view is not restricted by other buildings.

The unit comes with 2 carports.

Polofields amenities are right at your doorstep.

The Lifestyle Centre is state-of-the-art, offering a restaurant, concierge service, laundromats and dry cleaners, convenience store, bar, cinema, childrens play area, squash courts and mini sports fields, swimming pool and yoga deck, Train Science Gym with an instructor Vitality Approved, Camelot Spa, and an eco-friendly car wash.

Enjoy easy access to highways, shopping centres, and schools.
