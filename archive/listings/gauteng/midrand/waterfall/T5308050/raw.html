3 Bedroom Apartment in Waterfall

2908 ellipse, 2908 karkloof crescent
3 3.5 2 300 m²

The Best Address In Waterfall

Experience the pinnacle of urban luxury in this magnificent 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath penthouse boasting panoramic views and sophisticated modern finishes. High ceilings and eco-friendly design elements create an airy, elegant atmosphere throughout. The expansive open-plan living and dining areas flow seamlessly onto a large covered private balcony through double sliding doors, offering uninterrupted vistas of Gauteng and the bustling Waterfall City skyline below.

The designer high-gloss kitchen is fitted with premium Caesarstone countertops, a double sink with mixer, and fully integrated Smeg appliances, including:

Electric hob & extractor

Fridgefreezer combo

Microwave

Dishwasher

Washerdryer combo

All three spacious bedrooms are en-suite, featuring beautifully curated bathrooms with imported tiles and Hansgrohe fittings, creating a tranquil private sanctuary for each room.

Ellipse Waterfall Amenities

24-hour security & CCTV surveillance

Secure basement parking

Generator back-up power & water supply

Fibre-ready & access-controlled lifts

Exclusive Access to The Luna Club

Enjoy premium lifestyle, wellness, and business facilities at the sought-after Luna Club, including:

Indoor gym, steam shower, sauna & boutique spa

Business centre with conference rooms & boardrooms

Private lounge & cigar lounge

Childrens play area

Lap pool & temperature-controlled leisure pool

Private dining rooms & outdoor entertainment areas

Gas braai stations, boma, fire pits, library & coffee bar

Prime Waterfall Location

Ideally situated within walking distance of the Mall of Africa 130,000m² of premier retail and just 5 minutes from top restaurants and corporate headquarters. Nearby amenities include:

Curro & Reddam House private schools

Netcare Hospital

Virgin Active Gym

Running & cycling trails

Gautrain bus station

Live within the elite, award-winning mixed-use precinct of Waterfall City where convenience, security, and modern luxury converge.
