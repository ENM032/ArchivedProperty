3 Bedroom Apartment in Waterfall

5410 munyaka estate, 1 munyaka south
3 3 3 260 m²

Signature Apartment - Fully Furnished, 3 bedroom, 3 Bathroom Lagoon Facing Comfort

Step into this Signature fourth-floor apartment and be immediately captivated by the sweeping views across the prestigious Munyaka Estate and beyond - most notably the breathtaking crystal lagoon. Perfectly north-facing, the expansive wraparound covered balcony is designed for effortless outdoor living and entertaining, offering the ideal setting to relax, unwind and soak up a little sun.

The open plan living area welcomes you with a spacious and inviting lounge that flows seamlessly into the elegant dining space. Ultra-modern minimalist design is enhanced by the abundance of natural light and generous open spaces, creating a crisp and clean atmosphere. Large doors extend the living areas onto the balcony, embracing a sophisticated indoor-outdoor lifestyle perfectly suited to the climate.

The sleek, designer kitchen is a fine example of functional beauty: integrated appliances surround the central island, crowned by a beautiful Stephen Pinkus chandelier, with a separate scullery and laundry to add practicality to this curated hub. Soft white and pale grey cabinetry is backlit, with dramatic black stone splashbacks for the ultimate in gourmet design.

Privately positioned on one side of the apartment, the luxurious master suite offers a tranquil retreat, while the remaining two suites are situated on the opposite side for enhanced privacy. All the bedrooms enjoy the exquisite views and light-filled spaces, opening to the balcony. The master suite features a walk-in dressing room leading into the spacious en suite. Like the other suites, the design and styling echo the homes impeccable and understated attention to detail.

As expected of a residence of this calibre, the fully furnished apartment is equipped with every modern convenience: air conditioning, back-up power, fibre and DSTV ready. The home includes three secure basement parking bays and a personal storeroom.

Residents at Munyaka Estate enjoy exclusive access to the world-class Lifestyle Centre, featuring meeting and function rooms, concierge services, a fine dining restaurant, cinema, spa, gym, padel courts, aquatic centre and so much more.

A statement of elegance, sophistication, and effortless luxury this is more than a residence it is a lifestyle beyond compare.
