Modern First-Floor Apartment with Private Balcony Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate

Discover contemporary living at its finest in this beautiful first-floor apartment located in the highly sought-after Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate, Waterfall. Designed for comfort and convenience, this stylish home offers a perfect balance of modern finishes and practical living.

The open-plan dining and lounge area is enhanced has an inviting and relaxed atmosphere ideal for everyday living or entertaining guests. The modern kitchen is both functional and elegant, featuring a stove, hob, oven, and space for three appliances.

This apartment boasts three well-sized bedrooms. There are two bathrooms for added comfort: one fitted with a bathtub and the other with a shower for everyday convenience. Step out onto your spacious balcony a perfect spot for morning coffee.

Welcome to this private and modern Balwin classic located in the prestigious Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate in Waterfall. This unfurnished first-floor apartment offers contemporary living with premium convenience and is perfect for professionals, couples, or small families seeking style, comfort, and security.
