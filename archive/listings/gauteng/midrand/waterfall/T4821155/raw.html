3 Bedroom Apartment in Waterfall

3 2 107 m²

PROPERTY FOR SALE IN KIKUYU

Prime Investment Opportunity in a Premier Estate

Discover the perfect blend of comfort and modern living in this beautifully maintained 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom first-floor apartment, complete with two covered parking spaces. Designed for convenience and style, this apartment is ideal for families, professionals, couples, and smart investors alike. Its WiFi-ready, fitted with blinds, and features a security gate and a smart utility meter. Additionally, the newly installed Inverter System is available for separate purchase.

Step inside to an open-plan living area that effortlessly flows between the lounge and dining spaces, all connected to a sleek, Scandinavian-inspired kitchen. The kitchen stands out with its clean lines, wood accents, and glossy finishes, and comes fully equipped with four Defy appliances: a single-door fridge, dishwasher, washing machine, and tumble dryer.

The sunlit main bedroom includes an en-suite bathroom, while the two additional bedrooms share a full bathroom. These rooms are versatile enough to function as home offices or playrooms, offering flexibility to suit your lifestyle.

This secure estate provides a relaxed, resort-like atmosphere, with access to an impressive lifestyle center featuring: Amici Restaurant & Pizzeria, a heated swimming pool, a childrens play area, a Training Science gym, squash court, free Wi-Fi, lounge areas with fireplaces and TV, a mini sports field, a cinema room with DStv, a convenience store, boardroom, laundromat, concierge service, Camelot spa, games room, and the on-site Little Hill Montessori Preschool.

Dont miss out on this opportunityschedule your viewing today!
