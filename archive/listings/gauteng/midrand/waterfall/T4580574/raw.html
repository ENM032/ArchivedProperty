Luxurious Penthouse apartment for sale in the Waterfall area.

Live above it all in this ultra-luxurious 3-bedroom penthouse at Ellipse Waterfall by Tricolt. Offering breathtaking panoramic views, sleek designer finishes, and seamless indoor-outdoor living, this residence defines modern sophistication.
