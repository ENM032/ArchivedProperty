3 Bedroom Apartment in Waterfall

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 8.3 ha 110 m²

Luxury Apartment in a Secure Lifestyle Estate

This beautifully designed apartment offers the perfect blend of modern living and generous views in the sought after Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate.

The home features a spacious open plan living and dining area, three well sized bedrooms, and two bathrooms, with the main bedroom ensuite. The bright living space flows seamlessly into a sleek, contemporary kitchen fitted with space for four appliances.

Step out onto the private balcony and enjoy a relaxing space ideal for unwinding or entertaining.

Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate is renowned for its exceptional lifestyle offering and 24-hour security with controlled access. Residents enjoy exclusive access to a world class lifestyle centre that includes a fully equipped gym, swimming pool, squash courts, spa, restaurant and pizzeria, laundromat, childrens play area, and an on-site Montessori school.

Ideally located with easy access to major highways, healthcare facilities, schools, and shopping destinations such as Mall of Africa, this apartment presents a rare opportunity to enjoy luxury, convenience, and secure estate living in one of Waterfalls most desirable communities.
