This spacious and well-maintained three-bedroom ground floor apartment in Kikuyu Waterfall offers comfortable, modern living in one of Waterfalls most sought-after estates. Perfectly positioned next to one of the estates parks, the apartment enjoys a peaceful outlook and easy access to open green spaces ideal for families and outdoor lovers.

The home features three generously sized bedrooms and two contemporary bathrooms. The open-plan kitchen, fitted with a gas stove, flows seamlessly into the spacious living area, creating a warm and inviting environment for both everyday living and entertaining. Sliding doors open onto a private garden, extending your living space outdoors and offering the perfect setting for relaxed afternoons or weekend braais.

Kikuyu Waterfall is renowned for its secure lifestyle living and exceptional amenities, including 24-hour security, a world-class lifestyle centre, swimming pool, gym facilities, restaurant, and beautifully maintained communal areas. Conveniently located near major highways, leading schools, and the Mall of Africa, this apartment combines space, comfort, and prime positioning within a vibrant estate community.
