Exclusive 4-Bedroom Ground-Floor Residence for Sale in Ellipse Waterfall

Experience unparalleled sophistication and convenience in this one-of-a-kind, fully furnished ground-floor residence within the prestigious Ellipse complex in Waterfall City. Designed for luxurious modern living, this exceptional home features four spacious en-suite bedrooms, each opening onto the expansive outdoor area, patio and private garden. The generous layout includes an entrance hall, spacious lounge and beautifully appointed dining area, creating an ideal setting for both relaxed family living and elegant entertaining. The bespoke kitchen, complete with a separate scullery, offers both style and functionality. The residence features laminated flooring throughout the living areas and bedrooms, with tiled flooring in the kitchen and all en-suite bathrooms. Air conditioning in the lounge ensures year-round comfort, while four secure underground parking bays provide ample vehicle accommodation.

The residence is perfectly suited to those who enjoy outdoor entertaining, with a large patio and private garden providing an impressive extension of the living space. Ellipse offers an exceptional lifestyle with premium amenities including a fully equipped gym, outdoor gym, swimming pools, landscaped green spaces and barbecue areas. Residents can also enjoy the on-site restaurant and breathtaking panoramic views stretching from Kyalami to the Magaliesberg mountains and Sandton. Ideally positioned within Waterfall City, the complex provides convenient access to The Mall of Africa, Gautrain station, Netcare Waterfall City Hospital, restaurants and cafs, while state-of-the-art security ensures peace of mind. Discover a truly unique residence where luxury, comfort and effortless entertaining come together an exceptional opportunity to own a rare ground-floor home in one of Waterfall City''s most sought-after developments.
