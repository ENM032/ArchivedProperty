4 Bedroom Penthouse in Waterfall

Contact agent for street address
4 4 145 m² 14 m²

Experience the pinnacle of sophistication and convenience in this stunning property located in the prestigious Ellipse complex. Offering unparalleled amenities and a prime location, this residence provides a lifestyle of luxury and comfort that is second to none.

Nestled in the heart of the esteemed Waterfall precinct, the Ellipse sets the standard for luxurious living. Youll find yourself within walking distance of The Mall of Africa, the Gautrain station, Waterfall hospital, and an array of upscale dining establishments and cafes.

Indulge in a leisurely lifestyle with Ellipse''s impressive recreational facilities. Enjoy outdoor activities in the exclusive green zones and barbecue areas, perfect for social gatherings and relaxation. Stay active with the fully equipped gym or take a dip in the swimming pools. For those who prefer an outdoor workout, the complex also offers a unique outside gym location.

Savor culinary delights and captivating vistas at the complex''s exquisite restaurant. With a diverse menu catering to every palate, whether you desire a sumptuous meal or a simple coffee accompanied by decadent cake, this dining experience promises to tantalize your taste buds.

The four-bedroom ground floor apartment is meticulously finished to create an ambiance of sophistication and comfort. This one-of-a-kind residence offers exclusive accommodation features, including sliding doors that open onto a generous patio and private garden, seamlessly blending indoor and outdoor living.

Inside, the apartment features an entrance hall, four en-suite bedrooms with patio access, a spacious lounge, dining room, and a bespoke kitchen with a scullery. The air-conditioned lounge ensures comfort year-round, while the private garden provides a serene retreat. With four underground parking bays, there is ample space for vehicles, and state-of-the-art security measures provide peace of mind.

Embark on a lifestyle of luxury and convenience at the Ellipse complex in the esteemed Waterfall precinct. With its unparalleled location, world-class amenities, exquisite furnishings, and exclusive accommodation features, this property offers a unique opportunity to experience modern living at its finest. Dont miss your chance to call this prestigious address home.
