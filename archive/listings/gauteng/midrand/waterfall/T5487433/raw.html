3 Bedroom Apartment in Waterfall

the polofields waterfall, 1 polofields drive
3 2 2 115 m²

Exclusive Sole Mandate - First Floor Apartment with Designer Finishers and Kitchen

This 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom first-floor Unfurnished corner apartment is a must-have with designer finishes and kitchen appliances.

The open-plan living and dining room comes with tinted sliding door glass and lockable louver shutters as well as air conditioning.

The designer kitchen features custom marble countertops, a double-door fridge, and a freezer with Touch to view Glass

A free-standing Smeg 6 gas burner hob with an electric oven and a Smeg custom extractor fan is a must-have for people who like to spend time in the kitchen.

Additional appliances include a 9kg Samsung washing machine as well as a 9kg tumble dryer.

There is also a microwave oven, a Smeg-designed kettle, and overhead cupboards that extend to the ceiling.

The master bedroom features air conditioning, custom-built-in cupboards and a customized en-suite bathroom.

The second bedroom comes with a built-in study desk with custom cupboards.

The third bedroom also comes with built-in custom cupboards and shares a full bathroom with the second bedroom.

Customer lockable, adjustable American shutters are fitted in all rooms, as well as the patio sliding door.

The apartment has a large Inverter installed that can power the apartment for up to 3 weeks.

The patio is accessible from the lounge area and includes a built-in fireplace

Optional Extras are:

Samsung imported a large-screen TV on a Design For You media cabinet

Designer electric adjustable couch.

Maldini, luxurious Italian furniture comprising a wooden dining table with 4 custom-made chairs.

A designer bookshelf, a drinks cabinet, and a sculpture on a designer entrance table.

The unit comes with 2 carports.

Polofields amenities are right at your doorstep.

The Lifestyle Centre is state-of-the-art, offering a restaurant, concierge service, laundromats and dry cleaners, convenience store, bar, cinema, childrens play area, squash courts and mini sports fields, swimming pool and yoga deck, Train Science Gym with an instructor Vitality Approved, Camelot Spa, and an eco-friendly car wash.

Enjoy easy access to highways, shopping centres, and schools.
