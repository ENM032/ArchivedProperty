3 Bedroom Penthouse in Waterfall

1803 ellipse waterfall, 48 magwa crescent, midrand, gauteng, 2090
3 3.5 3 390 m²

Penthouse

This is an opportunity to purchase an exclusive north-facing 3-bedroom Penthouse, located on the 8th floor in Newton Tower, Ellipse Waterfall upmarket complex.

There is No Transfer Duty when purchasing this Penthouse.

This luxurious Penthouse is tastefully appointed with Caesar Stone kitchen counters and built-in appliances,

tiled and wooden floors, two dining and lounge areas, as well as a Corner Bar, a heated Jacuzzi and Pool, a wrap-around balcony with breathtaking sunsets, all of which make this expansive open planned area ideal for entertaining family or holding Corporate functions.

The three bedrooms on the lower level, each with there own bathroom, are well-appointed and open onto a small wrap-around balcony.

The Penthouse can be used as a permanent residence or a Corporate Investment.

The Ellipse Complex boasts 24-hour security with CCTV, backup water and electricity, as well as landscaped gardens with walking paths.

The Luna Club, situated on the ground floor of Cassini Tower, offers conveniences like The Olives and Plates Restaurant, heated pools, an indoor fitness area and a Life Day Spa.

Centrally located in The Waterfall City Precinct, this would be an investment not to be missed.
