Modern 3-Bedroom Home in Exclusive Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate Midrand

Welcome to contemporary living at its finest. This spacious 110m2, 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment on the 1st floor, is perfectly positioned in a private corner of the sought-after Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate, offering both comfort and tranquility.

The home features an open-plan kitchen fitted with built-in appliances, including a refrigerator, washing machine, tumble dryer, and dishwashermaking it move-in ready with all the modern conveniences you need. The main bedroom is en-suite, ensuring privacy and comfort, while the additional bedrooms are generously sized.

An added bonus is the inverter system, providing reliable backup powera must-have in South Africa.

Kikuyu Lifestyle Estate is known for its 247 state-of-the-art security, giving residents peace of mind. The estate also offers world-class amenities, including:

On-site preschool

Fully equipped gym with fitness classes

Concierge services

In-house restaurant

Meeting room and work-friendly spaces

Sparkling community pool

Scenic walking trails

Convenient laundromat

Childrens play area

This is the perfect home for modern families, young professionals, or investors seeking a secure, lifestyle-focused estate in the heart of Midrand.
