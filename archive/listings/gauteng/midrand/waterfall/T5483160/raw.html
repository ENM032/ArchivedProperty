6 Bedroom Penthouse in Waterfall

5501 munyaka waterfall, 1 munyaka south
6 6 5 1 230 m²

Penthouse Extraordinaire! Fully Furnished, 6 bedroom, 6 Bathroom Lagoon Facing Luxury

Experience absolute luxury in this exceptional north-facing Signature Penthouse, overlooking the magnificent crystal lagoon. This is city living at its most refined.

From its prime fifth-floor location, the expansive, sweeping balcony offers spectacular panoramic views, with uncompromised privacy. The open-plan living spaces reflect meticulous design and the ultimate in sophisticated finishes. The spacious TV lounge is welcoming, flowing seamlessly to the informal dining area, creating an effortless sense of comfort and style.

The gourmet kitchen is nothing short of breathtaking, featuring integrated appliances and elegant glass-fronted cabinetry, centred around the marble island crowned by a Stephen Pikus chandelier. A separate scullery and laundry enhance both functionality and convenience.

A double glass-sided combustion fireplace creates warmth and beautiful ambiance between the living spaces, with a second lounge opening to the balcony and inviting jacuzzi - the perfect setting for relaxation against a stunning backdrop. The formal dining room, equally suited for entertaining or executive meetings, is just beyond the chic fitted bar designed for effortless entertaining.

The dramatic circular atrium, illuminated by lofty skylights and another exquisite designer chandelier, leads to the private bedroom wing, where six luxurious suites await. Designed to maximize natural light and space, the interiors embody understated style with a minimalist feel, accentuated by fine features, warm wooden floors, and bespoke decor. The spectacular master suite includes a private lounge, spacious walk-in dressing room, and a beautifully appointed bathroom with endless views.

Outdoor living is equally impressive, with the wraparound patio offering multiple entertainment and relaxation spaces, all framed by the gorgeous view. Features include a stylish bar, built-in braai, boma fireplace, jacuzzi, and generous dining and lounge areas.

Every detail in this penthouse has been carefully considered to deliver exceptional comfort, convenience, and exclusivity.

Additional features include direct lift access, a private Butlers suite with separate entrance, five secure basement parking bays and a personal storeroom.

Residents also enjoy access to the world-class Lifestyle Centre at Munyaka Estate, offering meeting and function rooms, concierge services, a fine dining restaurant, cinema, spa, gym, padel courts, aquatic centre and so much more.

A residence of this calibre is more than a home it is a statement of prestige, sophistication, and unparalleled luxury living.
