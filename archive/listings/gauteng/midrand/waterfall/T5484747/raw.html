3 Bedroom Apartment for Sale in Waterfall

Modern Fully Furnished 3-Bedroom Apartment in Secure Waterfall Estate

Excellent option for homeowners and investors seeking quality.

Discover contemporary living in this inviting three-bedroom, two-bathroom apartment located within the sought-after Waterfall area of Midrand, South Africa. Spanning 114 square metres, this residence offers a comfortable and stylish environment, ideal for individuals or families seeking a blend of convenience and security.

The apartment features an open-plan layout, seamlessly connecting the lounge, dining area, and a well-appointed kitchen. A convenient guest toilet adds to the functionality of the living space. Step outside to a private patio and garden, providing a tranquil retreat for relaxation or entertaining, overlooking the beautiful Lagune.

The property is offered fully furnished, ensuring a smooth transition for its new occupants, and it''s very attractive opportunity for all investors.

The apartment has two dedicated covered parking spaces situated in front of the unit. Fibre ready.

Residents benefit from living within a secure estate, which includes 24-hour security and an intercom system. The estate further enhances lifestyle with access to a range of communal amenities, including a communal swimming pool, a fully equipped gym, and a social clubhouse. Additional practical features include laundry facilities, On-site restaurant and bar.

Situated in the spectacular Munyaka Life Estate in Waterfall, this apartment provides an excellent opportunity to enjoy a balanced lifestyle with easy access to local amenities and transport links. With easy access to Sandton, Rosebank, Kyalami, and Sunninghill.
