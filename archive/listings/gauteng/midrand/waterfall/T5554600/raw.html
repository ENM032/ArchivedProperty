Exquisite 3-Bedroom Penthouse at Munyaka Estate, Waterfall City

Welcome to an exceptional opportunity to own a fully furnished, signature penthouse located on the fourth floor of the prestigious Munyaka Estate in Waterfall City, Johannesburg. This stunning 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom residence is a true masterpiece, featuring ultra-modern design and state-of-the-art finishes that promise a lifestyle of unmatched elegance and comfort.

As you enter this luxurious home, you''ll be captivated by the open-plan living area that seamlessly extends to offer expansive views of the lagoon, creating a perfect setting for both relaxation and entertainment. With three dedicated basement parking spaces, convenience is at your doorstep.

Munyaka Estate offers its residents a wealth of world-class amenities designed to enhance their lifestyle. Delight in the pristine crystal lagoon beach or maintain your fitness routine at the fully equipped gym. For water enthusiasts, the estate features an aquatic center with paddle skis and paddle boards. Indulge in some self-care at the on-site spa and enjoy peace of mind with top-tier security features, including electric fencing, a perimeter wall, CCTV surveillance, a manned guard house, biometric access control, 24-hour security, and security gates.

Adding to your convenience, the estate includes a charming coffee shop, laundry facilities, an estate shop, concierge services, meeting rooms, a function room, and a fine dining restaurant. Experience the pinnacle of luxury living at Munyaka Estatea place where every detail is crafted to perfection.
