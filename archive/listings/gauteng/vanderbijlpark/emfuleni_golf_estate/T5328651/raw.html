4 Bedroom House in Emfuleni Golf Estate

Contact agent for street address
4 4 2 717 m²

Immaculate 4 Bedroom Home for sale on the Emfuleni Golf Estate. This beautifully maintained modern family home is situated on the prestigious Golf Estate, where spacious open plan living meets ultimate comfort, elegance and style. The heart of the home is a bright white kitchen with Ceasar- stone counter tops, Centre island , gas stove, plus a seperate walk in panty and scullery. The dining and lounge areas flow effortlessly out to a covered patio through scatter doors with indoor braai perfect for entertaining or relaxed family living. From patio you step out to a sparkling pool and also enjoy views over the golf course.

Downstairs you also have a quest room with sliding door to the outside and as well as guest bathroom ideal for visitors or extended family.

Upstairs is a pajama lounge TV Room and also a study area. Three additional bedrooms each with its own full en suite. The master bedroom features a walk in closet.

Extra

The whole house is fitted with low-maintenance white faux wood Fully synthetic throughout the house, waterproof, lightweight and very easy to clean.

Camera in front of the property

Double automated garages with cupboard space

Outdoor showerToilet conveniently located behind a privacy wall near pool.

Additional Entrance providing convenient access to parking .

The Estate is under 24 hour security surveillance.
