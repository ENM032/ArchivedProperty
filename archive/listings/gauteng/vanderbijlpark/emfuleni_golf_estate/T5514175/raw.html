An Exceptional Newly Built Home in Prestigious Golf Estate

This newly built double-storey masterpiece offers an unrivaled combination of contemporary elegance, luxury, and refined estate living.

Perfectly positioned within a prestigious golf estate and enjoying sweeping views across the fairways, this exceptional residence has been thoughtfully designed to deliver a lifestyle of effortless sophistication. Every corner of the home reflects meticulous attention to detail, with premium finishes, generous proportions, and a seamless flow between indoor and outdoor living spaces.

The heart of the home is an expansive open-plan living area, where light-filled interiors create an atmosphere of warmth and grandeur. Stacking doors open onto the outdoor entertainment area and sparkling pool, perfectly framing the picturesque golf course setting and creating an idyllic space for both relaxation and entertaining.

The designer kitchen features sleek counter tops, expansive island bench, Elba gas stove and ample cupboard space. A separate scullery and laundry adds to the home''s functionality and comfort.

Accommodation comprises five luxurious bedrooms, each boasting its own beautifully appointed en-suite bathroom, ensuring ultimate comfort, privacy, and convenience for family and guests alike.
