Exceptional Golf Estate Living in Secure Vanderbijlpark

Discover an exceptional residential property within the prestigious Emfuleni Golf Estate in Vanderbijlpark, South Africa. This secure estate offers an unparalleled lifestyle, featuring a country club and a golf course, ensuring a tranquil and active environment for residents. The property boasts a commanding presence, reflecting the quality of its surroundings.

Spanning 400 sqm on a 581 sqm erf, this home is designed for comfort and functionality. An inviting entrance hall leads to an open-plan layout, featuring a lounge and a familyTV room. Two modern kitchens with a pantry cater to diverse culinary needs. Accommodation includes thirty-one bedrooms, three with en-suite bathrooms, plus a guest toilet, totaling 3.5 bathrooms. A dedicated study, laundry room, and air conditioning enhance daily living.

Outdoor living is a delight with a private swimming pool, spacious patio, and balcony, perfect for enjoying the South African climate. A built-in braai and dedicated braai room are ideal for entertaining. The well-maintained garden and paved areas complement the exterior, while two garages provide secure parking. Pets are welcome. Security is paramount within this golf estate, benefiting from 24-hour security, an access gate, and a security post. Modern conveniences include fibre connectivity and a battery inverter, ensuring uninterrupted power and high-speed internet.
