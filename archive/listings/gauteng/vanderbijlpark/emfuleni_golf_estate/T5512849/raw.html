Executive Golf Estate Living at Its Finest

A STATEMENT RESIDENCE WHERE LUXURY, SPACE, AND GOLF ESTATE LIVING COME TOGETHER IN PERFECT HARMONY!

Perfectly positioned on a rare double stand within one of the area''s most sought-after and prestigious golf estates, this exceptional double storey residence commands picturesque views across the 4th fairway and delivers a lifestyle defined by luxury, sophistication, and effortless entertaining.

Designed with meticulous attention to detail and finished to the highest standards, every aspect of this remarkable home has been carefully curated to offer the perfect balance of elegance, comfort, privacy, and functionality. Expansive open-plan living spaces flow seamlessly throughout the home, creating an inviting atmosphere ideal for both relaxed family living and grand-scale entertaining.

At the heart of the residence lies a beautifully appointed chef''s kitchen, complemented by multiple reception areas, while the indoor wine and braai room provides the perfect setting for year-round entertaining. Every room has been thoughtfully designed to maximize space, natural light, and the spectacular golf course outlook.

The accommodation is equally impressive, offering five luxurious bedrooms, including two exquisite en-suite retreats, complemented by two additional full bathrooms and two conveniently located guest cloakrooms. A dedicated home gym and designated entertainment area further enhance the home''s exceptional lifestyle offering.

Stepping outside, the property continues to impress with beautifully manicured gardens, expansive decking, and tranquil outdoor living spaces designed to embrace the estate''s scenic surroundings. A borehole and automated irrigation system ensure the grounds remain lush and pristine throughout the year.
