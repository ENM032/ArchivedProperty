3 Bedroom House in Emfuleni Golf Estate

Contact agent for street address

Modern Golf Estate Living lock Up and Go

Nestled within the prestigious Emfuleni Golf Estate in Vanderbijlpark, South Africa, this modern residential property offers exceptional curb appeal. Its attractive exterior features warm-toned textured walls, terracotta roof tiles, and elegant stone cladding. Spanning a generous 600 sqm erf with a substantial 310 sqm floor size, the home welcomes you with an inviting entrance hall leading into an open-plan lounge and dining room, perfect for entertaining. A functional kitchen with a separate laundry area, a versatile study, and a cozy fireplace complete the main living spaces, complemented by a dedicated braai room.

The residence comprises three comfortable bedrooms, including a luxurious en-suite, and a second well-appointed bathroom. Each bedroom features air conditioning for optimal climate control and extensive built-in wardrobes. Modern bathrooms boast sleek finishes, including freestanding bathtubs, walk-in showers with rain heads, and floating toilets, all set against neutral tiling. Outdoors, a triple garage, paved driveway, and a beautifully landscaped garden with a swimming pool create a private oasis. This secure golf estate provides access to a golf course, clubhouse, tennis court, and a jetty. Enhanced by 24-hour security, electric fencing, a battery inverter, water tanks, and fibre connectivity, this home ensures peace of mind and modern convenience.

This exclusive lifestyle estate within Vanderbijlpark combines serene natural surroundings with premium amenities. This sought-after security estate provides a tranquil environment for families and golf enthusiasts.
