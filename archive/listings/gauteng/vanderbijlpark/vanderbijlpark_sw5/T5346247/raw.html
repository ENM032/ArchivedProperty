Elegant Luxury Residence in a Prime Neighbourhood

Discover refined living in this stunningly renovated home, where contemporary sophistication meets timeless comfort in one of the area''s most sought-after locations.

The residence boasts four generous bedrooms, with the master suite serving as a true sanctuary - complete with a luxurious ensuite bathroom, expansive walk-in wardrobe, air conditioning, fireplace and a mounted TV for ultimate relaxation.

Two additional full bathrooms ensure effortless convenience for family and guests alike.

At the centre of this exceptional home lies a breathtaking newly designed kitchen, featuring a premium gas stove, sleek Caesarstone countertops, and a striking central island that flows effortlessly into the expansive open-plan lounge and dining space.

A warm fireplace graces the main loungedining area, creating the perfect setting for cosy evenings, while a separate TV room with soaring vaulted ceilings and a second fireplace adds extra warmth and character.

A quiet, dedicated study offers the ideal workspace, and a versatile outside room with its own ensuite provides perfect accommodation for staff.

Practicality meets abundance with four garages, double carport, extra parking, and generous storage throughout.

Step outside to your private oasis: a crystal-clear sparkling heated pool, inviting fire pit, and meticulously landscaped gardens designed for effortless outdoor entertaining.

Complete peace of mind comes courtesy of comprehensive CCTV security, beams, electric fencing.

Off grid water supply with borehole and 2200L Jojo tank connected to the sprinkler system and house.

For energy saving this home has a 300L solar geyser.
