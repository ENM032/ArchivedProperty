EXCLUSIVE MANDATE - Modern Family Home with Pool and Lifestyle Amenities in Vanderbijlpark This well-appointed three-bedroom residence in Vanderbijlpark SW 5 offers comfortable family living with thoughtful design and practical amenities. Set on a generous 1,504 sqm erf, the property provides ample space for outdoor enjoyment and entertaining. The home features two full bathrooms, two lounges, a separate dining room, and a modern kitchen, creating flexible living arrangements for families of all sizes.

The property is enhanced by a range of lifestyle features designed for contemporary living. A swimming pool provides recreational opportunities, whilst the gym facility supports an active lifestyle. The open-plan design maximizes natural light and creates a sense of spaciousness throughout the living areas. Additional features include air conditioning for year-round comfort, a built-in braai for outdoor entertaining, and a fireplace for cozy evenings.

Practical amenities include two garages and two parking spaces, ensuring secure vehicle storage. The property benefits from battery inverter technology, offering energy efficiency and reliability. A laundry room and storage facilities provide essential functionality, whilst the patio and well-maintained garden with irrigation system create attractive outdoor spaces. A wendy house adds versatility for additional storage needs.
