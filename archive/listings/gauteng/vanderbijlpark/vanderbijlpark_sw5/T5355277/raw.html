Modern Family Home in Secure Vanderbijlpark Gated Community

Discover this inviting residential property nestled within a secure, gated community in Vanderbijlpark SW 5, Vanderbijlpark, South Africa. This home offers a harmonious blend of comfort and modern living, ideal for families seeking a tranquil yet connected lifestyle. The area provides a peaceful environment while maintaining convenient access to local amenities.

The interior boasts a spacious open-plan layout, seamlessly connecting the living, dining, and kitchen areas. The modern kitchen is a focal point, featuring an integrated island with bar seating, perfect for casual meals or entertaining. Large windows throughout ensure abundant natural light, complementing the comfortable living spaces. An air conditioning unit enhances comfort, while the overall design promotes a fluid and engaging atmosphere for daily life.

This residence comprises three well-appointed bedrooms, each designed for comfort and functionality. Three en-suite bathrooms provide privacy and convenience, alongside an additional guest toilet. The bedrooms offer generous space, with some featuring dedicated work zones and extensive storage solutions. The bathrooms showcase modern designs, including walk-in showers and a luxurious corner jacuzzi tub, creating a spa-like retreat within your home.

Outdoor living is catered for with a private garden and a built-in braai, perfect for entertaining. The property also includes a balcony, offering additional outdoor space. Parking is ample with a double garage. For peace of mind, the home is situated within a gated community, benefiting from an access gate and security gate. Sustainability is addressed with solar panels, and modern connectivity is ensured with fibre internet access. Pets are allowed, making this a truly family-friendly abode.
