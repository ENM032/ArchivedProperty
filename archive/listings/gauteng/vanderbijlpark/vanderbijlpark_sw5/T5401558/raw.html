5 Bedroom House in Vanderbijlpark SW5

Contact agent for street address

Spacious Golf Estate Home with Pool and Modern Living

This exceptional 5-bedroom residence is situated within a prestigious golf estate in Vanderbijlpark SW 5, offering a blend of modern design and comfortable living. The property presents an inviting kerb appeal, hinting at the spacious interiors and luxurious outdoor amenities within.

Step inside to discover an expansive open-plan layout, seamlessly connecting the living and dining areas. The home boasts four lounges and a dedicated dining room, providing ample space for relaxation and entertaining. The modern kitchen is a highlight, featuring a large island with seating, a gas stove, and stylish cabinetry. Large glass doors ensure abundant natural light and create a fluid transition to the outdoor spaces, complemented by quality tiled flooring and recessed lighting.

Accommodation comprises five well-appointed bedrooms and seven bathrooms one bathroom is the main en-suite, 3 other bathrooms and 3 guest toilets, offering privacy and comfort for all residents and guests.

The outdoor areas are designed for ultimate enjoyment. A private swimming pool, complete with a wooden deck and retractable awning, provides a perfect setting for leisure. The spacious patio features built-in seating and a central fire pit, ideal for social gatherings. Enjoy scenic views from the balcony, enhancing the indoor-outdoor living experience. Amenities include a private gym, 2 built-in braais 1 wood and 1 gas as well as a winter braai room and a wine room, a cozy fireplace, and three garages, ensuring convenience and luxury.

Located within a secure estate with access gate, this property offers peace of mind. Pets are allowed, making it a family-friendly environment. Practical features such as air conditioning and a borehole contribute to comfortable and efficient living.
