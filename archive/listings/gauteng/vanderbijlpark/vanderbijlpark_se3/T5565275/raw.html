TRANQUIL FAMILY LIVING IN AN EXCLUSIVE GATED CUL-DE-SAC IN SE3

OWNER ASKING R 3 199 000

CONSIDERING BEST OFFER ABOVE R 2 499 000

Tucked away within an exclusive gated cul-de-sac of just five homes in the highly sought-after suburb of Vanderbijlpark SE3, this architecturally designed residence offers an exceptional combination of privacy, security and timeless family living. Immaculately maintained and move-in ready, every detail has been carefully considered to create a home that is as practical as it is beautiful.

Stepping inside, youre immediately welcomed by warm, inviting living spaces designed to flow effortlessly from one to the next. At the heart of the home lies a spectacular sunlit atrium, where natural light pours through the glazed roof onto a beautifully positioned jacuzzi, creating one of the homes most distinctive architectural features. The atrium connects seamlessly to the indoor braai and entertainment area, creating a unique space that can be enjoyed throughout the year.

The homes thoughtful architectural design has been carefully planned to embrace the changing seasons, welcoming abundant natural sunlight during the colder winter months while providing increased shade and comfort throughout summer.

The lounge and TV room each feature their own fireplace, creating warm and inviting spaces to relax during the cooler months, while large sliding doors establish an effortless connection between the indoor living areas, the covered patio and the beautifully maintained garden.

The kitchen has been thoughtfully designed for both everyday family living and entertaining, offering granite countertops, a gas hob, ample cabinetry and a separate scullery. A cleverly positioned server window opens directly into the entertainment area, making hosting family and friends both practical and enjoyable.

One of the homes most distinctive features is the custom-crafted Oregon pine bookcase and breakfastcoffee nook, handcrafted from Oregon pine salvaged during the demolition of one of the original sprung dance floors of Vanderbijlparks early days. Seamlessly connected to the kitchen, this bespoke feature adds warmth, character and a unique sense of local history to the homes contemporary design.

Accommodation comprises four spacious bedrooms within the main residence, three of which enjoy the luxury of their own en-suite bathrooms. Underfloor heating extends throughout the home, including the bathrooms, ensuring year-round comfort, while three air conditioning units provide additional climate control where needed.

The fifth bedroom is currently operated as a successful B&B suite, complete with its own private bathroom, offering excellent income-generating potential or comfortable accommodation for extended family and guests.

Outside, the beautifully maintained garden creates a peaceful setting for everyday family life. A covered patio with a striking stone table provides the perfect place to entertain, while gated access from the backyard leads directly into a large community park maintained by residents, offering children a secure and expansive space to play just beyond your own garden gate.

Designed with both sustainability and convenience in mind, the home is equipped with a comprehensive 10 kVA solar power system, a 5 000-litre JoJo water tank supplying the propertys automatic irrigation system and providing backup water, as well as an automated double garage with an integrated work station and extensive built-in cupboards.

Security is exceptional, with the property benefiting from its location within a private gated cul-de-sac, complete with an automated entrance gate and CCTV surveillance. Additional peace of mind is provided by an alarm system, electric fencing, perimeter security beams and CCTV cameras positioned around the home, including monitoring of the entrance gate to the cul-de-sac.
