4 Bedroom House in Vanderbijlpark SE3

Contact agent for street address
4 2 2 1 050 m²

4 bedroom home in SE3 with borehole

Spacious 4-bedroom family home in serene SE 3 with off grid borehole connected to house and Solar.

Perfect for nature lovers, this charming face-brick home in tranquil SE 3 offers a warm entrance leading to a formal lounge with a jet master fireplace on the right and a TV/family room with a built-in bar and pool-access sliding door on the left.

The open-plan dining area connects to a melamine kitchen with a gas stove, blinds, and a scullery.

Four generous bedrooms, three with pool-access sliding doors, include a spacious main bedroom with a walk-in closet, dressing table, loft, and pool access.

Features include:

Lapa with kitchenette

Wooden guest toilet and basin

Double automated garage with storeroom

Wendy House

Borehole with Emfuleni registration and irrigation

Gas geyser with control panel

Solar panel with 2x 5kW batteries and optional 10kW inverter

Heated pool

HARMONY STRATEGIES (PTY) LTD trading as RE/MAX Triangle (Vanderbijlpark) operates in terms of a franchise agreement with RE/MAX of Southern Africa.
