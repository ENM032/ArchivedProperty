Spacious Family Home in Vanderbijlpark SE 1 with Entertainment.

This substantial 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom family residence is ideally situated in an established suburban area. Offering a generous 780 sqm erf, this property presents an excellent opportunity for comfortable family living and entertaining within a well-regarded community.

The interior boasts an impressive array of living spaces designed for versatility and comfort. Discover three distinct lounges, including a spacious area with natural light and durable tiled flooring, and another featuring characterful exposed wooden beams and brick accent walls. An open-plan layout seamlessly connects to a dedicated recreation area, complete with a pool table, perfect for social gatherings. The traditional dining room, illuminated by a skylight, provides an inviting space for meals. The functional kitchen offers ample cream-colored cabinetry, extensive countertops, a double sink, a gas hob, and a built-in oven, catering to all culinary needs.

Accommodation comprises four well-proportioned bedrooms, each offering a private retreat. The bedrooms feature wood-look laminate flooring, with one benefiting from an air conditioning unit and extensive built-in wooden wardrobes for ample storage. The three bathrooms are conveniently located to serve the household.

Further enhancing this home''s appeal are practical features such as a dedicated laundry area and parking for up to six vehicles. The property includes a built-in braai area, ideal for outdoor entertaining. Security is a consideration, with visible security bars on windows and CCTV cameras in key areas providing peace of mind. Pets are permitted, making this a welcoming environment for the entire family.
