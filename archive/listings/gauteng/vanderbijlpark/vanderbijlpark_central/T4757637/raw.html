3 Bedroom House in Vanderbijlpark Central

Contact agent for street address
3 2 2 400 m²

Prevalent Riverspray Lifestyle Estate. An exclusive residence, meticulously designed and built with the best finishes and floor plan for the keen entertainer.

Perfectly low in maintenance, with a grand lifestyle on offer.

This beautiful family home offers a unique and warm curb-appeal. A spectacular rose garden surrounds this home, absolutely breath-taking.

Vast open plan reception rooms, a stately kitchen fitted with solid wood and quartz tops. High end appliances, scullery and laundry space with a pantry.

The reception rooms open out onto an intimate outdoor space, covered patio, boma area and peaceful views.

The first floor features a lounge, three airy bedrooms and two contemporary bathrooms.

The estate offers excellent features and amenities- including rugby fields, ponds, tennis courts and swimming pool. Multiple recreation spaces, slipway and jetty''s- to name a few!

Worth a visit if it is a secure, low maintenance but lavish lifestyle you are looking for.

Pre-qualified buyers welcome.
