Charming Family Home in Vanderbijlpark CE7 with Ample Parking

Discover a welcoming family home situated in the established suburb of Vanderbijlpark CE7. This property offers a practical and comfortable living environment, ideal for those seeking a well-located residence. With its appealing frontage and functional design, it presents a solid opportunity for homeowners looking for convenience and space.

The interior of this residence features a thoughtfully designed layout, providing ample space for daily living. It includes a dedicated lounge area, perfect for relaxation and entertaining guests. The property also boasts a functional kitchen, ready to cater to your culinary needs, ensuring convenience for the household.

Accommodation comprises three comfortable 3 bedrooms, offering private retreats for all family members. Complementing these are three well-appointed bathrooms, with one being an en-suite, providing added privacy and convenience. These spaces are designed to ensure comfort and practicality for residents.

Externally, the property provides a compact yet functional outdoor area. Parking is well-catered for with two dedicated garages, alongside additional space for up to four vehicles in total, ensuring ample secure parking for residents and visitors alike.

For peace of mind, the home is equipped with an alarm system, enhancing the security of the premises. While focusing on essential safety measures, this feature contributes to a secure living environment within the community.

Vanderbijlpark, a prominent city, offers a range of amenities and services. The suburb of Vanderbijlpark CE provides a residential setting with convenient access to local facilities, making it a desirable location for families.
