Charming Family Residence in Vanderbijlpark SE 6

Discover this inviting three-bedroom, two-bathroom family home situated in the sought-after suburban area of Vanderbijlpark SE 6. Boasting a substantial erf size of 805 square metres, this property offers ample space for comfortable living and outdoor enjoyment. The residence presents a welcoming facade, complemented by practical features designed for a convenient lifestyle.

Inside, the home features a well-appointed kitchen, perfect for daily meal preparation and culinary pursuits. Adjacent to this, a dedicated dining room provides an ideal setting for family meals and entertaining guests. The property also includes a comfortable lounge area, offering versatile spaces for relaxation and recreation. These distinct living zones ensure that every member of the household has their own area to unwind.

The three generously sized bedrooms provide peaceful retreats, each designed to accommodate family needs. The two bathrooms are functionally laid out, catering to the demands of a busy household. With a focus on practicality, the home ensures a comfortable and efficient living environment for its occupants. Security is also a consideration, with burglar bars installed for peace of mind.

Externally, the property benefits from a well-maintained garden, offering a private outdoor sanctuary for leisure and play. The presence of paving adds to the neatness and accessibility of the exterior. Furthermore, the property includes two garages, providing secure parking and additional storage solutions. This pet-friendly residence is ideally positioned within a suburban setting, offering a tranquil atmosphere while remaining accessible to local amenities and community facilities. This home represents an excellent opportunity for families seeking a balanced and comfortable lifestyle in Vanderbijlpark.

There is an extra area that can be a potential flatlet as it includes a full bathroom and space for a kitchen , also has a sperate door entrance. It can also has potential to be turned into an entertainment area

We invite you to arrange a viewing to fully appreciate the potential and charm of this delightful property.
