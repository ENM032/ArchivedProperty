3 Bedroom House in Vanderbijlpark SE2

Contact agent for street address
3 2 1 759 m²

Spacious 3 Bedroom Family Home in SE 2. This spacious and versatile 3-bedroom family home, offers the perfect opportunity to comfortably combine home and work.

As you enter through the front door, you are welcomed by a private studio or study with its own separate entrance, making it ideal for a home office, consulting room, or creative workspace.

The heart of the home is a warm and sunny open-plan living space, where the dining area flows seamlessly into a well-appointed kitchen featuring elegant glass display cabinets, a centre island, and ample cupboard space. A separate, generously sized scullery provides additional storage and keeps the kitchen neat and functional.

The home offers three well-sized bedrooms, all with comfortable living space, and two bathrooms. One bathroom is fitted with a shower, basin, and toilet, while the second features a bath, basin, and toilet, ensuring convenience for the whole family.

Completing the property is a single garage with an additional storage room, providing plenty of space for tools, equipment, or extra household storage.

With its practical layout, business rights, and welcoming atmosphere, this property is an excellent investment opportunity
