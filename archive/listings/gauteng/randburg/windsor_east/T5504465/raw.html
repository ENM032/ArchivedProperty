Discover comfortable and secure living in this inviting 1-bedroom apartment, ideally situated within a well-maintained security complex in Windsor East, Randburg. This residential sale offers a balanced lifestyle, combining modern conveniences with peace of mind in a suburban setting. The property is thoughtfully designed to cater to contemporary living, making it an excellent opportunity for individuals or couples seeking a convenient and secure home.

Spanning 83 square metres, the apartment features a well-proportioned layout that includes one spacious bedroom and a single bathroom. The open-plan design enhances the sense of space, creating a fluid connection between the living areas. An entrance hall welcomes you into the home, leading to a comfortable lounge area. Modern amenities such as fibre internet connectivity are already in place, ensuring seamless digital access for work or leisure.

Step outside to enjoy the private balcony, offering scenic views that provide a tranquil escape. Residents also benefit from access to a communal swimming pool, perfect for relaxation and recreation. Practical features include a dedicated single garage and an additional parking space, addressing all your parking needs. The complex prioritises safety with 24-hour security, a security post, and a secure gate, ensuring a protected environment for all residents.
