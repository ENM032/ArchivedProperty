LINDEN LIVING WITH SPACE TO BREATHE - 1962sqm

Set in a beautifully treed garden with flowing lawns, this spacious face brick family home offers comfortable living with excellent indoor-outdoor flow. A large, covered patio with an expansive retractable awning and sparking swimming pool overlooks the garden, creating the perfect space for entertaining and relaxed family living. Tiled throughout the living areas, with carpeted bedrooms and charming cottage-pane windows.

The home welcomes you into an entrance hall, with an adjacent generous formal lounge and interleading large games room that opens onto the covered patio and swimming pool.

An open-plan lounge and dining area is filled with natural light from a skylight and flows seamlessly into a well-equipped kitchen featuring granite countertops, a 4-plate electric hob with extractor, oven, double sink, breakfast counter, pantry cupboard, and ample cabinetry. The interleading scullery offers a prep sink, space for three appliances, and direct access to the backyard via a stable door. A separate large study, with a separate guest bathroom with a shower, toilet and basin and easy access to the covered carport, makes this the ideal work from home opportunity.

Sleeping accommodation comprises five bedrooms positioned along a light-filled passage with skylight and ample linen storage. Four bedrooms open onto the patio and swimming pool and are serviced by a family bathroom and a separate shower, basin and toilet. The spacious main bedroom features a pajama lounge, walk-in cupboard, and full en-suite bathroom.

A double garage, one offering a convenient drive-through access to the backyard, provides both practicality and versatility. Covered dedicated spaces accommodates a trailer with ease, a single washing trough, a heat pump servicing the geysers and a covered wash line.

The home is well secured with Maxidor security gates fitted to all exterior doors, while the street enjoys the added benefit of camera surveillance, supported through the residents security contribution.

Further enhancing the property is an excellent solar power system comprising 10 solar panels, a 5KVA inverter, and a two 51.2V 100AH batteries, offering improved energy efficiency and peace of mind.
