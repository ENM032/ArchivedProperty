4 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
4 3 7 1 642 m²

Charming Linden Family Home. Set on a large 1,636m² stand in established Linden, this well-maintained family home offers space, solid infrastructure and practical features that make everyday living easier.

The home includes four generously sized bedrooms and three bathrooms, two of which are en-suite. The room sizes throughout are spacious, allowing flexibility for growing families or those working from home.

The main lounge and dining area open onto the patio, creating an easy indoor-outdoor flow. A separate sunken lounge can be used as a TV room, study or additional entertainment space.

The kitchen is large and functional, finished with quartz countertops and plenty of cupboard space. It includes bottled gas cooking, a reverse osmosis water purification system, and a separate scullery with space and connections for both a dishwasher and washing machine.

Outside, the property offers a heated swimming pool, two water features and a sizeable garden. There is ample parking with a three-car garage and a large driveway.

Energy and backup features include:

-14 solar panels

- 8kW inverter

- 51kWh battery system

- Solar system on rental contract 1 year remaining on 3-year term rental to be taken over by purchaser

- Two geysers

- Security features include an alarm system, external beams, electric fencing, burglar bars and security gates.

Additional features:

- Staff accommodation

- Feature zinc roofing at the entrance hall

This is a spacious, well-equipped property in a sought-after area, suited to buyers looking for comfort, practical upgrades and long-term convenience.
