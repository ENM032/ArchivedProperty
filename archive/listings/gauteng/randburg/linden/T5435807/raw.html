3 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
3 2 4 808 m²

Move in ready family home . This property offers a well-balanced layout with comfortable, practical living spaces throughout.

The home features 3 bedrooms and 2.5 bathrooms, including a main bedroom with its own en-suite for added privacy. A separate guest toilet adds everyday convenience for visitors.

A dedicated study provides the ideal space for working from home.

The kitchen is fitted with a gas stove and is complemented by a separate scullery, keeping the main space neat and functional.

Living areas include a lounge and dining area that flow naturally, creating a welcoming space for both relaxing and entertaining.

In terms of efficiency, the property is equipped with 15 solar panels, a 2,500L municipal backup water system, and a grey water system,helping reduce running costs while supporting sustainable living.

Additional features include a pond, a double carport, outside shower and a built toolshed that can easily be converted into staff accommodation.

Set on a generous 808 sqm stand, this home offers a practical layout with plenty of potential.
