Luxury Family Home, Solar Backup & Subdivision Potential

Welcome to a home where space, light and family living come together beautifully.

From the moment you step through the front door, you''re welcomed by breathtaking open-plan living spaces. The exposed beam ceilings create an incredible sense of volume, while natural light floods every corner of the home.

At the heart of it all is the stunning designer kitchen, complete with a premium SMEG gas stove, making it the perfect place to cook, entertain and gather with family and friends. The kitchen flows effortlessly into the spacious dining and living areas, all opening onto a generous patio where you can relax and enjoy beautiful city views.

For quieter moments, a separate TV lounge with a cosy fireplace offers the perfect retreat. There''s also a versatile games room, study or additional family loungea wonderful space where children can play while remaining within easy view of the adults.

The accommodation is equally impressive. The home offers four generous en-suite bedrooms, providing everyone with their own private sanctuary. Three spacious family bedrooms comfortably accommodate double beds and are all fitted with air conditioning for year-round comfort.

The luxurious main suite is a true haven, featuring a walk-in dressing room and an elegant en-suite bathroom with double vanities, an extra size walk-in shower and a deep soaking bath designed for complete relaxation.

Outside, the lifestyle continues. Set on an exceptionally large stand, the property offers a sparkling swimming pool, expansive gardens for children and pets to enjoy and exciting subdivision potential for future return on investment.

The staff accommodation also presents excellent cottage conversion potential, offering exceptional flexibility. Whether you''re looking for a private work-from-home office, a consulting studio, accommodation for extended family, a guest suite, or an income-generating cottage, this versatile space can easily adapt to your lifestyle and future needs.

Practicality is equally impressive, with solar backup power ensuring uninterrupted comfort and an extra-large double garage providing secure parking for two vehicles, along with generous lock-up storage space.

Completing this exceptional home is excellent security, offering peace of mind for the whole family.

If you''ve been searching for a home that combines luxury, comfort, space, exceptional lifestyle and future potential, this is one you simply have to experience.
