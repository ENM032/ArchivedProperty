6 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
6 6.5 6 1 015 m²

Bespoke Property in Sought-After Linden - 1015sqm

BY APPOINTMENT ONLY.

This exceptional double-story home combines elegance, style & functionality in the heart of Linden. Perfectly positioned, this property offers an abundance of space, sophisticated features & luxurious living.

The covered front entrance opens into a grand double volume entrance hall with an adjacent a guest toilet. The open-plan layout invites natural light throughout, A spacious lounge featuring cottage-pane doors that open to a wooden deck. A large study adds flexibility for working from home. The spacious guest suite is a true retreat, featuring a generous dressing area and a stunning en-suite bathroom. Another guest suite adjacent to the kitchen offers a lounge, kitchenette, bedroom & full bathroom. With a separate entrance, this versatile area of the house is ideal for a work from home business. The heart of the home is the farm style chef''s kitchen. A 5 plate gas stove & oven, a large walk-in pantry a separate scullery with space for 4 appliances. The kitchen seamlessly flows into the open-plan dining area and covered patio, with a built-in braai & pizza oven, overlooking a sparkling splash pool. The private, landscaped garden features a herb garden, firepit area & Wendy house.

Upstairs, features a spacious main bedroom leading onto a private balcony. The en-suite bathroom has a double vanity, jacuzzi bath & double shower and toilet. The 2nd spacious full en-suite bedroom includes a study nook. Separating the two rooms is a landing with a linen cupboard.

A large private cottage with its own external entrance or via the main house, is ideal for extended family, guests, or an income-generating unit. The open-plan living space includes a full kitchen with a 5-plate gas hob, electric oven, dining area and lounge. 2 bedrooms, a full main-en-suite, while the 2nd bedroom is serviced by a separate bathroom.

Additional features include, gas heaters in all bathrooms & kitchens, 8 solar panels, 200 amphr lithium battery, backup generator, beams, electric fencing, CCTV camera, alarm system, double garage with storeroom & motorized door, 2 separate entrances at the front of the property with motorized gates, secure off-street parking. Wifi connected.

Pet Friendly.
