4 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
4 2.5 4 1 086 m²

Style, Space and Spectacular Views 1086sqm

VIEWING STRICTLY BY APPOINTMENT ONLY.

A charming entrance, with modern Moroccan-inspired tiles, sets the tone for this inviting family residence. Step through the front door into a tiled entrance hall, complete with a conveniently positioned guest cloakroom featuring a toilet and hand basin.

The entrance hall flows seamlessly into the heart of the home a bright and spacious open-plan kitchen and dining area enhanced by striking architectural open beams that create a wonderful sense of volume and character. The well-appointed kitchen boasts granite counter tops, an American-style island with a built-in double sink, space for 2 undercounter appliances and a breakfast counter, perfect for casual family meals or entertaining guests. The generous prep counter is fitted with an electric four-plate glass hob and under-counter oven as well as space for an additional appliance, while the generous storage space makes it a dream for any home chef. Adding to the kitchen''s unique appeal is a conversation area, allowing the cook to remain part of the social interaction while preparing meals. The kitchen provides direct access to the courtyard, where you will find a practical laundry area with space for two appliances, staff accommodation with an en-suite shower, toilet and hand basin, as well as convenient access to the double Hollywood garage with a motorised garage door.

The expansive kitchen and dining area opens effortlessly onto a large covered patio overlooking the sparkling swimming pool and beautifully maintained terraced garden, creating the perfect indoor-outdoor lifestyle setting for family gatherings and entertaining throughout the year. Tucked away for added convenience and luxury is a private outdoor shower ideal for cooling off or rinsing off after a refreshing swim on a warm summer''s day.

The formal lounge flows naturally from the dining area also enjoying access to the patio, enhancing the home''s exceptional sense of space and connectivity. Beneath the staircase, a generously sized storeroom provides valuable additional storage space.

The lower level accommodation comprises three well-proportioned family bedrooms, two of which enjoy direct access to the front garden. These bedrooms are serviced by a separate toilet with hand basin and a spacious family bathroom featuring a bath, shower and two hand basins.

Upstairs is a private sanctuary reserved for the king and queen of the home. A spacious pyjama lounge leads into the luxurious main en-suite. The expansive bedroom with a skylight allowing sunlight to stream into the bedroom as well as the passage leading to the family bedrooms incorporates an elegant open-plan bathroom with a freestanding bath, walk-in shower and two hand basins, while a separate private toilet room includes a toilet, bidet and hand basin.

There can be few better ways to start the day than enjoying your first cup of coffee on the private balcony off the main suite while taking in the breathtaking panoramic views that stretch beyond the horizon.

This exceptional home offers the perfect balance of stylish family living, generous accommodation and superb entertaining spaces in a setting designed to be enjoyed for years to come.

Additional features include : Victron Energy inverter 2 x Pylontech batteries 2 x alarm systems 7 security cameras with 4 indoor screens electric fencing security cameras in street a minimal contribution from residents in street required. Pet friendly.
