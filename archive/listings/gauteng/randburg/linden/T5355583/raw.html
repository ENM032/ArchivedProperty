3 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
3 2 6 952 m²

A Private, Single-Level Family Home with Exceptional Parking, Sustainable Features, and Outdoor Living. This well-positioned single-level home is set on a generous 1,000 square metre panhandle erf, offering privacy, space, and excellent off-street parking. The property features three well-sized bedrooms and two bathrooms, all on one level with minimal stairs, making it both practical and accessible.

Designed with vehicle space in mind, the home offers covered parking for up to six vehicles, with ample additional off-street parking available ideal for families, entertainers, or those with caravans, trailers, or work vehicles.

The home is energy-efficient and future-ready, with a solar system included in the sale. This consists of 15 solar panels, an 8.5kW inverter, and two 5kW batteries, providing reliable power and reduced running costs. A gas stove, operated on bottled gas, adds further efficiency to the kitchen.

Outdoor living is a key highlight, with a swimming pool and an inviting outside living area featuring a built-in braai, perfect for entertaining and relaxed family gatherings. The property also includes a well-planned water system with three water tanks: one 5,000-litre tank connected to a borehole and filtration system, and two 2,500-litre tanks for rainwater harvested from gutter drainage.

In addition to the main living areas, the home offers a separate scullery and laundry space, which is not photographed in the advert. A further bonus is a separate cottage with its own bathroom, positioned off the courtyard, ideal for extended family, guests, or potential rental income.

This property combines space, functionality, and sustainability in a private setting, making it a versatile and highly attractive home.
