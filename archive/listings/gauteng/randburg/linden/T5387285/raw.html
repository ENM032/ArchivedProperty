4 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
4 4.5 4 1 736 m²

LUXURY, SOPHISTICATION, AND CONTEMPORARY LIFESTYLE 1736sqm

The property has been thoughtfully conceived to offer a premium quality modern lifestyle. It stands out as a distinguished expression of refined taste and design integrity.

A private, secure entrance leads into a manicured paved garden, setting the tone for this exceptional home. A striking entrance with statement tiled flooring creates a strong visual axis, drawing the eye through to the terraced garden beyond.

Featuring a bright, generously proportioned open-plan kitchen and casual dining area with Caesarstone countertops. The kitchen offers an American-style island with prep basin and wooden worktop, five-plate gas hob, electric oven, extractor, space for three appliances and a double-door refrigerator. It enjoys direct access to the double garage, private courtyard and staff accommodation comprising a bedroom, bathroom with shower, basin and toilet.

Flowing seamlessly from the kitchen is a spectacular open-plan living space designed for both comfort and effortless entertaining. The formal dining area is warmed by a wood-burning combustion fireplace, creating an inviting ambiance for intimate dinners and special gatherings. The generously proportioned lounge extends through timber stacking doors to a magnificent sparkling pool and expansive entertainment area, blending indoor and outdoor living with ease.

The covered patio boasts two distinct lounge areas, a dedicated dining space, and a separate relaxation zone, offering versatility for every occasion. Perfectly positioned to overlook the manicured terraced garden, this exceptional setting embraces the ultimate outdoor lifestyle in style and sophistication. A well-sized bar, complete with a private guest toilet and granite vanity, is accessible from both the formal lounge and outdoor entertainment area.

Accommodation includes the principal bedroom - this luxurious retreat opens directly onto the pool, entertainment area and garden. It offers a generous walk in dressing room, a full en-suite bathroom with double vanity and an outdoor shower set within a tranquil atrium. Adjacent to the main bedroom is a private, compact home office, ideal for working remotely. The second bedroom features a gas fireplace, and en-suite bathroom featuring a bath, basin and toilet. Bedroom 3 has an en-suite bathroom with shower, basin and toilet, opening onto a private garden enclave.

Upstairs, a spacious guest suite provides a bedroom, lounge with gas fireplace, dining area and separate bathroom featuring a shower, basin with vanity and balconyperfect for quiet mornings.
