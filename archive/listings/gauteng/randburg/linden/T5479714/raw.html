7 Bedroom House in Linden

Contact agent for street address
7 3.5 2 1 185 m²

THE TOTAL PACKAGE SPACE TO LIVE, STYLE TO LOVE - 1185sqm

Tucked away in a quite panhandle, this beautifully maintained and much-loved home welcomes you with a charming lounge, where a bay window frames tranquil views of the front manicured garden and fills the space with natural light. Adjacent is an entertainment pub room, which opens onto the covered patio with built in braai, ideal for indoor-outdoor living.

The open-plan design seamlessly connects the lounge, dining area and kitchen, allowing for effortless entertaining. The kitchen is well-appointed with granite countertops, a prep basin, a 5-plate gas hob with extractor and space for a single-door fridge. A separate scullery offers a double sink, space for two appliances and direct access to the back garden where there is a convenient built in trough and counter. Additional features include a walk-in pantry and direct access to the double garage with automated double door.

A separate TV lounge, accessible from the dining area, can be closed off with sliding cottage-pane doors for added privacy and also opens onto the garden.

The bedroom wing includes three bedrooms, all overlooking the lush garden. The main en-suite features a dressing area and full bathroom, while a family bathroom services the remaining two bedrooms. A guest toilet is conveniently located off the passage.

Upstairs offers excellent accommodation for extended family or guests, featuring a cozy pajama lounge. This level includes four bedrooms sharing a bathroom, with the 2nd bedroom interleading into a spacious main bedroom. The fourth bedroom includes its own basin and vanity.

Additional features include private staff accommodation with en suite bathroom with separate access, a tranquil fire pit area, and pet-friendly gardens.

Security and convenience are well catered for with an inverter 5kW and lithium battery 5.5kWh, perimeter beams, electric fencing, alarm system, automated front gate as well as secure off-street parking.
