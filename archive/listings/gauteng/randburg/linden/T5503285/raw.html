3 Bedroom House in Linden

13a 13th street
3 3 2 660 m²

PRIVATE, POLISHED, PERFECT - 660sqm

OFFERS FROM R2 850 000

A compact 3 bedroomed property, ideally suited for a young professional couple. The property is situated in a quiet, private panhandle and conveniently close to one of Lindens top schools, this beautifully renovated property has been completed to a high standard, showcasing quality workmanship and tasteful contemporary finishes throughout. Its thoughtful reconfiguration of the living areas has created an open-plan layout that maximizes the homes modern, stylish appeal.

The tiled entrance hall welcomes you to the lounge with a sliding door, the dining area enhanced by a skylight, allowing sunlight to stream throughout the home.

The contemporary kitchen is thoughtfully designed with Quartz countertops, a 5-plate gas hob with extractor fan, electric oven, space for two appliances and a fridge. Perfect for both family living and entertaining, the kitchen overlooks not only the indoor living areas, but also the covered patio with a built-in braai, sparkling swimming pool and private fully walled garden.

For added convenience, a guest bathroom with shower, basin, and toilet is positioned off the passage.

The main bedroom features a full en-suite bathroom, while the second and third bedrooms are privately positioned behind a sliding door off the dining area, creating an ideal family or guest wing. These bedrooms share a full bathroom with bath, shower, vanity and toilet. A linen cupboard in the passage provides additional practical storage space.

Additional features include a double carport, CCTV and an access intercom system programmable to a cellphone. The property is also pet friendly.
