Secure 3-Bedroom Simplex in Sunninghill Gardens

Tucked away in a quiet street within a secure complex in sought-after Sunninghill Gardens, this neat and comfortable north-facing simplex offers easy, single-level living with excellent security. The spacious lounge and living area opens onto a sunny patio and private garden, creating a welcoming space for relaxed entertaining or enjoying the outdoors. The home features three north-facing bedrooms, two bathrooms, with the main bedroom offering air-conditioning and a full en-suite bathroom, as well as a neat, functional kitchen. A double automated garage provides secure parking and added convenience.

Enjoy the peace of mind of 24-hour security in a well-positioned complex, while being ideally located close to leading shopping centres, popular restaurants, reputable schools, medical facilities and major highways. With easy access to Sandton, Waterfall and surrounding business hubs, this home is an excellent choice for comfortable family living or a sound investment in a consistently popular area.
