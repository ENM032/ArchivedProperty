3 Bedroom Family Home with Solar Power, Security and Exceptional Outdoor Space

Offers from R2.1 million will be considered by the seller. Situated in a sought-after pocket of Paulshof, this charming freehold residence offers the perfect blend of comfort, privacy, security, and energy efficiency. Accessed via a private pan-handle driveway, the property enjoys a secluded setting with reduced street exposure, creating a peaceful and secure environment ideal for family living.

The home offers three generously proportioned bedrooms and two bathrooms, complemented by a newly renovated kitchen that serves as the heart of the home. Interior repainting is currently underway, further enhancing the property''s presentation and appeal.

Designed for easy living and entertaining, the spacious living areas flow effortlessly to a large manicured garden, providing ample space for children, pets, and outdoor enjoyment.

Adding significant value is the inclusion of a solar panel and inverter system, ensuring greater energy independence and peace of mind. Security has been carefully considered, with electric fencing, an alarm system, and armed response in place.

Additional features include a lock-up garage, covered carport, and ample visitor parking.

Conveniently located close to leading schools, shopping centres, medical facilities, major transport routes, and business hubs, this property offers the ideal balance between suburban tranquillity and urban convenience.
