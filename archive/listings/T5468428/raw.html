3 Bedroom House in Sunninghill

Contact agent for street address
3 2.5 6 540 m² 325 m²

Effortless Luxury and Secure Estate Living in Prestigious Villa di Castello, Sunninghill

Set within the highly desirable Villa di Castello estate, this elegant 3-bedroom home offers a seamless combination of comfort, convenience, and modern estate living in one of Johannesburgs most established northern suburbs.

Thoughtfully designed for both family living and effortless entertaining, the home offers three spacious bedrooms, including a beautifully appointed main bedroom with its own private en-suite bathroom. A full family bathroom together with a guest cloakroom comfortably services the rest of the home.

The open-plan lounge and dining area create a welcoming flow, extending effortlessly to a private patio and private gardenperfect for relaxed outdoor living, entertaining guests, or simply unwinding in peaceful surroundings.

A standout feature of the property is the separate outbuilding, offering a dedicated private study and laundry room. Whether used as a work-from-home office, hobby space, or additional functional area, this versatile addition adds exceptional value and practicality.

The home is exceptionally well-equipped with modern lifestyle essentials, including a municipal backup water system featuring a 2,500L JoJo tank and a 0.75 kW automatic pump that seamlessly switches to tank supply during water outagesoffering uninterrupted convenience and peace of mind.

Energy efficiency is enhanced by a 5 kVA hybrid inverter, lithium battery, and 12 solar panels in total. Eight 455W panels power the home approximately 3.6 kW PV, while an additional four 540W panels operate on a separate array dedicated to running the pool pump off-grid during the daydelivering smart, cost-effective living.

Additional features include fibre-ready connectivity, secure double garage parking, ample visitor parking, pet-friendly garden spaces, and access to a sparkling communal swimming pool within the beautifully maintained estate grounds.

Villa di Castello offers 24-hour access-controlled security, ensuring complete peace of mind and secure estate living at its finest.

Perfectly positioned on Katumba Road, the estate enjoys close proximity to Sunninghill Village Shopping Centre, top schools, medical facilities, and major routes including the N1 and Witkoppen Road.

A superb opportunity to secure refined estate living in a prime locationthis exceptional home is ready to welcome its next owner.
