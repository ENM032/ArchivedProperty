A Warm, Effortless Family Home in a Quiet Cul-de-Sac

Offers from R 2 250 000, asking R 2 400 000.

Tucked away in a peaceful cul-de-sac, this inviting family home offers the perfect balance of comfort, convenience, and easy living all within quick reach of major routes for seamless daily travel.

Designed with young families in mind, the home welcomes you into a light-filled, open-plan living space where the lounge and dining areas flow effortlessly together. This central hub extends out to an enclosed patio, creating a natural transition to the garden, sparkling swimming pool, and fire pit an ideal setting for relaxed weekends and easy entertaining.

The modern kitchen is both practical and stylish, featuring granite countertops, warm wood cabinetry, a gas hob, and space for three appliances, making everyday living as functional as it is enjoyable.

Accommodation includes three well-proportioned bedrooms, all bright and airy, with ample built-in storage. The main bedroom offers a private en-suite bathroom, creating a comfortable retreat at the end of the day.

Additional features enhance the homes appeal, including two automated garages, an outside bathroom, a JoJo water tank, and a Wendy house for extra storage. Security elements such as burglar bars and security gates provide added peace of mind.

Set in a sought-after, family-friendly location, this home delivers low-maintenance living without compromising on lifestyle.

A home that ticks all the right boxes ready to be enjoyed.
